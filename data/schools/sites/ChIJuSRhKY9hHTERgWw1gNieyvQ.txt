 ABOUT Us 
 BAAN Five MuayThai Gym is a friendly Muay Thai gym focused on fitness, strength, and overall well-being. 
We welcome everyone &mdash; from beginners to experienced trainees &mdash; whether you're Thai or from abroad.
 Our training combines authentic Muay Thai techniques with a fitness-based approach, helping you get stronger, fitter, and more confident in a supportive environment. 
At BAAN Five, we believe Muay Thai is not just about fighting, but about building a healthy and active lifestyle.
