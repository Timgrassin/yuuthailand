 Sprouts International
Kindergarten Bangkok
 Nurturing curiosity, inspiring creativity, and growing bright futures for every child to learn, explore, and shine.
 Nurture. Learn. Grow.
 Sprouts offers a warm, personal environment with small classes, bilingual learning, and fresh, healthy food—helping little ones bloom in every way.
 SMALL SCHOOL 
 We keep our kindergarten small and personal, welcoming just two groups of 12–18 children. This allows our teachers to truly get to know each child and provide the individual attention and guidance they need. With our low student-to-teacher ratio, we can support every child’s strengths and learning pace in a nurturing and attentive environment.
 ENGLISH & THAI 
 Our bilingual program gently immerses children in both English and Thai, helping them build confidence, strong communication skills, and cultural awareness. Early exposure to both languages gives children a solid foundation for future learning, whether they continue in an international or local school setting.
 HEALTHY FOOD 
 We believe that healthy bodies nurture healthy minds. Every meal at Sprouts is freshly prepared in our on-site kitchen using mostly organic, nutritious, and child-friendly ingredients—encouraging positive eating habits from an early age.
 Why Families Choose Sprouts
 A genuinely nurturing International Nursery and Kindergarten Bangkok where children feel safe, confident, and excited to learn.
 Our Center
 Sprouts International Kindergarten is nestled in a peaceful residential area on Sala Daeng Soi 1, just steps away from Lumpini Park. Conveniently accessible from Sathorn, Silom, and Rama 4 Road, as well as MRT Silom and BTS Sala Daeng, our location makes us an ideal choice for families seeking a quality kindergarten in Sathorn.
 OUR PHILOSOPHY
 We encourage children to learn at their own pace while developing independence, confidence, and a genuine joy in discovery. Through our play-based learning approach, children grow into optimistic, curious, and kind individuals—equipped with the skills and mindset for lifelong learning.
 THE SPACE
 Inspired by German Kindergarten standards, our space is thoughtfully designed to be quiet, safe, and nurturing. We use natural materials such as solid wood play structures, natural rubber flooring, and lead-free paints, with noise-reducing ceilings and an inviting outdoor play area that fosters creativity and exploration.
 FIELD TRIPS
 From visits to Lumpini Park, local temples, and libraries to hands-on cultural experiences, our children explore and connect with the world around them.
These regular outings help them develop curiosity, social awareness, and a sense of appreciation for the community they live in.

 FAQS
 We understand that choosing the right kindergarten is an important decision. Here are some common questions parents ask about life at Sprouts.
 What age groups do you accept? 
 We welcome children from 1.5 to 6 years old, divided into two small groups to ensure each child receives personal attention and care.
 What curriculum do you follow? 
 Sprouts offers a bilingual, play-based program combining English and Thai. Our approach focuses on curiosity, creativity, and hands-on exploration—helping children develop social, emotional, and academic skills naturally.
 How big are the classes? 
 We keep our school intentionally small, with only 12–18 children per group, so teachers can truly connect with and support each child.
 What kind of food do you provide? 
 We serve fresh, homemade meals prepared daily in our kitchen. Our menu is designed to be healthy, balanced, and delicious—encouraging children to enjoy good nutrition from an early age.
 Do you offer nap time and outdoor play? 
 Yes! Every day includes quiet rest time for younger children and regular outdoor play to promote physical activity, imagination, and connection with nature.
