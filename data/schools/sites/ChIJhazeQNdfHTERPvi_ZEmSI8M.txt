 WELCOME TO AMC BOXING
 AMC Boxing is a division of Muang Chaiya Muay Thai.
 We are an Australian-registered business with a focus on three key areas:
 • BOXING GEAR - Bringing you a selection of quality boxing gear.
• KNIFE STORE - A diverse range of premium knives.
• INFORMATION - Genuine advice on Muay Thai.
 Our website is here to offer you information and advice on TRAINING WITH and FIGHTING AGAINST the Thais in THAILAND.
 We have a range of boxing gear that has almost everything you will need to train and prepare for fighting in the world's greatest sport - Muay Thai.
