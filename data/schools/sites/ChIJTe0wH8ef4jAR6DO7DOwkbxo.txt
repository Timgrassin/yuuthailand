 Knight Legal International was established to provide comprehensive legal services aligned with the operations of Knight Corporation Holding Co., Ltd. and its affiliated companies. Our objective is to deliver the highest quality legal solutions that effectively meet every client’s needs. We offer a full range of legal services including immigration law, doing business in Thailand, visa and relocation assistance, marriage registration with foreign nationals, as well as civil and criminal law. 
 With over 25 years of experience in the legal field, our team of seasoned attorneys possesses deep knowledge and understanding of the complexities of both Thai and international law. We are committed to delivering the highest level of service and client satisfaction. Our strong reputation for credibility and legal expertise ensures that every matter is handled with the utmost professionalism and care.

 LEGAL PROTECTION 
 The PDPA applies to the collection, usage, and disclosure by a data controller or a data processor under conditions and agreements. For other purposes, data owners must give us consent on a specific use with clear objectives. Data subjects can withdraw their consent at any time when a breach of data privacy is found.
 WE TAKE CARE 
 To ensure that privacy policies are implemented properly within the company, we have a disciplined and systematic approach we conduct relevant reviews. Our evaluations are used to improve the effectiveness of the company’s risk management, control, reporting, and governance processes, also regarding the protection of personal data. 
 PRIVATE 
 Personal Data Protection Act B.E.2562 (2019), known as PDPA, was published in Thailand’s Royal Gazette on May 27, 2019, and came into effect in a move to protect the rights of a data subject, the obligations of a data controller and the penalties for non-compliance.
