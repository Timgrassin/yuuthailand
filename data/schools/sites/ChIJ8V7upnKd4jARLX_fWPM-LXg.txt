 Accounting & Tax 
 How can you help me set up a Thai company as a foreign investor?
 We guide you through the full company registration process, from choosing the right structure and preparing documents to completing registration and post-setup compliance. If BOI promotion is suitable, we can also help you plan for it from the start so your business is set up efficiently and legally. 
 Why should you consider BOI promotion for your Thailand business?
 BOI promotion may give your business important advantages, such as tax incentives, easier investment conditions, and, in some activities, support for foreign ownership and work permit planning. We assess whether your project qualifies and manage the application process with a focus on accuracy and speed. 
 Can foreigners own 100% of a company in Thailand?
 It depends on the activity. The Foreign Business Act restricts foreign majority ownership in many service categories. Full foreign ownership is possible through a BOI promotion, a Foreign Business Licence, or a Treaty of Amity registration for US nationals. Nominee shareholder arrangements used to bypass these rules are illegal and can void the structure. Wisdom International Consulting advises on which route fits a specific business activity before incorporation.
