 Experience Highlight 
 Learn a new sport, eat some local Thai food, and make lifelong friends! We are the only Muay Thai Camp in Bangkok with resort accommodation behind the gym. With local knowledge of Bangkok, we can provide transportation and guidance to a range of activities, restaurants, and tourist attractions.
 Our Muay Thai Camp has 80 boutique rooms located directly behind the gym. It’s never been easier to wake up, go train, and go back to the comfort of your own room. Secure, private, and clean, we want your sports and fitness holiday to be comfortable.
 You can find all types of cuisines in Bangkok and there are literally HUNDREDS of choices to choose from! Upon booking a package, you'll be able to access our special map that we've created which lists all our favorite places nearby the gym.

 MUAY THAI VISA New 
 Interested in long term stay? Khongsittha Muay Thai can provide you with an Educational Muay Thai Visa for 3 months or 12 months. This is perfect for aspiring fighters looking for an immersive experience.
 Only a handful of Muay Thai Schools have been honored with this privilege and we have been newly selected to provide this prestigious visa status for foreign students!

 Earn a Muay Thai Certificate
 For completion of training (of 30 days or longer), we will provide you with our Muay Thai Certificate! This certificate is certified by the Ministry of Education of Thailand and follows the Muay Thai Curriculum established by the Sports Authority of Thailand.
 Show it off to your friends and family and frame it on your wall. Completing at least 30 days will allow us to teach you solid fundamentals, which will in turn allow you to improve your Muay Thai skills and knowledge.

 Learn Authentic Muay Thai
 If you’re looking to learn Muay Thai for the first time or are preparing for a fight, you’re in good hands when you train with us. We are a registered Muay Thai School by the Ministry of Education Thailand and our training curriculum is Certified by the Sports Authority of Thailand.
 Beginners are Welcome
 Muay Thai gyms can be intimidating. But here at Khongsittha Muay Thai, we promise to make you feel safe and welcomed. It doesn’t matter if you’ve never trained Muay Thai or any other form of martial art before. Learn and master the basics with us when you get here!
 Safe for Children and Families
 Muay Thai training for children can help them improve their focus, confidence, fitness, and respect. Our trainers will adjust the training intensity for your children to make sure that they are safe and fun throughout the entire learning process.
