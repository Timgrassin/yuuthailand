 Centara Udon
 The gateway to the region with access to Laos and onwards to northern Vietnam and southern China, Udon Thani is one of northeastern Isan region’s most vibrant destinations. Not only is it a bustling hub, visitors to the region are also invited to discover Udon Thani’s historical and cultural attractions, including timeless temples and significant archaeological sites.
 Located in the heart of the city, Centara Udon is just steps away from the province’s largest shopping and lifestyle centre, Central Plaza Udon Thani, spanning five floors and comprising a Robinson’s department store and TOPS supermarket together with a Major Cineplex.
 Here, 259 spacious rooms and suites are designed to welcome guests of all ages and interests with six distinct room types and flexible bed layouts to comfortably fit different sized groups. The hotel also offers 2,900 square metres of versatile conference facilities, as well as a full range of leisure facilities, including a spa, an outdoor swimming pool and a fully-equipped fitness centre.
 Udon Thani International Airport is a 20-minute drive away. Additionally, the city’s train station is just 800 metres from the hotel, and the central bus station is also within easy walking distance.
