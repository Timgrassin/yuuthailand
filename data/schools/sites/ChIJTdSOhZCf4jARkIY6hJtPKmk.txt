 What is the new Thailand's residency visa? 
 Thailand’s residency visa, previously known as ‘Thailand Elite,’ has been rebranded as the Thailand Privilege Card. This card offers four membership tiers: GOLD (5 years), PLATINUM (10 years), DIAMOND (15 years), and RESERVE (20 years). Membership fees range from THB 900,000 to THB 5,000,000.
 Is the Thailand Elite visa cancelled? 
 The Thailand Elite visa has been rebranded as the Thailand Privilege Card. It continues to offer long-term residency visas for Thailand, along with a range of exclusive long-stay privileges.
 How do you qualify for a Thailand Privilege Card? 
 To qualify for the Thailand Privilege Card, the applicant or member must meet several criteria. They must be legally permitted to stay in Thailand under immigration laws or related regulations and hold a foreign passport. Additionally, they must not have been sentenced to imprisonment in any country, except for offenses committed through negligence. The applicant must also not have been declared bankrupt or deemed a person of unsound mind, incompetent, or quasi-incompetent.
 How much is the Thailand Privilege Card Gold Membership? 
 The Thailand Privilege Gold membership is a favored choice among business travelers who visit Thailand frequently, with a starting fee of THB 900,000. This membership provides excellent value for a 5-year residency in Thailand. Please note that the Gold card is non-transferable and does not extend to family members.
 How much is the Thailand Privilege Card Diamond Membership? 
 The Thailand Privilege Platinum membership is perfect for those seeking to enrich their stay with a 10-year residency in Thailand. It offers a range of complimentary privileges for both business and leisure. This membership can be upgraded, and family members, including spouses and children, can be added as supplementary members within this membership option.
 Which Thailand Privilege Membership can I add my family? 
 Under the Thailand Privilege Platinum, Diamond, and Reserve membership packages, supplementary memberships for family members, including spouses and children, can be added.
 Is there a new 15 years membership for Thailand Privilege Card?

 What are the fast-track benefits of Thailand Elite? 
 The Thailand Privilege Fast Track offers comprehensive services at immigration and international airports in Thailand. Successful applicants—Thailand Elite members—will be greeted by an Elite Personal Assistant (EPA) at designated airports, who will assist with all immigration procedures for each flight. Members can arrange for an EPA before their departure or arrival, specifying their flight times. A Thailand Elite vehicle will transport the member from their residence to the airport. Upon arrival, an EPA will meet them at the car, guide them through the fast-track immigration lane, and provide additional services such as complimentary access to the Thailand Elite lounge and golf cart transport from the plane gate if needed.

 What is Thailand Elite? 
 Thailand Elite is a long-term residency program offered to members of the Thailand Privilege Card. Established in 2003, Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd, the operator of Thailand Elite, operates as a state-owned enterprise within the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT), which is its sole shareholder.  What is Thailand Elite Visa?

 Can you work with the Thailand Elite visa? 
 The Thailand Privilege Visa is classified as a long-term tourist visa, meaning members are not permitted to work in Thailand under this visa. However, they can apply for a business visa and work permit separately while holding their Privilege Visa. The Privilege membership will remain active until its expiration. Members can continue to enjoy Elite services as usual, but please note that the 90-day reporting, visa extension, and transferring of the visa or arrival stamp into a new passport will not be covered under the Thailand Privilege services.
 Can Thailand Elite be used for family members? 
 The Thailand Privilege Family Visa is one of the most straightforward immigration programs available in Thailand for dependent family members. Membership packages, such as the Thailand Privilege Platinum, Diamond, and Reserve, offer options to include family members such as legally married spouses, children (regardless of age), and grandparents for a supplementary fee per person. The processing time is efficient, typically taking only 45 days from the submission of a complete application with all necessary documents. The required documents are minimal, usually consisting of the application form, a copy of your passport, and a photo. For dependents, proof of relationship, such as a birth certificate or marriage license, is needed.  Can foreigners buy real estate in Thailand? 
 Residential condo units can be legally purchased by foreigners, including Thailand Privilege Card members. The foreign ownership quota for a condo building must not exceed 49% for freehold purchases. Land and houses cannot be bought directly by foreigners, unless a government bond worth 40 million baht with a maturity of 5 years or more is invested in. In such cases, 1 rai of land can be acquired for residential purposes only. Otherwise, a long-term lease agreement, with a maximum duration of 30 years, must be entered into.
