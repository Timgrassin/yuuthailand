 LTR VISA 
 The Long-Term Resident (LTR) Visa grants qualified foreign nationals up to 10 years of stay in Thailand. Designed for high-value individuals across four key categories. This visa includes work authorization, tax incentives, expedited processing, and the ability to bring eligible family members. 
LTR Visa Categories
The LTR Visa is available to four groups of qualified applicants, with spouses, parents, and legal dependents eligible for the same visa status.

 The Thailand Privilege Card (TPC), formerly known as the Thailand Elite Visa, is an exclusive, long-term, multiple-entry visa program designed for individuals seeking extended stays in Thailand.
