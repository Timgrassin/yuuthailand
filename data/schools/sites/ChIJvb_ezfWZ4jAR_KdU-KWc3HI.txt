 Chirashi Sushi
 Hinamatsuri is a special day in Japan on 3 March, celebrating girls’ health and happiness. Families display hina dolls and gather to share traditional dishes.
 One of the most popular foods is Chirashi Sushi — a colourful bowl of seasoned rice topped with beautifully arranged ingredients, each symbolising good fortune and harmony.
 For the occasion, our sensei showcases how to prepare chirashi sushi neatly and elegantly, honouring both tradition and presentation.

 Food Styling &#038; Commercial Photography
 Discover the art and strategy behind professional food styling with Ajarn Duangrit Klaewplodtook, one of Thailand’s leading food stylists. This hands-on class explores the fundamentals of food styling, from developing concepts and themes to selecting tableware and plating techniques that enhance the visual appeal of food.
 Participants will gain insight into the role of food styling in restaurants, menu design, advertising, and food photography, while practising styling techniques through guided workshops. The class concludes with an opportunity to style and photograph participants’ own products, helping them develop practical skills for presenting food and brands more effectively.

 Duo Tiramisu &#8211; Original &#038; Matcha
 Discover the delicate harmony of East meets West in this hands-on dessert class, where you’ll craft two refined versions of the classic tiramisu—including a vibrant Matcha twist—under the guidance of an ALMA chef instructor, the world-renowned Italian culinary school. 
 Learn to build this elegant dessert from scratch: from making a fragrant matcha syrup and light sponge layers to whipping silky mascarpone cream. You’ll be guided step by step through each component before assembling your own duo of tiramisu—the original and a matcha-infused version that blends Italian technique with Japanese finesse. 
 Perfect for pastry lovers, aspiring chefs, or anyone with a sweet tooth and a passion for culinary creativity. This class is a rare chance to expand your skills and take home two beautifully crafted desserts you’ll be proud of.
