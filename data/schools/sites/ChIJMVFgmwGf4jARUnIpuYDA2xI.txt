 Welcome to Rockwell.
 Course Type
 Flexible Schedule
 Flexible schedule lessons are designed for private or semi-private students who have busy and unpredictable schedules. Students can choose their own specific goals. Flexible schedule students have no set time or day for study and may study at any time (if teacher’s schedule allows) by notifying staff in advance
 Use material of textbook of students’s own choosing or chosen with the teacher
 Intensive courses are possible
 A courses time limit will apply for flexible schedule students, beginning from the first day of the course
 Each lesson is at least 90 minutes
 Semi-Private Lesson
 Semi-private lessons are for 2-3 students who wish to arrange a class with friends, using materials of their choosing in a schedule most convenient for the group.
 Use material of textbook of students’s own choosing or chosen with the teacher
 Intensive courses are possible
 Student may choose day and time of lessons
 A maximum of 3 make-up classes is allowed when cancelled in advance
 Each lesson is at least 90 minutes
 Private Lesson
 Private Lessons are custom-designed for students who prefer to study one-to-one with a teacher to work on specific goals, weaknesses and etc. Students can study at their own place using materials of their own choosing or texts chosen with the teacher. Private lessons allow students most flexibility in arranging schedules convenient to them.
 Use material of textbook of students’s own choosing or chosen with the teacher
 Intensive courses are possible
 Student may choose day and time of lessons
 A maximum of 3 make-up classes is allowed when cancelled in advance
 Each lesson is at least 90 minutes
 Group Lesson
 Group lesson for 4 to 10 students who enjoy studying, practicing and socializing with others at the same level. These classes are lively, fun and give you a chance to practice everyday language in role-play situations with other students.
 Placement test is provided
 A textbook is used
 Each lesson lasts from 90-120 minutes
 Make-up class are not allowed
 Classes are held 2-3 times a week
 **See Time Schedule in Course Offering
