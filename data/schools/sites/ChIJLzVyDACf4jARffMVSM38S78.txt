 The Family Behind The Table
 The stories, the souls, and the South Italian secrets that define every bite at Segreto.
 Born in Castellammare di Stabia
 Great-grandmother’s Chocolate Parmigiana
 From Wan Chai to Bangkok
 Ermanno grew up in a food-loving family on the Gulf of Naples, between Naples and the Sorrento Coast. Four generations of cooks shaped his love of the kitchen.
 His great-grandmother’s Chocolate Parmigiana is a distinctive family recipe. Family stories are part of what reaches the Segreto table.
 In 2016, friends encouraged Ermanno to open Segreto Private Kitchen in Wan Chai, Hong Kong, sharing authentic family recipes. That personal spirit continues around his Bangkok table.

 The Format & Feeling
 How does the shared dining format work?
 We share the warmth of the South Italian table. Dishes are served family-style for the group to share, fostering conversation and a festive energy. This is not a formal tasting menu, but a social dinner where the table stays full and the wine stays flowing.
 Is Segreto a traditional fine-dining restaurant?
 Not in the hotel-standard sense. We offer a chef-led dinner with top Italian ingredients and personal service, but the atmosphere is designed to be a high-energy dinner party—vibrant, musical, and social. We prefer clinking glasses over hushed whispers.
 Can you accommodate specific dietary needs?
 Absolutely. Chef Ermanno prepares every dish with care and can adapt many family recipes. Please notify us of allergies or dietary preferences at least 48 hours in advance so we can ensure the menu is tailored to your requirements.
 Is there a dress code for the evening?
 Dress for a party: smart-casual, colourful, no jacket needed.
 What is the typical duration of the experience?
 A full seating at Segreto is an evening affair, typically lasting between 2.5 and 3 hours. We move through the menu at a relaxed pace, encouraging guests to linger over wine, watch the live cooking, and enjoy the social energy.
