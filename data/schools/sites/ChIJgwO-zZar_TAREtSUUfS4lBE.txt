 Hua Hin's Language Centre 
 Whatever your language goal, ILC is here to help you reach it. We are Hua Hin’s British Council registered English learning centre, specialising in IELTS training and B1 exam preparation, with courses for children, teenagers, and adults.
We are an authorised British Council exam centre and the only dedicated English language centre in Hua Hin offering this level of accreditation. Our courses are taught by qualified native and bilingual teachers in a modern, purpose-built learning environment.
For younger learners, our English Club and Cambridge Primary programmes build speaking confidence and communication skills from age six. For teenagers and adults, we offer Cambridge English courses at Pre-A1, A1, and A2 levels, Business English, IELTS preparation, B1 exam coaching, Trinity GESE, and one-to-one private lessons. We also offer premium one-to-one Thai language coaching for expats and international residents looking to build real confidence in everyday Thai.

 Residential English Camps in Hua Hin
 ILC is also home to one of the only dedicated residential English facilities in the Hua Hin area. Through our Dragon Study Tours programme, we welcome groups of up to 39 students for immersive residential English camps, combining structured classroom learning with activities, excursions, and full-board accommodation in Hua Hin.
Every learner receives a personalised learning plan, regular progress feedback, and access to internationally accredited exams without needing to travel to Bangkok.
