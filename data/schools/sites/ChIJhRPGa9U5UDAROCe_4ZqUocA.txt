 WHO ARE WE ? 
 Bangtao Surf School – A Local Ocean Family
 At Bangtao Surf School, we’re more than just a surf school, we're a family born of the sea. Nestled in the heart of a coastal paradise, we offer authentic, personalized surf experiences led by local instructors who know the waves like home. 
 Whether you're a beginner or looking to refine your skills, our friendly, family-style atmosphere and deep local knowledge will make every session feel like surfing with old friends. 
 Join us, ride the waves, and become part of our ocean-loving family. 
 Our location is perfect for beginners, with gentle waves and a safe environment for kids. Just 25 minutes from Phuket International Airport, we’re easy to reach and hard to leave.
