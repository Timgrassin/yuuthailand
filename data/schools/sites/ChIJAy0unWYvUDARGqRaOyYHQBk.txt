 The name LUDUS refers to the Latin word meaning play , learning , or training 
 In ancient Rome, it was a term used to describe gladiator schools, where combat skills and physical conditioning were taught.
 This symbolism beautifully reflects the philosophy of the sports complex, which provides its guests with a space for development, self-improvement, and education through engaging activities.
