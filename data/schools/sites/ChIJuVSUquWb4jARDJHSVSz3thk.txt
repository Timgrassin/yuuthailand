 A program for every age 
 From first words to fluent conversations.
 Every child is placed by level after a free assessment, then grows through four stages built on the Montessori developmental model.
 2 – 3 years 
 International Preschool
 A full-day, five-day-a-week preschool: English, Chinese and Thai immersion, French-system foundations and Montessori practical life.
 1 – 3 years 
 Play Group
 Music & Movement with a parent at your side: toddlers soak up English or Chinese while building motor skills and confidence.
 3 – 6 years 
 Kid Group
 Our best seller. Phonics, counting, handwriting and handicraft — the strongest start for school life.
 6 – 12 years 
 Junior Group
 Listening, speaking, reading and writing, aligned with CEFR (English) and HSK (Chinese), for confident primary-school learners.
