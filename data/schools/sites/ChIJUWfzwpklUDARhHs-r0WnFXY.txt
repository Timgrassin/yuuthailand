 FAQ
 Questions, answered. 
 Is it safe? Will I get injured? + It's a real combat sport, coached like one. You train in protective gear, beginners are progressed gradually by level, and the trainers are experienced. Train hard, go home in one piece.
 I'm a beginner — will I be the weakest? + No. Groups are by level and your 1:1 welcome session sets your starting point. Plenty of our guests had never thrown a kick before they arrived.
 Do I sort the visa and accommodation myself? + You arrange your own stay, but we make it painless — visa guidance, a pre-arrival guide, and WhatsApp support in your language before and during your trip. Tell us your dates and we'll point you to good places to stay near the gym.
 Is it worth the money? + Compare it to paying for a gym, a hotel and a holiday separately — then add real fighter-coaching and a crew. You're investing in ten days that change how you train and feel.
 Do the coaches speak English? + Yes — and we support you in your language over WhatsApp before and during your stay.
