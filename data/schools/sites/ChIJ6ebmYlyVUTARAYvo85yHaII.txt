 Our Story 
 Welcome to THE COZY RETREAT 
 A small, family-run yoga retreat on the quiet edge of Ao Nang, Krabi, just 10 minutes' drive from Ao Nang Beach.
 ​ 
 Whether you want to deepen your practice, become a yoga teacher, or simply slow down and recharge, you'll find space for it here. Our classes are small, our setting is peaceful, and every guest is welcomed like family.
 ​ 
 Nothing here is perfect, and that's perfectly okay. We value authenticity over perfection, so you can learn, rest and grow at your own pace.
 Yoga Teacher Training in Ao Nang, Krabi 
 Learn to teach in a small, supportive group, from your first steps to a Yoga Alliance&ndash;recognised 200-hour certificate. 
 ​ 
 Whether you want to deepen your own practice or start a new path as a teacher, our trainings meet you where you are. Everyone begins together in one small cohort: start with the 50-hour foundation and continue to 100 or 200 hours when you're ready. You'll learn through daily practice, personal mentoring and real teaching experience, including leading classes for our retreat guests. 
 Yoga Foundation 
6 days &middot; 50 hours &middot; from 30,900 THB* 
 Build a strong base in alignment, anatomy and yoga philosophy, and discover what makes a class truly well taught. 
 Teacher Confidence 
13 days &middot; 100 hours &middot; from 53,500 THB* 
 Find your voice as a teacher, with breathwork, sequencing, class planning and guided teaching practice with feedback. 
 Professional Certification 
24 days &middot; 200 hours &middot; from 87,900 THB* 
 Our complete Yoga Alliance&ndash;recognised training, with supervised teaching practice and one-to-one mentoring.

 Our Mission 
 We believe yoga is for everyone, whatever your age, body or experience. Our mission is to create a calm, welcoming space for yoga, healing and personal growth, where everyone is both a student and a teacher.
 ​ 
 ​ 
 Our experienced, qualified teachers combine yoga with a solid understanding of anatomy, physiology and psychology. With small groups, they have time to give you personal attention, whether it's your first class or your teacher training.

 Our Facilities 
 A peaceful home base on the quiet edge of Ao Nang, just 10 minutes' drive from the beach.
 ​ 
 Practice in our yoga and meditation shala, unwind in the relaxing area, and enjoy home-style breakfast and lunch with fresh homemade bread (plant-based options available). Our rooms are simple and comfortable, from twin rooms to private deluxe rooms with a king-size bed and kitchenette. All rooms have air conditioning and a warm water shower.
 ​ 
 Whether you're here for a weekend, a retreat or a teacher training, you'll have everything you need to rest and focus on your practice.
