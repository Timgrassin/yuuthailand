 Professors
 Meet the Professors 
 Jovan Petrovski
 A fashion designer with over 20 years of industry experience and 10 years in education. He earned a Master in Fashion as Art from Accademia Italiana, Florence, and worked with Sergio Tacchini, Aspesi, and MAX&Co.
 Jelena Sinclair
 An educator with over 15 years of design experience and 10 years in teaching.
She holds a  Master of Arts Interior Design from Arts University Bournemouth, UK and has taught across Asia and Europe.
 Daniel Martínez
 A designer and educator with over 10 years of experience in luxury and design.He holds a Master of Luxury from ECAL, Switzerland, has taught internationally, and is a recipient of the Prix Émile Hermès.
