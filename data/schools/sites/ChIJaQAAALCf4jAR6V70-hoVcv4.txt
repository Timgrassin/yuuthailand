 Holders of Thai OFFICIALS passports traveling to Spain for official reasons must send an e-mail to emb.bangkok.vis@maec.es presenting their case (including copy of the required Note Verbale from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Thailand). The Embassy of Spain will inform the applicant about next steps to follow. 
Holders of Thai DIPLOMATIC passport do not need visa to travel to Spain, as per the bilateral agreement on exemption of visas for diplomatic passports between the Kingdoms of Spain and Thailand, 2nd June 2010. 
 Holders of diplomatic and official passports from other nationalities can also apply directly at
the Embassy. In order to get the required application appointment, they must send an e-mail
to: emb.bangkok.vis@maec.espresenting their case (including copy of the required Note
Verbale from their Embassy).

 DO NOT FALL INTO FRAUD AND BEWARE OF SCAMS ! 
 BLS International advises visa applicants to exercise caution and vigilance regarding the
 following points: 
 Our staff never ask visa applicants to make cash payments or any other form of payment to
obtain an appointment, expedite the visa application process, secure an earlier appointment,
or influence decision-making. We do not recommend any direct payments into personal bank
accounts. . If you receive such a request (in writing or verbally), please report it immediately by
completing the complaint form or by sending an email to antifraud@blsinternational.com .
 Appointments must be booked exclusively through the BLS website. Please refer to the
 website for authorized service fees payable to BLS International.
 Always request a receipt for mandatory fees and fully optional value-added services available
 at our center.
 The decision regarding visa applications is made solely by the Embassy of Spain, and BLS
 International has no role or influence in this process
 Be cautious of intermediaries selling visa appointments under the name of BLS International or
individually outside our center. That is a scam. All appointments must be booked through the
BLS International website. Please refrain from purchasing appointments through a third party:
this is a fraudulent practice that BLS International strongly condemns. As a responsible service
provider, we make every effort to inform applicants to be wary of fraudulent intermediaries
charging fees to clients for appointment bookings.

 Holders of Thai diplomatic passports do not need visa for Spain, as per the bilateral agreement
on exemption of visas for diplomatic passports between the Kingdoms of Spain and Thailand,
2nd June 2010.
 Holders of Thai official passports traveling to Spain for official purposes need a visa and must
apply directly at the Embassy. In order to get the required application appointment, they must
send an e-mail to: emb.bangkok.vis@maec.es presenting their case (including copy of the
required Note Verbale from the Thai Ministry of Foreign Affairs)
Schengen visas on official passports are free of charge.
