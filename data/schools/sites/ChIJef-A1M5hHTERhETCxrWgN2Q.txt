 Thai Visa Services
 A set of services perfect for your "longer than vacation" trip to Thailand.
 Australia Visa Service 
 Australia wants your finances and your working life laid out in more detail than most countries ask for, and it is easy to under-do without knowing it. We gather it and present it properly, so you can think about the coast, the grandchildren, or the summer you are flying into.
 Home Finder 
 Finding a home in Bangkok from a listings site is a gamble: the wrong neighborhood, a lease you cannot read, and a price foreigners always overpay. We find the right place in the right area, view it with you, negotiate the rent, and get you moved in with the internet, utilities and everything working.
 Business Visa Service 
 You want to work for a Thailand based company or plan to start a business in Thailand and do not want visa uncertainty. We assess your employer or company, select the correct work/business visa route, prepare work permit and visa documents, and keep you legal.
 Canada Visa Service 
 Canada decides on paper, and what it is really asking is whether your life is rooted here. Most people leave that part thin without realising. We put it beyond doubt, so you can get on with the visit, the reunion, or the autumn you have been promising yourself.
 China Visa Service 
 Whether you need a Chinese visa at all, how many of your ninety days are left, and which category fits your trip are three easy things to get wrong. We settle all three and file the right application, so you can stay as long as you actually meant to.
 Schengen Visa Service 
 A European trip means one visa, twenty-nine countries, and a quiet rule that decides which embassy your application belongs to before you have booked anything. We take the whole thing off your hands, so you can think about the cities and the trains and not the checklist.
 Germany Visa Service 
 Germany's appointment queue can cost you your travel dates long before anyone reads your documents. We move early and handle the whole application, so the only thing on your mind is Berlin, Munich, or the fair you are flying in for.
 Japan Visa Service 
 Most nationalities need a visa to enter Japan, and Thai passport holders need one for any trip longer than 15 days. We prepare the documents, complete the forms and submit the application, so you are not working through Japan's checklist yourself.
 Korea Visa Service 
 A K-ETA refused with no reason and no appeal, or a passport that needs a full visa, and flights already booked either way. We put a properly documented application in front of a real officer who reads it, so you get to Seoul.
 New Zealand Visa Service 
 New Zealand wants six months of bank history and a convincing reason you are coming back, which is a bad thing to discover a fortnight before you fly. We start early and build both, so you can get on with planning the South Island.
 Switzerland Visa Service 
 Switzerland is expensive, and the consulate knows exactly what a week there costs, which is where good applications quietly come apart. We make the money and the trip tell the same story, so you can get on with Zermatt, Lucerne and the mountain railways.
 90 Day Report Service 
 Every foreigner who stays in Thailand more than 90 days in a row must report their address to immigration. Miss it and you pay a fine and carry the record. We track your date, file the TM47, and confirm immigration recorded it.
 Talk to a real Thailand expert 
 One hour with someone who actually lives here and has helped hundreds of foreigners build a real life in Thailand. We cover visas, retirement, business setup, family logistics, where to live, how to move, banking, healthcare, schools and Thai tax basics. You leave the call knowing your options and what to do next.
 Dependent Visa Service 
 You have a foreign spouse or children and need them to live in Thailand legally under your long term visa. We map the dependent visa options tied to your visa type, draft the documents, guide you through immigration, and keep your family together in Thailand.
 DTV Visa Service 
 Thai Kru builds your DTV file for a fixed ฿13,500, with half paid when your visa is issued. A clear yes or no before you pay the embassy fee, backed by the Thai Kru guarantee.
 Education Visa 
 You want to live in Thailand for a year or more, learn the language properly, and skip the constant border runs. An education visa built around Thai language classes lets you stay legally while you study, and it is one of the few routes with no financial requirement that still lets you open a Thai bank account. We work with a trusted language school in Bangkok, handle the paperwork, and guide you through immigration whether you switch from a tourist visa inside Thailand or apply from home.
 General Expat Services 
 You are already living in Thailand and must stay legal. Visa extensions, visa renewals, TM-30, Resident certificate, 90-day reports, change of visa and more. We review your current status, advise exactly what you must file, and prepare or assist in submission.
 Thai Family Visa Service 
 Your wife or husband or children are Thai citizens and you want to live or visit them in Thailand without recurring visa drama. We check your marriage or child status, pick the Marriage Visa or parent-child visa, prepare your documents, and manage submission.
 Retirement Visa Service 
 You are age 50 + and want to live in Thailand long-term without the guesswork. This service handles your retirement visa application, checks your bank/income, asks the right questions, prepares your documents, and guides you through the submission before you even come to Thailand.
 Tourist Visa Service 
 If your passport does not allow visa-free entry and you need a Thailand tourist visa, we prepare your application, review your documents, and help you apply so you can travel to Thailand without delays or mistakes.
 UK Visa Service 
 There is no UK interview. Someone you will never meet decides from your paperwork alone, which is a hard way to explain a whole life. We build a file that speaks for you, so you can get on with London, the family you are visiting, or the meetings you are flying in for.
 USA Visa Service 
 The US visa comes down to a few minutes at a window, and most people walk up to it without knowing what they will be asked. We get the form right and rehearse the conversation with you, so you can go and see your daughter, take the road trip, or make the meeting.
