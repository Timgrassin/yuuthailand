 Before you book
 Questions, answered
 What people ask before travelling to Chiang Mai to train.
 Where is the gym? Pro Boxing Gym Chiang Mai is at 401/2 Chiang Mai-Lam Phun Road, Wat Ket, Chiang Mai 50000, Thailand, inside the Bangchak gas station. Open it in Google Maps .
 When is the gym open? The gym is open Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 18:00. It is closed on Sundays. You can contact us by WhatsApp, email or phone every day of the week.
 What experience level do I need? Training is suitable for beginners through to experienced fighters. Tell us your level when you get in touch and we will talk through the right program with you.
 What programs are available? Western Boxing (1 month), Muay Thai (1 month or 1 week) and the 45 Days Challenge, a structured physical transformation program. See prices and details on the Programs page .
 Is accommodation available? Yes. Every program includes 4-star hotel accommodation. Rooms are around 40 m² with a private balcony, air conditioning, Wi-Fi and an en-suite bathroom. More on The Gym page .
 How do I book, and can I talk to you first? Yes, please do. Schedule a call on Calendly , or message us on WhatsApp , by email or on +66 94 408 0699. We will answer your questions before you travel.
