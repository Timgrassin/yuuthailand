 More FAQs 
 Do I need to book in advance? 
 Yes! We&#8217;re popular and fill up quickly, especially during peak season. Book online or call us.
 Can you pick me up from my hotel? 
 Free pickup and dropoff within 5km radius of Chiang Mai old city in our traditional songthaew trucks.
 What if it&#8217;s too spicy for me? 
 No worries! You control the spice level, and we&#8217;ll teach you how to adjust flavors to your taste.
 How much does a cooking class in Chiang Mai cost? 
 The normal price starts at 1,000 Baht (with an upgrade option for an air-conditioned room).
 Which cooking class includes a market tour? 
 The local market tour is part of our Chiang Mai Thai cooking class course to select fresh ingredients.
 How long does a cooking class in Chiang Mai take? 
 The class takes approximately 3-4 hours (including the garden visit and meal).
 Is this worth it if I can&#8217;t cook? 
 Perfect for beginners! You&#8217;ll be surprised what you can create with our step-by-step guidance.
 Do you have vegetarian options? 
 Absolutely! We offer delicious vegetarian versions of all our dishes. For more information, please visit: https://www.mamanoicookingschool.com/vegetarian-cooking-class-chiang-mai/
 How long does it take? 
 4.5 hours total &#8211; market tour, garden walk, cooking 3 dishes + dessert + Thai tea, then eating your creations.
 Is the class suitable for beginners? 
 Absolutely! Our classes are designed for food enthusiasts of all skill levels, with close guidance from our local instructor.
 Are the ingredients locally sourced or organic? 
 We prioritize using ingredients from the school&#8217;s organic garden and some from the local markets in Chiang mai.
 Should I book in advance or can I walk in? 
 We recommend booking in advance to secure your spot, especially during the high season, as our Thai cooking class Chiang Mai classes are very popular.
