 About
 Muay Thai Manufacturing (MTM)
 Muay Thai Manufacturing (MTM) is Thailand’s premier production facility for handcrafted Muay Thai and combat sports equipment. We produce boxing gloves, Muay Thai shorts, shin guards, heavy bags, focus mitts and OEM / private label equipment for brands, gyms and distributors worldwide. Every product is handmade in Thailand with premium materials and true Thai craftsmanship.
