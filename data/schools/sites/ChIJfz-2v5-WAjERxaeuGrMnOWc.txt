 Certified Translation in Pattaya — FAQ
 Where can I get certified translation in Pattaya? Siam International Translation is located at Pratamnak Soi 5, Pattaya. Our translators are listed and approved by embassies in Thailand, and our certified translations are accepted by Thai government offices, courts, and foreign embassies. Call +66 91 599 4999 or email pattayanotary@gmail.com. 
 Is there a notary public in Pattaya? Yes. We provide notarial services through a licensed Notarial Services Attorney (ทนายความรับรองลายมือชื่อ). Services include signature witnessing, certified true copies, affidavits, and powers of attorney accepted by embassies and international institutions. 
 How long does certified translation take? Standard certified translation takes 1–2 business days. Same-day express service is available for most documents including passports, birth certificates, and marriage certificates. 
 Which languages do you translate in Pattaya? We translate between Thai and English, French, German, Dutch, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. Our translators are embassy-listed for French, German, Italian, Dutch, and English-speaking embassies in Thailand. 
 Are your translations accepted by Thai embassies and government offices? Yes. Our certified translations are accepted by all Thai government offices, district offices (Amphur), courts, and foreign embassies in Thailand. We can also arrange Ministry of Foreign Affairs legalization and apostille for international use.
