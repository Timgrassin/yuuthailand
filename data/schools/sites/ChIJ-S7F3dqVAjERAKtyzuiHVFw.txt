 OUR STORY 
 Welcome to Thai Business and Visa Services, your trusted partner for navigating the complexities of visas, work permits, and business licensing in Thailand. Founded with a passion for helping individuals and businesses thrive in this vibrant country, our company has grown into a dedicated team of experts committed to providing exceptional service and support. 
 How It All Began 
 Our journey started when our founder, inspired by the challenges faced by expatriates and businesses in Thailand, saw a need for a reliable and knowledgeable resource to guide them through the intricate legal and administrative processes. With a vision to make Thailand more accessible and business-friendly, Thai Business and Visa Services was born. 
 ​ 
 Our Mission 
 Our mission is simple, to simplify the process of relocating to and doing business in Thailand. We aim to provide comprehensive, tailored solutions that address the unique needs of our clients, ensuring a smooth and successful transition. 
 ​ 
 What We Offer 
 From worldwide visa services and Thai work permits to specialized visas like the LTR and Smart Visa, BOI promotions, FBL, and various business licenses, we cover all aspects of your relocation and business setup needs. Our expertise and personalized approach make us the go-to service provider for individuals and businesses alike.
