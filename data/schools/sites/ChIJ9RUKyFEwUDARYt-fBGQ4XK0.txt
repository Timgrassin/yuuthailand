 Welcome to Lawmark & Associates Office, Phuket, Thailand
Recognized as one of the leading international law firms in Phuket and the South of Thailand, Lawmark & Associates (LM) has been providing international-standard legal representative with exceptional services, and timely, innovative solutions. LM aims to exceed expectations by understanding clients’ needs, and delivering practical advice that achieves business objectives.
Established in 2010, previously named Thai Legal & Lawyer and later changed to Lawmark & Associates in 2013, LM provides mostly international clients with highest quality service across a broad range of practice areas. We are a dynamic, growing, multi-service, general practice law firm experienced in transactional real estate, business investment, immigration, taxation, licensing, customs, intellectual property and corporate/commercial matters as well as litigation proceedings in civil and criminal cases.
 Having top-rated lawyers across a wide array of practice areas, our team of legal professionals and support staff offer professional services with a service-minded attitude, experience, expertise and devotion in handling every matter for the clients. The firm consists of highly experienced civil/criminal attorneys at law, legal consultants and tax accounting professionals.
