 WECOME TO KRU FRAME MUAY THAI
         Train with professional Thai coaches at Kru Frame Muay Thai Training Club, a beginner-friendly Muay Thai and fitness gym in Chiang Mai, conveniently located near Night Bazaar and Chiang Mai Old City. With over 10 years of coaching experience, Kru Frame and our team provide authentic Thai-style Muay Thai training in a safe, supportive, and motivating environment. 
         Whether you are completely new to martial arts or already experienced, our small group classes and private sessions are designed to help you improve your technique, build strength, and boost confidence at your own pace. We focus on proper fundamentals, effective pad work, and real Thai training methods suitable for beginners, tourists, digital nomads, and long-stay visitors.
