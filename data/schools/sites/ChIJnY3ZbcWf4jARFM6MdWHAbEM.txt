 About Spear Translation
 Award-Winning Translation Service
 Spear Translation is an award-winning translation company that delivers professional, high-quality human translation services to clients worldwide.
 We have a team of over 60 in-house translators and editors working in our operation centers. By integrating the entire process into clients' routine business operations, we provide seamless professional translation and responsive management support for any project requiring specific attention.
 By adopting the patented 3-layer Proofreading and Editing Mechanism , Spear gains more control over the quality, capacity and consistency of every translation job. Additionally, Spear has a team of typesetting specialists who work 24/7 alongside the translation department to ensure services are tailored to clients' specific needs.
 Thanks to our global network of over 4,000 qualified translators and our strict project management policy, we can meet any specifications regarding language pairs, capacity and turnaround time. Spear is now trusted by over 50,000 multinational companies worldwide.
 In 2021, Spear was awarded the title of Best Translation Company in Asia . Aiming to become a reliable partner for your business, we bring more power behind every word, providing you with the best translation services you have ever received.

 OUR TRANSLATION SERVICES 
 Professional Translation Service 
 97 Language Pairs | 60 In-House Translators | Award-Winning Translation Service
 Our Professional Translation Service offers expert, accurate translations tailored to your needs. Our skilled team specializes in legal, medical, technical and marketing fields, ensuring precise and culturally appropriate communication. Committed to quality and reliability, we integrate seamlessly into your business operations, providing responsive support and exceptional results.
 Editing 
 Articles are now more Readable and Marketable
 Our Editing Service offers meticulous, professional editing to ensure your documents are polished and error-free. Our expert editors refine grammar, style, and clarity across various fields, delivering precise and reliable results tailored to your needs.
 Proofreading 
 To ensure all the messages are accurately delivered
 Our Proofreading Service ensures your documents are error-free and polished. Our expert proofreaders meticulously review your content for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and clarity, enhancing readability and professionalism.
 Copywriting & SEO Writing 
 Keywords and Online Search Ranking Engineering
 Our Copywriting & SEO Writing Service delivers compelling and optimized content to engage your audience and enhance your online presence. Our skilled writers create persuasive copy tailored to your brand's voice and objectives while incorporating effective SEO strategies to improve search engine rankings.
 Website, APP & Software Localization 
 Localize User Experience and Capture more Markets via our System
 Our Website, App & Software Localization Service ensures your digital products resonate with global audiences. We adapt your content to local languages and cultures, enhancing user experience and engagement. Our expert team ensures seamless integration, maintaining the functionality and integrity of your original design.
 Translation Memory Management 
 To Keep the Consistency of your Brand Image
 Our Glossary Management Service streamlines your terminology to ensure consistency and clarity across all your communications. We create and maintain a comprehensive glossary tailored to your industry and brand, enhancing accuracy and coherence in translations and content creation.
 Transcription and Subtitling 
 Localize Transcript and Add Subtitles to Videos
 Our Transcription and Subtitling Service provides accurate and professional transcription of audio and video content, along with precise subtitling for various languages and formats. We ensure clarity and accessibility, enhancing your content's reach and impact.
 Desktop Publishing 
 50+ Language Supported
 Our Desktop Publishing (DTP) Service combines design expertise with linguistic precision to create visually appealing and culturally appropriate materials. We handle layout, formatting, and localization across multiple languages and platforms, ensuring your content maintains its integrity and impact.
 Certified Translation 
 Get a Certification for your Personal Documents
 Our Certified Translation Service ensures accurate and legally recognized translations for official documents. With meticulous attention to detail and adherence to industry standards, we provide certified translations that meet the requirements of governmental bodies, courts, and official institutions worldwide.
