 PASSIONFOOD COOKING SCHOOL, CHIANG MAI
 Welcome to Passionfood Thai Cooking School, nestled in the charming old town square of Chiang Mai. Immerse yourself in the captivating world of Thai cuisine through our engaging half-day courses, where you have the opportunity to learn and cook your preferred selection of Thai dishes. With our deep-rooted passion for culinary arts and extensive experience, we are dedicated to providing a memorable and enriching experience for every client. Join us on a culinary journey and discover the vibrant flavors, aromatic herbs, and expert techniques that make Thai cuisine truly remarkable. Come, let us guide you through this delectable exploration and unlock the secrets of Thai cooking in a warm and welcoming environment. 
 Feem - Owner, Passionfood School
