 Muaythai Sitsongpeenong Muay Thai Camp 
 Sitsongpeenong Muay Thai Camp is the place to go to for authentic, high-caliber training, set in a world-class facility where you can train alongside professional fighters and under some of the most respected trainers in the sport.
Sitsongpeenong Muay Thai Camp is a duly registered Bangkok-based Muay Thai camp that provides authentic, high-caliber training in a world-class facility with hotel-standard accommodations.The gym has a roster of professional fighters, winners in sought-after local and international competitions, who train alongside the guests.
