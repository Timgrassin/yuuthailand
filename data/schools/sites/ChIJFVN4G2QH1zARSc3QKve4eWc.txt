 Winter is Here! Prime Training Weather! 
 It's that time of year again! The weather is cooler, not raining often, and the humidity is much lower. Thailands winter season welcomes many tourists. Among those tourists are those who come for the sports. Many people head south for camps, but those who come North for Musy Thai are in for a treat.  
 Training in northern Thailand means you feel the true "winter" of Thailand. With cool mormings and evening, training becomes less of a battle between yourself and the heat. Morning private sessions and evening group classes start to fill up fast!  
 Pound Boxing Gym welcomes athletes for weeks at a time for those who want to live in one of the cheaper cities in Thailand and train at great prices! 
 Compared to Bangkok, Phuket, and Chiang Mai, where you will spend at least 500 baht on a group session. Chiang Rai offers Muay Thai Group sessions for 200 baht an hour and 500 for private sessions . Living expenses in the city are also significantly cheaper! Making Chiang Rai the ideal location for long term training.
