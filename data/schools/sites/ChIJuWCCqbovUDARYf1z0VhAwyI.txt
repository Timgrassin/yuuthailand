 WHY ALPHA LANGUAGE SCHOOL 
At Alpha Language School Phuket you receive:
• Licensed education provider recognized by the Thai Ministry of Education
• Thai language programs up to 3 years
• English & Chinese programs up to 18 months
• Structured 120-hour level system
• Professional experienced teachers
• Small groups (max 7–8 students)
• Friendly international atmosphere
We focus on structured, serious language development — not tourist lessons.
 WHO OUR COURSES ARE DESIGNED FOR

• Expats
• International students
• Travelers who want structured learning
Whether you live in Thailand or plan to move here, we provide a stable learning path.

 Education Visa Support 
 We are licensed by the Thai Ministry of Education. 
 If you choose one of our approved courses, we prepare the required documentation to support your Education Visa application in accordance with Thai regulations. 
 The process typically takes 3–4 weeks. 
 You focus on studying. 
 We guide you through the paperwork.
