 Cooking Class Koh Samui with Bung, Mong and Tony! 
 LOCAL MARKET
 A 30 Minutes educational tour at Ko Samui local market to learn the beauty of thai herbs, spicies and vegetable.
 COOKING CLASS
 2 hours cooking class, cook 4 dishes of your choice and banana in coconut milk in dessert!
 SAVOUR
 Enjoy a delectable thai lunch or diner of your own making! A wonderful way to meet others and sharing around the table!
 Smiley Cook is a lovely cooking class located at Koh Samui, Bophut, close to the fisherman village. 
 The cooking class area is a nice and quiet place covered (in case of rainy day), in a lovely garden. We offer free pick up and local market tour everyday.
 The 2 chefs, Bung and Monk are very talented Chefs with years of experience and are very happy to teach how to make delicious Thai food for people from all other the world!
