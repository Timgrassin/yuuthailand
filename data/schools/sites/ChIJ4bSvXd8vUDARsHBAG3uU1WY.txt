 SAMPLE RECIPES YOU WILL LEARN - A TASTE OF THAILAND 
 Ancre 3 
 SOM TAM 
 Spicy green papaya salad 
 Enjoy the fresh, zesty crunch of Som Tam, a vibrant green papaya salad with bold flavors of lime, chili, and peanuts. A perfect balance of spicy, sour, and sweet! 
 KENG KIOW WAN 
 Thai green curry 
 Indulge in the aromatic flavors of Keng Kio Wan, a fragrant green curry with tender meat, fresh herbs, and a perfect blend of spicy and creamy. A true Thai comfort dish! 
 TOM KRA KAI 
 Coconut galangal chicken soup 
 Savor the creamy, aromatic taste of Tom Kha Kai, a rich coconut soup with tender chicken, lemongrass, and galangal. A perfect balance of tangy, spicy, and smooth flavors! 
 KAI PAD MET MAMUANG 
 Chicken with cashew nuts 
 Enjoy the perfect crunch of Kai Pad Med Mamuang, a delicious stir-fry with tender chicken, crispy cashew nuts, and a rich, savory-sweet sauce. A true Thai favorite! 
 PAD KRA PAO KUNG 
 Stir-fried shrimps with holy basil 
 Enjoy the bold flavors of Pad Krapao Kung, a spicy stir-fry with shrimp, Thai basil, and a savory kick. A true Thai favorite that's both fragrant and flavorful! 
 PANANG KAI 
 Thai panang curry 
 Discover the rich and creamy taste of Panang Kai, a flavorful Thai red curry with tender chicken, coconut milk, and aromatic spices. A perfect blend of sweet, salty, and spicy! 
 PAD THAI KAI 
 Stir-fried rice noodles with chicken 
 Delight in the flavors of Pad Thai Kai, a classic Thai stir-fry with tender chicken, peanuts, and a perfect balance of sweet, salty, and sour. A dish you can't resist! 
 KRAO NIAOW MAMUANG 
 mango sticky rice 
 Enjoy the sweet delight of Mango Sticky Rice, a perfect mix of ripe mango, creamy coconut-infused sticky rice, and a hint of sesame. A true Thai classic!

 FRENQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION
 where is the cooking class located?
 the cooking class is located in Rawai, Phuket. 
 Do i need prior experience to join?
 Our cooking school provides several kind of classes suits the different cooking experience of each customer. Our class is for beginners who has no experience in Thai cooking but also is for the skilled cooks who would like to extend their cooking skills and knowledge. 
 What dishes will i learn to cook
 In our standard Discovery Class, you will prepare 4 authentic Thai dishes. The menu varies by class but often includes iconic Thai dishes such as Pad Thai, Som Tam, Green Curry, Tom Yum Soup, and Mango Sticky Rice.
