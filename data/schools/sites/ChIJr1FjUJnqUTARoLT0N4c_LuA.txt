 What we offer
Group & Private Rock climbing 
 Real Rocks offers guided group and private rock-climbing trips in Railay, located in Krabi Province, southern Thailand. We provide climbing tours on some of the world’s most breathtaking cliffs, welcoming climbers of all levels and abilities—even those completely new to the sport. 
Our internationally certified climbing instructors are committed to safety, professionalism, and, above all, creating a fun experience for everyone.
STUDY COURSE 
 Master climbing techniques and earn an internationally recognized certification! Our instructors are the only licensed professionals in southern Thailand authorized to issue Sport Climbing Certifications for Levels 1-3.
Each course is private, ideal for beginner and novice climbers, with personalized, one-on-one instruction to help you build skills quickly and efficiently.

 How to climb?
 Learn to climb like a pro and get certified to international standards! Our instructors are the only licensed
instructors in the South of Thailand authorized to issue certifications for Sport Climbing Levels 1-3. Each
course is PRIVATE, recommended for beginner and novice climbers! One-on-one teaching to maximize
your time practicing skills and accelerate your learning. Each course includes: hotel pick up and drop off
from Ao Nang, an official sport climbing certification card, student sport climbing training manual and log
book, gear and equipment rental, and private instruction.
