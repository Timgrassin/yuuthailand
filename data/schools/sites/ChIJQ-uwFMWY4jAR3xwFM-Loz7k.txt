 Education Visas
 Would you like to stay in Thailand and enjoy all that it has to offer for longer than the usual 30 or 60 days allowed with a tourist visa?
 Perhaps the easiest and most convenient way to enjoy your long stay in Thailand is by obtaining an Education (“ED”) Visa. Why not enrol for a langauge course at Insight?
 Initial Consultation 
 Let us know your current visa situation, travel plans and where you are in Thailand. Our team will then contact you with information about the courses, application process and costs. 
 Application Process 
 We submit your application to the Minsitry of Education.  They approve the application and send us the documents needed for you to apply for an Education visa. This takes about 3 weeks. 
 Study Programs 
 Once you receive your visa, you can start your language journey with a Thai or English course.
