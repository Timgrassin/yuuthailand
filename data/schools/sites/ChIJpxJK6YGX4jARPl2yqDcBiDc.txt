 Our Services
 Ihdos Legal provides a One-stop service for clients wishing to start up or operate an existing a business in Thailand. 
Our services are summarized below. 
 Business Consultancy
 Prior to setting up a new business in Thailand, it is advisable for you to seek appropriate guidance from a reliable legal source. 
Ihdos Legal has an experienced team of fluent, English-speaking professional who can provide you with all the relevance information required to actually start up a new company.  
Setting up a company is not a difficult task. But finding the right person to guide you might help you save so much of time and resources.

 Business Registration & Licenses
 We provide all kinds of assistance in registering a Private Limited Company, a Partnership Limited Company, a Holding company or a Representative office based upon your requirement with the Department of Business Development, Ministry of Commerce. If required, we also provide VAT registration with the Revenue Department and social security registration service.
