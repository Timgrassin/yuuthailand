 Frequently Asked Questions 
 What is an education visa in Thailand? 
 &plus; 
 An education visa is a visa route for eligible foreigners studying through an approved educational programme in Thailand.
 Can I stay in Thailand long term with an education visa? 
 &plus; 
 An education visa may allow eligible foreigners to stay in Thailand longer than a short tourist visit, provided they meet the conditions of their programme and immigration status.
 Can I switch from a tourist visa to an education visa? 
 &plus; 
 In some situations, a visa conversion or change of status may be possible without leaving Thailand, depending on your current entry, nationality, timing, documents and immigration requirements.
 Do I need to attend classes? 
 &plus; 
 Education visas are intended for genuine study, and approved programmes normally have attendance or participation requirements. However, we also offer online Thai language study options that may be completed remotely where applicable.
 How much does the education visa package cost? 
 &plus; 
 Our education visa package, including the Thai language course and visa support, is typically 49,500 baht depending on the programme structure and immigration requirements.
 Can I work on a Thailand education visa? 
 &plus; 
 A Thailand education visa does not automatically give work authorisation. Anyone wishing to work in Thailand should obtain proper legal advice and the correct work permission.
 How long does the process take? 
 &plus; 
 Processing time can vary depending on your current visa status, chosen programme, document readiness and immigration workload.
 Can you help after my visa is approved? 
 &plus; 
 Yes. We can assist with future extensions, 90-day reporting guidance, renewal preparation and ongoing immigration support where applicable.
 Why Opt for an Education Visa in Thailand? 
 An education visa in Thailand allows you to stay for an extended period while engaging in approved study programmes. This option is especially beneficial for individuals keen on learning Thai, training in Muay Thai, or expanding their cultural and linguistic knowledge. With the right guidance, obtaining your education visa in Thailand can be straightforward and cost-effective.
