 F.A.Q.
 How do I pay for my booking? 
 To confirm a reservation at FitKoh, the following payment terms apply:
 Accommodation – 100% prepayment is required at the time of booking.
 Training & Wellness Packages – 50% prepayment is required at the time of booking.
 The remaining 50% balance of the selected package must be settled prior to or upon check-in .
 Please note that bookings are only secured once the initial payments are received. Please note the cancellation policy before committing to a reservation.
Payment for accommodation and additional services can be made by card.
 Can my FRIEND stay in the room? 
 One friend is eligible to stay in your room free of charge. However, program package will need to be book additionally.
 What are check in and check out times? 
 The check in time is 14:00 and the check out is 12:00. We will of course do our best to have a room available for you whenever you arrive. However this can never be guaranteed and is dependent on the occupancy level.
 What is the minimum stay? 
 The program runs in 1 week, 2 weeks and 30 days cycle. Guests are welcome to stay for as long as they wish.
 How long I need to stay to see the result? 
 Every day is specifically designed to provide you with the activities, education, nutrition and personal attention you need to get healthy and stay motivated. Our program will challenge you physically and mentally to reach and surpass your own expectations while empowering you to change your life. We have different programs with different minimum stay needed to achieve the results.
 How much do I need to pay at the time of booking? 
 At time of booking you will be required to pay a 50% deposit. On the arrival date you will have to cover the remaining balance.
 Are taxes and fees included? 
 Prices are subject to 7% government tax and 5% service charge
 Are airport transfers provided? 
 Airport transfer are not included in the price of the room and should be arranged prior to arrival, except for those on our Body Transformation Program. A flight number and phone number is required for a pick-up to be arranged. Please send full guest name, arrival date, time, and flight number at least 48 hours prior to arrival. Transfers can be provided to and from Koh Samui airport.
 Do I need to get a visa to come to Thailand? 
 Standard tourist visa upon arrival is normally valid for 30-60 days upon arrival to Thailand without prior application for the most of passport holders. Please consult a Thai embassy representative in your home country just to double-check. To extend your visa duration you have several options.
 Travel Insurance 
 Although our staff will try to provide supervision, instruction and assistance during training sessions, physical exercise involves the risk of injury. As the company is not responsible for self-inflicted accident or injury caused throughout your stay (within reasonable grounds), we advise all guests to equip themselves with full comprehensive travel & health insurance prior to any travels!
