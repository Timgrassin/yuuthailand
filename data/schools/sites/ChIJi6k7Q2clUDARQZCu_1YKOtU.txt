 FAQ
 Where is the cooking class located in Phuket?
 Thai Cooking Class by Chef Tan takes place in the open-air garden kitchen at Ka La Restaurant in Rawai, Phuket, near Chalong Pier.
Surrounded by greenery and nature, the garden offers open views of the surrounding mountains and Big Buddha, creating a calm and beautiful setting for our farm-to-table Thai cooking experience.
 Do you provide hotel transfer?
 Yes, FREE hotel pickup and drop-off is available from selected areas: Kata, Karon, Chalong and Rawai. Transfer from other areas may be available upon request and may require an additional charge.
 Do guests need to bring anything?
 No, you don’t need to bring anything.
We provide all ingredients, equipment, cooking tools, and recipes. Just come hungry, curious, and ready to enjoy the experience.
 Vegan / vegetarian / gluten-free options?
 Yes! 
We offer vegan and vegetarian options, and can adapt the menu upon request.
Please let us know your dietary preferences when booking so we can prepare everything in advance.
 Are children allowed?
 Yes.
For safety reasons, children can join the hands-on cooking class as participants from 12 years old and above .
Children of any age are welcome as visitors. We have a small kids’ play area with a slide and swings, a garden to walk around, and drinks available from our restaurant.
Parents are responsible for supervising their children during the visit.
 How does cancellation / rescheduling work?
 You can reschedule or cancel your booking free of charge up to 48 hours before the class.
Cancellations made less than 48 hours before the class may not be refundable.
If you have any issues, please contact us — we are always happy to help when possible.
