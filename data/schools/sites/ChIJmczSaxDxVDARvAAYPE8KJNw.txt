 WHAT WE OFFER
 Elite Fight Center opened its doors in January 2025 with one goal—to create the ultimate training environment for combat sports and functional fitness.
 Located in Ko Samui , we offer world-class coaching in  Muay Thai , MMA , Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu , HYROX , and functional training , catering to both beginners and professional athletes.
 Muay Thai
 Muay Thai or Thai Boxing is the national sport and cultural martial art of Thailand. We offer all levels. 
Join us now!
 MMA
 Mixed martial arts (MMA) is a full-contact fighting sport based on striking and grappling; incorporating techniques from various combat sports.
 BJJ
 Brazilian jiu-jitsu, often abbreviated to BJJ, is a self-defense system, martial art, and combat sport based on grappling, ground fighting, and submission holds.
 Functional Training
 Functional Strength Training is a fitness approach designed to enhance the body&#8217;s ability to perform everyday movements with ease and efficiency.
 Hyrox
 We are a Hyrox-affiliated gym. HYROX keeps it simple — just you against the clock, testing how well you can sustain effort across multiple challenges.
