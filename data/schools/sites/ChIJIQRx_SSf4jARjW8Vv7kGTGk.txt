 Overview
 Bangkok Privacy Notice (Rev. June 9, 2022)
 Repeatedly recognized as a leading law firm for projects and energy and corporate/M&A in various legal directory guides, including Chambers Asia Pacific , The Legal 500 Asia Pacific , and IFLR1000 , our Bangkok office provides our clients with experience and advice across numerous areas of law. 
 Since opening our Bangkok office in 1997, our lawyers have worked on landmark transactions for both local and international companies. The firm's pan-Asia domestic and international cross-border Asia Pacific practice is centered in Bangkok. With a team of over 30 lawyers based in Bangkok, we are exceptionally positioned to deliver legal and commercial advice that meets international standards. 
 Within our Bangkok office, we have lawyers licensed to practice law across numerous jurisdictions, including Thailand; Australia (Queensland and Victoria); the United States (North Carolina, Virginia, California, and New York); and England and Wales.
 We are home to a robust energy and infrastructure development practice and have achieved recognition with renewable energy projects where we have executed transactions of varying complexities for businesses throughout the Asia-Pacific region. We have represented developers, purchasers, sellers, sponsors, borrowers, lenders, and an array of other participants in some of the largest and most complex energy and infrastructure projects and M&A transactions in the region. 
 Our corporate/M&A team has significant experience representing clients in all types of mergers, acquisitions, divestitures and other foreign direct investment, commercial and institutional lending and regulatory work, project development and privatization, project and structured finance and securitizations, rehabilitation and restructuring, investment and trade agreements, capital markets and other commercial transactions. 
 Directions
 Transportation by MRT at Lumpini Station. 
 The Bangkok office is easily accessible by subway (MRT). Exit at Lumpini Station and walk 100 meters to the Q. House Lumpini building, located at the corner of South Sathorn and Rama 4 Roads.
