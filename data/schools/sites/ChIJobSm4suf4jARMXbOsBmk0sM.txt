 Welcome to Thai Christian School 
 • Pre-K – G.12 English Program, including Thai and Chinese.
• We emphasize learning from native speakers.
• Kindergarten follows a Montessori curriculum.
• Located on Sukhumvit 71, founded in 1967.
• To learn more, click &#8220;English Program&#8221; in the menu.
