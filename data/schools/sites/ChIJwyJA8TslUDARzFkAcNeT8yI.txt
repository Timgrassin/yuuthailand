 FAQ 
 How fast is the eFoil surfboard?
 Surfboard electric can reach speeds of up to 25-35 mph (40-56 km/h), depending on the model and rider’s weight.
 Can you surf waves on an eFoil?
 Yes, but eFoils are best suited for calm waters. Experienced riders can use waves to glide, but their motorized design shines in flat or slightly choppy conditions.
 How difficult is eFoiling?
 E foil pwr is easier than traditional surfing but requires balance and practice. Beginners often stand within 1-2 hours of instruction, especially with a professional coach.
 Are electric surfboards hard to ride?
 Electric surfboards are beginner-friendly, with intuitive controls. However, mastering balance and ride at speeds control may take practice, especially on models with higher speeds.
 How do electric surfboards work?
 Electric surfboards use a battery-powered motor and a handheld remote to control speed. The motor propels the board, allowing riders to glide smoothly over water.
 Does foiling feel like surfing?
 Foiling offers a unique experience, blending surfing with flying. It feels smoother and more elevated than traditional surfing, giving the sensation of gliding above the water.
