 Muay Thai Training in Chiang Mai, welcome to Tiger Muay Thai Chiang Mai
 TMT Chiang Mai Video Tour Established over 15 years ago, our main camp Tiger Muay Thai & MMA Training Camp, Phuket, Thailand is world renowned as a leader in Muay Thai, MMA and fitness instruction.
 Tiger Muay Thai has opened a new training camp in San Sai, a large suburb of Chiang Mai , approximately 24 kilometers north of the city and airport. The camp covers in excess of 4,000 square meters of land and is set in a tranquil spot, directly adjacent to protected National Forest and close to mountains and many areas of outstanding natural beauty (including a large reservoir and recreational lake) .
 We commenced operations on 1st November 2019 and the camp currently offers Muay Thai training and has accommodation for up to 20 or more quests, a swimming pool and weight-room.
