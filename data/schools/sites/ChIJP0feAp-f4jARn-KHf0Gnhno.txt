 What is Creative Ukuleles? 
 Creative Ukuleles is a fantastic project where thousands of students have made their very own high quality custom made Ukulele .  
 The course combines Arts & Crafts , DIY and Music in a cross curricular project that inspires the students to be as creative as they can be.  
 Students will assemble, paint, string, tune and play their very own custom ukulele, take it home / use in Music class , with their very own carry case . 
 The students learn to use many different tools and resources such as  
 sanding, gluing joints, screwdriver, wood filler, painting, stencils, carbon copy paper and much more. 
 Creative Ukuleles provides everything the students will need including the ukulele kit and all resources.
