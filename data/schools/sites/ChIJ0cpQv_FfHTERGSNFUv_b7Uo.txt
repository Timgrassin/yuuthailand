 Welcome to  MABIS, 
 a wonderful learning community that nurtures the unique potential of each child. Established in 2010, we offer students an authentic Montessori education delivered in a multilingual environment. Students learn English, Chinese, and Thai in our language immersion program serving children from two years old through high school. 
 Benchmarked to international standards, including the US Common Core, Next Gen Science, Singapore Chinese, and the Thai National Curriculum, MABIS ensures a robust academic program in a nurturing Montessori environment. 
 What is Montessori 
 Montessori education is a child-centered approach, emphasizing hands-on learning, self-discovery, and tailored instruction to meet each child&#8217;s unique developmental needs. 
 Why Montessori 
 In the nurturing Montessori environment that offers a personalized learning journey, students thrive holistically, exhibiting self-motivation, creativity, critical thinking, independence, resilience, social skills and self-esteem. 
 A Foundation for Life 
 Through cultivating a positive mindset and character traits, Montessori education builds a strong foundation for life that is essential for academic success and personal growth in an ever-changing world.

 Welcome to MABIS, 
 a wonderful learning community that nurtures the unique potential of each child. Established in 2010, we offer students an authentic Montessori education delivered in a multilingual environment. Students learn English, Chinese, and Thai in our language immersion program serving children from two years old through high school. 
 Benchmarked to international standards, including the US Common Core, Next Gen Science, Singapore Chinese, and the Thai National Curriculum, MABIS ensures a robust academic program in a nurturing Montessori environment. 
 A pioneer in offering quality education, MABIS is the first Montessori school outside of the United States to receive accreditation by WASC along with the Thai Ministry of Education and ONESQA.
