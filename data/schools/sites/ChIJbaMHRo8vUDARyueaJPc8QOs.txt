 About Cookie Muay Thai Gym
 Cookie Muay Thai Gym in Phuket offers authentic Muay Thai training led by high-level, experienced trainers who focus on real technique, discipline, and fight development. The gym is suitable for both beginners and experienced fighters, with training tailored so everyone can progress at their own pace while still being challenged.
 The atmosphere is welcoming and community-driven, making it easy for newcomers to feel comfortable while still pushing serious athletes to improve. You can train in structured group classes for energy and motivation, or choose private sessions for more focused, personalised coaching.
 Alongside Muay Thai, the gym also provides strength and conditioning work and weight training equipment to build power, endurance, and overall fitness. Located close to beautiful beaches like Nai Harn and Rawai, it offers the perfect balance between serious training and recovery in a relaxed Phuket setting.
