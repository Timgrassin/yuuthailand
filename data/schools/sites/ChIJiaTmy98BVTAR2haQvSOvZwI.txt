 Welcome to Samma Karuna!
 Samma Karuna is an international Tantra school specializing in Tantra, Yoga, and Breathwork Teacher Training Courses and year-round transformational retreats. We offer retreats internationally , with our main school located on the beautiful island of Koh Phangan, Thailand. 
 We provide a nurturing environment where people can better understand themselves, build meaningful connections, and gain practical tools for personal transformation , learning to live happier and more meaningful lives.

 What make us special 
 What make us special 
 Samma Karuna is more than a place to take classes. We are an international, non-dogmatic Tantra school of personal development and self-realization, with a structured curriculum developed through more than a decade of daily practice. 
 Our teaching is based on The Archegram (also known as The Tantragram), our educational model for self-observation and personal growth . Each class builds on the previous one, creating a clear and practical learning process.
 We go beyond temporary breakthroughs. Our goal is to help students develop self-awareness, inner strength and practical tools for lasting personal transformation that continues beyond the retreat.
 Our teachers follow the same educational approach and standards, providing a consistent and meaningful learning experience .
 At Samma Karuna, personal growth and celebration go hand in hand. Music, dance, playfulness and meaningful connections are part of everyday life. We believe that understanding ourselves is the foundation for learning to live happier, more fulfilling lives.
 Community is at the heart of Samma Karuna. Students share experiences, support one another and build genuine connections that often become lifelong friendships.
