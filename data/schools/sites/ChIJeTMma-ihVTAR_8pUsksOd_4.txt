 Contact Us 
 Welcome to my Cooking Class! 
 My name is Idjang and I have been living on the wonderful island of Koh Tao for over 15 years. During this time, I have been serving delicious Thai and Western dishes at my restaurant ‘Idjang’s Kitchen’. Most recently I decided I wanted to share my recipes with anyone that wanted to learn how to cook authentic and tasty Thai food and launched ‘Idjang’s Cooking School’ which has been running for over 5 years.
 I invite you to join me and learn about the ingredients and the cooking techniques to make the perfect Thai food and impress your friends and family when you return home! You are welcome to book online directly from this website using our quick and easy online booking & secure payment system.
 I look forward to seeing you soon!
 Welcome to my Cooking Class! 
 My name is Idjang and I have been living on the wonderful island of Koh Tao for over 15 years. During this time, I have been serving delicious Thai and Western dishes at my restaurant ‘Idjang’s Kitchen’. Most recently I decided I wanted to share my recipes with anyone that wanted to learn how to cook authentic and tasty Thai food and launched ‘Idjang’s Cooking School’ which has been running for over 5 years.
 I invite you to join me and learn about the ingredients and the cooking techniques to make the perfect Thai food and impress your friends and family when you return home! You are welcome to book online directly from this website using our quick and easy online booking & secure payment system.
 I look forward to seeing you soon!

 Important Information - please read: 
 Class bookings non-refundable. However, we understand that circumstances can change sometimes so we permit one change per booking at least 48 hours before the booking date.
 You can select to either pay a 50% deposit or pay in full now. If you select to pay the deposit then the remaining amount will be due at the start of your class (only cash or bank transfer are permitted).
