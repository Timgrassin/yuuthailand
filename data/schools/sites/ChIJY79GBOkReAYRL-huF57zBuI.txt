 Finding a qualified native French teacher in Bangkok is harder than it sounds. Most language schools rotate expat teachers with no formal teaching qualification. I arrived in Bangkok in 2019 with a DAEFLE certification — the diploma in teaching French as a foreign language — and joined Berlitz as a full-time certified instructor. Today I specialise in DELF, DALF, TCF, TEF, IB and A-Level preparation.
 I&#x27;ve since built my own private academy. My students in Bangkok range from complete beginners (many of them Thai professionals) to exam candidates preparing DELF, TCF Canada, IB, and A-Level. I teach in person across Bangkok and online via Zoom to students on five continents.
 Importantly: ผมพูดภาษาไทยได้ครับ — I speak Thai at B1. That means I understand exactly where Thai speakers struggle with French pronunciation, grammar, and vocabulary. No other certified native teacher in Bangkok can say that.

 Frequently asked
 Questions about French lessons in Bangkok
 Does Rémi teach French in person in Bangkok? Yes. Rémi is based in Bangkok and offers both in-person private lessons in Bangkok and online lessons via Zoom worldwide. He has been teaching in Bangkok since 2019, including a full-time role as a certified instructor at the Berlitz Language Institute.
 Can Rémi teach French to Thai speakers? Yes. Rémi speaks Thai at a B1 level, which means he can explain concepts in Thai when needed and understands the specific difficulties Thai speakers face when learning French — such as tonal language differences, pronunciation, and grammar structure.
 What French exams can I prepare for in Bangkok? Rémi prepares students for all major French exams: DELF (A1 to B2), DALF (C1, C2), TCF Canada, TEF Canada, DELF Pro, and the French components of IB and A-Level programmes. Lessons are personalised to your exam date and current level.
 How much do French lessons with Rémi cost in Bangkok? Lessons start at 950 THB per class with a package of 20 online classes. Single classes are 1,100 THB online, or 1,250 THB face to face in Bangkok (there is no face-to-face package). Every class lasts 50 minutes, one-to-one only — no group classes. Edito textbooks in PDF and access to the Parisly self-study platform are included at no extra cost.
 Does Rémi teach beginners or only advanced students? Rémi teaches all levels, from complete beginners (A0) to advanced exam candidates (C1/C2). He adapts each lesson to your current level, learning style, and goals — whether you want conversational French, exam preparation, or business French.
