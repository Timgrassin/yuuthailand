 How to Enter Thailand 
 Recent News : Effective 15 July 2024, Thailand amended the Visa Exemption (VE) to 60-days from the previous 30-day stay.​ 
 ​ 
 More Recent News : In May 2026, the Thai cabinet voted to rescind the 60-day VE and return to the previous 30-day VE. However, as of late June 2026, this decision has not been published in The Royal Gazette , which is a necessary step to become law. 
 ​ 
 A VE can also be extended once for a further 30 days&mdash;extending your total stay .
