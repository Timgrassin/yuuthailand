 The Home of Muay Thai on Koh Tao, Thailand 
 Located in Sairee on Koh Tao, Island Muay Thai training facility provides the Island of Koh Tao with all the needs for visitors, locals and local fighters!
 Established in 2002, Island Muay Thai is the first Muay Thai training camp.
 With a combined 350+ fight experience our staff will ensure your techniques are correct and that you have a thorough exercise session.

 Hello from Kru Rin, Owner of Island Muay Thai 
 Kru Rin is one of the most experienced trainers on the island, with over 30 years working with Muay Thai.
 His gym has been operational for the last 20 years and he has played an important role in organising the regular fight nights on Koh Tao, with many of his students participating (and winning!).

 How to find us 
 We are located towards north Sairee about 30 metres past Asia Divers Resort turning. Continue on the main road where you will see the gym on the right hand side.
 Opening times: 
 Monday - Saturday 
Group Class: 8:00am and 4:00pm
Private Class: 10:00am and 6:00pm
Sunday open for Private Class Only
