 FAQs
 Q: Who is eligible for a Thailand Privilege Card? 
 Ans: The applicant for a Thailand Privilege Card should meet some specific criteria. It includes a foreign passport. That makes one legally able to live in Thailand according to the immigration law. It’ll prove that the applicant has stayed lawfully in Thailand. Moreover, this program has no age limitations. 
 Q: How much does a Thailand Privilege Card cost? 
 Ans: The price for a Thailand Privilege Card ranges from approximately 650,000 THB to 5,000,000 THB. (It’s about $17,700 to $136,000 USD), with different tiers and packages offering varying durations of stay (5-20 years), benefits, and family options. However, the most affordable option, the Bronze package, costs 650,000 THB. Also, it provides a 5-year visa. While higher tiers like the Reserve package can cost 5,000,000 THB. 
 Q: Can I work in Thailand with an elite visa? 
 Ans: No. Thai elite visa acts like an extended travel visa. On the contrary, you can live, travel, and manage personal affairs. To be able to work, you must have proper work clearance. You can look for work in Thailand, but after having a job, you must exit Thailand. You can re-enter on a non-immigrant visa and file for a job approval. 
 Q: Can I Add Family Members to a Thailand Privilege Membership Card? 
 Ans: Yes, on selected packages (Gold add-ons vary; Platinum/Diamond/Reserve commonly support family add-ons). You can add family members by paying an additional fee. It depends on the membership tier:RESERVE: 2,000,000 THB per additional family member. 
 Q: What is Tax Residency in Thailand? 
 Ans: Thailand evaluates your taxable income as an Elite Visa user for tax residency. If you spend 180 days or more in Thailand in a year and have received income from outside, you will be identified as a tax resident. In addition, Personal Income Tax (PIT) applies in Thailand. 
 Q: Can a Thai Elite Visa lead to permanent residency? 
 Ans: An Elite visa acts like an extended travel visa. It does not provide a direct path or authorization for permanent residency in Thailand. While it allows for extended stays, elite visa holders would need to switch to a different, work-authorized non-immigrant visa to qualify for permanent residence. 
 Q: Can I use my Elite Visa for my business purpose? 
 Ans: Of Course! This special visa is an ideal opening for business individuals. With this you can make unlimited networking, attend high-profile business meetings, and a great opportunity to seek investment.
