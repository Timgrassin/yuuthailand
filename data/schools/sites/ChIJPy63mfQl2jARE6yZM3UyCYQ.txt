 Long Term Resident Visa
 The LTR Visa offers a 10-year stay in Thailand with special benefits like low tax rate or even tax exemption , fast-track immigration, and a digital work permit. It was designed for qualified individuals in these four categories:
 Wealthy Global Citizens 
 Must have at least $1 million in assets and at least $500,000 invested in Thai property, bonds, or businesses.
 Wealthy Pensioners 
 Age 50+ with $80,000+ annual income (or $40,000+ with a $250,000 investment in Thailand). 
 Work-From-Thailand Professionals 
 For remote workers with a $50M+ revenue employer. Applicants need $80K+ annual income or $40K+ with a master’s, IP ownership, or $1M+ funding. No work experience required, and subsidiaries can use parent company financials.
 Highly-Skilled Workers 
 Experts in targeted industries (tech, R&D, healthcare, etc.), earning $80,000+ per year (or $40,000+ with a master’s in science/tech). Can benefit from a low tax rate of up to 17%.
 If qualified, the visa is approved within 1 month. Spouses and children can apply as dependents under the same visa.

 Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)
 The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) is a 5-year multiple-entry visa designed for digital nomads, remote workers, and freelancers who want to stay in Thailand while working for foreign employers. Each stay allows up to 180 days, with the option to extend for another 180 days.
 Age Requirement 
 Must be at least 20 years old. 
 Financial Proof 
 Minimum bank balance of 500,000 THB (~$13,600 USD). 
 Employment 
 Must work for a company outside Thailand, provide a professional portfolio if self-employed, or join programs like Muay Thai, Thai culinary training, or medical treatment. 
 The DTV Visa allows long-term, flexible stays in Thailand with the freedom to work remotely. Holders can enjoy the country’s modern infrastructure, affordable lifestyle, and cultural experiences while maintaining a foreign income. Spouses and children are also eligible to join under the visa program.

 1-year Retirement Visa
 The Thai Retirement Visa is a non-immigrant visa allowing foreigners aged 50 and above to live in Thailand for one year. To qualify, applicants must meet financial requirements and have a clean criminal record. 
 Financial requirements:
 Maintain a Thai bank balance of at least 800,000 Baht for a minimum of two months before applying.
 Earn a monthly income of at least 65,000 Baht, including pension.
 Alternatively, have a combined bank deposit and annual income of at least 800,000 Baht.
This visa provides a secure and straightforward way for retirees to enjoy long stay in Thailand.
