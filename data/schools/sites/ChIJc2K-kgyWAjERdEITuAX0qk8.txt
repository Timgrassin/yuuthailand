 Although 2020 has been a very shaky year with the Covid pandemic, the SLS 120 hour/4 week TEFL course was akin to finding a jewel you didn't expect. I researched a half-dozen TEFL/CELTA. providers in the Bangkok/Pattaya corridor. What attracted to me the SLS TEFL course was that despite the pandemic, the course was being taught in person with a full teaching practicum ... and NOT on-line. At the invitation of Cillian Hayes (TEFL Instructor), I attended one of his lessons and observed several candidates conducting lessons the prior month to my start date. I began the course several weeks my visit and found the SLS family atmosphere, instructional content and wide-ranging teaching practicum to varied student lots invaluable. The follow-up support from resume guidance to job placement advice/assistance was also a nice touch. I'd encourage anyone in Pattaya and vicinity to enroll at a SLS course as it's been a very meaningful experience.
