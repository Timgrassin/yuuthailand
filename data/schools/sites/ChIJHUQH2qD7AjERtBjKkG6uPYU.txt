 One-stop service
 RJ-SS started business as a manpower supply management company. We have over 20 years of experience in providing manpower management support for both Thais and expatriates. Our services include recruitment, project management, comprehensive payroll services, tax submissions, and various benefits under Thai labor law. Furthermore, at RJ-SS, we have a team of experts to assist in Thai visa and work permit, ensuring compliance with the Immigration Act and Labor Laws in Thailand.
