 Lighthouse International School Phuket
 You Belong | Your Pathway | Future-Focused
 Lighthouse has a Kindergarten, Primary School and Secondary School all with supported excellent English learning. We are a caring school community where every student is known by name and supported as an individual. Choose a personalised pathway to success with; flexible course options, project-based learning, and tailored support. Students are future ready with the Canadian Alberta curriculum & Cambridge option combined with AI literacy, entrepreneurship, and business studies giving students global opportunities.
 Learning Pathway
 We offer the complete personalised learning journey for children aged 2-18 years. Children graduate with the internationally accredited Canadian Alberta Education Diploma. We also have Cambridge pathway with IGCSE, as well as French department, opening the door to international universities.
