 Frequently Asked Questions
 Find answers to the most common questions about our massage courses and training.
 Who can join these massage courses? 
 Our courses are suitable for beginners, therapists, spa professionals, and anyone interested in learning authentic Thai massage techniques.
 Do I need previous experience? 
 No previous experience is required. We offer training for both beginners and advanced learners.
 Will I receive a certificate after completing the course? 
 Yes, students receive a certificate upon successful completion of the course.
 Are international students welcome? 
 Yes, we welcome students from around the world and provide a friendly and supportive learning environment.
 How much practical training is included? 
 Our programs focus strongly on hands-on practical training, allowing students to build real confidence and professional skills.
 Which course should I start with? 
 If you are new to massage training, Thai Massage or Oil Massage is usually the best place to start. We can also guide you based on your goals.
