 Thailand Visa Services 
 When you need to make sure that you have the right paperwork to apply for a new visa, think Star Visa Service. We will assist you with all your Thai visa requirements and make sure your visa applications and interactions with Chiang Mai Immigration office are easy and effective. Whether you need assistance with your Retirement Visa, Dependent Visa, Business Visa, Student Visa or Thai Marriage Visa.

 Legal Services 
 Star Visa Service provides legal assistance for foreigners in Thailand. Whether you need assistance with a marriage registration in Thailand, a divorce registration in Thailand, a birth reporting, a foreign passport issuing, a business or a property issue or a family law matters.
