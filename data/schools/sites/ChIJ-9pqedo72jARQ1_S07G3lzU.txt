 Welcome to Rungjaras Muay Thai Gym
 Rungjaras Muay Thai is run by Bash, a Thailand Muay Thai Champion operating in Chiang Mai. Kru Bash offers one on one and group classes. From Beginner to Advanced Professional Fighter. At Rungjaras we prefer one on one and smaller classes so you get the attention you deserve in order to catapult you into an advanced fighter. Our classes are fun, friendly and we will listen to your needs and monitor your fitness capabilities.
 Why Learn Muay Thai?
 Boost your Fitness
 Muay Thai isn&#8217;t just fighting and self defence, it is great for cardiovascular fitness. Our sessions will push you to your limits. 
 Mindful Training
 Muay Thai is great for the mind. The brain is a muscle that benefits from training too, you will develop focus, confidence and discipline. 
 Core strength
 Muay Thai will help develop your core strength, building balance and endurance that will help in all aspects of your life.
