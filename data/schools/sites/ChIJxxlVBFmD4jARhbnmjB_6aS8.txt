 Curious at every age
 Early Years
 Ages 1-5
 The Early Years Programme provides a play-based foundation where curiosity and exploration lead the way. Children develop confidence, independence, and the social, emotional, and early academic skills they need to thrive.
 Learn More 
 Elementary School
 Ages 6-11
 Grades 1-5
 The Primary Years Programme empowers students to explore , question , and take action through inquiry-based learning . By connecting classroom learning to the real world, students develop strong academic foundations alongside global awareness, empathy, and a lifelong love of learning.
 Learn More 
 Secondary School
 Ages 11-17
 Grades 6-11
 MYP + AP
 The Middle Years Programme up to Grade 11 challenges students to think critically , collaborate , and apply their learning to real-world issues . As students prepare for their future, they can pursue Advanced Placement (AP) courses, providing a rigorous pathway to universities around the world.
 Learn More
