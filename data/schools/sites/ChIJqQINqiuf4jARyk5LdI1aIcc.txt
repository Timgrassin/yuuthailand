INDEPENDENT LAW FIRM
IN BANGKOK, THAILAND
 Blumenthal Richter Sumet & Schuler is a leading law firm in Bangkok, Thailand. We provide legal services in all practice areas with advice that is commercial, responsive and efficient. We create simplicity for clients in the face of increasing complexity for them to make the right decisions, achieve their strategic priorities and identify new opportunities. Our lawyers take the time to understand each of our client’s unique needs, and provide advice with in-depth knowledge of your sectors and markets.
 For more than 45 years, our licensed Thai, European, U.S. and Chinese attorneys have worked at our clients’ side, understanding your business while serving as a trusted counterpart who knows the law. Assisting you as a team, we collaborate with our clients to navigate not only the legal issues but the regulatory factors affecting your business sectors.
 Our law firm’s experience in the automotive, real estate, hospitality, manufacturing, infrastructure, construction, transportation, technology, media, telecommunications, energy, chemicals, insurance and healthcare sectors allows us to understand your needs and add value for our clients to succeed.
