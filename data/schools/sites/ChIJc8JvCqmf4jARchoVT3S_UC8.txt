 Welcome to Bangkok International Preparatory & Secondary School
 Academic excellence
 In our most recent A Level cohort, over half of all grades were A*–A, and across IGCSE and KS2 students exceeded expectations , making on average half a grade more progress than predicted.
 Outstanding British education
 Progress with a personalised English National Curriculum , challenging students to think critically and creatively, connect ideas to the real world, and work together to solve problems.
 Proudly sustainable
 Join Thailand’s premier international Eco School. Lead sustainability practices through our Forest Campus, on-site Wander Woods Nature Trail and transformative eco-friendly projects.
 Proven pathways to top universities
 Enter top QS-ranked universities worldwide , including Oxbridge, Ivy League and UCL. Excel empowered by Bangkok Prep’s Futures Team of dedicated and qualified counsellors.
 Life values
 Strengthen social, emotional and physical competencies guided by the 11 Bangkok Prep Values and our Learner Profile .
 Vibrant school community
 Join a values-driven community of 60&#43; nationalities. Together, we embrace sustainability, support charities and celebrate two decades of proven success in Bangkok.
 Prep Pups Playgroup
 Curious explore from walking stage up to 5 years old are warmly welcomed in our Prep Pups Playgroup with fun-filled sessions tailored by age and designed by a dedicated leader.
 Multilingual proficiency
 Receive English language support while mastering Thai, Japanese, Spanish, French or Mandarin. Enriched by the ISP Multilingualism programme , 97% of students earn multilingual proficiency.
 Exceptional learning environment
 Learn in our world-class spaces . The school campus features innovative spaces that nurture curiosity and creativity. These modern facilities provide advanced resources for academic and personal growth.
 Exceptional educators
 Benefit from qualified teachers specialised in international education. Through a low student-to-teacher ratio, they craft personalised learning paths, enabled by close monitoring and ongoing feedback.
 Enriching school life
 Keep learning with 200&#43; extracurricular activities , the Highly & Exceptionally Able Learners programme, 100&#43; leadership opportunities, community service and international experiences.
 Where confidence grows
 Bangkok Prep learners collaborate with peers around the world through International Learning Opportunitie s for Students as part of the International Schools Partnership network.

 Growing curious, confident minds
 As part of International Schools Partnership, every child’s journey is enriched with proven learning experiences that spark curiosity and grow confidence.
 Academic achievement
 Strong academic outcomes open pathways to leading universities and future opportunities worldwide. 
 Life competencies
 Build the human skills needed to adapt, collaborate and keep learning throughout life.
 Multilingualism
 Develop fluency across languages,  cultures and global environments. 
 International opportunities
 Take part in global experiences that broaden perspectives and build confidence.
 Life and career pathways
 Explore personalised university and career pathways shaped around individual ambitions.
 Academic achievement
 Strong academic results begin with students who feel challenged, supported and engaged in learning. Students across ISP schools consistently achieve results above local and international benchmarks through learning experiences that challenge thinking, deepen understanding and help every learner keep getting better.
 Life competencies
 Students are growing up in a world shaped by rapid technological change, uncertainty and challenges we cannot yet predict. To thrive, they need the curiosity to question, the confidence to adapt, the ability to collaborate and the resilience to keep learning throughout life. International Schools Partnership – ISP has partnered with Hughes Hall, University of Cambridge through Camtree and DEFI to embed life competencies into everyday learning experiences across ISP schools worldwide.
 Multilingualism
 Language opens doors to new cultures, perspectives and opportunities. Students across ISP schools develop proficiency in multiple world languages while continuing to strengthen their home or first language. Through meaningful learning experiences and international collaboration, students build the confidence to communicate across cultures and thrive in global environments.
 International opportunities
 International learning experiences help students see beyond their own environment and understand the world from different perspectives. Students across ISP schools take part in unique collaborations, exchanges, competitions and global experiences that encourage curiosity, teamwork and independent thinking. Working with global partners, these life-changing opportunities help students build confidence, strengthen relationships and adapt to new experiences across cultures and countries.
 Life and career pathways
 Every student’s future pathway is different and through our Future Pathways programme, families are supported by specialist counsellors and advisors who guide subject choices, university applications and career decisions with personalised support throughout the learning journey. University fairs, global experts and our A–Z Speaker Series help students explore emerging careers and future industries with confidence. Students benefit from exclusive ISP scholarships and opportunities with some of the world’s most recognised institutions.

 Begin your Bangkok Prep journey
 Start your child&#039;s journey with confidence. Complete your application and prepare for an exciting start. 
 01
 Enquire now
 Take the first step by completing our short enquiry form - this helps our Admissions Executives prepare for your visit and understand your family’s needs, ensuring students get the right care and support from day one. 
 02
 Book a tour
 Receive an invitation for an introductory visit and campus tour , where you can explore our vibrant learning spaces and meet the team. If you’re not in Bangkok, we’re happy to arrange a virtual meeting so you don’t miss out. 
 03
 Submit the application
 Once you’ve experienced Bangkok Prep, submit your application form and supporting documents to take the next step towards joining our school community. 
 04
 Schedule an assessment
 We’ll arrange an age-appropriate assessment to ensure students are placed in the right year group and supported to thrive from the start. 
 05
 Offer and enrolment
 We’ll review your child’s application, school reports and availability of places, then respond quickly with an admissions decision. Successful applicants receive a formal offer and details for fee payment to secure their place. 
 06
 Visa support
 Our Government Liaison Officer (GLO) supports international families in obtaining students&#039; Non-Ed and/or Parent visa application(s) . 
 07

 Join a community where students lead, grow and thrive, preparing for life - we’re excited to welcome your family and support a smooth transition into school life.
