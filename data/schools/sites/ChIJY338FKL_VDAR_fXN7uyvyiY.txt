 Located in the heart of Koh Phangan, Jomhod Muay Thai Gym offers authentic traditional Thai boxing training in a welcoming environment. Our experienced trainers have competed at the highest levels and are passionate about sharing the art of Muay Thai.
 Whether you are a complete beginner looking to learn the basics, a fitness enthusiast wanting an intense workout, or an experienced fighter preparing for competition, we have programs tailored to your needs.

 Frequently Asked Questions
 Do I need experience?
 No! We welcome complete beginners. Our trainers will teach you proper technique from day one.
 What should I bring?
 Bring water, a towel, and comfortable workout clothes. We provide all training equipment.
 Can I try a class first?
 Yes! Train once/day (THB 300) to try a class before committing to a longer package.
 How do I book?
 Contact us on WhatsApp or just show up 5 minutes before class. No advance booking required for group sessions.
