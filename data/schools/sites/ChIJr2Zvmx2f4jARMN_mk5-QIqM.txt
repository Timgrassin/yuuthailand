 Looking to Enjoy Thailand a Bit Longer? 
 If you're captivated by Thailand and wish to extend your adventure, we've got the perfect way for you to stay longer&mdash;an Education (ED) Visa! 
Studying with us at Knowledge Language Center allows you to dive deep into the language and culture of Thailand. We provide courses in Thai, English, and Mandarin Chinese, all fully approved by the Ministry of Education (MOE), ensuring you receive top-notch education. 
Our courses are designed to help you not only learn a new language but also experience the vibrant culture of Thailand firsthand. Whether you're here to enhance your career or simply to explore, we promise an unforgettable experience. 
Curious to know more? Reach out to us, and let's start your exciting journey in Thailand! 
 ​ 
 At our school, you'll join a vibrant community of students from all walks of life. It's a fantastic place to make new friends while learning. Many of our students find that their language skills improve significantly, boosting their confidence in everyday conversations and at work. Plus, you'll have the chance to share and explore different cultures and languages with your classmates, making your learning experience even more fun and unique. 
 Our amazing teachers have helped countless students from all over the world learn Thai, English, and Mandarin Chinese. They can't wait to support you on your language journey! When you join us, you'll find our teachers are always ready to lend a hand, answer your questions, and offer extra help whenever you need it.

 WHO IS ELIGIBLE ? 
 All foreigners on excemption 30, 45, 90 and 60 
 days visas. Section 17 visas. 
 Tourist visa 60 days 
 OUR PROCESS 
 Apply in Bangkok 
 Option 1 
 Visit our school with your passport and passport photos. 
 Enroll our Thai course 
 Wait for the approval letter from the Ministry of Education ( up to 5 weeks) 
 Collect the approval letter from our school. 
 Apply for the Non-Immigrant ED visa at Immigration 
 Contact us with your passport 
 within the first 7 days after you get ED visa 
 OUR PROCESS 
 Apply from your home country 
 Option 2 
 Enroll our Thai course 
 Our team will contact you via email for the necessary document 
 Wait for the approval letter from the Ministry of Education 
 ( up to 5 weeks) 
 The approval letter will be mailed to you via DHL 
 Apply for the Non-Immigrant ED visa at Thai Embassy or Thai Consular 
 Contact us with your passport 
within the first 7 days after your arrival
