 The first and the most unique Thai cooking class in Chiangrai since 2007 with more than 50, 000 students from all over the world. 
 Learn to cook Thai like a local, so you can impress you family and friends back home. A real hands on experience &hellip;you will be amazed what you can cook!!! 
 There is no better way to learn Thai cooking once you are in Thailand than taking a Thai cooking class. 
The cooking class at Suwannee is an ideal home-style learning environment that is different from most of the opportunities in the hotels and restaurants in Thailand. Not will you be only watch and participate in the cooking of a number of traditional Thai dishes; also as part of our course we visit a local market to learn about local exotic fruits and vegetables, there is always an abundance of new things to see&hellip;&hellip;and taste! 
 Why choose Suwannee 
 Cooking Class in Thai family style 
 All ingredients are organic 
 Class size is limited to 12 persons in order to maximize individual attention 
 We are fully licensed and insured 
 Support the local farmers and sellers 
 Free transportation in downtown area and meet up location nearby the market ( downtown ) 
 Free pictures and videos of your activity per e-mail and on the Internet 
 Free market tour/tasting tour with a temple visiting 
 Free WIFI in the kitchen area. 
 We offer an individual cooking class, everyone has his/her own cooking station! 
 Take a great taste of Thai food back home and becoming a Thai chef! 
 And of course we welcome vegetarians/vegans in all of our course, we will show you how to substitute vegetables or tofu for meat or fish in any dishes. 
 Please join and learn how to cook real Thai food with us
