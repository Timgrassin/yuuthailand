 What is Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (BJJ)?
 Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (BJJ) is a martial art that focuses on ground fighting and submission holds . It came from Japan and got popular in Brazil, thanks to the Gracie family. 
 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bXxD_gLW0R0 
 History of BJJ Brazilian Jiu Jitsu 
 Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (BJJ) started as a form of judo. Mitsuyo Maeda brought it to Brazil in the early 20th century. He taught Carlos Gracie, who then adapted these techniques. These changes were the start of Brazilian Jiu Jitsu. The Gracie family helped make it popular around the world. This martial art focuses on ground fighting and submission holds. It allows smaller people to defend against bigger ones. Over time, BJJ has grown into a key part of mixed martial arts (MMA). Fighters like the Gracies have shown its effectiveness in various competitions globally.

 Adult GI BJJ 
 Adult Gi Grappling focuses on fighting with the traditional uniform, known as a gi. This style emphasises technique over strength. It lets fighters use holds and leverage to control their opponent. In Gi Grappling, students learn from basics to complex moves. They practice chokes, locks, and how to gain a superior position. The class suits both beginners and experienced grapplers. Everyone trains hard while respecting their partners' safety.

 Benefits of BJJ
 Training in BJJ makes your body stronger and fitter. It also sharpens your mind, teaching you how to stay calm and think under pressure. 
 Physical fitness 
 Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (BJJ) offers a great way to get fit. Classes involve lots of movement and training drills that boost your stamina, strength, and flexibility. Rolling on the mats burns calories just like any intense workout at the gym would. This makes BJJ an excellent choice for anyone wanting to improve their overall fitness level. Training regularly in BJJ also teaches you how to move your body in new ways, increasing your agility and coordination. Whether you're practising takedowns or working on ground techniques, every session is a full-body workout. Practitioners often notice improvements not just in their muscle tone but in their cardiovascular health as well. 
 Mental discipline 
 Practising Brazilian Jiu Jitsu teaches you to focus and stay calm. You learn to control your mind during challenging situations, both on the mat and in daily life. This sport helps build strong willpower as fighters must push through tough training sessions at gyms like Born to Roll Club Phuket. As you keep training, your concentration improves. Over time, you become better at setting goals and achieving them. This skill is not just about sports; it helps in school or work too.BJJ challenges make you mentally tougher, preparing you to face any obstacle with confidence. 
 Self-defence skills 
 Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu teaches you to defend yourself. People learn how to control an opponent using grips and leverage. This means even if someone is bigger, you can protect yourself by applying smart techniques instead of brute force. Training in BJJ builds confidence outside the gym too. You'll know how to handle tough situations calmly. Skills like locks and holds make sure you can keep safe in many scenarios without needing to strike hard. 
 Tips for Beginners
 Tips for Beginners: 
 Start your Jiu Jitsu journey on the right foot by picking a gym that suits your style.

 Focus on mastering basic moves before jumping into complex ones.

 Finding the right gym 
 Choosing the best martial arts club in Phuket depends on your goals. If you want to focus on Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, check infamous coaches like Craig Jones or André Galvão's influence on the training programmes. Big names bring great techniques and fresh perspectives. Make sure the gym offers classes that fit your schedule and level. Beginners need places with introductory courses, while more experienced fighters might look for advanced drills. See if they have special sessions for kids or no-gi grappling if that’s what interests you. Visit a few places, meet the instructors, and take a trial class to feel the vibe. The right environment can boost your learning and make training enjoyable. 
 Preparing for classes 
 To get ready for classes, wear comfortable clothes that allow you to move freely. This could be a T-shirt and shorts or any athletic gear designed for exercise. Make sure your outfit is clean and fits well so you can focus on learning without distractions. Bring a water bottle to stay hydrated and a small towel to wipe away sweat during training. Learn the basic rules of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsubefore your first class. You can find videos online or read guides about BJJ fundamentals. Knowing the basics will help you understand what the instructor is teaching and make it easier to follow along with the lesson. Show up early to introduce yourself to the teacher and let them know if you have any injuries they should be aware of. 
 Proper techniques 
 After you prepare for classes, learning the right way to perform moves in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsuis key. Use your body and mind together. Keep a strong focus and stay calm. This helps you learn faster and stay safe. Practice makes perfect in BJJ. Work on your holds, throws, and groundwork often. Watch skilled fighters like Ffion Davies or Josh Hinger to see techniques in action. Ask your coach at the gym for tips to improve. Always wear protective gear like mouthguards and rash guards during practice to avoid injuries.
