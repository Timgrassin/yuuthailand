 OUR STORY

 FAQ
 Frequently Asked Questions
 What is Revolution Gym? + Revolution Gym is a Muay Thai training camp in Phuket, Thailand, based at Surin Beach. Former professional champions coach group and private sessions for all levels, from complete beginners to competitive fighters.
 Where is Revolution Gym located? + Revolution Gym is located at Surin Beach, Phuket, Thailand, within walking distance of the beach.
 Do I need experience to train at Revolution Gym? + No. Revolution Gym welcomes all levels, from first-time beginners to professional fighters. Classes run morning and afternoon, six days a week (Monday–Saturday).
 Does Revolution Gym offer accommodation? + Yes. Revolution Gym offers partnered and onsite accommodation close to the gym, ideal for solo travelers, training partners, or families staying for a week or longer.
 How do I book training at Revolution Gym? + Training packages can be booked directly online. For custom lengths, private sessions, or Camps (training + accommodation), contact Revolution Gym via WhatsApp or email.
