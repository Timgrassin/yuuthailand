 Thai Cooking Masterclass
 Exploring the flavours of a destination and mastering its cooking techniques allow you to take delicious memories home with you to enjoy again and again. Our interactive Thai cooking classes, led by our talented Thai chef, let you uncover the secrets of Thai cuisine and its vibrant flavours and spices. Suitable for individuals or groups, each session runs for three hours and concludes with a delightful lunch of your own creation.
