 BaiPai Thai Cooking School
 Baipai Thai Cooking School 
 Baipai Thai Cooking School was established in April 2002 and accredited by the Ministry of Education of Thailand. Discover luxury and calm at Baipai Thai Cooking School in Bangkok. A family bonding Thai cooking experience for tourists, with hands-on classes, fresh ingredients, expert local chefs, and a beautiful traditional Thai house.
Our school is an ideal home-style learning in Zen-like environment. You will be cooking in the opened-air Thai Style charming two storey house with wooden decor in tranquil surroundings, and relaxing atmosphere. We welcome all interested residences, foreign expatriates and visitors with the use of English as at teaching medium.
 *Baipai nearby 4.8 km from Chatuchak weekend market (approx. 10 min.)
 Half-Day Cooking Class 
 Looking for completely hands-on cooking experience? You come to the RIGHT place!!! Learning Thai Cooking Class with us not only provides you knowledge, tips and tricks but also makes you know three words: fun, easy and yummy!!!  Do you have Questions?
 Most frequently questions answered here
 How many class do you have each day? 
 There are 2 sessions per day. Morning and afternoon class. 
Our morning class is during 9.30 to 13.30hrs. and afternoon class start is during 13.30 to 17.30.
 Do you have a private course? 
 Yes, we can. We can arrange the Private Cooking Course with multiple menu option can be select. More information will be enquire via our direct contact email.
 Do you offer the course in English? 
 Yes. Our course is normally conducted in English.
 What is maximum capacity in one course? 
 12 persons is maximum capacity in one course for Individual Cooking station. However, there are 2 workshops at Baipai. So, we are able to operate maximum 24 persons per session for Individual Cooking station. 
 Can you accommodate Team Building Activity and How many person ? 
 Yes. We are able to accommodate Team Building Activity and customized your activity from 12 persons up to 60 persons
