 Rachai Muay Thai Gym – Authentic Training in the Heart of Patong
 Located in the vibrant heart of Patong, Rachai Muay Thai Gym has been a pillar of the Muay Thai community for over 20 years. As a family-owned and operated gym, we pride ourselves on offering a welcoming and supportive environment for everyone—whether you’re a complete beginner, a fitness enthusiast, or a seasoned fighter preparing for your next professional bout.

 meet your trainers
 TUM
 MUAY THAI SPECIALIST
 Dekwat MT is a seasoned Muay Thai fighter with a wealth of experience in the ring. With 150 fights under his belt, he has battled against top-level competition in Thailand and internationally. Known for his resilience, technical precision, and powerful striking, Dekwat has claimed multiple championship titles, including prestigious stadium belts in Phuket. His participation in the Kunlun Fight 8-Man Tournament in Beijing, China, further showcases his ability to compete on the global stage.
 Guided by his trainer, Tum, Dekwat continues to sharpen his skills, proving himself as a formidable presence in the Muay Thai world.
 JJ
 MUAY THAI SPECIALIST
 Trainer JJ started his boxing journey at the young age of 5, developing his skills through years of dedication and competition at some of Thailand’s most prestigious boxing venues. He has fought at Eastern Region Boxing Stadium, Pattaya, Chonburi, Lumpinee Boxing Stadium, and Rajadamnern Boxing Stadium, earning a reputation for his technique, speed, and strategic fight intelligence.
 With an impressive 160 fights in his career, 130 wins, 27 losses, and 3 draws, Trainer JJ brings unmatched experience to the ring. Now, as a professional boxing coach, he is committed to training fighters of all levels—from beginners to professional athletes. His coaching style focuses on technical precision, footwork, defensive mastery, and fight strategy, ensuring his students develop both skill and confidence inside the ring.
 OIL
 MUAY THAI SPECIALIST
 Oil trained in Muay Thai for 15 years, fighting under two boxing names during his career. He trained at Muay Camp Pranchai, where he fought as Thong Pranchai, and at Muay Camp Beautiful Car Sergeant, where he was known as Pajero Beautiful Car Sergeant.
 He competed at Thailand’s top stadiums, including Lumpini Boxing Stadium and Rajadamnern Stadium. His achievements include winning the 100-class Southern Championship (Lekka) and the Muay Thai Championship at Patong Boxing Course in Phuket. Over his career, he fought a total of 250 fights.
 Sansab 
 MUAY THAI SPECIALIST
 Trainer Sansab is a highly skilled and accomplished Muay Thai fighter with years of experience in the ring. Starting his boxing journey at the age of 9, he has dedicated his life to mastering the art of Muay Thai. His career is decorated with numerous trophies and championship belts, solidifying his reputation as an elite fighter.
 Recognized throughout the boxing industry, Trainer Sansab has competed in some of Thailand’s most prestigious stadiums, including Rajadamnern Stadium, Lumpini Stadium, Channel 7, and Max Muay Thai. His deep knowledge of the sport, combined with his technical expertise and battle-tested experience, makes him a respected trainer and mentor for aspiring fighters.
 jackpot
 MUAY THAI SPECIALIST
 Trainer Jackpot began his Muay Thai journey at just 7 years old, fighting under the name Saen Sanae in numerous arenas across Southern Thailand. With a deep passion for the sport, he has built an impressive record of 71 fights, 64 wins, 4 losses, and 3 draws, earning multiple championship belts along the way.
 His extensive experience and technical expertise make him a highly skilled and knowledgeable trainer, dedicated to passing on the art of Muay Thai to the next generation of fighters.
