 Welcome to Railay Thai Cooking School 
 Come cook with us in one of the most beautiful corners of Thailand — Railay Beach, Krabi. At our cooking school, it’s not just about learning recipes. It’s about hearing the stories behind the food, discovering Thai culture, and having fun while creating real, delicious dishes. 
 Led by a seasoned chef with decades of experience in Thailand’s top 5-star resorts, our hands-on classes give you the skills (and confidence) to bring Thai flavors home. Whether you’re new to cooking or a seasoned foodie, you’ll leave with more than just a full stomach 
— you’ll leave with a real taste of Thailand. 
 Join a class 
 Refresh your taste buds 
 What Makes Us Different? 
 It’s more than just great food. Our chef brings years of real experience from Thailand’s top kitchens, and every dish comes with a story. You won’t just follow a recipe — you’ll learn the how and why behind each ingredient, just like it’s taught in Thai homes.
