 Why Choose
Lex Nova?
 At Lex Nova, we assist our clients in navigating the dynamic legal landscape of Thailand, marked by its frequent updates and a certain level of unpredictability in how laws are applied. This environment can be particularly challenging for foreign investors. Our team is well-equipped to tackle the operational hurdles unique to the Thai market, boasting the legal expertise , resources, and adaptability required to address various complexities. 
 Our role as local legal advisors is to be the first point of contact for our clients when they seek top-notch and efficient legal guidance to support their operations in Thailand. This includes helping them through the intricacies of business and investment within the Thai legal framework . Our services extend to negotiating and drafting complex joint-venture agreements with Thai entities, securing BOI promotions , and acquiring the necessary licenses for business operations, as well as facilitating work permits and visas for executives and staff. 
 Located in Bangkok, Thailand, Lex Nova serves a diverse client base, including multinational and publicly traded companies, small to medium-sized enterprises, startups, individual investors, and entrepreneurs.  Lex Nova offers a comprehensive range of legal services including corporate and M&A, real estate, immigration, tax, labor law and employment, and family law. Our services are designed to support both new entrants and established businesses and investors navigating the Thai legal landscape.
 How does Lex Nova assist foreign investors in Thailand?
 We help foreign investors by offering expert guidance on the intricacies of business and investment within the Thai legal framework. Our services include negotiating and drafting joint-venture agreements, securing BOI promotions, acquiring necessary business licenses, and facilitating work permits and visas for executives and staff. Additionally, we provide incorporation services to help businesses establish their presence in Thailand. 
 What makes the Thai legal environment challenging for foreign investors?
 The Thai legal landscape is marked by frequent updates and a certain level of unpredictability in the application of laws. This can create significant operational hurdles for foreign investors. Lex Nova’s local expertise and adaptability help clients overcome these challenges effectively. Our lawyers in Bangkok are well-versed in navigating these complexities.

 What are the benefits of BOI promotion for businesses?
 The benefits of BOI promotion for businesses include corporate income tax exemptions or reductions, import duty exemptions on machinery and raw materials, permission to own land, and simplified visa and work permit procedures for foreign staff. These incentives make Thailand a more attractive destination for investment and help businesses reduce operational costs and enhance competitiveness.
