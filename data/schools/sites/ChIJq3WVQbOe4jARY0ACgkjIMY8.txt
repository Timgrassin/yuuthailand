 OPFIT
 OPFIT — Operational Fitness
OPFIT is a high-intensity, functional fitness system developed by Jean-Paul Jauffret, a French Foreign Legion instructor and internationally recognized Krav Maga expert.
Originally developed to help soldiers build strength, endurance, speed, and resilience in short, demanding training sessions, OPFIT is designed around one simple principle: maximum results with minimum equipment and time.
The training combines functional movements, bodyweight exercises, conditioning, strength work, and military-style physical challenges that can be performed almost anywhere—whether in a gym, outdoors, at home, or in the field.
Train anywhere. Train efficiently. Build operational fitness.

 Eskrima
 Cacoy Doces Pares Eskrima.
Eskrima (also known as Arnis or Kali) is a Filipino martial art focused on practical self-defense using sticks, blades, and empty-hand techniques. In this class, students learn coordination, timing, footwork, and situational awareness through drills and partner practice. Training emphasizes speed, control, and adaptability, making Eskrima effective, engaging, and suitable for beginners and experienced martial artists alike.
