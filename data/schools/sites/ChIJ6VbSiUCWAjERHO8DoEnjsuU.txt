 Fitz Club
 The Best Luxury Pattaya Sports Club and Fitness Centre in Pattaya, Thailand.
 Established as the Eastern Seaboard’s leading luxury fitness center in Pattaya, FITZ Club stands as the most prestigious Pattaya sports club, distinguished by the triple  TripAdvisor Traveler’s Choice Award . The fitness space spans an impressive complex surrounded by tropical landscapes on the Royal Cliff Hotels Group grounds. It offers world-class sporting amenities for both casual enthusiasts and serious athletes, boasting state-of-the-art exercise equipment including high-quality cardio machines, comprehensive strength-building equipment, an aerobics dance studio, and a jogging track. FITZ Club offers a diverse range of fitness classes led by certified instructors, including Yoga, high-intensity interval training, circuit training, and more.
 The highlight of our Pattaya sports club is its exceptional racquet sports facilities, featuring 7 floodlit tennis courts, 2 indoor glass back air-conditioned squash courts, and table-tennis equipment. The tennis facilities feature hard-surface courts that are on par with the Grand Slam courts, making it the go-to for top-tier players aiming to acclimate themselves to the Australian tournament conditions. Professional coaching is available for Tennis, with experienced ITF & ATP instructors providing private and group lessons for all skill levels.
 To add to our varied offerings, FITZ Club boasts a newly redesigned multi-level swimming pool, complete with a Jacuzzi that evokes the essence of the Amazon forest through the trees that surround it. The swimming pool also features water slides and water scooters to entertain our younger guests. It’s an excellent tropical paradise to unwind after an intense workout.
 FITZ Club has also added a new multipurpose sports courts using the latest  CoolTop  technology that helps dissipate heat efficiently-delivering a significantly cooler and more comfortable playing surface. Designed for pickleball, basketball, football, and badminton, these state-of-the-art courts make Fitz Club the premier destination in Pattaya, offering the widest variety of sports under one roof.
 Moreover, members and guests can benefit from professional fitness assessments, customised training programs, and expert nutrition advice provided by our certified fitness instructors. Guests staying at the Royal Cliff enjoy complimentary access to the gym and swimming pools, while non-hotel guests are always welcome and can choose from a range of membership plans or convenient day passes.

 7 FLOODLIT TENNIS COURTS
 Open daily from 07.00 am – 08.00 pm, our 7 floodlit hard courts are of Grand Slam standards.
 Equipment (Tennis racquets, balls, socks and shoes) can be rented at the reception and professional tennis coaching is available to help you improve your game. 
 We can also help set-up friendly tennis matches with other members. 
SQUASH
 We offer 2 air-conditioned squash courts with the proper flooring so that you can have fun and improve your game.
 TABLE TENNIS
 Practice your hand-eye coordination and foot speed with this fun and challenging sport.
 MULTIPURPOSE COURT (Pickleball, Badminton, Basketball and Futsal)
 Fitz Club is delighted to introduce the brand-new multi-purpose court. This exciting addition features a pickleball court with CoolTop technology, which can be converted for basketball, badminton, and futsal, making Fitz Club the only sports club in the Eastern Seaboard region to offer such an impressive range of activities. (If you wish to play sports other than pickleball, please speak to our staff in advance.)
 FITNESS CENTRE
 Our air-conditioned gym provides state-of-the-art cardio and weight training equipment to help get you into the shape of your life. Kindly note that proper sportswear is required to enter and children under 14 years are not permitted inside.
 SAUNA & STEAM ROOMS
 Enjoy and relax in our brand-new Sauna and Steam Rooms! We are excited to offer the latest infra-red technology heating, premium equipment and stylish design. A new experience in sauna and steam rejuvenation awaits.
 Please note that a maximum of 4 people is allowed per time with a time limit of 30 minutes per session. For sauna room usage we kindly ask that you wear only towels.
 Please notify our staff in advance of your arrival time and the temperature you desire so you can maximise your enjoyment. 
 SWIMMING POOL
 Enjoy the Fitz Club swimming pool, complete with a Jacuzzi that evokes the essence of the Amazon forest through its surrounding beautiful trees. Plus, there are water slides to keep the kids entertained. The tropical paradise to unwind after your intense workout.
 AEROBIC STUDIO
 Enjoy your workouts and experience exciting fitness classes inside our Aerobic Studio.
 We have installed shock-absorbing padded cushions underneath our waterproof engineered hardwood flooring that would help provide much-needed cushion for your joints and knees. This material provides overall floor protection underneath and absorbs motion and impact when exercising. We can’t wait for you to drop in and experience it!
 THAI BOXING TRAINING
 Improve muscle strength and cardio endurance with our high-intensity interval training that focuses on the power and agility of Thai Boxing.
 1. Class Reservation: Reservations must be made one day in advance.
 2. Registration and payment must be done at least 20 minutes before classes start.

 OUR SQUASH AND TENNIS COURTS IN PATTAYA
 FITZ Club has the best Pattaya Tennis court facilities with 7 new floodlit, hard-surface tennis courts. It is ideal for tournaments, Thailand Tennis academy, and Tennis camps. The Tennis courts are the same standard as the Grand Slam courts and the only ones in Thailand that uses the vibrant velocity blue and Florida green combination making the balls very visible. The Pattaya Tennis community is very lively here with a multitude of players from all standards.
 Our Squash courts are second to none with glass back and high-quality surface. It can be compared to courts used on the professional competition levels. The Squash facilities are also designed for competition and high-level tournaments with areas for spectators. For Tennis or Squash in Pattaya this is the ideal venue.
 MEMBERSHIP INFORMATION AND PRICES
 The FITZ Club – Racquets, Health & Fitness is perfect for those living in the Pattaya and Jomtien region, as well as for people who often come to Pattaya on weekends. In addition to hotel guests and members, our Pattaya sports club is happy to welcome visitors who would like to use the fitness centre on a daily basis. The club provides a number of membership programs. Members enjoy many special privileges, including access to the facilities at Royal Cliff. You are welcome to drop by FITZ club, as this will give you the opportunity to see why it is one of the best luxury sports centres in Pattaya and the Eastern Seaboard region. Our team will be more than happy to show you around.
 ONE DAY PASS
 We are pleased to welcome first-time visitors with a complimentary trial of our Pattaya sports club facilities, which includes access to our gym, pool, sauna and steam room, as well as one hour of your choice of either tennis, pickleball, or squash. For guests seeking a short-term workout option, we also provide convenient  daily passes , available for purchase at the reception. A variety of pass types are offered to suit your preferences.
