 Programs
 Full Camp Experience
 Our Muay Thai camp covers every level. From beginners to pros, each program is built to push your skills with real experience, focus, and care.
 Beginner
 New to Muay Thai? We’ll teach you the basics step by step. Stance, movement, and simple combos. No experience needed. Just show up and start learning.
 Advanced
 Sharpen your skills, improve timing, and push your limits. Perfect for those with experience who want to level up with strategic pad work, sparring, and drills.
 Pro
 We can organize matches at top stadiums like International Stadium,   Phetchbuncha and  Rajadamnern , support your weight cut and be in your corner.
 Kids
 Fun, safe, and active sessions for kids aged 6+. We build confidence, discipline, and fitness through playful Muay Thai routines.
 Ice Bath
 Recover faster and come back stronger. Our ice baths help reduce soreness, speed up recovery, and keep you ready for the next session.
 Hydration
 Stay fueled in the heat. We have cold water and hydration drinks ready in our fridge, so you don’t have to carry anything. Train hard without burning out.
 Equipment
 Train with quality gear. Gloves, Wraps, and Shin Guards are available in the gym. All equipment is cleaned, disinfected, and air dried after every session. 
 Merch
 Take a piece of the gym with you.  We’ve got T-shirts and Muay Thai shorts here at the gym, fierce designs and ready to wear. Just show up and get what you need. 
 All Inklusive

 We offer airport pickups, accommodation near the gym and motorbike rental, so everything is taken care of. No planning stress. Just focus on your training and enjoy your time here.

 Prepare!
 You’re about to book your Traincation? Nice.
 But here’s the truth most people ignore.
 Muay Thai in Thailand isn’t your normal workout. It’s longer, harder, and more demanding on your body than anything you’re used to. 
 Show up underprepared, and most likely you won’t keep up. You’ll gasp for air just in the warm-up, cut pad rounds short because your chest and shoulders burn like fire, and then you spend more expensive time recovering than actually training.
 A Traincation should be progress, not survival.
