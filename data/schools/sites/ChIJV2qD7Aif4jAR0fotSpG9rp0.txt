 Our team 
 United by Excellence
 Bastien Trelcat
 Partner
 Bastien Trelcat is an experienced international lawyer specializing in investment immigration, corporate law, and cross-border transactions. Admitted to the Paris Bar in 2004, he holds a Master’s in Business Law and Corporate Taxation from Aix-Marseille University and an LL.M. in International Business Law from City University of Hong Kong. 
 Matthieu Bonnici
 Partner
 Matthieu Bonnici is an international lawyer with over 15 years of experience advising clients on investments and operations in China, Hong Kong, and Southeast Asia. Specializing in M&A, corporate restructuring, regulatory compliance, and dispute resolution, Matthieu focuses on technology-driven industries. He also advises startups and entrepreneurs on structuring, operations, and fundraising. 
 Who we help
 Global investors and cross-border clients come to Friedland Law when the legal issue spans more than one jurisdiction.
 Global investors (cross-border): We advise on cross-border transactions, investment structuring, and legal risk management across key markets. 
 Multinational corporations (cross-border): We support international businesses with M&A, reorganizations, compliance, and commercial contracting. 
 High-net-worth individuals and families (global mobility): We advise on investment immigration strategies—residency and citizenship by investment—aligned with cross-border planning needs. 
 Entrepreneurs and founders (international expansion): We help founders set up and scale internationally through structuring, partnerships, contracts, and IP support. 
 Family offices and professional advisors (cross-border support): We coordinate cross-border legal workstreams to support investment execution and mobility planning.

 Frequently asked questions
 What does Friedland Law do? 
 Friedland Law is an independent international law firm advising global investors and international businesses on cross-border legal matters, including corporate/M&A, investment immigration, compliance, IP, and commercial law. 
 Do you handle cross-border corporate transactions and M&A? 
 Yes. We advise on cross-border transactions including acquisitions, divestitures, joint ventures, and related corporate structuring and documentation. 
 Do you advise on residency or citizenship by investment? 
 Yes. We provide legal support for investment immigration strategies, including residency and citizenship by investment pathways, subject to jurisdiction-specific rules and eligibility. 
 Which offices do you have? 
 Our locations include Paris, Bangkok, Hong Kong (Quarry Bay), Shanghai, and Dallas, with additional cooperation/collaboration locations listed on our contact page. See: Offices 
 Do you provide U.S. legal advice? 
 No. Friedland & Partners LLC does not provide U.S. legal advice and will refer U.S.-law matters to local U.S. counsel. 
 How do we get started? 
 Send an inquiry through the contact page with your jurisdictions involved, timeline, and goals. We’ll respond with the next steps and the right point of contact.
