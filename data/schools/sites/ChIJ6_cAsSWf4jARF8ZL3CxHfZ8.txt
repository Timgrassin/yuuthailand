 FAQs 
 For any other questions, Contact us. 
 How are the courses conducted ? 
 - For Staff Trainings, the courses are half-day or Full Day. 
 - For individuals and students, the classes are 2 to 6 hrs per week - easily spread during the week, to maximize practice. 
 What if I miss a Session? 
 We try our best to adjust the group according to your availability. But if not, an extra session is conducted. Such extra sessions are limited to 2, per course. 
 Can I study alone? 
 Yes. You may. However, the prices are doubled , in that case. 
 Do you have After work & Week-end Classes? 
 Yes. However, the timings on weekends are limited and varying depending on the session schedules. Do contact to know further. 
 How large is the group? 
 - For Staff Training, we recommend not more than 20 for Basic Subjects and 10 for intermediate/advance. 
 - For individual trainings, the group is of maximum 5-8 persons. The Group age is matched to have a smooth conduct of course. 
 Language of Training ? 
 Although the trainer does speak thai, the training is conducted in English only.
