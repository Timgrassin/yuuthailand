 What we teach
 Our classes
 Eight classes across three families, six days a week. Every one is coached — nothing here is a room you let yourself into.
 Martial arts

 The people who coach you
 Meet the team
 Between them our coaches have spent a lifetime in the ring, and one of them is a kickboxing world champion. What matters more is how they teach. 
 Expect honest corrections, real attention on the pads, and enough patience to actually get you there. First class or fiftieth, you get coached properly.

 Stay with us · MAA Paradise Village
 Paradise 
 Village 
 Three private garden bungalows on the same grounds as the gym, for people who train hard and need to recover properly. Air-conditioned, 30 sqm each, with a balcony and a private garden. Roll out of bed, walk to the ring, eat well, sleep, repeat.
