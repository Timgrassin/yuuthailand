 Crafting Excellence in Every Sip: About Cocktail Academy Bangkok
 Welcome to Cocktail Academy Bangkok , where passion meets craftsmanship in the art of mixology. Our mission is to inspire, educate, and empower cocktail enthusiasts and professionals alike to create exceptional drinks while enjoying a memorable experience.
 Located in the vibrant heart of Bangkok, we offer hands-on workshops and tailored courses designed for all skill levels—from beginners exploring the basics to seasoned professionals looking to refine their techniques. Led by experienced mixologists, our sessions blend creativity and expertise, focusing on everything from classic cocktails to innovative, trend-setting creations.
 More than a drink
 At Cocktail Academy Bangkok, we believe cocktails are more than just drinks—they’re an expression of art, culture, and individuality. We take pride in curating an engaging environment where you can experiment with flavors, discover new techniques, and perfect your craft, all while sharing the joy of mixology with others.
