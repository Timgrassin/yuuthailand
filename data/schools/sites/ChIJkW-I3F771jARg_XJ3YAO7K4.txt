 Chiang Kong Border Chiang Khong Border is a key international crossing point between Thailand and Laos, located in Chiang Rai Province, northern Thailand. It connects to Huay Xai in Laos via the Fourth Thai–Lao Friendship Bridge over the Mekong River. This border is a gateway for travelers heading towards Luang Prabang or exploring the scenic Golden Triangle region. Chiang Khong is also known for its peaceful riverside atmosphere, local markets, and cultural charm, making it not just a transit point but a destination in itself. 
 Pai Pai, Mae Hong Son is a small mountain town in northern Thailand, nestled in a lush valley along the Pai River. Known for its laid-back atmosphere, Pai blends natural beauty with a vibrant bohemian vibe, attracting both local and international travelers. Surrounded by mountains, hot springs, waterfalls, and rice fields, it’s a perfect escape for those seeking peace and adventure.
