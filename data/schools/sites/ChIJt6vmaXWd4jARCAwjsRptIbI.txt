 Living and Studying in Bangkok
 Bangkok is one of Asia&#x27;s most dynamic cities and a popular starting point for people who want to teach English in Thailand . As a trainee in our program, you&#x27;ll experience a vibrant international city known for its incredible food, welcoming culture, and excellent transport connections across the country.
 Affordable Living
 Bangkok offers a high quality of life at a cost that is often much lower than major Western cities. From street food to modern apartments, daily life here is comfortable and affordable for TEFL trainees.
 Cultural Immersion
 Living in Bangkok allows you to experience Thai culture first-hand. Explore temples, markets, parks, and neighborhoods while meeting people from around the world who are also beginning their teaching careers.
 Explore Thailand
 Bangkok’s central location makes it easy to explore the rest of the country. From the mountains of northern Thailand to the beaches of the south, weekend trips and travel opportunities are always close by.
 Some people consider taking a TEFL course in Chiang Mai if they plan to live there long term. However, completing your TEFL training in Bangkok gives you the flexibility to teach anywhere in Thailand afterward. Many TEFL One graduates go on to teach in destinations such as Chiang Mai , Phuket , Pattaya , and other cities across the country, while others choose to stay and begin their teaching careers in Bangkok . If you are deciding between locations, you can read our guide on whether to take a TEFL course in Chiang Mai or Bangkok .

 Frequently Asked Questions
 What are the requirements for the course?
 No prior teaching experience is required. All you need is a strong command of English and a genuine interest in teaching. Many of our trainees arrive in Thailand with little or no classroom experience, which is exactly why the course is designed to provide structured training and practical preparation from the ground up.
 Throughout the course, trainees develop the skills and confidence needed to begin teaching in real schools. Because TEFL One works closely with schools across Thailand, many graduates transition directly from training into paid teaching positions after completing the program.
 You can also learn more about teaching English in Thailand without a degree and what opportunities exist for non-native English speakers who want to teach in Thailand .
 Is job placement assistance provided?
 Yes. We provide active job placement support to all TEFL One graduates, including resume preparation, interview guidance, and introductions to schools within our network across Thailand. Because of our relationships with schools, many graduates begin interviewing for teaching positions during or shortly after completing the course.
 While some TEFL programs simply provide a certificate and leave graduates to search for jobs independently, our approach focuses on helping teachers transition into real teaching roles as smoothly as possible.
 To see how we connect graduates with schools across Thailand, visit our job placement page .
 Where can I find more information about accommodation?
 Detailed information about accommodation options, including typical pricing, recommended areas to live in Bangkok, and the different types of housing available to trainees, can be found on our accommodation page .
 Bangkok offers a wide range of affordable accommodation options, from serviced apartments to modern condominiums near the training center. Many trainees choose housing within easy reach of Tipco Tower so that commuting to the course is simple and convenient during the training period.
