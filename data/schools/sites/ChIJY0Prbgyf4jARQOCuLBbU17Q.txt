 Calum von Moger is a three-time Mr. Universe, IFBB Pro classic physique competitor, and one of the most recognised bodybuilders of his generation.
 He co-founded UnReal Gym Bangkok with a clear vision: build the gym that serious lifters actually want. Calibrated plates, IPF-spec bars, a real posing room, and recovery on the roof. He picked the equipment, signed off on every layout, and trains here whenever he&#x27;s in town.
