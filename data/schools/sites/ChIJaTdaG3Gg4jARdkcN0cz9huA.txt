 With a career spanning over five decades, Master Toddy is one of the most renowned Muay Thai trainers in the world. Born and raised in Thailand, the home of Muay Thai, Master Toddy dedicated his life to the art of eight limbs, mastering its techniques, philosophy, and discipline. His passion for the sport has not only shaped world-class fighters but has also helped spread the discipline of Muay Thai across the globe.
