 Who we are; 
 Welcome to a well-established legal law firm in Thailand "Klarna Thai Corporate Law". Our Main Headoffices are in Watthana, Bangkok and Ottawa, Canada respectively. Both offices, are strategically situated in the heart of the business centers of the nation.
 Our dedicated team of experts endeavor to understand our clients’ needs to provide them with unparalleled legal services, that not only aim to match but even surpasses beyond our clients’ expectations.
 At Klarna-Thai Corporate Law we fully understand the stressful time our clients are facing, relating to whatever legal issues they need to settle therefore, we do not want to add another burden of charging (high fees) to the pre-existing predicament. Our law firm offers fixed and capped fee packages with legal cost that are highly competitive and fair, although we offer a very  reasonable capped legal fee, we do not compromise the international standard of professional service that our clientrs deserve to get.
 Triple C’s Core Values of Klarna Thai Corporate Law 
 Credibility – Is the firm foundation of our proven track records.
 Commitment – Is the pledge we give to our clients for the guidance in every step of the way.
 Compassion – Is to protect and care for our client’s welfare.
