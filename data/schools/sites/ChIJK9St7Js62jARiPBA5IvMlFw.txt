 Home Cooking Class and Local Market Visit
 Join an intimate, hands-on culinary adventure in the heart of Chiang Mai! Our classes welcome everyone—vegans, vegetarians, halal, gluten-free, and guests with food allergies—set in an authentic outdoor space without AC, but with fans to keep you comfortable. 
Whether you’re a total beginner or a passionate foodie, our small groups of 1 to 10 people offer a cozy, personalized experience. We guide you step-by-step, sharing insider tips to help you confidently master authentic Thai flavors. Your journey begins with a lively walk through the local market to pick fresh herbs, aromatic spices, and seasonal ingredients like a true local chef. Then, we head back to the kitchen to cook delicious dishes in morning, afternoon or evening sessions. 
Come hungry, learn timeless techniques, and bring home the skills to recreate Thailand’s flavors anywhere! Highlights include intimate groups, beginner-friendly guidance, a market-to-table experience, and a full menu of five authentic dishes. Reserve your spot today to cook, taste, and make unforgettable memories!

 Having fun
 You will meet new people and simply enjoy a new experience are all great reasons to do in a cooking class. Whether you sign up as a couple, with your best friend or a mother and daughter experience, you’re sure to have a few laughs.
 Teaching you to cook like a pro
 Have you ever wondered how TV chef’s make that flame erupt from the pan without burning the food or their eyebrows? Or do you wish you knew how to peel an onion without crying? Our cooking class can teach new techniques that can make you look and feel like a pro.
 Teaching you new things
 You can watch all the cooking TV show you want, but nothing gives you the hands-on experience of being in a real live class with a real live teacher. You can learn from an instructor who does things in real time without “the magic of TV” speeding up all the steps. Your instructor can also coach you on proper techniques while answering your cooking questions.
 No cleaning
 You know that whole “I cooked it, you clean it” debate that couples love to get in? In a cooking class you don’t have to flip a coin for who gets stuck with dish duty. You get the fun of cooking with none of the cleanup.
 Cooking Courses
 We offer three classes daily - a morning session, afternoon session and an evening session. O sessions begin with a guided tour of the local market to handpick fresh ingredients together. Afterward, we will head back to our home kitchen, where everyone will enjoy a hand-on cooking experience and prepare their own authentic dishes from scratch.

 5 dishes 
 Massaman Curry
 Mild 
 CURRY 
 Massaman Curry or Gaeng Massaman is a rich, relatively mild Thai curry that is celebrated globally for its deeply aromatic and complex flavors. It is heavily influenced by Malay and Indian cuisines, combining traditional Thai ingredients with dried spices.
 Khao Soi
 Mild 
 CURRY 
 Khao Soi is an iconic northern Thai noodle soup renowned for its rich, fragrant, and deeply comforting flavor. It features a mildly spiced, coconut milk-based curry broth served over tender egg noodles and a choice of meat (traditionally chicken on the bone or beef). The dish is famously crowned with a handful of crispy, deep-fried egg noodles, creating a wonderful contrast in textures.
 Green Curry
 Medium 
 CURRY 
 Green Curry or Gaeng Kiew Wan is one of Thailand’s most famous and globally beloved culinary exports. The name literally translates to Sweet Green Curry which refers to the creamy, pastel green color of the sauce rather than an overly sugary taste. It features a vibrant, highly aromatic paste made from fresh green chilies, herbs, and spices, simmered with coconut milk, proteins (such as chicken, beef, or pork), and local vegetables like Thai eggplants.
 Panaeng Curry
 Mild 
 CURRY 
 Panaeng Curry or Gaeng Panaeng is a popular, rich, and relatively mild Thai curry known for its thick, creamy texture and nutty flavor profile. Unlike juicier curries like green or red curry, Panaeng is a dry-style curry where the chicken is simmered in a concentrated coconut cream sauce infused with crushed peanuts and finely shredded kaffir lime leaves.
 Red Curry
 Medium 
 CURRY 
 Red Curry or Gaeng Phet is a rich, classic Thai curry simmered in creamy coconut milk with a flavorful red chili paste. Cooked with your choice of protein, eggplants, pea eggplants and fresh Thai basil. Served with hot jasmine rice.
