 Special Education &#038; Therapy Services in Bangkok
 New Day Learning & Therapy Clinic (NDL) is a multidisciplinary learning and therapy clinic in Bangkok, Thailand, supporting children and young people with diverse learning and developmental needs. Our full-day learning program combines an adapted UK curriculum with small class sizes, individualized learning and an interdisciplinary team of educators and therapists.
 Our approach brings together academic learning, communication, life skills and opportunities for greater independence. Students learn in a supportive English-language environment where teaching is adapted to their individual strengths and needs. Small class sizes, multisensory learning and hands-on experiences help students feel engaged, understood and supported as they develop skills for everyday life.
 Alongside our full-day learning program, NDL provides specialist therapy and support services including Occupational Therapy (OT), Speech and Language Therapy (SLT), Literacy Support, Physical Therapy and assessment services. Our educators and therapists work collaboratively with families to create consistent, meaningful support across learning, communication, independence and daily life.

 Who We Support
 NDL supports neurodiverse children and young people with a wide range of learning and developmental needs. Our students may include learners with autism, ADHD, dyslexia, dyspraxia, Down syndrome, developmental differences, physical disabilities and other additional learning needs.
 Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, we consider each student&#8217;s individual strengths, communication needs, learning profile and goals when planning support.
 At NDL, learning and therapy work together. Our multidisciplinary team supports each child and young person through individualized education, therapy, assessments, activities and opportunities to build greater independence. We also share our experience beyond the clinic through professional development and training for schools and educators in Bangkok.
 New Day Learning & Therapy Clinic is located in the Sukhumvit area of Bangkok, near BTS On Nut. We support local and international families living in Thailand, as well as families relocating to Bangkok who are exploring individualized education, therapy and specialist support for their child.
 New Day Learning & Therapy Clinic is licensed through the Ministry of Public Health in Thailand.
