 About us
 Founded in 2016, Fly Me to the Moon Pole & Aerial Studio is more than just a place to exercise—it’s a home away from home. Our studio is a welcoming sanctuary where fitness meets art, and creativity flourishes in a supportive community. With a focus on movement, performance, and self-expression, we empower individuals to embrace their authentic selves while feeling truly at home.
Our slogan, “Let your inner self shine,” reflects our belief in creating a safe and liberating space where students of all levels can explore their passions, discover new strengths, and connect with like-minded individuals.
 Circus on the moon
 Fly Me to the Moon Pole & Aerial Studio is inspired by the whimsical and enchanting vision of a Circus on the Moon. Our studio offers a unique blend of fitness and artistry in a space that feels both magical and liberating. We aim to evoke a sense of wonder and creativity, transforming every class into a performance where students are the stars.
Our Classes

 Self-Practice Hours at Fly Me to the Moon 
 Want to spend some extra time polishing your moves? Our self-practice sessions are the perfect way to revisit what you’ve learned and let your body grow more familiar with the tricks at your own pace.
 What to know: 
 ✨ Self-practice is an open studio session without an instructor. You’re welcome to review any techniques or routines your teachers have taught.
 ✨ The space is shared, so there may be other students practicing at the same time.
 ✨ To book a self-practice slot, please message us directly. These sessions are available during non-class hours (no group or private classes at that time) and during studio operating hours 9.00-20.00 stated in the schedule
 ✨ We recommend booking at least 1 day in advance of your desired time.
 ✨ Please note: instructors will not be available for teaching during self-practice hours.
 We hope your self-practice brings you confidence, growth, and lots of fun!
 If you have any questions or would like to book a slot, just send us a message anytime. We’re here to help! 🫶
