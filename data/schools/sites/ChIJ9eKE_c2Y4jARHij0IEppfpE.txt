 Ancient Science for modern lifestyle
 Located in Silom near the Sri Maha Mariamman Temple, Suananda is a homely vegetarian garden cafe & Ayurveda wellness centre. 
 Suananda started since 2009 with a strong desire from Mrs. Sukesh, who has a lifelong passion for yoga, to spread health awareness with a holistic approach through Vegetarian food, Yoga and Ayurveda.
 Suananda serves home style healthy vegetarian meals, which is fresh, delicious and cooked in a way that preserves its nutritional properties. Try our daily Ayurvedic Set Menu or choose from various types of cuisines and fusion dishes. Check out our new menu and come enjoy a healthy meal in our backyard garden!
 We also offer yoga classes , various workshops and Ayurvedic herbal massages .
