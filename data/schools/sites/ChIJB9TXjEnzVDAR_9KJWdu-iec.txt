 A Progressive Approach to Online Learning 
 Learning Legends is a community of expert teachers, creating unique learning experiences for students. We explore subjects through personalised lessons, learning apps, projects, and one-on-one interactions . Parents receive detailed reports on progress, achievements and learning goals.

 Testimonials 
 Hear firsthand from families in the Learning Legends Community 
 JD has been learning with Legends Labs for two years using a brainstorming approach to co-create a customized curriculum between parents and a group of teachers (5-6 native English speakers). This was designed to align JD's learning experience as closely as possible with his needs and natural inclinations. As a result, JD has been able to study subjects he enjoys and is genuinely happy with his learning experience. His English skills have improved dramatically thanks to one-on-one sessions and constant interaction with teachers through speaking, writing, and reading. The group of teachers serves not only as his instructors but also as his friends. JD learns in a fun and engaging way, and he's been able to express his ideas, individuality, and inner potential in a remarkable manner. 
 ​ 
 Today, JD both studies and works at the same time, as the learning is based on project-based learning. This approach equips him with skills directly applicable to real life, and the outcomes have been highly satisfying. At this point, I have no concerns about his education at all&mdash;he learns and works alongside me joyfully and meaningfully. It's truly something wonderful. 
 Kanit & Anatta
