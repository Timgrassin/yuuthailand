 "Wech Pinyo" is a Thai boxing gym in Lamai, Koh Samui 
 Train at a 5-Star certified Muay Thai camp led by Master Wech — Southern Thailand Champion and #2 at Rajadamnern Stadium. Our team of 5 title-holding champion coaches and one of Koh Samui's top suppliers of Phetbuncha Stadium fighters. All levels and nationalities welcome.
 Group Muay Thai Training classes
 Monday - Saturday: 
 8:30 AM - 10:00 AM (Morning Training) 
 3:30 PM - 5:00 PM (Afternoon Training) 
 We offer group Muay Thai training classes (mornings and afternoons) for all levels. Whether you are traveling to Koh Samui for vacation and want to train Thai boxing for health & fitness or if you're going to become a world-class fighter - we have all of the resources for you!
 Train the best Muay Thai technique like a world champion and transform your body with running, skipping, strength & cardio exercises, heavy bag, and pad work drills.
 Private Muay Thai Training Sessions
 Want to reach your Muay Thai training or Health goals faster ? Train in one-on-one sessions with a world-champion trainer!
 You can schedule a (1 hour) private session with one of our trainers and get results fast! Learn basic Muay Thai techniques, hit pads, and train like a champion
 Strength & Conditioning Training 
 Tuesday and Thursday: 
 14:00 - 15:00 
 Strength & Conditioning Training is an intensive general fitness program designed specifically for the Wech Pinyo Muay Thai gym.
 These sessions combine cardio and functional exercises aimed at developing strength, endurance, coordination, and overall body control.
 The training helps improve overall physical condition, increase work capacity, strengthen muscles, and improve posture, while staying true to the spirit and philosophy of Wech Pinyo.
 Suitable for all fitness levels, this program is a perfect complement to Muay Thai training, helping athletes build explosive power, endurance, and full-body stability.
 Children's Group 
 Monday, Wednesday, Friday - Beginners 
 18:00 - 19:00 
 These engaging and beneficial sessions focus on overall physical fitness with elements of Muay Thai. Our classes help develop coordination, strength, and endurance while incorporating active games to make learning fun and dynamic. Children not only strengthen their bodies but also learn discipline, self-defense, and confidence in a safe and supportive environment. Classes are suitable for all levels of physical fitness.
