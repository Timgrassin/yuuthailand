 Muay Thai Gym in Chiang Mai
 Xlion Muaythai Gym — Professional Training for All Levels
 First of all, welcome to Xlion Muaythai Gym!
 Here, you can truly experience the authentic and professional atmosphere of Muay Thai training. Our spacious facility is equipped with a standard boxing ring, punching bags, and training tires, designed to support practitioners of all levels and goals. Under the guidance of Kru Xlion and the coaching team, you will steadily improve both your technique and physical fitness, beginning your own journey of growth in Muay Thai.
 The Xlion team is known for their dedication to training and patient instruction. They are friendly and welcoming to every student and also have a fondness for animals. With a great sense of humor, they enjoy playing football and often take motorcycle trips, bringing energy and warmth to the gym.
 We sincerely welcome everyone to join the warm and caring Xlion family and enjoy the strength and joy that Muay Thai brings!
 Coaching Team
 Xlion Gym brings together a team of highly experienced and skilled trainers. No matter your current level, the coaches provide structured and personalized guidance to help you refine your techniques while steadily achieving your fitness and training goals.
 Training Facilities
 Xlion Gym features a spacious training area of over 600 square meters, designed with a semi-open layout that ensures excellent ventilation and abundant natural light. The clean and comfortable environment allows you to stay focused in a setting that blends nature with professional training conditions. The facility includes a standard 8×8 meter boxing ring, 12 high-quality punching bags, and training tires to support both technical practice and strength-endurance workouts.
 With well-planned functional zones and ample equipment, the gym is ideal not only for individual training but also for group classes and partner drills. Whether you are just starting out, refining your techniques, or building physical strength, you can enjoy an efficient and safe training experience here.
