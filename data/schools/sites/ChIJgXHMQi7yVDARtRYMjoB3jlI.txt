 Fitness & Weight Loss Retreats
 Koh Fit Thailand offers all-inclusive fitness holidays and weight loss retreats in Koh Samui. Based in Lamai, we’re just minutes from the beach and surrounded by lush jungle, creating an ideal environment to reset both body and mind.
 Our retreats combine structured training, expert coaching, and supportive accountability with the relaxed rhythm of island life.
 Guests enjoy daily group classes, optional outdoor activities, nutritious meals, and dedicated recovery time, allowing you to focus fully on sustainable fat loss, improved fitness, and long-term lifestyle change in one of Thailand’s most beautiful destinations.
 Koh Fit combines elite tropical training with Myzone™ data to track every calorie and guarantee results. Your permanent transformation starts here.
 In the perfect setting, our accommodation allows you to focus on your fitness goals during your stay. Set back in the jungle you&#8217;ll have no distractions, allowing you to fully commit to achieving your goals.
