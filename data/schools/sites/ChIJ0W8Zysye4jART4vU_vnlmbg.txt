 quality is one of our core values 
 All ULS teachers have completed a proprietary teacher training program designed by ULS and are certified to teach the ULS curriculum.
 All teaching is conducted only in the Thai language and students are expected to use the Thai language in the classroom from the first day onward.
 While the teachers may use many enjoyable methods for helping students to learn, competency in the language is at the core of every activity.
