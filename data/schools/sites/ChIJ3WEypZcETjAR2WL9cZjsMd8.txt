 WELCOME: 
 Learn to cook your favorite Thai dishes, experience the exotic herbs and recipes, that makes Thai cuisine one of the world's culinary delights!! 
 AON has many years cooking and chef experience
 from restaurants and is widely renowned for her good cooking. Since a couple of years, she is now sharing the teaching with her daughter PRIM, educated as a chef in Bangkok. As well, they get very good reviews for teaching Thai cooking and they have very good knowledge of spices herbs and other plants from the garden. 
 AON has the genuine and deep knowledge to make and teach almost any existing Thai Dish available. By that we think Aon is unique for teaching Thai Cooking on the island. Almost everything is available for private classes or after agreement. 
 You learn by practical, hands-on approach to cook and to impress your friends back home, with green Thai curry fragrant soups or stir fried chicken with cashewnuts and many more dishes. 
 Aon also has a very good knowledge of many other types of kitchens, studying it both in Thailand and abroad growing her knowledge of both food in general and also to understand and explain the differences between different kitchens. Enjoy the food you have created, just the perfect thing, when you want a break from the beach!

 COURSE INFO & HOW IT WORKS: 
 The group chooses 4 out of 10 dishes together, Individually you can choose type of meat etc. as well as spice level. All preparing and cooking are done individually.
 All dishes can be made vegetarian/vegan.
 The course starts with an introduction of Thai herbs, spices & vegetables. Depending on the season we can take a tour in our garden to see most of the herbs, spices & vegetables.
 After cutting the ingredients the teacher show how to cook the dish and everyone can taste and adjust to a personal spice level, sweetness etc.
 When the time allows, individual extra dishes can be made for 400 Baht each
 Location & Transport
 Koh Lanta lies on the west coast of Andaman Sea in between Krabi and Phi Phi Island. Large Size Island with 6 km wide and over 30 kms long, 9 beaches lie from north to south along the sunset coast.
 Stunning scenery, fantastic white sandy beaches, coastlines have more than 70 small islands and plenty with forest, coral reefs and under water life. The geography of the island is typically mangroves; coral rimmed beaches; and rugged tree covered hills.
