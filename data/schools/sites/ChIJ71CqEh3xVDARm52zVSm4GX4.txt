 Thai Local Cooking Home 
 The food is definitely one of the highlights of travelling through Thailand.
 Thai food is so full of flavours and fresh ingredients, and every meal feels like an adventure for your taste buds.
 The local cuisine differs slightly depending on which region of Thailand you are in.
 So if you’re a true foodie you may want to take a few different Thai cooking classes throughout the country.
 Koh Samui has lots of cooking schools that offer full-day tours cooking classes, most of which include a tour of the local market followed by guided instructions for making classic dishes.
 This one though is the highest rated , so make sure you book it ahead of time.
 The food you prepare yourself may be some of the best you eat in Thailand.
 So dedicate at least one day in Koh Samui to a fun Thai local cooking home.

 Day 1 : 
 A culinary journey in Koh Samui, where the vibrant flavors of Thai cuisine come alive under the guidance of a local expert. This unique experience, perfect for families, couples, or solo adventurers, seamlessly blends a lively market tour with a hands-on cooking class. Small group sizes ensure personalized attention from your instructor, making it a cozy and enriching adventure. 
 Your day begins with a comfortable pick-up from your hotel by appointment time, whisking you away to the cookery school where your culinary adventure unfolds. Arriving 30-50 minutes before the scheduled start, you'll have a moment to immerse yourself in the anticipation.
 Before setting of on your adventure, you will request 3-4 dishes to prepare before heading to the market tour:
 All of our food is cooked  
 • Appetizer : Spring roll 
 • One-dish meals : such as stir-fried basil, Pad Thai, 
• Soup : Tom Yum (Spicy soup), Tom kha Kai , 
• Dessert : Thai local dessert, Mango sticky rice 
 Kick-off your adventure with a 30-minute guided tour of a bustling local market. Here, you'll be introduced to the vibrant world of Thai herbs, vegetables, and spices, setting the stage for the culinary creations you'll soon be crafting.
 As you return to the cooking school, a fully equipped kitchen awaits, ready to facilitate your 2-3 hour culinary exploration. Under the guidance of a certified Thai teacher, you'll learn to craft four authentic dishes and a dessert, unraveling the secrets behind Thai cuisine's renowned flavors and textures.
 Finish your culinary session and indulge in a delightful meal crafted by your own hands, a true testament to your newfound skills. Your enriching journey concludes with a convenient transfer back to your hotel, leaving you with cherished memories and impressive culinary skills to take home.
