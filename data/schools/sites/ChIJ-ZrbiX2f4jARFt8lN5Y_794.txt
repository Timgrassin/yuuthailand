 About Us
 Thai Relo Services is part of the Transpo International Group, a trusted leader in relocation services established in Thailand in 1973. For over 50 years, we have been a trusted partner to individuals, families, and global organizations, delivering seamless relocation solutions built on the core principles of care, commitment, and an uncompromising focus on customer satisfaction.
 Today, Thai Relo Services is widely recognized as one of Thailand’s most reputable and established relocation and visa specialists. Our comprehensive services span Visa & Immigration, Destination Services, and Real Estate Agency support—delivered by experienced professionals who understand the complexities of relocating to and from Thailand. Each year, we support thousands of clients with tailored, end-to-end solutions designed to ensure a smooth, efficient, and stress-free transition.
 In 2023, Thai Relo Services was officially appointed by the Thailand Board of Investment (BOI) as one of only four Certified Agencies (CAs) for the Long-Term Resident (LTR) Visa. Since the launch of the program, our team has successfully facilitated more than 400 LTR visa applications for both corporate and individual clients, demonstrating our deep expertise and proven track record in navigating complex requirements with confidence and precision.
 In 2024, we achieved another significant milestone with our appointment by Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd. (under the Tourism Authority of Thailand) as an authorized General Sales and Services Agent (GSSA) for the Thailand Privilege Card membership program (formerly known as Thailand Elite Card). This distinction places Thai Relo Services among a select group of agencies in Thailand officially recognized by the government for both of the country’s flagship long-term residency programs – an achievement that underscores our unique positioning and credibility in the market.
 These credentials reinforce our position as a leading authority in Thailand’s relocation and long-term visa landscape. Whether you are applying for an LTR Visa, obtaining a Thailand Privilege Card, or planning your relocation to Thailand, Thai Relo Services provides trusted guidance, professional expertise, and a seamless, end-to-end experience at every stage of your journey.
