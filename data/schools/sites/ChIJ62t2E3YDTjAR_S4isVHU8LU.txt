 Beginner 
 Designed to provide learners with a basic understanding of the Thai language. 
 The course covers essential skills, such as speaking and listening, and focuses on building a foundation for effective communication in everyday situations. 
 Stories engage students in conversations after the first 60 hours by talking about themselves or other familiar topics rather than just asking or answering short questions.

 Intermediate
 Designed for learners who have a basic understanding of the Thai language and want to develop their skills to a more advanced level. 
 Students will learn to tell stories and discuss in a communicative and context-rich environment. 
   Slang and idioms are introduced more during this level. Students should be able to communicate more confidently and effectively in a broader range of situations.

 Advanced 
 The course focuses on advanced language skills, including speaking, listening, reading, and writing, emphasizing fluency, accuracy, and cultural understanding. 
 Authentic materials such as videos, articles, news, and real-life scenarios are used to expose learners to real-world Thai language usage.
