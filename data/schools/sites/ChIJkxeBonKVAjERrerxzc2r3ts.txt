 Law Firm in Pattaya
 Pattaya, once a sleepy fishing village, has developed into a booming city that is not only well known globally for its nightlife and stretched out beaches, but this bustling center of entertainment and recreation on Thailand's Eastern Seaboard with its sizeable community of expatriates from Europe, Russia and the United States is attracting numerous business ventures and property developments. A reliable and experienced Pattaya lawyer or solicitor should always be consulted before any important decision or transaction, due to the complexities of the Thai legal system.
 Pattaya Lawyer Services
 Notary Public in Pattaya
 Thailand does not have a notary public, however, as an alternative some Thai lawyers are authorized by the Lawyers Council of Thailand to function as notarial services attorneys. Our notary lawyer in Pattaya may certify copies of legal documents, verify and certify the authenticity of signatures, administer and notarize oaths of instruments such as power of attorney, to be used in other jurisdictions as normally accepted by the competent officer of the respective countries.
