 WELCOME TO COOKING WITH MON 
 Book your cooking class online. Scroll down for more information!
 Welcome to our cooking school! :
 Cooking with Mon is the number 1 cooking class in Koh Lanta since 2017. The classes are hands-on, you learn to do everything from scratch. You taste, smell, cut, chop and smash. You will not just follow a recipe but you will develop 'a feeling' for Thai food and master balancing the flavours. So when you go home there will be no doubt that you can cook your favorite Thai dishes.
 Our instructors are charismatic chefs who will introduce you in a relaxing and fun way to the secrets of Thai cooking. If you want to know more about our instructors press here. 
 COOKING CLASSES - How it works 
 We have 2 types of classes available: 
 Day Class from 10:00-14:30 
 Evening Class from 16:30-21:00 
 We cook 3 dishes; one curry from scratch, an additional main dish and Mango Sticky Rice for dessert. Every person joining can choose their two favorite dishes from the list below. So if you're joining with your partner, you both can choose 2 dishes individually, so total 4 dishes. 
 You can book your spot on our online booking system and pay with credit card (no extra charge) so it's easy and convenient. 
 Read the information below regarding the price, dishes to choose from and the (free) pick up. When you're ready, you can book!
 After choosing "Book now" you can take the following steps:
 1. Specify the number of people joining
 (kids cooking with parents can be added later).
 2. Choose your date and time on the calendar. 
 3. Add the free pick up if required and the number of kids joining
 4. Share your details such as email , WhatsApp number, the dishes each person wants to cook, if you eat chicken and shrimp or have any special diet we need to know about and your hotel name if you would like a pick up..
 5. Confirm the booking and pay with credit card. 
 Your booking is now confirmed! The instructor will contact you the day before the class (usually on WhatsApp) with a welcome message and confirm the pick up time once more. For general information about PICK UP TIMES, click here. 
 Book your class! 
 The price is 1750 baht per person using a cooking station/spot. 
 If you cook with a child 8-12 years old you pay an additional 750 baht. Total price 1 adult 1750 baht + 1 child (8-12) 750 baht= 2500 baht*. Please note that we can accommodate one child per adult . If you feel your child is mature and confident enough to cook independently, you're welcome to book an adult spot for them. 
 Children 13 years and older must book a regular cooking class with their own stove and cooking station.
 Younger children (0-7) can join with parents free of charge (one child per adult). 
 *Parents with a baby (0-2 years old) may also use this option if one adult needs to take care of the baby during the class while the other participates in cooking.

 Important information 
 We offer a free pick up and drop off service from any hotel on Koh Lanta included in the price. So it will be great to know in advance where you stay. You can also click the extra "Free pick up & drop off service" on the booking system. See pick-up times below. 
 We usually cook with chicken and shrimp. But we are able to adapt the food to any special diet , such as vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian or gluten-free. Just make sure to let us know about your special diet in advance so we can adapt the groceries accordingly.
 We will provide you with a recipe book in pdf format of all the dishes we have ever cooked so you can experiment with other dishes after you get home.
 Included are the amazing food cooked by you, fruit and there is also water, tea and instant coffee available for you all day.
 It is also possible to order delicious freshly ground coffee, beers and soft drinks for additional payment. You can pay cash or with credit card.
 We are cooking and eating all day! So please come hungry, have a small or early breakfast or skip it completely if you wake up late. We cook and eat all day, and at the end of the class you will be stuffed!
