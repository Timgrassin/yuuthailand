 WHO WE ARE 
 Our team of expert instructors have spent decades dedicating themselves to the mastery of the martial arts and are passionate about passing it on to their students. Their goal is for every member of our school to achieve their unique goals, becoming healthier and more capable versions of themselves through the martial arts – allowing them to surpass their limitations.
Every session is unique, well-structured, energetic, and inspiring. All of our programs are designed to build solid foundations, supporting your progression and keeping you focused on your goals; whether you want to get in amazing shape, learn self-defense, or become a world-class athlete. 
All levels are welcome, no experience necessary.

 Why choose Core Combat Chiang Mai?
 Book A Class 
 Expert Coaches
 Our expert coaches are dedicated towards your transformation in the martial arts. They’re fully certified and battle-tested, most importantly they’re the best at what they do – teaching others the ways of combat.
 Community
 We promise that the student environment at Core Combat Chiang Mai is like no other gym. All our members are friendly, approachable, and passionate about the martial arts. Toxicity is never tolerated.
 Established in 2013
 Core Combat Chiang Mai is the only multi-disciplinary school in Northern Thailand fully licenced by the Ministry of Education, offering Martial Arts, Self-Defense, Muay Thai, MMA, Western Boxing, Eskrima and Jr Kickboxing classes – all under one roof.
 Support & Motivation
 Our entire team of coaches, assistant instructors and front desk staff are here to ensure the utmost value to the service we’re providing. The martial arts community here will help you along your journey, every step of the way!
 1-on-1 Training
 Fine tune your personal growth and development to meet your specific needs, working one-on-one, with our experienced, certified instructors. We offer private training in fitness, martial arts (Muay Thai, MMA, Western Boxing, Jeet Kune Do, Wing Chun, Eskrima, Silat and more), competition preparation, civilian self-defense and tactical training.
 Commitment
 We’re serious about our commitment to your journey in the martial arts. If you haven’t reached your martial arts and fitness goals within your first 3 months of training with us, we’ll give you another month for free.
 Meet Our Coaches
 Our expert instructors at Core Combat Chiang Mai are highly regarded for their skill and patience. Most importantly, they are friendly and approachable – both on and off the mat.
