 FAQ
 Rules, documents and deadlines for each visa: Guides 
 How much does it cost? Every fee is on our price list. A retirement extension starts at 3,500 THB, with one of our staff going with you to Chiang Mai Immigration. We confirm the final price in chat before you pay.
 Do I have to go to Immigration myself? It depends on the service. For 90-day reports, DTV extensions and full-service retirement extensions you do not go. For visa-exempt extensions and the 3,500 THB accompanied retirement extension you go to Chiang Mai Immigration in person.
 I don't live in Chiang Mai. Can you help? Yes, for some services. DTV extensions are done by post from anywhere in Thailand. Full-service retirement extensions need no visit, so you can post your passport to us. Services where you attend Chiang Mai Immigration are for people in Chiang Mai.
 Can I leave Thailand while my application is in progress? Stay in Thailand until the new visa or extension is issued, unless the visa is applied for from abroad. If you need to travel, ask us first.
 Which languages do you speak? English, Chinese and Thai.
 How do I start? Message us on LINE, WhatsApp, WeChat or Messenger with your nationality, current visa and plans. The first consultation is free.
