 The Thailand DTV Visa at a Glance
 A 5-year visa for remote workers and Thai cultural programmes — what it costs and who qualifies.
 What it costs with Migrify
 Item Cost 
 Migrify service fee ฿9,500 
 Thai government fee ฿10,000 
 Total ฿19,500 
 Service fee waived when you book an in-house qualifying course (Muay Thai, Thai Culinary, or Medical & Wellness).
 Who qualifies
 Category Who it's for 
 Workcation Remote workers, freelancers & business owners earning from outside Thailand 
 Thai Soft Power Muay Thai, Thai cooking, medical & wellness, sports training 
 Dependent Spouse and children under 20 of a DTV holder 
 Valid 5 years, multiple entry &middot; Stay 180 days per entry (extend once to 360) &middot; Financial proof of 500,000 THB required.
