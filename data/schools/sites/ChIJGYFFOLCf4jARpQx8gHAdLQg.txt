 Legal Services 
 PB Legal Services 
 Welcome to PB Legal Services, dedicated to supporting businesses and individuals across Thailand.  
 Established in 2004 as part of the Sutlet Group (founded in 1998), we bring over two decades of expertise in providing assistance with legal matters  from our base in Bangkok.  
 Our mission is to simplify your legal needs, ensuring compliance with Thai regulations while helping your business or personal endeavors thrive. 
 ____________________ 
 Our Core Services 
 Visas 
 We provide comprehensive visa services tailored to your specific needs. From tourist and business visas to long-term residency and extensions, our certified legal experts handle applications, documentation, interviews, and appeals with precision and efficiency. Let us navigate the complexities so you can focus on your goals.  
 Work Permits 
 Securing the right to work in Thailand is essential. Our work permit services include eligibility assessments, application submissions, renewals, and compliance with Thai labor laws. We manage everything from initial filings to ongoing requirements, reducing administrative burdens and ensuring seamless operations for expatriates and businesses. 
 Company Registration 
 Starting or expanding a business in Thailand? We streamline company registration processes, including entity formation (e.g., limited companies, partnerships), BOI promotions, licensing, and regulatory filings. Our services cover everything from initial setup to compliance with Thai Commercial Code, helping you establish a strong legal foundation for growth.
