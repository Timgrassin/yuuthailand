 WELCOME TO YOKKAO TRAINING CENTER BANGKOK 
 One of the Top 10 Things to Do in Bangkok
 Located in the heart of Thailand’s bustling capital, YOKKAO Training Center Bangkok stands as one of the world’s most iconic Muay Thai gyms — a place where champions are made, legends train, and global fighters gather. Whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned athlete, this is your opportunity to train alongside history in the making.
 TRAIN IN THE HEART OF SUKHUMVIT 
 Our training center is located on Sukhumvit Soi 16, in the vibrant Asoke district, one of Bangkok’s most dynamic and accessible areas. Surrounded by cafes, hotels, malls, and BTS/MRT stations, it’s the perfect spot to balance intense training with everything the city has to offer. You're not just coming to a gym — you're stepping into the heartbeat of Bangkok.
 LEGENDS AND CHAMPIONS WALKED THIS FLOOR 
 Over the years, YOKKAO Bangkok has welcomed more than 50 world-recognized fighters, a true testament to its reputation as one of the most iconic Muay Thai gyms in the world as featured on CNN.
 Fighters who have trained here include legends such as:
 Saenchai, Liam Harrison, Superlek, Jordan Watson, Jonathan Haggerty, and Buakaw Banchamek.
We’ve also been the choice of top MMA athletes from UFC and PFL, including Johnny Eblen and Khalil Rountree, as well as international celebrities seeking an authentic Muay Thai experience. 
 Whether they come to sharpen their tools, prepare for title fights, or simply embrace the culture, they all step into the same ring.
 WHY TRAIN AT YOKKAO BANGKOK? 
 World-Class Training : Learn authentic Muay Thai from top-level Thai trainers with experience coaching international champions.
 Real Muay Thai Culture : You’re training where fighters live, breathe, and bleed Muay Thai. It’s the real deal.
 All Levels Welcome : Whether you’re just starting or preparing for a fight, our team will tailor the training to your needs.
 A Global Community : Join a diverse mix of travelers, fitness lovers, and pro athletes from all corners of the world.
 Unforgettable Experience : It’s not just a workout, it’s an experience that stays with you for life.
 A TOP 10 EXPERIENCE IN BANGKOK 
 Training Muay Thai in Bangkok is a must for anyone visiting the city and YOKKAO is the ultimate place to do it. From the electrifying energy of the sessions to the chance of sparring alongside Muay Thai champions, every moment here is intense, inspiring, and unforgettable. Whether you stay for one class or one month, this will be one of the highlights of your trip.
 TRAIN. SHOP. EXPERIENCE. 
 YOKKAO Training Center Bangkok is more than just a gym, it’s a complete Muay Thai destination.Right inside the facility, you’ll find the official YOKKAO Store , where you can shop the latest gear, apparel, and exclusive releases worn by the champions themselves. From boxing gloves and Muay Thai shorts to lifestyle apparel and accessories, everything is available on-site so you can train in style and bring home a piece of the YOKKAO experience.
 Whether you're here for a single session or long-term training, don’t miss the chance to gear up at the source authentic, premium quality, straight from the brand that’s redefining Muay Thai.
