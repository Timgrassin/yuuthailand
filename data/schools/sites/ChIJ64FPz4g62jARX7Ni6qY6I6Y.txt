 We believe that cooking should be and that food should be enjoyed. Our aim is to give all our students an
 enjoyable cooking experience.
 Learn how to cook healthy Thai food in a cozy, friendly atmosphere. Learn about vegetables, fruits, herbs
 and other ingredients while we shop for fresh ingredients at the market.
 Unlike other cooking schools that have 10-20 people per class, we keep our classes small, only 1-7 people
 per class, so we can spend quality time with you.
 As we enjoy cooking together we encourage you to ask any question, or share and compare your experiences
 of cooking to Thai cooking so we can learn from each other.
