 FAQ
 Rawai Boxing Stadium 
 Rawai Boxing Camp and Gym 
 Where can I buy tickets?
 You can buy tickets online or directly at the door. 
 How can I fight or apply for a fight?
 THE ONLY WAY to apply for fights is through our fight promoter. Please contact these number: 
 0923361334 or 0955753538. 
 Are there any online streams available?
 YES! We are always streaming the fight night on our Instagram Livestream. 
 Klick the link to open our Stadium Instagram. 
 https://www.instagram.com/rawaiboxingstadium/ 
 Where can I see the fight card and fight times?
 Our fight card is normally posted every Tuesday sometimes Wednesday morning. However, the final schedule for the fights will be available right before the fight due to unforeseen happenings and injuries of some fighters.
