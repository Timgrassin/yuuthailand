 Traditional Thai Massage School in Chiang Mai, Thailand.
 We provide quality Thai Massage training with highly qualified and motivated teachers in Chiang Mai.
 We are, as always, committed to maintaining excellence in teaching Thai Traditional Yoga Massage in the spirit of Asokananda, who translated this Eastern tradition into a holistic format that is understandable for westerners. 
Are you visiting Thailand and looking for an authentic and enriching experience in an English speaking traditional massage school ?
 Are you a Therapist, a practitioner, or preparing yourself to become one ? Wanting to explore the principles of Thai massage therapy as part of Thai traditional medicine ? 
 In all cases, you have found the right school to start or further your journey and training. 
 Sunshine Thai Massage School, Chiang Mai
 Our 10 day beginner's course  is a wonderful opportunity to explore and study Thai Traditional Massage 
 If you are looking for a strong foundation in Traditional Thai Massage training you have come to the right place in Chiang Mai. 
 A new beginner's class starts every Monday of every week, all year long, and runs for 2 weeks.

 Combo 5 : Foundation Builder (5 weeks) 
 Perfect for students beginning their Thai massage journey or for those seeking to build strong fundamentals. This program combines essential learning with practical application, creating confident practitioners through repeated practice and real-world experience.

 Combo 8 : Professional Pathway (8 Weeks) 
 Our comprehensive program for those serious about practicing Thai massage professionally. This carefully balanced curriculum includes fundamental training, advanced techniques, and practical experience, with a strategic rest period to optimize learning.
