 TSL (Thai as a Second Language) Chiang Mai
 TSL Chiang Mai is a leading Ministry of Education&ndash;accredited language school in Chiang Mai , trusted by over 15,000 learners from around the world .
 We offer official Education Visa (ED Visa) support , with a proven track record of over 5,000 successful ED Visa students , and Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) support , with over 200 successful DTV applicants .
 Together with our visa support, we provide practical Thai, English, and Japanese courses designed for real-life communication and daily life in Thailand .
 Located on the 4th floor of Pantip Plaza , just steps from the Night Bazaar , our newly renovated campus is home to 39 qualified teachers , 24 classrooms , and 850+ active students . Our proven real-life speaking system helps students speak naturally, build confidence, and make clear progress faster. 
 Our Commitment to Teaching Quality 
 At TSL Chiang Mai, we are committed to continually improving the quality of our teaching. Our teachers take part in regular training and observe one another's classes to share ideas, learn from each other, and strengthen their teaching skills. Through this ongoing development, we strive to make every lesson more engaging, effective, and rewarding for our students. 
 TSL Method 
 Learn Thai for Real Life. Live Thailand for Real. 
 At TSL, students do more than study a language. They learn to communicate in real situations , understand Thai culture , and feel part of a warm, supportive community.

 How does it work in TSL Chiang Mai Language School?
 Real-Life Thai at TSL Chiang Mai &mdash; Speak Naturally like a Local 
 Master everyday Thai fast with our real-life conversation curriculum. We teach the high-frequency words and common expressions you hear in markets, caf&eacute;s, offices, and daily life &mdash; so your Thai sounds natural, accurate, and truly local.
 A Clear Learning Path from Beginner to Advanced & Mastery Thai 
 At TSL Chiang Mai, our Thai program is supported by 20 levels of well-structured textbooks , covering CEFR A1 to B2 levels . This allows students to build Thai step by step &mdash; from absolute beginner to advanced communication &mdash; with a clear and systematic learning path.
 For higher-level learners, we also offer Advanced and Mastery Thai classes (CEFR C1-C2) , designed for students who want to understand deeper Thai expressions, cultural understanding, and professional communication.
 Why it works 
 Frequency-first: Learn the top words, patterns, and expressions Thai people use every day
 Conversation-centered: Role-plays, situational dialogues, and real-audio practice
 Culture-informed: Politeness levels, particles, body language, and what sounds natural
 Pronunciation & tones: Systematic tone drills with teacher feedback
 Practical grammar: Classifiers, aspect markers, question forms, and sentence patterns you can use immediately
 Structured textbooks: 20 levels from CEFR A1&ndash;B2
 Results 
 Handle daily tasks in Thai &mdash; food, transport, small talk, shopping, and work basics
Sound more local with correct particles, rhythm, tones, and word choice
Improve listening speed and real-Thai comprehension &mdash; not just textbook audio
Progress clearly from beginner to advanced Thai communication
