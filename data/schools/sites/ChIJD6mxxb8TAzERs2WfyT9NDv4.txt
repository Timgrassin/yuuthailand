 Frequently Asked Questions
 Can foreigners buy property in Bangkok? 
 Foreigners can generally own qualifying condominium units in their own name, subject to Thailand’s legal requirements and foreign ownership limits. 
 Does the buyer or tenant pay the agent fee? 
 Fees vary by transaction. In many cases, the seller or landlord pays the commission. We explain all applicable costs before you proceed. 
 Can you help me find a property near BTS or MRT? 
 Yes. We can shortlist condos, houses, and offices based on your preferred station, travel time, budget, and lifestyle. 
 Do you provide virtual property viewings? 
 Yes. Virtual viewings are available for overseas buyers, investors, and clients who cannot attend in person. 
 Can Triumph help me sell or rent out my property? 
 Yes. We can assist with pricing, listing preparation, marketing, enquiries, viewings, negotiation, and transaction coordination. 
 Do you provide property management? 
 Yes, we provide property management services like tenant sourcing, rent collection, maintenance coordination, and reporting, depending on the property and location.
