 Welcome to Tingly Thai Cooking School — one of the best Thai cooking classes in Thailand for travelers who want a real, authentic Thai food experience.
 Looking for the perfect Thai cooking class in Bangkok? At Tingly, we offer hands-on Thai cooking experiences with fresh ingredients, local market tours, traditional Thai recipes, and expert cooking techniques taught in a fun and friendly atmosphere.
 Whether you join our affordable group cooking class or book a private Thai cooking class, you will learn how to cook famous Thai dishes like Pad Thai, Tom Yum Goong, Green Curry, Mango Sticky Rice, and more — just like a local Thai chef.
 Our classes are perfect for couples, families, solo travelers, team building groups, and food lovers from around the world. Easy to learn, exciting to cook, and unforgettable to taste.
