 ABOUT
 LION'S HEAD
 We’re Here to Teach You How to Box. Whether you've never put on a pair of boxing gloves before or you've been training for years, there's something we believe strongly at Lion’s Head Boxing: Good boxing starts with good coaching. We're not here just to put you through a workout, count your rounds, or tell you to hit the bag harder. We're here to teach you. To help you understand why you're throwing a punch, how to move your feet, how to defend yourself, how to put combinations together, and how to keep improving when things get difficult. And you don't have to walk through our doors already knowing what you're doing.
 Everyone Has to Start Somewhere. 
A lot of people come to us for the first time wondering: I've never boxed before. 
 Am I going to be okay?" 
The answer is Absolutely! 
 You don't need to be fit. You don't need boxing experience. And you certainly don't need to know how to throw a perfect punch on day one. We'll start from where you are and build from there. Our coaches take the time to explain things, watch how you move, correct your technique, and adjust the training to suit your level. That's something our members regularly mention in their reviews—and it's a big part of what makes training at Lion's Head feel different.
