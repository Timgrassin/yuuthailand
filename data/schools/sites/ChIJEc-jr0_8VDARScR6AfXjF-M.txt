 How Pupils Learn 
 Connected Curriculum 
 Subjects connect through texts, projects and real-world contexts, helping pupils make meaningful links across English, Humanities, Art, Design Technology and more.
 Learning by Doing 
 Practical science, investigation, coding, technology and making. ​ 
 Beyond the Classroom 
 Outdoor learning, sustainability, sport, performance and community experiences extend learning beyond lessons.
 Learning in Action 
 Real projects. Practical skills. Meaningful outcomes. 
 Experience 
 Pupils work with waste plastic through our Precious Plastic programme, taking part in the process from sorting and preparation through to creating new materials and products.
 Learning 
 The project brings together sustainability, Design Technology, problem-solving and practical skills, while helping pupils understand how materials can be reused rather than discarded.
 Outcome 
 Pupils see their ideas become physical objects and experience how learning in school can connect directly with environmental and community issues.

 Experienced Teachers 
 Our teachers are fully qualified and highly experienced, with strong subject knowledge and specialist expertise across Primary and Secondary. 
 ​ 
 In classrooms, this means carefully structured explanations, practical investigation, adaptive teaching and opportunities for pupils to think, discuss, create and work independently. 
 Beyond the Classroom 
 Learning continues through experiences, challenges and opportunities across school life. 
 Outdoor Learning & Adventure 
 Pupils learn beyond the school site through fieldwork, outdoor education, trips and challenges that build independence and confidence. 
 Sport & Teamwork 
 PE, competitions and sporting events give pupils opportunities to develop skills, resilience, teamwork and healthy habits. 
 Performance & Creativity 
 Productions, performances and creative projects give pupils opportunities to rehearse, make, perform and share their work with an audience.
