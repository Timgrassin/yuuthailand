 FAQ
 Questions founders ask us
 Something else on your mind? Ask our team . 
 How much does accounting cost with Plizz? 
 Monthly bookkeeping starts from THB 14,900 per month for our SME package (Up to 50 transactions / month. Monthly VAT, WHT and SSO filings, PND.51, Kor Tor 20 and PND.1 Kor.), excluding 7% VAT. It includes VAT, withholding tax and social security filings. Prices are fixed and published, and larger volumes move to the next fixed tier, so you know the fee before you commit. 
 Can foreigners own a company in Thailand? 
 Yes. Most foreign founders use a Thai limited company, which needs at least 2 shareholders. For activities restricted under the Foreign Business Act, foreign shareholding is capped at 49% unless the company holds a Foreign Business License (FBL), BOI promotion or, for US citizens and US companies, the US-Thai Treaty of Amity. Plizz handles each of these routes and helps you choose the one that fits your business. 
 How fast can you register my company? 
 As fast as 3 days once your documents are signed. We reserve the company name, prepare the incorporation documents and file them with the Department of Business Development (DBD), so you can start trading quickly. 
 Is Plizz licensed? 
 Yes. Plizz (Thailand) Co., Ltd. is a registered Thai company (registration no. 0105558155093). We are a member of the Federation of Accounting Professions (FAP), the Thai member firm of DFK International since 2018, and a Xero certified advisor. 
 Can I switch accountants mid-year? 
 Yes. We take over the books from your current accountant, reconcile the opening balances and handle all filings from the handover month onwards, so nothing falls between two firms. 
 How quickly do you reply? 
 Within 1 business day, in English or Thai. Send us a question or build your quote online and a member of our team replies in writing.
