 Lanta Complex Thai Cooking School 
 Discover the world of Thai cuisine at Lanta Complex Thai Cooking School - a place where everyone can learn to cook delicious and flavorful dishes.
 Know more 
Why Choose us?
 A cozy atmosphere, exceptional dishes, flexible schedule, convenient location, and small groups - that’s what makes our cooking classes truly special. 
 Exceptional dishes
 Master iconic Thai recipes like Papaya Salad, Tom Kha, Pad Thai, Green Curry, and Mango Sticky Rice — each class features a unique selection of traditional dishes cooked with fresh local ingredients. Whether you prefer chicken, seafood, or veggie options, every session brings new flavors and techniques to discover. 
 Cozy atmosphere
 Here, you’ll feel right at home, relax, and enjoy every moment of the experience. 
 Great location
 Our school is conveniently located and easy to reach - Klong Dao. We also offer pick-up service upon request. 
 Small groups
 Small groups allow us to give personal attention to each participant, making the learning experience more effective. 
How the Workshop Works
Our cooking class is a hands-on experience from start to finish. You’ll start by learning about fresh local ingredients and Thai herbs, then cook step by step under the guidance of our experienced chef. Each participant prepares their own dishes - and of course, you’ll enjoy your delicious creations together at the end of the class!
 Free 2 way transport Saladan - Long Khong
 We offer complimentary two-way transport between Saladan and Long Khong, making it easy and convenient for you to join our cooking classes without worrying about travel.
 High Quality
 We are a leading firm in providing quality and value to our customers. Each member of our team has at least 5 years of legal experience.
 Practical Session
 Under the chef’s guidance, you’ll cook selected dishes step by step. Each participant has their own cooking station and learns the secrets of balancing sweet, sour, salty, and spicy flavors. It’s an engaging, fully hands-on experience - you don’t just watch, you create authentic Thai food with your own hands.
 Tasting
 After cooking your dishes, it’s time to enjoy the fruits of your labor! During the tasting session, you’ll savor the authentic flavors of your creations, learn to appreciate traditional Thai spices, and share the experience with fellow participants. This step allows you to fully enjoy your culinary journey and discover the harmony of Thai cuisine firsthand.
 Recipe Card
 Receive your very own recipe card with all the dishes you’ve prepared. Take it home and recreate your favorite Thai meals anytime, keeping the flavors and techniques you learned alive in your own kitchen.
 Class Completion
 We wrap up the class by sharing our experiences, giving thanks, and reflecting on what we’ve learned. It’s a moment to celebrate your achievements, connect with fellow participants, and conclude your culinary journey on a positive note.
