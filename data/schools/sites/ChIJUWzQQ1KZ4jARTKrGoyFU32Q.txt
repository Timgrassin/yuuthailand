 Welcome to St. Andrews Dusit
 It is an absolute privilege to be Head of School at St Andrews Dusit and to work alongside such kind students, dedicated staff and welcoming families.
We want every child in our care to be confident, happy and to develop a real love of learning which they will carry with them through life. Academics are important, but they need to be underpinned by wider personal growth. Whether it’s gardening on our rooftop, signing up for the student council or joining the coding club, we provide opportunities for all our students to discover their strengths and shine.
We are committed to providing a high-quality, safe and innovative learning environment, continually seeking to grow and adapt to the rapidly evolving world around us.
I look forward to welcoming you to St Andrews Dusit and introducing you to our happy and dedicated community.
Best wishes,
 Ms. Louise Stout

 Culture of Care
 We have a highly skilled and supportive team, who understand the importance of knowing the children and who invest time in building strong relationships with them. We want every child to feel a sense of belonging when they come to school and believe that there is always an opportunity for every child to succeed.
 Happy Community
 Community is at the heart of St. Andrews Dusit. Staff, parents and students work alongside each other to promote the Dusit values, support the local community and drive school improvement. We tailor the English Curriculum to our Thai context, weaving local culture with a global mindset to set our students up for success wherever they go!
 Academic Achievement
 We know that every child is unique and take great pride in individualising learning experiences and offering a range of opportunities to spark their curiosity. Our exceptional attainment scores are a testament to the outstanding support that our students receive in all areas of learning.
 Our Values
 At St. Andrews Dusit, our values are lived every day—both in and beyond the classroom. Each week at Friday assembly, students who embody these values are celebrated with special certificates.

 St. Andrews International School, Dusit is a small British international
school set just outside the grounds of the Chitralada Palace in Bangkok.Dusit is a fully inclusive and co-educational school for up to 400 Thai and expatriate children aged two to 11. Since opening with five children in 2010, it has expended to its current roll of just under 340 children distributed throughout 15 classes.
At. St Andrews, Dusit campus, the focus is on how children learn and the curriculum is delivered to meet all learning styles. Dusit prides itself on providing an exciting, engaging and challenging curriculum which enables each individual student to make excellent progress and achieve their full potential. Students are recognised as individuals at St. Andrews and are encouraged to challenge themselves in all areas of school life and to do their best.
