 BXB - Brick by Brick.
 We build fighters the same way real strength is built: one brick at a time. BXB is a high-performance fight camp offering Muay Thai, Western Boxing, and strength & conditioning programs for all levels — from complete beginners to experienced athletes. Whether you’re here for a short camp or a long-term transformation, our focus is simple: structured training, experienced coaches, and consistent progress. No shortcuts. No gimmicks. Just real work, done brick by brick.
 Muay Thai 
 (Pads, Clinch, Sparring)
 Muay Thai training is designed to enhance your striking skills, conditioning, and overall performance. Our program focuses on technique, strength, and agility to prepare you for both competitive and recreational practice. Whether you&#8217;re a beginner or an experienced fighter, we&#8217;ll help you reach your peak potential.
 Western Boxing 
 (Handwork, Footwork, Defence)
 Western Boxing training focuses on enhancing your punching technique, footwork, and overall conditioning. Our program is tailored to improve your speed, power, and defensive skills, suitable for all levels from beginners to advanced boxers. Train with us to reach your peak boxing performance.
 Strength and Conditioning
 (Fight Specific)
 Strength and endurance go beyond mere muscle gain. With each session, we will enhance your mental confidence and increase your work capacity.
