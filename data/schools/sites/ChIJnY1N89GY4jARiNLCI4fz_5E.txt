 About Us 
 THE HOP BANGKOK 
 A dance school. A jazz club. A place to move, 
 connect, and feel good. 
Founded in 2012, The Hop has been a home for dancers, music lovers, and curious beginners for over a decade. Tucked in the heart of Bangkok, it's where people of all ages and backgrounds come together &mdash; even if they've never taken a dance step in their life. 
You don't need dance shoes. 
You don't need experience. 
Just bring your curiosity &mdash; and let the music do the rest. 
We move to rhythms that have traveled through time &mdash; shaped by struggle, joy, creativity, and community. 
At The Hop, we honor the history, not just in how we dance, but in the way we come together to celebrate it. 
We share swing dances &mdash; both partnered and solo &mdash; born in the jazz era and made to be danced together. Some come here to learn. Others come to let loose. Either way, the music is at the heart of it all. 
Every week, The Hop comes alive with: 
Dance classes, social nights, and live jazz &mdash; all designed to get you moving and keep you inspired. 
Whether you're here to dance, listen, or just feel the rhythm &mdash; there's always a place for you at The Hop.
