 Cardiovascular Conditioning
 Being both aerobic and anaerobic, muay Thai, with continued practice, will make your body adapt to the demands of the sport, and improved cardiovascular performance is one of the payoffs.
 Leg Strength
 Kicking and footwork play a large role in muay Thai. From muscle endurance, force production, agility, to just plain old good looking calves, muay Thai delivers.
 Core Strength
 Your core is every single muscle located on your trunk; striking, defensive movements, and clinching will all help the muay Thai student develop a strong core.
 Stress Relief
 Having an outlet for that stress feels fantastic and can work wonders for your mental and physical health, and muay Thai is a great avenue for stress relief.
