 Global Partnerships
 Our institute is dedicated to fostering meaningful collaborations with universities across the globe. Through our student mobility programs, we aim to facilitate both inbound and outbound programs, providing students with rich, diverse educational experiences.
 Our high-quality partnerships, spanning more than 30 active collaborations across North America, Europe, and the Asia Pacific region, are designed to enhance undergraduate education through experiential learning. This approach enables students to develop essential future ready skills needed for success in today&#8217;s demanding and competitive globalized world.

 Testing
 The CMU TEGS , which stands for Chiang Mai University Test of English for Graduate Studies , is an English proficiency test designed specifically for individuals who wish to pursue graduate-level education at Chiang Mai University .
 This test assesses the English language skills necessary for academic success at the graduate level, including reading comprehension, listening, grammar, vocabulary, and writing ability. It serves as an important requirement for admission into master’s and doctoral degree programs offered by the university.
 The CMU TEGS ensures that all graduate students possess a sufficient command of English to engage with academic texts, participate in seminars, conduct research, and write academic papers effectively. The test is administered regularly throughout the year and is recognized internally by Chiang Mai University as a standardized measure of English proficiency.
 In some cases, students who do not meet the minimum required score may be allowed to enroll conditionally, provided they complete additional English courses or pass the test at a later date.
