 Three years ago when we re-opened BrassWok Thai Cooking Class after covid. A blank wall turned into a beautiful symbol of Thai abundance. ✨🎨 Watch as these two talented artists bring our mural to life here at BrassWok Thai Cooking Class Phuket!
Beyond being incredibly beautiful, this painting carries a deep meaning. In Thai culture, the banana plant and its blossom represent the sheer fertility and prosperity of our land. Thailand’s soil is so rich that these plants thrive naturally, blooming and offering food that never stops giving. 🍌🌿
It is the perfect symbol for our kitchen, where the love for Thai food is always blooming and always shared. Come see it in person during your next class!
