 My Story 
 I'm Wandee Boonsai, a Thai massage master and traditional healer with over 32 years of experience. I began my journey in a small farming village in Thailand, where my parents treated every illness with Thai massage and herbs. Their care inspired my lifelong passion for natural healing. 
 ​ 
 After 12 years of working and teaching at the Old Medicine School &mdash; the original school of Jivaka Komarabhacca(Shivago Komarpaj), the Father of Thai Medicine, in Chiang Mai &mdash; I founded my own training centre to share the true essence of Thai massage. My goal was not only to teach Thai massage, but also to help people live healthier lives and care for their bodies. 
Through years of experience, I've learnt that Thai massage should not be limited to a physical routine for muscle relaxation. To bring true healing, it must be integrated with Thai herbal medicine, anatomy, and psychological understanding. 
I teach from the heart, guiding students from around the world to heal through knowledge, compassion, and touch.​ 
 ​ 
 If you'd like to learn from me, anything is possible. Short one-, three-, or five-day courses cover the basics, while a month of training offers a deeper understanding &mdash; especially for beginners.
