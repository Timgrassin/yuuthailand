 DTV Program Insights
 Program Details 
 The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) is a 5-year multiple-entry visa designed for remote workers, cultural enthusiasts, and individuals participating in Thailand’s soft power activities such as Muay Thai training.  Key features of our program include:
 Duration: 9 months, with an option to extend. 
 Cost: Prices start at 23,000 Baht for 15 Muay Thai sessions over 9 months, including an acceptance letter for a 5-year Destination Thailand Visa (DTV).  Unlimited training sessions are also available.
 Training Schedule: Access group classes (Monday to Saturday).

 DTV Visa Application Process 
 Complete the Application Form : Choose our DTV Visa packages and fill out the form with accurate personal information.
 Secure Payment : Process your payment through our secure payment portal to confirm enrollment. Retain the confirmation as proof of payment.
 Acceptance Letter : Receive your official acceptance letter and other documents, including:
 • A certificate from the Muay Thai school confirming your enrollment or participation.
 • Official business registration documents of the gym.
 • A receipt displaying the gym’s Tax ID number along with your name.
 • A copy of the Thai ID card belonging to an authorized signatory (typically the gym owner or camp leader).
 Prepare Required Documents : Gather all necessary documents for submission.
 Submit to Thai E Visa Website : Visit thaievisa.go.th to complete your application.
 Why Choose our Program? 
 Receive Your Official Acceptance Letter for the 5-Year Destination Thailand Visa (DTV): The DTV allows stays of up to 180 days per visit, with the option to extend for another 180 days.

 Extended Stay: Enjoy initial 9-month stay in Thailand, with the possibility of extending your stay to further explore and experience.
