 Muay Thai in Bangkok Authentic Muay Thai training in Bangkok
 Kiatphontip is an authentic, family-run Muay Thai camp on the quiet western edge of Bangkok, in Thaweewatana. We have trained fighters here since 2008, and we welcome visitors from all over the world &mdash; complete beginners and experienced fighters alike &mdash; to train alongside our Thai fight team, not in a separate tourist class.
 Drop in for a single session, or stay at the camp on a Train & Stay package with an on-site room and two Thai meals a day. Two sessions daily, real coaching from working krus, and small numbers so no one gets lost in the crowd. If you want to train Muay Thai in Bangkok somewhere genuine rather than a tourist gym, this is it.

 Our story A real Bangkok camp, born from the South of Thailand
 The Kiatphontip name began at a fighting camp in Phattalung, southern Thailand. Since 2008 its Bangkok home in quiet Salathammasop has run as a genuine working camp &mdash; a small Thai fight team, experienced krus on the pads, and visitors from around the world treated as part of the family.

 FAQ Common questions
 Is Kiatphontip a real Muay Thai camp or a tourist gym? It is a real, working family camp that has trained in Bangkok since 2008. Numbers are kept small and visitors train alongside the Thai fight team — not in a separate tourist class.
 How much does it cost to train? Drop-in sessions start from ฿400, and Train & Stay packages — training, an on-site room and two Thai meals — from ฿8,900 a week. Prices are indicative; see the full rates & packages .
 Where is the gym? We are in Salathammasop, Thaweewatana, on the quiet western edge of Bangkok — around 20–30 minutes by car from the city centre and about 40 minutes from Don Mueang Airport (DMK).
 How do I book? Send us your dates through the Contact page , by email at kiatphontipbkk@gmail.com, or on WhatsApp, and we will sort the rest.
