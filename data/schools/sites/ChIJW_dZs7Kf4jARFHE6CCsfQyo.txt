 What we Offer
 Hi there, it’s Paron here. 
This year we have created a collection of workshops and programmes which we think you will love. There’s something for everyone.
 4-6 YEARS OLD 
 These classes give young kids a fun, safe way to explore art, learn new skills and build creativity.
 7-10 YEARS OLD 
 We give students the tools to develop both in their technical abilities and also in creative confidence.
 11-15 YEARS OLD 
 These classes help our older students improve their art techniques through proper guidance and more complex projects.
 ADULTS 
 Whether you are new or a returning artist, you will be amazed at what you can create with us in a short period of time.

 PAST WORKSHOPS
 Songkran Temple Fair
 Our young artists stepped into the vibrant atmosphere of a traditional Thai temple fair, exploring the colours, culture, and joyful spirit of Songkran. Through drawing, painting, and storytelling, they discovered how art can preserve and celebrate Thailand's rich heritage.
 More Programmes Here
 Woodland Wonders
 Students explores the transience of nature through autumnal forests. Artists use ink and oil pastels to study chiaroscuro—the dramatic play of light and shadow. This session fosters phenological awareness, teaching students to observe how shifting climates impact the fleeting brilliance of seasonal cycles.
 More Programmes Here
 Pop Art Party Chefs
 Creators transitioned from 2D planning to 3D cardboard engineering constructing oversized cake slices inspired by Wayne Thiebaud. Artists manipulate puffy foam frosting and high-contrast palettes. Scaling desserts to monstrous proportions celebrates the ready-made tradition, teaching students how dramatic scale changes elevate mundane, commercial objects into celebrated works of fine art.

 Paper Playground
 Foraged magazine collage was perfect to capture the Rooster’s rich texture and it's busy, hardworking nature, while bold primary colours embody its extroverted confidence and solar brilliance.

 Permission to Mess Up
 Wonderful watercolours and mindful strokes to evoke inner calm and creative serenity. Our Mindfulness art programme.

 Fast Food Frenzy
 A mouth-watering exploration of Pop Art's culinary history. Turning hamburgers, sushi, and sodas into rich pen drawings, students mix complex colour tones for realistic ingredients. Collaging these designs into vibrant advertisements for their own food chains, creators brilliantly bridge the gap between commercial marketing and fine art.

 The Mango Festival
 Students celebrate the Thai summer by painting a "mango and sticky rice" still life. They master mixing vibrant yellow hues and refining the precision needed to illustrate complex textures, from striped tablecloths to delicate cocktail umbrellas.

 Underground Secret Garden
 We subvert traditional perspectives by adopting an
earthworm’s view. Through tactile collage, students elevate the "unseen world" of soil
and roots. This project highlights soil health as the foundation of life, turning humble root
systems into vital symbols of growth.

 Monochrome Mindset
 Utilizing subtractive charcoal drawing, students "reverse" their process by lifting material to reveal the form. This reductive method captures the nurturing, ethereal nature of the Goat, allowing the art to emerge peacefully from the shadows.
 Click Here
 Dynamic Names
 We invite our students to investigate the explosive comic book style of Roy Lichtenstein. Layering bold textures, speech-bubble aesthetics, and vibrant colours around their own names, students merge graphic design with personal self-expression. This playful, high-impact project solves an exciting visual challenge: utilizing democratic illustration techniques to make a flat, static artwork feel genuinely loud.
 More Programmes Here
 Impressionist Wat Arun 
 Inspired by Claude Monet’s focus on light, students use acrylics to capture a glowing sunset. They master specific brushstrokes to depict dramatic river waves, learning how shifting light transforms the city’s most iconic silhouette.
 More Programmes Here
 Portraits of Emotion
 Students externalise the internal through Portraits of Emotion, gaining a rich
introduction to tonal work using oil pastels and markers. By examining their faces
entirely through parameters of light and shadow, artists gracefully decouple from rigid
perfectionism. They pair intuitive colour choices with internal emotional states,
celebrating personal vulnerability and the liberating joy of working loosely.
 More Programmes Here
 Planting Peace
 The immersive programme culminates beautifully in Planting Peace, a reflective
workshop rooted deeply in material simplicity and non-attachment. Inspired by Thai
alms bowls, participants build rich layers of acrylic paint on terracotta pots. Planting
seedlings in their finished vessels serves as a living metaphor for their wellness journey,
proving that with patience, abundance and wonderful things grow.
 More Programme Here
 Modern Muses
 The experience launches with Modern Muses, where artists immortalise modern celebrity icons through large-scale portraits. Inspired by Andy Warhol, students analyse facial tonal values across post-it note squares using dynamic colours and varied print techniques. The project prompts a fascinating creative question: does portraiture simply celebrate fame, or does it critique the massive platform of celebrity culture?
 More Programmes Here
 Ocean Exploration
 Ocean Exploration draws inspiration from Matisse’s "drawing with scissors"
philosophy. By blending clay, painted cut-outs, and repurposed household waste, artists
construct vibrant "art-bouquets." This assemblage technique serves as a poignant
critique of marine pollution, transforming discarded items into symbols of thriving
biodiversity.
