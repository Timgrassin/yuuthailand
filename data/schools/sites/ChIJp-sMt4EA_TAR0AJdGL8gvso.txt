 Established Legal Counsel for Foreign Citizens in Thailand
 Founded in 2013, Legal Services Hua Hin operates as a specialized legal practice dedicated to safeguarding the corporate, property, and civil interests of international citizens residing or investing in Thailand. Led by Managing Director Parichat Chaiparitasana (Khun Mod), our firm bridges the gap between complex Thai statutory frameworks and international compliance standards.
 Our legal advisors provide formal representation and dual-language (Thai/English) structural protections across core areas of Thai jurisdiction:
 Thai Property Law & Real Estate Due Diligence: Comprehensive title searches on Chanote deeds at the Department of Lands, contract drafting, and structural registration of 30-year leases to secure foreign investment.
 Civil & Commercial Litigation: Providing formal courtroom representation for international clients before the Thai Courts. Our litigation practice handles probate proceedings, inheritance/estate conflicts, breach of contract actions, property ownership and rental disputes, developer and construction defaults, consumer protection claims and road traffic accident and settlement agreement.
 Family Law & Civil Protections: Assist you with registering your marriage and arranging all required documentation, preparing Thai Wills and Testaments.
 Immigration Representation: Navigating formal visa structures, financial seasoning compliance, and administrative representation before the Thai Immigration Bureau.
 Notarization of documents: Witnessing and identifying yourselves and certifying your documents to the international standards and requirements.
 Legal Services Hua Hin, is a one stop service center for foreigners who want advice on Thai law from an English Speaking Lawyer.
