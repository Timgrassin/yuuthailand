 Discover Bangkok Through a Cooking Class
 Bangkok, Thailand's bustling capital, is a food lover's dream. Nestled amidst modern malls and ancient temples, the city’s vibrant streets offer a culinary journey like no other. And what's the key to unlocking these gastronomic treasures? A cooking class in Bangkok!
Our classes are not just about recipes; they're a passport to the heart of Thai culture. Kickstart your culinary adventure with a market tour, picking fresh ingredients under the warm Bangkok sun. As the city buzzes around, you’re whisked into traditional kitchens where the real magic happens. Under the gentle guidance of local chefs, you’ll discover the secrets of Thai cuisine.
Now, how about a farm visit? Some of our classes offer a rustic detour to local farms, a chance to pluck fresh ingredients right from the soil. And fret not, vegans! There’s a cozy spot for you too in our cooking class in Bangkok experiences.

 FAQs
 What types of cooking classes are available in Bangkok?
 We offer a variety of cooking classes in Bangkok, including traditional Thai cooking, street food, vegan and vegetarian classes, and even specialized courses like fruit carving and dessert making. Some classes also include market tours to source ingredients.
 What popular Thai dishes are usually taught in a cooking class in Bangkok?
 Most classes cover classic Thai staples such as Pad Thai, Tom Yum Goong, Green Curry, Som Tam, and other street‑food favorites.
 Are there vegan or halal cooking classes available in Bangkok?
 Yes, many schools offer vegan, halal, and gluten‑free options, adapting recipes with plant‑based or certified ingredients.
 What types of cooking classes can I find in Bangkok for different interests?
 From street‑food workshops and market‑tour classes to family‑friendly sessions, vegan specialties, and even Thai dessert tastings.
 How long do the cooking classes last and what is the typical group size?
 Classes usually run 3–4 hours, with groups of 6–12 participants to ensure hands‑on experience.
 Can I join a cooking class in Bangkok if I only speak English?
 Many classes are taught in English, and some even offer bilingual instruction or translated handouts.
 What cultural etiquette should I keep in mind during a Thai cooking class?
 Remember to remove shoes before entering the kitchen, follow the chef’s guidance, and be ready for the heat and spice level typical of Thai cuisine.
 Will I get to try seasonal dishes or special festival foods during a cooking class in Bangkok?
 Seasonal classes often feature ingredients like mango for mango‑sticky‑rice, or the spicy Baan Phra Ngan dishes during the Songkran festival.
 What are some tips for solo travelers, couples, or families with kids attending a cooking class in Bangkok?
 Solo travelers can meet new people, couples can enjoy a shared activity, and families get a fun, hands‑on lesson with kids‑friendly recipes and a chance to explore markets together.
