 ABOUT THE FIRM 
 Trusted Legal Expertise Since 1969 
 Luangthep Narin Law Firm was established in 1969 by Mr. Therdpan Isarangkul Na Ayutthaya, a graduate of Thammasat University and a member of the Thai Bar since 1963. The firm is named in honor of his father, a distinguished former regional public prosecutor who later practiced as a lawyer under the name Luangthep Narin. 
 ​ 
 Today, the firm is jointly led by Mr. Therdpan and Mr. Soontorn Jenkarnkit, also a Thammasat law graduate. With decades of experience, the firm has represented clients in hundreds of civil and criminal cases, alongside a full range of legal services. 
 ​ 
 Built on a legacy of integrity, professionalism, and practical legal expertise, Luangthep Narin Law Firm continues to deliver reliable and results-driven legal solutions to clients in Thailand and beyond.
