 Inclusive Pathways. Holistic Growth. Bright Futures.
 St. Andrews Sukhumvit 107 is a leading British international school in Bangkok, offering world-class education from Early Years through Sixth Form.
 Welcome to St. Andrews International School, Bangkok (S107) 
 A world-class education in the heart of Bangkok 
At St. Andrews International School Sukhumvit 107, we believe in a personalised approach to learning - where every child is empowered to thrive through our exceptional international curricula: the English National Curriculum and the International Baccalaureate (IB). All of this takes place within a caring , inclusive community that values each student’s individuality.
 “Every child is unique - and so is their path to success.” 
 43% of IGCSE grades awarded A or A* 
 100% pass rate for IGCSE 
 100% pass rate for the IB Diploma Programme 
 With students from over 40 nationalities, our vibrant campus - conveniently located next to Bearing BTS Station - is more than just a place to learn. It’s a dynamic international community where young minds grow into confident, compassionate, and future-ready individuals.
Caroline Ratcliffe
Head of School

 Why Families Choose St. Andrews S107 for Their Children? 
 Collaborative, Caring Community 
 S107 is more than a British school - it’s a close-knit, inclusive community where students grow with purpose, learn with passion, and care deeply for each other and the world around them.
 Excellence in Every Classroom 
 At S107, every teacher holds Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) or equivalent. 70% have education degrees from the UK and Europe, and 30% hold a master’s or PhD. With 18 nationalities - nearly half British - our diverse team brings global expertise to every classroom.
 A Campus That Inspires 
 Spacious, green, and well-connected - our Bangkok campus near BTS Green line (Bearing) and easily transited to by MRT Yellow line (Samrong) is designed to spark success in every student.
