 Trained by champions
 Three world champions on the team, plus coaches with hundreds of fights under their belts.
 Bangkok Pongsaklek
 18-time WBC world champion · 16 straight defences
 Boxing · Muay Thai
 Bio An 18-time WBC boxing world champion, Pongsaklek holds the record for the most consecutive successful title defences: 16. He also fought several times at Rajadamnern Stadium under Muay Thai rules, and has more than 150 pro fights across both sports.
 Bangkok Benz
 WBC Muaythai heavyweight world champion · 100+ fights
 Muay Thai · Boxing
 Bio A former WBC Muaythai heavyweight world champion and Rajadamnern fighter, Benz boxed for Thailand’s national team at the 2026 Asian Games and won gold at the 2024 GAMMA amateur MMA World Championships. He has more than 100 pro fights.
 Hua Hin Dimitri
 WMO and WPMF Muay Thai world champion
 Muay Thai
 Bio Dimitri Cerberes is a former WMO and WPMF world champion and French FMDA champion who has fought at Thai Fight, MAX, Kunlun and Prison Fight: about 50 amateur and semi-pro fights, and some 70 as a pro. He holds France’s BPJEPS in combat sports and the USMTA instructor certification, and has worked fight camps for Alistair Overeem, Sergei Kharitonov and Mark Godbeer, among other UFC, Bellator, ONE and Karate Combat fighters.
 Bangkok Kyo
 Thailand national MMA team coach
 MMA
 Bio Coach of Thailand’s national MMA team, Kyo has taken its athletes to Asian and world championships, the SEA Games and the Asian Games since 2023. The team brought back gold from the 2023 and 2025 World Championships, the 2025 SEA Games, the 2026 Asian Championships and the 2026 Asian Games. Kyo is also a BJJ purple belt.
 Bangkok Ice
 Active Rajadamnern fighter · 100+ fights
 Muay Thai
 Bio Ice still fights at Rajadamnern Stadium, on RWS and RKO cards. A former amateur national champion and gold medallist, he has more than 100 pro fights.
 Hua Hin Keng
 200+ fights · 15 years coaching abroad
 Muay Thai
 Bio Keng fought at Omnoi Stadium, on Thai Channel 3, and at Rajadamnern: more than 200 pro fights. He then spent 15 years coaching overseas.
 Hua Hin Nan
 Lumpinee and Rajadamnern · 200+ fights
 Muay Thai
 Bio A former Lumpinee and Rajadamnern fighter with more than 200 pro fights, Nan has been coaching professional fighters for 15 years.
 Bangkok Poey
 Rajadamnern and Lumpinee · 150+ fights
 Muay Thai
 Bio Poey fought at both of Bangkok’s great stadiums, Rajadamnern and Lumpinee, and has more than 150 pro fights.
 Bangkok Toey
 Rajadamnern and Lumpinee · 150+ fights
 Muay Thai
 Bio Poey’s brother, Toey also fought at Rajadamnern and Lumpinee, and has more than 150 pro fights.
 Bangkok Joe
 12+ years coaching · started under Ricky Hatton’s team
 Boxing · Muay Thai
 Bio Joe Varden started coaching at 18 under Ricky Hatton and his team in Manchester. In more than 12 years since, he has trained athletes of every level, from members of Russia’s national reserve team to pro boxers around the world. Old-school methods, and the belief that nothing replaces hard work.
 Bangkok Miro
 BJJ brown belt · IBJJF and ADCC referee
 Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu
 Bio A Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu brown belt under Morgan Perkins, from the Pedro Sauer lineage, Miro has taught since 2021: fundamentals, positional control, self-defence and a solid technical base. A certified IBJJF and ADCC referee, he knows the competition rules inside out, and every level is welcome on his mats.
 Bangkok Sydney
 14 years of Muay Thai · 6 years coaching S&C
 Muay Thai · Hyrox · CrossFit · S&C
 Bio Fourteen years of Muay Thai and six years coaching strength and conditioning, with a Certificate III in Sports Coaching and a Certificate IV in Fitness. Sydney builds programmes for fighters and general fitness clients — strength, power, muscle and physique — in person and online.
 Hua Hin David
 CrossFit Level 2 · ISSA personal trainer
 Hyrox · CrossFit · S&C
 Bio A CrossFit Level 2 Trainer, ISSA Certified Personal Trainer and ISSA Nutrition Coach, David López Pardo pairs functional training with programming and nutrition to get athletes and members to their performance goals.
 Hua Hin Bonus
 CrossFit Level 2 · ACE personal trainer
 Hyrox · CrossFit · S&C
 Bio Bonus specialises in strength and conditioning, certified as an ACE Personal Trainer, CrossFit Level 2 trainer and weightlifting coach (levels 1 and 2).
 Hua Hin Victor
 119-79-1 · former GLORY No. 3 in the world
 Bio Victor Pinto fought at Lumpinee Stadium (30+ fights), on Thai Fight (15+), at ONE Championship and at GLORY Kickboxing, where he was ranked No. 3 in the world. Lumpinee named him Fighter of the Year in 2011, he was Siam Ayala Athlete of the Year in 2014, an award presented by the Thai royal family, and he received France’s Prix du Rayonnement Français à l’étranger in 2026. Record across Muay Thai and kickboxing: 119 wins, 79 losses, 1 draw.
