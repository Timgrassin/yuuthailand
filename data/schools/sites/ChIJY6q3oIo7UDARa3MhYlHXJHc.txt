 Authentic Thai Cooking on Phuket's Beautiful West Coast
 Located in the heart of Kamala on Phuket’s beautiful west coast, Kankara offers an authentic Thai cooking experience just moments from the beach. Our unique location allows guests to discover the charm of a traditional local village while learning the art of Thai cuisine.
 At Kankara, we are passionate about preserving Thailand’s rich culinary traditions. Guided by experienced local chefs, you will learn to prepare authentic Thai dishes using fresh ingredients, traditional techniques, and recipes passed down through generations.
 Whether you are a beginner or a passionate food lover, Kankara invites you to experience the true flavors of Thailand in a warm, welcoming, and inspiring setting.

 ABOUT KANKARA 
 At Kankara, we believe the best way to experience Thailand is through its food. Our authentic Thai cooking classes bring together traditional recipes, fresh local ingredients, and the warmth of Thai hospitality in a relaxed and enjoyable experience.
 Located just moments from Kamala Beach, Kankara is the perfect place to discover the flavours and techniques behind Thailand’s favourite dishes. Whether you are a passionate foodie or simply looking for something different to do in Phuket, come cook with us, share a meal, and take a little taste of Thailand home with you.
