 About the Gym
 Razorback is a small gym based at Pai Park Cottage in Wiang Nuea, just north of the river and a short scooter ride from the walking street. Training follows the Pai rhythm: a morning session from 9:00 to 11:00 and an afternoon session from 16:00 to 18:00, with private lessons available from 10:00. The gym publishes a clear price list, a first session at 300 baht, 400 baht for each session after that, 4,000 baht for a week and 16,000 baht for a month of twice-daily training, and throws in a free meal with the weekly and monthly packages. The price board is in English and Hebrew, which tells you something about who trains here, and the gym answers WhatsApp quickly, which is not a given in Pai. Reviews are few but all positive. Note that Warhog Muay Thai Gym is listed at the same address, so ask which name the trainers use when you get in touch.
 Class Schedule
