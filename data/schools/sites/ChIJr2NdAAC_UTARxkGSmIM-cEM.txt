 TYPICAL TRAINING SESSION @SAENRAK MUAY THAI 
 RUNNING: RUNNING IS NOT MANDATORY (UNLESS YOU ARE PREPARING TO FIGHT) BUT IS STRONGLY SUGGESTED. FIGHTERS - RUNNING 30 MINUTES BEFORE CLASS MORNING AND EVENING. NON FIGHTERS WELCOME! 
 WARM UP: RUNNING ON MATS, SKIPPING. FOLLOWED BY STRETCHING 
 SHADOW BOXING: WHILST SHADOW BOXING THE TRAINERS WILL OBSERVE AND START CORRECTING YOUR TECHNIQUE AND GAUGE WHAT SORT OF LEVEL YOUR ARE. SHADOW BOXING IMPROVES FOOTWORK, CO-ORDINATION, TECHNIQUE, VISUALISATION, CARDIO AND CONDITIONING. 
 PAD WORK: HERE AT SAENRAK YOU ALWAYS GET 5 ROUNDS LASTING 3 MINUTES OF HITTING PADS WITH THE TRAINERS. THIS DEVELOPS CORRECT TECHNIQUE, SPEED, POWER AND STAMINA. 
 TECHNIQUE: INSTRUCTION AND CORRECTION ON VARIOUS STYLES AND COMBINATIONS BY THE THE TRAINERS. 
 BAG WORK: AT LEAST 5 ROUNDS OF WORKING TECHNIQUES ON A VARIETY OF DIFFERENT BAGS. 
 CONDITIONING: DRILLS ON THE BAG, BODYWEIGHT EXERCISES AND CIRCUITS. 
 COOLDOWN: STRETCHING AND MOBILITY DRILLS
