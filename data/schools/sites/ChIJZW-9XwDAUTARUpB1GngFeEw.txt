 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
 14 Years of Excellence: Leading Thai Language Education in the Heart of Ao Nang
 Alongkorn Language School (ALS) is a premier educational institution fully accredited by the Ministry of Education. Established on July 19, 2012, the school was founded by Mr. Alongkorn Pamornpol (Kru Korn) , an expert educator with profound experience in teaching Thai to speakers of other languages, including a distinguished tenure at a renowned language institute in Sydney, Australia.
 Built upon a vision of excellence in both pedagogy and administration, our school was honored with the "Outstanding Educational Management" award (Rated: Excellent) in 2015. Today, while the leadership has been passed to the next generation— Ms. Kanokporn Pamornpol , the founder’s sister—our commitment to the core values and high standards established by Kru Korn remains unwavering.
 Ideally located on the main road in the heart of Ao Nang , we are just 500 meters away from the pristine beach. Our school offers a serene and professional learning environment, featuring Thai language programs that cater to all levels, from absolute beginners to advanced learners. To ensure a seamless experience, we also provide comprehensive ED Visa consultancy , allowing our students to reside and study in Thailand with peace of mind.
 Throughout our 14-year journey , our success has been measured not just by numbers, but by the smiles and heartfelt "word-of-mouth" recommendations from students worldwide who trust in our warm, professional service.
 If you are looking for a place to master the Thai language that offers more than just a classroom, Alongkorn Language School is your perfect choice .
 ABOUT US 
 Alongkorn Language School It has been approved by the Ministry of Education. Opened for teaching on July 19, 2012. Mr. Alongkorn Pamornphon, an experienced teacher in teaching Thai to foreigners, was a teacher at the Thai Language teaching Institute in Sydney, Australia. 
 Alongkorn Language School is located on the main road in Central Ao Nang only 500 meters from the beach front in Ao Nang Krabi. Ideal for learning the traditions and culture of Southern Thailand while studying Thai language. 
 Kru Korn and Kru Aoy have been working together for more than 10 years with understanding, support and encouraging each other with honesty and sincerity. 
 Today we joined hands to teach Thai to over 180 of our students. We organize teaching and organizing visa services to the fullest and better than ever. 
 Kru Aoy has a lot of teaching experience, is calm and listens to everyone. She holds a bachelor's degree in Business English Program. 
 Kru Korn graduated from Thai Teacher University with long experience teaching Thai to foreigners including teaching abroad. 
 Our school has passed a rigorous assessment from the Ministry of Education until receiving the award for the management of education within the school at a very good level. 
 The real atmosphere in the classroom is that students compose sentences by themselves and practice conversation in Thai. Learn Thai 10 - 200 hours choose to study in a group, one-on-one, online or in a classroom and an ED visa is available. 
 Teachers and staff ready to provide Thai language teaching services to foreigners who are fully interested. If you are looking for an experience Thai language school we invite you to come and experience the place of study school atmosphere before deciding to study Thai in Ao Nang we think might be a good alternative. 
 Alongkorn Language School has a permit from the Ministry of Education to conduct a Thai Language Course for Foreigners; from Beginner, intermediate to Advanced levels.
