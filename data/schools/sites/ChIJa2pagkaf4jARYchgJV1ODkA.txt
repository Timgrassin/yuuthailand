 Can Siam Center Law Group assist me if I am a foreigner? 
 Yes. We assist both Thai and international clients with litigation, criminal matters, family law, business and commercial disputes, immigration-related matters and other legal issues in Thailand. Our English-speaking Thai lawyers will explain how Thai law and procedure apply to your circumstances. Acceptance of a matter is subject to an initial review and conflict-of-interest check.
 Will I speak directly with an experienced Thai lawyer? 
 Yes. You discuss your matter directly with an experienced Thai lawyer, online or at our office at True Digital Park, Bangkok. You can book a time here: Book a consultation .
 What should I prepare for the initial consultation? 
 Send us a short summary of your situation, typed or as a voice message, together with any documents or evidence, on LINE @siamcenterlawgroup ( https://lin.ee/YN5HvCu ) or WhatsApp +66 86 565 1791 (messages only). Our team will contact you within 1 business day. Please tell us right away about any court date, detention, bail condition or visa expiry.
 Will you give me an honest assessment of my case? 
 Yes. We will explain the strengths and weaknesses of your position, the evidence required, available options, procedural risks and practical consequences. We will also identify important information that is still missing. No responsible lawyer can guarantee an outcome, but you should receive a clear and candid assessment before deciding how to proceed.
 How are legal fees assessed? 
 Fees depend on the complexity, urgency and scope of your matter. After the consultation, we send you a written proposal within 1 business day. The scope of work is tailored to each case in your best interest.
 What should I do if my matter is urgent? 
 If you have been arrested, have a court date coming up, or your visa is about to expire, send "Urgent" on LINE @siamcenterlawgroup or WhatsApp with a short summary. For genuine emergencies, message us on WhatsApp +66 86 565 1791 (messages only).
 What happens after I engage Siam Center Law Group? 
 After the engagement terms are agreed, we will confirm the responsible lawyer, immediate priorities, required documents and the next procedural step. We will keep you informed of material developments and contact you when instructions or decisions are required. You will know what is happening, what we need from you and what comes next.

Arrested in Thailand? Your Rights to Silence, an Interpreter, a Lawyer and Bail 
04/11/2026 
 If you are arrested in Thailand, you have rights concerning statements, interpretation, private legal consultation and provisional release. You can request release during custody without waiting 48 hours. State-provided lawyers depend on statutory conditions, while bail does not always require cash security. The stage of proceedings determines how each safeguard applies.

Working on a Student Visa in Thailand? What Happens After Arrest 
02/11/2026 
 A Thai student visa does not itself authorise employment. Working without permission can lead to a fine and removal, even while permission to stay remains valid. The procedure, custody, immigration orders and future work-permit eligibility must be assessed separately. Employers face their own penalties; neither automatic detention nor a fixed re-entry ban should be assumed.

Zombie Pods in Thailand: Etomidate Charges, Bail and Immigration Risks 
01/11/2026 
 Zombie pods in Thailand may expose users to psychotropic-substance charges, not simply a vaping penalty. Liability depends on the substance, laboratory findings, knowledge and circumstances. Personal-use possession has a separate penalty provision. Bail and immigration consequences require individual decisions; one pod does not automatically establish dealing or make a foreign suspect ineligible for bail.
