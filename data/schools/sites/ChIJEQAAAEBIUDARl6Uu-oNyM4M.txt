 Welcome to Indigo
 For over 15 years, Indigo Kids has provided a warm, welcoming environment where young children develop confidence, curiosity and a lifelong love of learning. We combine the British Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) curriculum with play-based experiences, caring teachers and individual attention to help every child flourish.

 Meet the Indigo Team
 Experienced teachers inspiring confident young learners.
 Poria
 Principal
 Poria is an experienced early years educator and curriculum designer with over ten years in the field. 
 Born and raised in London, he developed a deep passion for supporting children's development and learning after studying child psychology and educational sciences. 
 Drawing on a diverse background that includes journalism, banking, and piano teaching, Poria founded the nursery with a vision of creating a nurturing environment where children can thrive.
 -->
 Carla
 Teacher
 Carla brings years of teaching experience from international schools across Southeast Asia, working with children from early years through to primary education. 
 Passionate about child development, she combines professional expertise with a naturally nurturing and caring approach. 
 Carla is dedicated to creating a warm, supportive environment where every child feels valued, confident, and inspired to grow.

 Letecia
 Teacher
 Letecia has been a cherished member of the Indigo team for many years, bringing warmth, dedication, and a deep love for children to her role. 
 With extensive experience in early years education, she supports each child with patience, enthusiasm, and genuine care. 
 Known for her kind heart and maternal nature, Letecia is loved by children and families alike. She creates a supportive, joyful environment where children feel safe, valued, and encouraged to flourish.
