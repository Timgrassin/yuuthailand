 ABOUT US 
 about us
 The Pikture Gallery is nestled in Bangkok’s labyrinth of small streets called “Soi” in the Sukhumvit neighbourhood. We are located within walking distance (just about in the heat) of Promphong station on the sky train. So if you happened to be wandering around Bangkok, visit us for some art or just to cool down.
