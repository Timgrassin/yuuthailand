 About Us 
 CWW AGENCY is an international full service company that provides bar and restaurant consulting bartender training and bar catering services.

 Thanks to its long experience, CWW Agency has proven key solutions to bring your business to the highest standards and accompany you in the path to achieve your goals.

 Know More 
 Chris Messinanou 
 ​ 
 is the founder of CWW AGENCY, the first agency in France to offer auditing, consulting and training services, specific to the bar and restaurant industry. The company started as THE BARMEN AGENCY PARIS in 1996, and was rebranded to Cww Agency in 2011. 
 Beside working on many missions worldwide, he developed one iconic well known "Paris Equip Hotel Show" in France, where with other actors (hotels managers, architects, designers, engineers...), presented new futuristic concepts to the food and beverage industry. 
 ​ 
 As well in Morocco 2011, he established the first Moroccan bartender association organized every year the Moroccan bartending competition in order to find the best talents, promoting knowledge, technics and a luxury standard in bar & restaurant business. 
 2017 A new journey to Asia, CWW AGENCY has launched a concept "Kossivi Bar Academy" in Bangkok, Thailand. 
 The academy offers a high-level bartender training with 5 stars quality bar program , real ingredients and professionally international bar experience instructors.
