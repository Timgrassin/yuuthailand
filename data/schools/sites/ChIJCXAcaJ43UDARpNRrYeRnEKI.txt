 Most popular · DTV
 The DTV: a 5-year visa. 
 The Destination Thailand Visa is for remote workers, freelancers and people coming for Thai activities like Muay Thai or cooking, and for their spouse and children.
 Valid for 5 years, multiple entries, up to 180 days per entry.
 One extension per entry in Thailand, for up to 180 days.
 Financial evidence of at least THB 500,000 for every applicant.
 Since 31 August 2026, you apply in your country of nationality or permanent residence.
