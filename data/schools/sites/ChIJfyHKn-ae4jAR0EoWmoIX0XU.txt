 Do I need any experience to start? 
 Not at all! Most students enroll with zero prior experience. We&#039;ll quickly assess your level before you start and place you in the right group.
 Can I try a class before committing? 
 Absolutely! You can join a free trial class for 60 minutes or 15 minutes for an online class. An added bonus is that you can meet your teacher and experience the learning environment before enrolling.
 Where is the school located? Is it near the BTS or MRT? 
 Yes, Duke Language School is located in Bangkok on Soi Sukhumvit 13, a quick 4 minute walk from the Nana BTS station and around a 7 minute walk from the Sukhumvit MRT station.
 How long does it take to complete a level or course? 
 Our Thai and Chinese courses are 20-day programs (40 hours), while our English course is a 15-day program (30 hours).
 Do you offer online or group classes? 
 We offer small, energetic group classes designed to optimize interaction and support. We also have options for online learning and flexible schedules. Get in touch with our team to find the class that best fits your daily routine.
