 Welcome to Thai Scandinavian Law Office
Thai Scandinavian Law Office are an international law office specialized in legal services to foreigners in Thailand. All our lawyers are registered at the Lawyers Concil of Thailand and we cooperate with law offices in Sweden and Denmark
 Our lawyers have totally more than 50 years experience from legal work and we have a broad experience with foreign companies and customers in Thailand. 
We speak Thai, English, Russian as well as Scandinavian which is a huge benefit in order to avoid misunderstandings. Our law firm provides a variety of legal services to foreigners and thai nationals. We have offices both at Jomtien and in Hua Hin.
 Our assistance is billed according to our price list published on our web pages or according to spesific agreement. This gives you as a customer the necessarey predictability. 
Our law firm provides a variety of legal services to foreigners and thai nationals. You can also find information about us on our YouTube channel.
