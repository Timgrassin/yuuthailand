 Frequently Asked Questions
 Do you offer a free initial consultation? 
 Yes! We offer a free 30-minute consultation . Moving to or living in Thailand should be an exciting experience, not a stressful one. During this chat, we’ll listen to your situation and outline the exact legal steps needed to protect your interests.
 What languages do you speak? 
 While our Thai attorneys are fluent in English, we can also arrange support and translation in German to ensure clear communication and peace of mind. We make sure you understand every document and process before you sign anything.
 Do you help with government offices in person? 
 Absolutely. We know that visiting the Immigration Office, the Land Department, or the Ministry of Commerce can be intimidating. Our team provides personal assistance and will accompany you to government offices to ensure everything goes smoothly.
 What types of visas do you handle? 
 We assist with all major Thai visas for expats, including:
&#8211; Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) for digital nomads and remote workers.
&#8211; Non-Immigrant O Visa for Retirees and Spouses of Thai Nationals.
&#8211; Long-Term Resident (LTR) Visa. 
&#8211; Non-Immigrant B Visa & Work Permits for business owners and employees.
 What is included in the &#8220;Retiree Peace-of-Mind Bundle&#8221;? 
 We designed this bundle to take the headache out of retiring in Hua Hin. It includes your 1-Year Retirement Visa (Non-O), a Thai Last Will and Testament, a Living Will, assistance opening a Thai bank account, and handling your mandatory 90-day reports. It also comes with an Emergency Legal Hotline.
 Can you help me set up a Thai bank account? 
 If you have the correct visa, we can help. This typically excludes Tourist visa and DTV visa holders. Opening a bank account as a foreigner in Thailand has become more complicated recently, but we provide the necessary documentation and in-person assistance to get your account open securely.
 Can foreigners legally buy property in Hua Hin? 
 It is possible under certain cicumstances, the rules depend on the type of property. Foreigners can directly own condominium units (freehold) in their own name (as long as the foreign quota is not exceeded). For landed houses and villas, foreigners typically utilize long-term registered leaseholds (up to 30 years). We guide you through the safest, most legally compliant route for your specific situation.
 What does your &#8220;Home-Buyers Property Protection Kit&#8221; cover? 
 Buying property in a foreign country is a major investment. Our protection kit safeguards you from start to finish. It includes full legal Due Diligence on the property/developer, Contract Review & Negotiation, Transaction Support at the Land Office, and 1 year of post-purchase support. We also include a free Property Succession Will.
 I want to start a business in Hua Hin. Where do I begin? 
 You should have a business plan and also a Thai business partner who will hold the majority of shares. Our Entrepreneurs Startup Legal Kit is the perfect starting point. We handle the entire process: Thai Company Registration, obtaining the correct Business Licenses, and securing your Non-B Visa and Work Permit. We also provide an initial legal compliance kit, essential contract templates, your company seal, and 3 months of legal advisory to get you off the ground safely.
 Do I need a separate Thai Will if I already have one in my home country? 
 Yes, we strongly recommend it. While foreign wills can be valid in Thailand, translating them, getting them legalized, and processing them through the Thai court system can take months or even years. Having a specific Thai Last Will and Testament for your assets located in Thailand ensures a smooth, cost-effective transfer of property and bank accounts to your heirs.
