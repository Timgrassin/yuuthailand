 It's All About People.
 Hua Hin Muay Thai School began its journey in 2017 with a vision to provide quality Muay Thai training to individuals at every level. Our head coach Kaisuwit, with over 20 years of experience, leads the team in offering training sessions that cater to the needs of everyone, whether you are a beginner or a pro fighter.

 Programs Tailored To You.
 Hua Hin Muay Thai School offers a range of training options to cater to our diverse community of members. Here&#8217;s a breakdown of our programs. 
 1 on 1
 In these sessions, you get personal attention from our experienced coaches. Whether you are new to Muay Thai or looking to advance your skills, 1 on 1 training provides tailored guidance to meet your goals.
 Class
 Our class training sessions are structured to provide a well-rounded Muay Thai training experience. Here, you will engage in a mix of technique learning, practice, and sparring, all under the guidance of our seasoned coaches.
 Group
 Group training at our gym is about everyone participating with everyone. It&#8217;s a dynamic environment where you can learn from others, share your own knowledge, and improve your Muay Thai skills as a collective.
