 FAQ
 Thailand visa and driving license questions
 Which visa is right for me? 
 Answer 3 to 5 quick questions and we recommend the best visa type, total cost, required documents and exact processing time. You can also use the Visa Finder at the top of this page.
 Can you convert my foreign license to a Thai driving license? 
 Yes. We arrange translations, the medical certificate, the residence certificate and your Land Transport Office visit in Phuket.
 How fast can I start? 
 Send your passport copy before 15:00 local time. In many cases we start the same day.
 Can I extend my stay on a tourist visa? 
 Yes, typically 30-day extensions are available at Thai immigration offices.
 Do I need insurance? 
 Insurance is strongly recommended and sometimes mandatory, depending on the Thai embassy or consulate.
 Do you assist with work permits? 
 Yes. We include company registration, BOI partnership setup and full legal compliance.
 Is 90-day reporting included? 
 Yes. We handle reminders and online or in-person filings with Thai Immigration.
 What are the requirements for a retiree visa? 
 We guide you through deposits, monthly income proofs and health insurance coverage so you meet all Thai Immigration rules.
 Do you check my eligibility before I pay? 
 Yes. We verify your profile first to avoid failed submissions and wasted fees.
 Can family members be included? 
 Yes. A spouse and children can be added if they meet the criteria.
 Can I switch from a tourist visa? 
 Sometimes. We advise the fastest compliant route for your case.
 How long does a driving license take? 
 Usually 1 to 3 visits, depending on the province and how ready your documents are.
