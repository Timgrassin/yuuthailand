 Authentic Thai Cooking Class in Pai with an experienced chef

 Learn all about authentic Thai food in a small-group tour of the local food scene. Visit a local market to learn about the ingredients used in Thai cuisine and then enjoy your own cooked food and an experience to remember. 
 Our Journey 
 Immerse yourself in the flavors of Thailand with a small-group cooking class in Pai. Learn to prepare authentic Thai dishes with personalized instruction, suitable for adults, couples, and families.
 Vegans and non-vegan welcomes.
 Begin by visiting a local market to gather fresh ingredients. Discover the key to authentic Thai cooking as you learn what to buy and how to choose the best produce.
 Head to the kitchen and follow step-by-step guidance from Thom. Prepare and cook a variety of traditional Thai dishes, mastering the techniques and flavors that make Thai cuisine so unique.
 Enjoy your creations at the end of the class, leaving with new knowledge and a happy stomach.
