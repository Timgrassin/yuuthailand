 FOR THOSE SERIOUS ABOUT LEARNING THAI COOKING IN BANGKOK 
 Since 2010, Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy has trained thousands of students—from complete beginners to professional chefs—inside a professional kitchen environment in Bangkok. Located just minutes from BTS On Nut, our fully air-conditioned school offers hands-on Thai cooking classes designed for both travelers and serious culinary students.
 Join a half-day cooking class where you choose your own dishes, prepare curry paste from scratch, and cook authentic Thai food from start to finish, or take your skills further with our Worldchefs-Approved professional chef training courses, ranging from beginner foundations to intensive programs up to 10 weeks.
 Beyond our core classes, we offer private cooking experiences, vegetarian courses, fruit carving, team building events, school field trips, and DTV visa-supported training programs. Whether you&#8217;re visiting Bangkok for a day or planning a long-term culinary journey, our school is built to give you real skills—not just a tourist experience.

 Frequently Asked Questions About Thai Cooking Classes in Bangkok
 What is included in a Thai cooking class in Bangkok? 
 Our Thai cooking classes in Bangkok include hands-on cooking instruction, all ingredients, recipe guidance, equipment, and a full meal prepared by you during the class. Depending on the course selected, classes may also include a local market tour, welcome refreshments, dessert, and additional culinary instruction focused on authentic Thai cooking techniques. All classes are taught in English by experienced Thai chefs.
 What dishes will I learn at Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy? 
 At Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy, students can choose their own dishes from a wide selection of authentic Thai recipes rather than following a fixed menu. Our Thai cooking classes in Bangkok are designed to give students flexibility while learning real Thai cooking techniques.
 In our popular tourist cooking class, students select dishes from multiple categories including Thai curry, soup, noodles, stir fry, and salad. Options include favorites such as Pad Thai, Tom Yum Goong, Green Curry, Massaman Curry, Khao Soi, Som Tam, Pad See Ew, Drunken Noodles, Chicken with Cashews, and Thai Basil Stir Fry. Complimentary Thai desserts including Mango Sticky Rice and Fried Bananas are also included.
 For private Thai cooking classes, students can choose from more than 130 authentic Thai dishes, making the experience ideal for food lovers, families, groups, and travelers looking for a more personalized cooking experience in Bangkok.
 Are your Thai cooking classes beginner friendly? 
 Yes. Our Thai cooking classes in Bangkok are designed for beginners, travelers, couples, families, and home cooks of all experience levels. Our chefs guide students step-by-step through each dish while also teaching important Thai cooking fundamentals such as balancing flavors, preparing curry pastes, using fresh herbs, and understanding Thai ingredients.
 What makes Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy different from other cooking schools in Bangkok? 
 Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy has been teaching Thai cooking in English since 2010. Unlike many tourist-focused classes, we focus on authentic Thai culinary techniques, professional instruction, and hands-on learning. Students prepare their own dishes individually and learn practical cooking skills that go beyond simply following recipes. We also offer advanced masterclasses, professional chef training, and long-term cooking programs for students seeking a deeper culinary experience in Thailand.
 Do you offer vegetarian Thai cooking classes in Bangkok? 
 Yes. We can accommodate vegetarian diets and many other dietary preferences during our Thai cooking classes in Bangkok. Vegetarian alternatives are available for many traditional Thai dishes while still maintaining authentic Thai flavors and cooking methods. Please inform us of any dietary requirements when booking your class.
 Does the cooking class include a market tour? 
 Yes, selected classes include a guided local market tour where students learn about Thai herbs, vegetables, spices, curry ingredients, coconut products, and traditional Thai cooking essentials. The market visit helps students better understand authentic Thai ingredients and how they are used in everyday Thai cuisine.
 Where is Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy located? 
 Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy is conveniently located in Bangkok near the On Nut BTS station and is accessible from central Bangkok areas including Sukhumvit. Our cooking school is easy to reach by taxi, BTS, and MRT, making it convenient for travelers staying in different parts of the city.
 Do you offer professional chef classes in Bangkok? 
 Yes. We offer professional Thai cooking courses and advanced chef training programs in Bangkok for culinary students, restaurant owners, professional chefs, and serious home cooks. These programs focus on authentic Thai techniques, ingredient knowledge, curry preparation, wok cooking, seafood preparation, plating, and kitchen workflow. Multi-day and long-term culinary training options are available.
 Do you offer DTV visa eligible cooking courses in Thailand? 
 Yes. Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy offers eligible long-term Thai cooking courses that can be used for Thailand’s DTV visa application requirements. Our DTV qualifying programs are designed for students seeking immersive culinary education in Thailand while learning authentic Thai cuisine and professional cooking techniques. Please contact us directly for current course options and visa documentation details.
 How do I book a Thai cooking class in Bangkok? 
 You can book your Thai cooking class in Bangkok directly through our website here . Advance booking is recommended, especially during busy travel seasons. After booking, you will receive confirmation details, class information, and meeting instructions from our team.
