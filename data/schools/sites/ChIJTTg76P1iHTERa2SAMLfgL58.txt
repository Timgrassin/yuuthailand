 Authentic Muay Thai Gym in Bangkok, Thailand &ndash; Keatkhamtorn Gym 
 Keatkhamtorn Gym is a Muay Thai training camp in Bangkok, Thailand, founded in 1999 by "Jakae Kuankrod" (In-sri Dam Keatpongsak) &mdash; a passionate fighter from Thung Song, Nakhon Si Thammarat. 
 Inspired by his uncle, founder of the renowned Keat Ban Chong Gym, Jakae built a place where Thai champions and international students train together, preserving the art and heart of authentic Muay Thai. 
 Located near Ram Inthra Kor Mor 9 MRT Station, our gym welcomes everyone &mdash; from beginners to professionals &mdash; to train Muay Thai in Bangkok and experience Thailand's true fighting spirit.
