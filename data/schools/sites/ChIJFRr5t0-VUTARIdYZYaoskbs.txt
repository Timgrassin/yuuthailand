 Why Montessori
 Children who are trusted to do it themselves
 Montessori is not a style of nursery furniture. It is a way of arranging a room, and a day, so that small children get to be capable.
 Independence
 A child who can pour their own water, fasten their own shoes and clear their own plate is a child who has learned something about themselves, not just about water and shoes.
 Concentration
 Work is chosen by the child and continues for as long as it holds them. Nobody interrupts a child who is absorbed — that is where attention span is actually built.
 Mixed ages
 Children stay in the same classroom for three years. Older children teach the younger ones, which is the fastest way to find out how well you really understand something.

 Questions
 The things parents ask first
 If yours isn’t here, ask us — we would rather answer a question than have you worry about it.
 Ask us anything 
 What ages do you take? Children from 1½ to 6 years, in three mixed-age classrooms. Get in touch if your child sits near a boundary — placement follows readiness as much as birthday.
 Is the school English-speaking? Yes. English is the language of the classroom and Thai is taught alongside it. Our families are a mix of Thai and international, and children pick up both faster than their parents expect.
 Can we visit before deciding? Please do. Visiting during a working morning tells you more than any brochure. If you are still abroad we will do the same tour over a video call.
 Do you take children mid-term? Yes, subject to space in the right age group. A good number of our families arrive in Krabi partway through the year, so this is normal rather than exceptional.
 What does it cost? Fees are published in full on the fees page — ฿18,000 or ฿24,000 per three-month term depending on age, with no registration fee. Half-days and drop-in sessions are available on request.
 What is included in the fees? Meals, all Montessori materials and the extra-curricular programme. Swimming lessons and off-site trips are charged separately.
 Come and see a morning
 Half an hour in the classroom while the children are working will tell you more than this entire website. If you are still abroad, we will walk you round on a video call instead.
