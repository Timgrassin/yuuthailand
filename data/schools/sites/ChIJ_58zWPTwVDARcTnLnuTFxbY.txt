 Thai Cooking Classes on Koh Samui, Private and Professional Courses 
 Discover authentic Thai cooking classes on Koh Samui. Learn to prepare traditional dishes in a relaxed atmosphere with a stunning sea view, with private 3-hour, 5-hour and intensive classes, professional chef training, and a complete 12-month Thai cooking course. All our classes can be adapted for vegetarian and vegan participants , so everyone feels welcome.
 Explore our classes 
 3-hour private cooking class 
 Enjoy a private three-hour Thai cooking class including a lively market tour. Choose from a menu of more than 200 authentic Thai dishes and prepare two recipes completely from scratch, then enjoy your creations with a stunning sea view.
 5-hour private cooking class 
 A full culinary journey with four dishes of your choice, a guided market visit, and convenient pick-up anywhere on the island. Learn to cook from a menu of over 200 authentic recipes and savor your dishes overlooking the sea.
 Intensive private cooking class 
 An intensive private Thai cooking experience designed for passionate cooks and travellers looking to go further. This course includes a guided market visit followed by the preparation of six dishes of your choice, combining classic Thai recipes with regional specialities. Fully private and limited to one or two participants, the class takes place in our sea-view cooking school, with hotel transfer included on Koh Samui.
 Professional training 
 Intensive Thai culinary training designed for chefs and passionate food lovers. In 3 to 6 days you will master 24 to 48 dishes, with personalized guidance and a professional certificate.
 12-month Thai cooking course 
 For those wishing to stay long term on Koh Samui and immerse themselves in Thai food culture. This one-year program combines over 60 authentic recipes with monthly certifications, offering the most complete cooking experience in Thailand.
 In addition to our Thai cooking classes, we also offer a collection of 12 recipe books , perfect for continuing your culinary journey at home. Gift cards are available for friends and family, and our online cooking lessons make it easy to join us from anywhere in the world. Visit our blog for authentic Thai recipes and stories from Koh Samui.
