 About Us 
 Teaching 
 Vantage Siam offers expert English teaching tailored to meet specific requirements. We create and administer proficiency tests and design courses for individuals and classes. Additionally, we develop customized courses for corporate clients. 
 Training 
 Vantage Siam empowers learners to excel in their careers by building a strong foundation in linguistic proficiency tailored to real-world applications. 
 ​ 
 Through our customized courses, individuals gain the confidence to present ideas clearly, negotiate effectively, and compose professional correspondence with precision. 
 Testing 
 At Vantage Siam, we deliver comprehensive language tests to assess the four key language skills: listening, speaking, reading, and writing. 
 ​ 
 These tests serve as benchmarks for evaluating linguistic proficiency, ensuring individuals meet the required standards for effective communication while also guiding the development of customized training programs. 
 TEFL Training 
 Our award-winning TEFL certification program trains new teachers for Thai schools, equipping them with the skill set and knowledge to be successful in the classroom. 
 Through rigorous training and our observed teaching practices, our trainees develop confidence in classroom management, lesson planning, and tailored instruction.
