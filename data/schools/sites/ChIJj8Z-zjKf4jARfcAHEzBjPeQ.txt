 What is the Thailand Privilege Card Program?
 Explore your Thai Elite Visa options today
 The “Thailand Privilege Card” program, also known as the Thailand Elite, is a long-term Thailand residency programme given to the Thailand Privilege Card members. It is categorized under Tourist Visa (Privilege Entry “PE”) allowing residency in Thailand from 5- 20 years, depending on the privilege membership level.
 Initiated in 2003, Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd, the operator of Thailand Elite Card, gained its state-owned enterprise status within the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) as its sole shareholder. It is a government-sponsored initiative in Thailand that allows lengthy residency privileges and other exclusive benefits to foreign individuals seeking to live in Thailand.
 Thailand Elite Visa, or Thailand Privilege Card Programmes, has originated with a singular mission: to present esteemed foreign guests with the finest way of life in Thailand. For over 19 years, Thailand Elite has rapidly gained popularity and became the world’s first country membership package with exclusive benefits to immigration, leisure, business, and much more.
 For those looking to make Thailand their second home, explore the advantages of the Thailand Privilege Card (ThaiElite visa) membership, for you and your whole family up to 5-20 years.

 What are the qualifications to be a
 Thailand Privilege Card Member?
 Applicants/members must meet and uphold the following criteria:
 Hold valid permission to reside in Thailand in accordance with immigration laws or relevant Thai regulations. 
 Possess a foreign passport. 
 Have not been sentenced to imprisonment in any jurisdiction, except for offenses resulting from negligence. 
 Have not declared bankruptcy. 
 Have not been declared mentally incompetent or quasi-incompetent.

 FAQ
 What is the Thailand Privilege Card (Thai Elite visa)? 
 The Thailand Privilege Card, previously known as the Thailand Elite Visa, and classified as a long-term tourist visa with a multiple-entry tailored for those looking to enjoy an extended stay in Thailand with flexible packages offering from 5 to 20 years, it stands apart from standard visa options by delivering exclusive benefits and privileges. Whether you’re an investor, entrepreneur, digital nomad, retiree, or frequent traveler, this program ensures a luxurious and seamless experience during your time in Thailand.
 Who is eligible for the Thailand Privilege Card (Thai Elite visa)? 
 The Thailand Privilege Card (Thai Elite visa) is available to foreign nationals of all nationalities who meet the program&#8217;s requirements, including financial capability and a clean criminal record.
 What are the benefits of the Thailand Privilege Card (Thai Elite visa)? 
 Benefits include long-term visa privileges, VIP airport services, exclusive access to golf courses and spas, concierge services, healthcare benefits, and more.
 Can I work in Thailand with a Thailand Privilege Card (Thai Elite visa)? 
 The Thailand Privilege Card (Thailand Elite visa) does not automatically grant work rights, but members can apply for a separate work permit if they meet the legal requirements.
 How long does the application process of the Thailand Privilege Card (Thai Elite visa) take? 
 The application process typically takes 1 to 3 months, depending on the applicant’s nationality and the completeness of the submitted documents.
