 Our Services

 Manufacturing
 We manufacture every product with highly selected raw materials sourced from trusted global suppliers and rigorous quality control at every stage. Our R&D and production teams ensure German-tested performance, delivering consistent excellence and fast customization for color, texture, and function to meet each project’s specific needs.
 Retail & Distribution
 We make our products easily accessible through multiple channels — from online orders via Facebook, LINE, and our website, to leading e-commerce platforms like Shopee and Lazada. Customers can also visit our showroom or request on-site consultations. With reliable delivery and active trade show participation, we ensure seamless service and strong customer connection.
 Training
 Our highly skilled technicians provide comprehensive installation training for contractors at both basic and advanced levels. We focus on hands-on learning to ensure professional application and lasting results. On-site training sessions are also available for clients with qualifying minimum order quantities (MOQ), ensuring every partner gains the technical confidence to achieve perfect finishes.
 Contracting
 We provide complete contracting services for projects of all types, sizes, and locations — from residential to large-scale commercial developments. Our experienced team ensures precise application, premium results, and on-time
completion. With support from our expert admin team, we also help clients select the most suitable products and finishes for their specific project requirements.
 OEM
 If you’re looking to build your own professional brand, we’re ready to make it happen. Quaco offers OEM and private label production with flexible MOQ and contract options to suit your business goals. We can create custom formulas
that meet your specific functional or design demands, and provide full packaging and labeling services under your brand. For large projects or distributors, we also offer bulk delivery options with consistent quality control — ensuring reliability.
