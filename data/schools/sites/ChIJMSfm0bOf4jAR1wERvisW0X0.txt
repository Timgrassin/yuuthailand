 About Somdet Phra Nyanavajirodom (Phra Ajarn Luangphor Viriyang Sirintharo) 
 Somdet Phra Nyanavajirodom (Luang Phor Viriyang Sirintharo) was ordained as a novice monk on October 22, 1935, at the age of 15. He accompanied Phra Ajarn Kongma on meditation pilgrimages (tudong) to various places for a period of eight years.
 During that time, he was fully ordained as a monk on May 20, 1941, at the age of 20. One day, Phra Ajarn Kongma led him on a pilgrimage where they met Phra Ajarn Mun Bhuridatta. Luang Phor Viriyang was chosen to serve as Phra Ajarn Mun’s attendant for four years during the rains retreats and five years outside the rains retreats, a total of nine years.
 He practiced tudong and meditation alongside Phra Ajarn Mun, studying meditation and profound Dhamma. Later, he disseminated this meditation knowledge to the public through the Willpower Institute, which he founded on July 24, 1997, at Wat Dhammamongkol.
 Meditation promotes reason, responsibility, and loving-kindness. 
 About our meditation courses
 All Meditation Courses of the Willpower Institute Foundation are based on the teachings of Somdet Phra Nyanavajirodom Phra Ajarn Luang Phor Viriyang Sirintharo — the late and highly revered Thai Buddhist monk, Abbot of Wat Dhammamongkol, meditation master, and founder of the Willpower Institute Foundation.
 The meditation methods originate from the direct lineage of his teacher, Luangpu Mun Bhuridatta, a renowned Vipassana meditation master in the Thai Forest monastic tradition.
 The Willpower Institute Foundation aims to teach meditation with an emphasis on Samma-Samadhi (Right Meditation). Learning meditation and accumulating mind power bring great benefits and help cultivate virtues. Meditation promotes reason, responsibility, and loving-kindness. Those who practice meditation will experience benefits both physically and mentally.
