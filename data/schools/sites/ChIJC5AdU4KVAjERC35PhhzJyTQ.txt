                                                            PAD THAI 
 Master Pad Thai – Thailand’s Most Loved Noodle Dish! 
 Craving that perfect plate of sweet, sour, and savory noodles? At PATTAYA THAI COOKING, you’ll learn to cook Pad Thai just like a local. 
 We’ll guide you step-by-step — from prepping fresh ingredients to stir-frying noodles with the perfect balance of flavors. You’ll also learn how to make the signature Pad Thai sauce from scratch (no bottled shortcuts!). 
 It’s fun, beginner-friendly, and seriously tasty. If you love Thai food, Pad Thai is a must-learn! 
 Come cook it, taste it, and take the recipe home with confidence!
