 Our Story
 Phuket Consultancy Co., Ltd. was registered as a juristic person as per the Civil and Commercial Code on 24 October 2017, juristic person registration no. 0835560015191. We provide business consulting and real estate services. The head office is located at 90/5 Village No. 2, Wichit Sub-district, Mueang Phuket District. Phuket Province 83000.
 Phuket Consultancy Co., Ltd. was founded by a group of highly skilled and experienced professionals. By concentrating on consulting from the start of doing business in Thailand, including company registration, legal advice, accounting, and taxation, as well as obtaining relevant business licenses to conduct business legally. Apart from that, we offer services such as application for investment promotion (BOI), legalization of documents with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the embassy, Notary Public by Attorney, Translation and Notarization of Documents, Resident Permit Consultation, Work Permit, Work Visa Application, Family Visa, Marriage Visa, Student Visa, Retirement Visa, and Thailand Elite Visa. We also take care of and offer comprehensive services for buying, selling, and renting real estate.
 Phuket Consultancy Co., Ltd. is a customer-oriented organization, including attention to customer needs, impressions, and the maximum benefits that customers should receive, as well as resolving issues and answering pertinent questions, which is our primary mission.
