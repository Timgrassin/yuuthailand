 01 Discover Ten seconds of Blue Chitta 
 Where movement, water and island life meet.
 The shala Two sea-view shalas, a few steps up from the road. The practice Ashtanga through to Yin, every day of the week. The line The freediving school. Courses start Wednesdays and Fridays. The boat Out most mornings, to the line and over the reef. Prana Café Fuel for yogis and freedivers. Plant-based, island-fresh, cooked with intention. Something to eat Breakfast after yoga practice, brunch after freediving. No booking needed, just come and eat. Somewhere to sleep A pod bed comes with every stay package, from Bed & Breakfast at 1,000 thb. The people Teachers come here to keep learning. Students come here and become teachers. 
 One breath above the water, one below. Focus the mind, soften the body, depth is a side effect of relaxation.

 A day at Blue Chitta.
 Everything sits on one site a short walk from the beach, so a day here can hold all of it. Nothing below is compulsory, and every stop has its own page.
 06:30 Sunrise at High the Moon viewpoint Above the island, beside the coffee shop at the top. The mats are already up there. 500 thb 
 08:00 Meditation & Breathing Class Start the day with the breath — the practice that carries both the yoga and the freediving here. On the daily schedule 
 09:30 Ashtanga, Vinyasa — or Freediving Choose your morning: a strong practice in the sea-view shala, or the morning freediving session from our boat. Yoga classes 350 thb 
 11:30 Yoga for Beginners or Slow Flow A gentler pace for those who value a slow morning — connecting with the breath and the ocean breeze. Just come as you are. 350 thb 
 12:30 Brunch at Prana Café Signature smoothie bowls, delicious coffee and plant-based food cooked with intention — and a great place to connect and make friends. Open 8am–5pm (Fri until 9:30pm, Sat until 2pm), no booking needed. Open from 8am 
 15:00 Yoga & Snorkeling on the Boat Twice a month: move through a creative yoga flow to the rhythm of the waves, breathing the sea breeze and opening your mind — then slip into the sea to discover the reef and its fish. Twice a month · 1,200 thb 
 16:00 Breathwork & Ice Bath Guided breathing, then the cold — the workshop that resets the nervous system. 600 thb 
 17:45 Sunset Yoga Moving with intention and fluidity, blending yin and yang energies as the sun goes down over the water. 350 thb 
 19:15 Yin & Sound Healing A low-lit restorative practice of sound, stillness and intention — moving past the physical body into the emotional, energetic, mental and spiritual. Deep rest, and a beautiful way to end the day. On the evening schedule 
 19:00 Friday: Community Dinner & Music Jam Every Friday, 7:00–9:30pm, the long table fills and the instruments come out. Everyone is welcome — please pre-book at Prana Café before 4pm. Fridays · 350 thb 
 21:00 A pod bed, curtain drawn Mixed or female wellness dorm, each bed with a privacy curtain, a fan and a reading light. A pod bed comes with every stay package — Bed & Breakfast from 1,000 thb, Eat Sleep Yoga from 1,300 thb. From 1,000 thb with breakfast
