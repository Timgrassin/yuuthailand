 Our courses will show employers that you: 
 Direct contact with an experienced native speaker 
 Professional and knowledgeable training that you won’t forget 
 Flexible and tailored course design 
 Ongoing support and assistance with your next step 
 Qualified professionals with a track record of success 
 Up to date and modern facilities and equipment 
 Close and easy access to MRT public transport 
 Quick responses to any enquiry, day or night

 Reasons to Choose Kidsangsan Language School: 
 We are based in central Bangkok very close to the Lat Phrao MRT station, so getting to the school couldn’t be easier. 
 You will receive expert training and professional feedback throughout your enrollment with us. 
 Our courses will help you build a very strong foundation in using English whether for private or professional use. 
 We are regulated by the Ministry of Education. 
 We have many years of experience on delivering the best programs with a proven successful track record.
