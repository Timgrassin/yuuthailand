 Thai Voice Language School
 Thai Language School for Foreigners in Ao Nang, Krabi, Thailand
 Learn Thai the practical way — for real life in Thailand. We teach foreigners how Thai people actually speak, so you can handle everyday situations with confidence: ordering food, making friends, renting a place, dealing with services, and traveling around Thailand.
 What you get
 • Real spoken Thai, not textbook-only Thai
• Clear structure: you always know what to study next
• Native Thai teachers who correct your pronunciation and tones
• Small classes and personal feedback
• Lessons built around common situations you face in Thailand
 Courses for every level
 • Beginner: start from zero and speak from the first lessons
• Intermediate: expand vocabulary, improve grammar, speak more naturally
• Advanced: fluency, nuanced speech, reading and writing if you want
• Private lessons: fastest progress with a program made for you
 Flexible formats
 • Group classes at our school in Ao Nang, Krabi
• Private lessons in person or online
• Weekly schedules that fit work and travel plans
 Why students choose us
 Thai is not just words — it is tones, rhythm, and real usage. We focus on pronunciation, listening, and speaking first, then add reading and writing when they support your goals. You learn what you can immediately use.
 Start learning Thai with us
 Tell us your level and your goal (travel, daily life, relationship, work, long-term stay). We will recommend the best course and schedule for you.
