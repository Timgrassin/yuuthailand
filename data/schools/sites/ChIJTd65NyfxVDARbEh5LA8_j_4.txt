 ABOUT US 
 Infinity Accounting and Legal is a trusted business services firm based in Koh Samui, Thailand. We specialise in providing reliable, professional support to individuals, entrepreneurs, and companies looking to navigate the complexities of doing business in Thailand. Our services cover company formation, visa and work permit assistance, accounting, taxation, licensing, and ongoing legal compliance. 
 ​ 
 With years of local experience and a deep understanding of Thai regulations, our team is committed to delivering clear, accurate, and timely support. We pride ourselves on being a true one-stop solution - whether you're starting a new company, expanding an existing one, or simply need help managing your monthly accounts. 
 ​ 
 At Infinity, we believe in building long-term relationships based on trust, transparency, and results. Our clients range from small local businesses to foreign-owned companies and individuals relocating to Thailand. No matter your background or business type, we take the time to understand your goals and provide practical solutions tailored to your needs. 
 ​ 
 Let us handle the paperwork, so you can focus on growing your business with confidence.
