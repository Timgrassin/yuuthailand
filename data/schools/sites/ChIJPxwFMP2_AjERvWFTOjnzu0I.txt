Early Years & Primary School
 “ Where the lessons of life matter ”
 Lovell International School seeks to provide the highest globally accepted international education standards in a safe, caring and enabling environment. Our teaching focus ensures that students realise their full potential in academics, language and communication, sports, expressive arts and technology in order to grow as holistic and knowledgeable individuals.
 EARLY YEARS 
 PRIMARY 
 FACILITIES 
 The physical environment in a Reggio Emilia-inspired kindergarten is intentionally designed to be inviting, stimulating, and conductive to learning. It is seen as the ‘third teacher’, alongside teachers and peers, providing opportunities for exploration, discovery, and independent thinking.
 EXECUTIVE FUNCTIONS (EF)
 THE IMPORTANCE OF EXECUTIVE FUNCTIONS FOR ALPHA GENERATION
 Executive functions (EF); are defined as the cognitive abilities needed to control thoughts, emotions, and actions. EF helps children develop their acquired skills of teamwork, decision-making, problem solving, adaptability, and being aware of emotions.
When children have opportunities to develop executive functions, it results in self-regulation skills and lifelong benefits.
 “ A school that grows like a tree house ”
 Our campus is thoughtfully designed around the natural trees that have thrived on this land, emphasizing our commitment to environmental preservation by refraining from cutting any mature trees. This intentional approach not only imparts a genuine tree house ambiance to our school but also underscores our deep inspiration drawn from nature. As we aspire to create a nurturing environment, our design reflects the organic growth and interconnectedness observed in the natural world.
 LOVELL FACILITIES 
 HERB GARDEN 
 Our Herb Garden is more than just a garden; it's a dynamic learning space where children can engage with the natural world in a meaningful way. The Herb Garden provides a platform for learning about plant growth, sustainability, and the importance of caring for our environment. These lessons are integrated into our curriculum, ensuring a holistic education.
 CHICKEN COOP 
 The Chicken Coop at our school embodies our commitment to a well-rounded education that extends beyond the classroom. Children get the chance to actively participate in caring for our feathered friends, collecting eggs, and understanding the life cycle of chickens. This hands-on experience nurtures a sense of responsibility and empathy.
 BUG HOTEL 
 In our Bug Hotel, children can explore the fascinating world of insects. It's a miniature ecosystem that offers an up-close look at these tiny creatures and their essential role in our environment.
 CLIMBING FACILITIES 
 Our campus is equipped with climbing facilities designed to inspire adventure, build confidence, and promote physical development  in a secure and controlled environment.  It's an ideal setting for beginners to learn the basics of climbing techniques, such as balance, coordination, and strength.
 COMMUNITY AREA 
 Our Community Play Area is a vital part of our educational philosophy, offering an environment where children can learn through play, make friends, and develop their physical and social skills. It also includes spaces for creative and imaginative play, sparking curiosity and creativity.
 GYM 
 Exercise is an essential part of our curriculum, promoting a healthy and active lifestyle. Classes like P.E, Taekwondo and Dancing encourage the development of fine and gross motor skills, balance, coordination, and strength, all while fostering a love for physical activity.
 SWIMMING POOL 
 Our swimming class is designed to instill essential water safety skills from an early age, ensuring that children are comfortable and confident in the water. Certified instructors from Baby Shark Swim Club provide expert guidance, ensuring children develop their swimming skills effectively.
 CLASSROOM 
 Our Reggio Emilia-inspired kindergarten classroom is a dynamic and captivating environment designed to ignite the curiosity and creativity of young learners. We prioritize natural light and materials like wood and natural textures to create a warm and inviting atmosphere.
 COOKING STUDIO 
 In our Cooking Studio, children and parents can engage in hands-on baking experiences. It's a space where they can prepare and enjoy cooking activities together, fostering an appreciation for different cuisines.
 ART STUDIO 
 We offer workshops and activities for children and parents to participate in, nurturing their artistic abilities and igniting their imaginations.
 FAMILY LIBRARY 
 Our Lovell Family Library is not just a place for books; it's a space where families come together to celebrate the joy of reading and lifelong learning.
