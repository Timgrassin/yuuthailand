 How we help you acheive your goals? 
 We focus on skills not flying hours.
 Flying hours don’t define how well you fly. We don’t focus on pushing you to fly. Instead, we focus on providing essential skills for you, so you can fly confidentially.
 No accident guarantee
 We guarantee zero accidents during your training if you follow instructors properly. Our instructors have 27 years of teaching experience and we’ve been through various kinds of situations so we can guarantee zero accidents.
 Best training hills
 Wind is the most important factor for training. We have the best training hills next to the beach, so we have sea breeze almost every season. Most importantly, you can finish the course within 7 days.
