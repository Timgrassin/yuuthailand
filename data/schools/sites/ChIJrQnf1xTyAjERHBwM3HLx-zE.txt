 Principal’s Welcome
 A warm welcome to the Garden International School, Eastern Seaboard.
 Garden is a unique learning establishment that offers a broad and balanced education. We strive to support one another and every student to awaken aspirations and to fulfil dreams. It is a school for students who want to achieve fantastic academic results, take responsibility and ownership for their own learning, and who love coming to school.

 Our Curriculum
