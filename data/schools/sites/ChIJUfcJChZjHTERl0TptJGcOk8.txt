 Frequent Asked Questions
 What is WPMTA, and is it trustworthy? 
 WPMTA is the World Professional Muay Thai Association, a global professional Muay Thai organization that includes academies for training in Muay Thai. These academies are designed for individuals interested in fitness, providing standardized equipment and facilities. The training is conducted by certified Muay Thai instructors, and there is also a certification program for Muay Thai trainers accredited by SAT and GAC. Additionally, WPMTA is involved in organizing international Muay Thai competitions for the World Championship under the brand M-One Grand Muaythai.
 I have no knowledge of Muay Thai at all, but can I learn it for fitness? 
 Yes, you can. Our academy offers training for individuals interested in Muay Thai for both fitness purposes and long-term training for those aspiring to become professional fighters. We provide a Muay Thai training environment with standardized equipment, including a well-equipped Muay Thai arena and comfortable accommodation for those interested in long-term learning. We welcome students of all ages, including children and adults, and our Muay Thai instructors are highly skilled. Our courses are certified by SAT and GAC, ensuring the quality of our training programs.
 I want to open a Muay Thai school. What should I do? 
 WPMTA can grant you the right to use the Lumpeenee Academy brand for opening a Muay Thai training institute in your country. We will provide training for your staff in Muay Thai by experienced Muay Thai instructors. If you are interested, please contact us for further information.
 I'm interested in becoming a Muay Thai instructor. What should I do? 
 We provide Muay Thai instructor training at all levels A, B, and C. Our courses are officially certified by the Sports Authority of Thailand (SAT), ensuring the highest standards of training. If you&#8217;re passionate about becoming a Muay Thai instructor, we welcome you to join our certified programs.
