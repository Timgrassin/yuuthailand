 Open AIR MUAY THAI Gym
 Our facility features a huge, open-plan Muay Thai gym that allows for ample space to move and exercise comfortably with no overcrowded classes. Top quality PRIMO equipment with punching bags and boxing ring.
 FULLY EQUIPPED
 With treadmills, air bikes, ski ergs and more, Evolution has all the fitness equipment needed to build your strength. Additional classes are held in our Hyrox gym and Yoga studio all next to each other.
 GROUP CLASSES
 We offer a range of group classes including Muay Thai, Yoga, HIIT, Boxfit and Hyrox available as part of our retreat packages or as drop in sessions.
 One to one and private classes
 Private classes and one to one sessions are available with any of our trainers. Get individualised advice and train according to your goals. The most efficient way to level up your fitness and skills.
 Cleanliness and Hygiene
 We take cleanliness seriously at our facility. Our dedicated staff ensures that our floors are mopped, and all equipment is thoroughly cleaned and sanitized between every training session.
 PRIMO MUAY THAI STOCKIST
 Primo fully stocked shop onsite for all your equipment needs. You can use our Muay Thai equipment in our gym or simply buy what you need at discounted prices.

 WHY CHOOSE EVOLUTION? 
 Muay Thai EXPERTS
 Whether you're a beginner or an experienced fighter, with our knowledgeable coaches you will have the opportunity to learn and grow in this traditional martial art right in the heart of Thailand.
 REal Fight experience
 We organise real fights right here in Koh Samui's local stadiums, taking you from the punching bags to the ring.
 HEALTHY ONSITE Meals
 A wide range of healthy options that cater for all to ensure that you fuel your body with the nutrients it needs to support your active lifestyle.
 Recovery classes
 We understand that to train hard you need to optimise recovery. With a range of recovery options such as on-site ice baths, breathwork, yoga classes, massages & sound healing you will feel ready to take on anything.
 Personalized EXPERIENCE
 Small class sizes with several experienced trainers to ensure you feel supported. We even offer personal training sessions so you can take your practice to the next level.
 Train in paradise
 We are located only a short walk away from the beautiful Lamai Beach. Recharge your battery between classes, fill up on the fresh local cuisine and experience what this island has to offer.

 JORDAN WATSON 
 Co-Founder - Head Coach - Former Muay Thai World Champion 
 Evolution's Co-founder, Jordan Watson began training at the age of 6 under Richard Smith at Bad Company in Leeds, UK. Known for his powerful kicking techniques, Jordan became the first ever Yokkao 70 kg champion which he defended multiple times. After years of fighting against some of the greatest Muay Thai fighters in the world, Jordan started Evolution in order to share his knowledge and love of Muay Thai. 
 Record: 52 - 16 - 2 
 OUR TEAM 
 Alongside Jordan, our trainers at Evolution hold many years of experience as fighters and trainers of some of Muay Thai's best. Our team will be there every step of your journey. Whether you're new to the sport or seeking to enhance your skills, Jordan and our experienced trainers cater to individuals of all levels. With their passion, expertise, and years of experience, the Evolution team are committed to supporting you in reaching your goals.
