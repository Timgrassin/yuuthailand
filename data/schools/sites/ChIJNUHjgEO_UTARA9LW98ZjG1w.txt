 Honouring Muay Thai traditions
 สืบสานวิถีมวยไทย
 Kiat Payak Muay Thai opened on 20 October 2026 with a traditional blessing ceremony, the way camps in Thailand have always opened. The mongkol is tied, respect is paid, and then the work begins.
 Train with us and you learn more than kicks and elbows. You learn the history and the etiquette of Thailand's national sport, from the Wai Kru to the way you bow into the ring.
 4 Thai trainers holding the pads 
 3 a day Classes Monday to Saturday at 08:00, 16:00 and kids at 18:30

 Meet our trainers
 แนะนำทีมครูผู้สอน
 Four Thai trainers run the camp: two former champions and two trainers recognised among the best in Thailand. They hold the pads themselves, correct every detail of your stance and strike, and teach the ring craft that only comes from years of fighting.
 Group sessions are well managed, with all our trainers on the floor, so you hit the pads every day.
