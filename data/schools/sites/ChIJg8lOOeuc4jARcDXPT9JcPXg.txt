 We Are SKIP 
 Satit Kaset International Program, celebrating its 30th anniversary in 2024, is a leading international educational program devoted to the development of global Thai citizens with strong ethics and academic excellence. As part of Kasetsart University Laboratory School, SKIP prepares students to thrive in top universities worldwide and successful careers in a rapidly changing landscape.
 For over three decades, SKIP has proudly maintained its enduring commitment to providing a dynamic, student-focused education that blends international standards with local values.
 Real World Skills and Hands On Learning 
 Our curriculum involves lots of active learning opportunities where student get to practice, in real life, what they learn in the classroom. By applying their academic knowledge in creative ways our students prepare themselves for real life; not just exams.  
 Achieving on the International Stage 
 Our students frequently come in top place in multiple international competitions ranging from Hong Kong to Singapore. Through these events students at Satit Kaset IP gain valuable knowledge and skills to achieve not only in Thailand but also abroad. 
 Building Leadership and Teamwork Skills 
 Satit Kaset’s International Program stresses teamwork and cooperation both in the classroom and during camp trips that begin in Grade 5. “If you want to go fast go alone, but if you want to go far go together,” is a motto that our students learn early from a young age. By learning how to cooperate and achieve more together our students are well prepared for the evolving workplace of the modern world. 
 Support to Help You Reach Your Dreams
 Our school has many support structures in place for all of our students. With two homeroom teachers in every classroom, there is always an instructor available to answer questions and provide extra assistance to any student. There is also a full-time counseling team and senior mentors who help students reach their next life goal after graduation.
