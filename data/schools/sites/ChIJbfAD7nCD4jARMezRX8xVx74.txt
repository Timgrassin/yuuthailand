 Authentic Thai Flower Garland Workshop: An Immersive Cultural Experience for International Seminars | Sriwangying
 In the realm of international seminars and corporate gatherings, offering delegates a memorable and authentic encounter with local culture adds profound value to the overall event. The traditional Thai flower garland, known as Phuang Malai, represents one of the most exquisite expressions of hospitality, respect, and meticulous craft in royal Thai culture. At Sriwangying, we curate specialized Thai Flower Garland workshops tailored specifically for international attendees, expatriates, and foreign delegations. This experience transforms delicate fresh blossoms into a mindful cultural journey, allowing participants to connect deeply with Thai heritage through sight, scent, and focused artistic creation.
