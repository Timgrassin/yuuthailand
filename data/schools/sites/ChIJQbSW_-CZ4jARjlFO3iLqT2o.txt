 DEAR Burma is a school for migrant workers living in Bangkok, Thailand.
 We strengthen democracy in Burma
 Through DEAR Burma, every student is given the knowledge and strength to combat racial and political issues, election fixings and numerous other problems holding back the advancement of their homeland. TACDB, the foundation that runs the school, is an organization in Thailand with a strong mandate to strengthen regional networks and work on Burmese democratic issues.
 We teach migrants
 Mandate: To give every migrant a chance to gain educational skills, confidence and improve their lives linguistically.
 Student Enrollment: Over 1600 per semester.
 Dear Burma Students taught: Over 26,000 since 2002.
 We provide free legal aid
 Migrant workers who have encountered a legal problem can contact the TACDB (Thai Allied Committee with Desegregated Burma Foundation). The official foundation located centrally in Bangkok can help with your troubles.
