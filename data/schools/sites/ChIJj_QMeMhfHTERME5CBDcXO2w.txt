 Payment & service access
 Transparent payments, refundable deposits, and nationwide service.
 We require payment upfront for visa work, but protect clients with a written refundable deposit agreement. You can also work with us in person, via free Bangkok courier, or by secure post from anywhere in Thailand.
 Payment methods & refundable deposit contract
 All payments for our visa services are taken as a refundable deposit under a written agreement that clearly explains the service, timeline, and conditions.
 ✓ We accept PromptPay, cards, Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, LINE Pay, bank apps, and other major payment processors shown below. 
 ✓ If we successfully complete your service, the deposit is retained as agreed in your contract. 
 ✓ If we do not complete the service as agreed, you receive a full refund of your deposit. 
 ✓ Terms vary slightly by service, but the refundable deposit model is documented for every client before work begins.

 Office visits, free Bangkok courier, and nationwide coverage
 You can always visit our Bangkok office to drop off or pick up documents. We also make visa support accessible without a visit whenever possible.
 ✓ Walk-in office visits at our Bang Na Bangkok location during business hours. 
 ✓ Free courier in Bangkok. We can send a courier to pick up documents from your residence and return completed documents to you at no extra charge.
