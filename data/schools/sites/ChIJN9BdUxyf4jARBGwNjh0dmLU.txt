 Experienced Law Firm in Thailand 
 Experienced Legal Advisors from Bangkok since 1998
 Thai advisory practice founded in Bangkok in 1998. The group has expanded with representation in Tokyo, Luang Prabang, Paris, Dublin, Cotonou, Antananarivo, Islamabad and with offices in Shanghai, Yangon, Phuket, Pattaya, HuaHin and Samui. 
 The group represents project sponsors, investors in development, acquisitions, divestitures, multijurisdictional trade and project finance. 
 Clients include private and public companies, government agencies, academic institution, NGO, football clubs and sports federations. 
 The practice focuses on ASEAN, EU and Africa.
