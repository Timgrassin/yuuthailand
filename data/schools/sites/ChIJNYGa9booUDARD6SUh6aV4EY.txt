 welcome to By The Lake 
 STAY IN STYLE
 By The Lake are a collection of two- and three-bedroom luxurious pool villas ideally located just 800 metres from Nai Harn Beach in southern Phuket. Designed and built by a european Company to the highest standards in quality, far above the regular Standard. The highest standards in quality and service are assured all times, while the 24-hour security services provided within the gated community ensures the safety of guests at all times.
Multi-lingual staff can communicate in a number of different languages, namely English, Thai, Danish, Icelandic, Norwegian and Swedish, while the 24-hour security services provided within the gated community ensures the safety of guests at all times.
 PROPERTY HIGHLIGHTS
 Villas are tastefully decorated with Zen décor throughout to give guests a true sense of Southeast Asia. 
Each villa enjoys its own private pool, fully equipped kitchen with western appliances, state-of-the-art home theatre and a spacious rooftop terrace, which boasts an al fresco dining area, sofa seating scattered with an assortment of cushions and magnificent views towards the jungle-clad hills of the surrounding area.
