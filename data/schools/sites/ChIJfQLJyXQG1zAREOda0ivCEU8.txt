 Thai/Akha Cooking class 
 Chef Jaruwan will be your personal Chef and teacher. 
 ​ 
 Your experience begins with a visit to a local market, where you will discover fresh, seasonal ingredients used in Thai cooking. After the market, we head to our home kitchen to begin cooking together. 
 Each class starts with an introduction to Thai and Akha culture, followed by the preparation of a special traditional Akha dish, "Sa-pee-tong" &mdash; a recipe made exclusively by the Akha people and rarely shared outside the community. 
 ​ 
 You will then continue your culinary adventure by preparing four popular Thai dishes, chosen by the class, with hands-on guidance at every step.
