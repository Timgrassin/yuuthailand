 About us 
 JPL Lawyers is a Phuket-based firm representing Thai and international clients in litigation and disputes throughout Thailand. From criminal defense and family matters to civil and commercial conflicts, we take cases of every kind to court — and see them through to judgment.﻿ 
 Because many of our clients are foreign nationals facing an unfamiliar legal system, we explain every document, hearing, and decision in clear English. And we believe good legal advice is honest advice: we tell you where you stand and what to expect — not just what you want to hear. 
 What sets us apart is how we work. Our team pairs specialist civil and criminal litigators with a final quality-control review on every case before it is filed — a second layer of protection on your matter. Your case deserves a team that fights for the best outcome and keeps you informed at every step.
