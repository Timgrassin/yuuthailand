 THE BUAKAW LEGACY
 Buakaw Banchamek, one of the most celebrated Muay Thai fighters in history, founded Buakaw Village Samui to share his passion for authentic Muay Thai with the world. Nestled on the beautiful island of Koh Samui, this training camp offers an immersive experience rooted in discipline, respect, and the true spirit of Muay Thai.
 Whether you are a beginner seeking a new challenge, a fitness enthusiast looking for transformation, or a seasoned fighter wanting to train alongside world-class coaches — Buakaw Village Samui welcomes you.
