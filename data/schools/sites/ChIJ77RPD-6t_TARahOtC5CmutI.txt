 Welcome to 
 Takiab Thai Cooking Class
 Your Gateway to Authentic Thai Cuisine! 
 Sawasdee ka/khrap! Embrace a culinary journey that will tantalize your tastebuds, through the aromatic, vibrant, and delicious world of Thai cuisine. Here at Takiab Thai Cooking Class, we&#8217;re passionate about delivering a truly immersive Thai cooking experience, whether you&#8217;re a seasoned cook or a novice in the culinary arts.
 Thai food is much more than the internationally renowned Pad Thai and Tom Yum Soup. It&#8217;s a harmonious blend of five fundamental taste senses: sweet, sour, salty, bitter, and spicy. Each Thai dish is a testament to this intricate balance, each bite an explosion of flavor. At Takiab Thai Cooking Class, we’ll help you unravel the secrets of Thai cooking, from sourcing the right ingredients to mastering the art of creating harmonious Thai flavors.
 Explore our wide variety of courses, from mastering street food classics, to diving into the rich tapestry of Thai curries, to crafting decadent Thai desserts. You can choose courses based on your cooking level, specific Thai dishes you&#8217;re interested in, or the regional cuisine you&#8217;d like to explore. We also offer specialized classes for vegan and gluten-free Thai cooking.
