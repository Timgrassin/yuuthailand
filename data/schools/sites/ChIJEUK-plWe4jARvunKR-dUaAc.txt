 The learning journey English skills at Amaze
 Phonics is an important starting point at Amaze, helping children understand how sounds blend into words. From there, learning expands naturally.
 📖
 Reading & Phonics
 Sounds become words, then sentences.
 As phonics skills grow, children begin connecting words into sentences using sight words, punctuation, capitalization, and simple grammar patterns. Reading skills develop through guided practice and storytelling.
 ✏️
 Writing & Expression
 Ideas take shape on the page.
 Writing develops alongside reading through picture prompts, structured activities, and independent thinking exercises. Children learn to describe, build sentences, and express ideas clearly.
 💬
 Listening & Speaking
 Conversation, games, and confidence.
 Listening and speaking skills are woven into every class through games, songs, conversations, and age-appropriate topics – such as family, daily routines, colors, shapes, and the world around them.
 Because Amaze is an after-school program, our lessons are designed to feel relaxed and enjoyable. When children smile, laugh, and feel safe to try, they build confidence and perform at their best.

 Course Learning Focus

 Reading Development
