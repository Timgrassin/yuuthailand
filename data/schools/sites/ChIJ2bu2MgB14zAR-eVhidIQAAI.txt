 At River Muay Thai, we offer something truly unique — a peaceful training camp surrounded by nature at 10.000 m² on the riverside, just minutes from the city center of Kanchanaburi. Here, training is personal and focused, with plenty of space to learn, rest, and grow. We combine authentic Muay Thai with a modern, calm and natural setting — creating the perfect balance between intensity and relaxation. Far from the crowds, close to what truly matters.
