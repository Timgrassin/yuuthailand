 What is the Thailand Elite Visa ?
 The Thailand Elite Visa , rebranded as the Thailand Privilege Card in 2023, is a long-stay membership visa issued by Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd., a state enterprise wholly owned by the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT).
 Membership costs a one-time fee of THB 650,000 to THB 5,000,000 and gives a multiple-entry visa for 5 to 20 years , with airport fast-track and concierge services. There is no income, savings or age requirement. Applicants must pass a Thai government background check: Thailand Privilege aims to approve applications within 2 months, and most complete applications are approved in 6 to 8 weeks.

 Thailand Elite Visa (Thailand Privilege Card): Frequently Asked Questions
 What is the Thailand Privilege Card?
 The Thailand Privilege Card, formerly the Thailand Elite Visa, is a government-backed membership program operated by Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd. under the Ministry of Tourism and Sports. It grants a multiple-entry Thai visa valid for 5 to 20 years in exchange for a one-time membership fee of THB 650,000 to THB 5,000,000. The program launched in 2003 and was renamed in October 2023.
 Is the Thailand Elite Visa the same as the Thailand Privilege Card?
 Yes. The Thailand Elite Visa and the Thailand Privilege Card are the same long-term visa program. It launched in 2003 as Thailand Elite and was renamed the Thailand Privilege Card in October 2023. It is operated by Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd. under the Ministry of Tourism and Sports, and many applicants still search for it as the Thailand Elite Visa.
 How much does the Thailand Privilege Card cost in 2026?
 Membership costs a one-time fee of THB 650,000 to THB 5,000,000 depending on tier: Bronze THB 650,000 (5 years), Gold THB 900,000 (5 years), Platinum THB 1,500,000 (10 years), Diamond THB 2,500,000 (15 years) and Reserve THB 5,000,000 (20 years, by invitation only). There is no annual membership fee. The annual Immigration extension fee is THB 1,900. Hawryluk Legal Advisors charges no agent fee on top of the membership price.

 Which Thailand Elite Visa membership is the best value?
 Per day of validity, Bronze is the cheapest membership at about THB 356 a day, followed by Platinum at about THB 411. Gold works out to about THB 493 a day, Diamond THB 457 and Reserve THB 685. For anyone staying longer than five years, Platinum is better value than Gold: it costs THB 600,000 more but covers twice the years, adds 15 Privilege Points a year and allows family members to join.

 Is the Bronze tier still available?
 Bronze, at THB 650,000 for 5 years, is open to applicants until 31 December 2026. It is the lowest-cost entry to the program and includes the visa, 90-day reporting assistance and airport fast-track, but no annual Privilege Points. After that date, Gold at THB 900,000 becomes the entry tier.

 Can my family join the Thailand Elite Visa?
 Yes, on Platinum, Diamond and Reserve. A spouse, children and parents can be added as Next Members. Until 31 December 2026, each Next Member costs THB 750,000 on any of the three tiers. After that date the standard prices apply: THB 1,000,000 on Platinum, THB 1,500,000 on Diamond and THB 2,000,000 on Reserve. Bronze and Gold are individual memberships only.

 Do I need to show income or a bank balance to qualify?
 No. The Thailand Privilege Card has no minimum income, savings, age or employment requirement. Applicants qualify by paying the membership fee and passing the Thai government background check. This differs from the Thai retirement visa, which requires THB 800,000 in a Thai bank account or proof of monthly income every year.
 Can I work in Thailand on the Thailand Elite Visa?
 No. The Thailand Privilege Card is a long-stay visa, not a work permit. Members who plan to work for a Thai company need a work permit and an appropriate visa, such as the Long-Term Resident (LTR) visa. Our lawyers can advise which route fits your situation.
 How long does a Thailand Privilege application take?
 Approval takes 6 to 8 weeks from submission for a complete application. Documents are screened simultaneously by four Thai government agencies: the Immigration Bureau, the Department of Consular Affairs, the Royal Thai Police Foreign Affairs Division and the Central Investigation Bureau. Incomplete files are the most common cause of delay. After approval, the visa must be affixed within 15 working days.

 When do I pay, and is my money safe?
 The membership fee is paid only after the government background check is approved, and it is paid directly to Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd., not to the agent. Once payment is confirmed, the member receives a welcome letter and membership ID, and the visa is affixed within 15 working days.
