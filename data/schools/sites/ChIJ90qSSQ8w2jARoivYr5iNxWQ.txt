 About Us
 Meet Your Instructors, Oun & Kae 
 Sa was dee krub / kha!
 I have learned how to cook several kinds of food with my grandmother, parents and relatives for a long time. I have also traveled all over Thailand to taste plenty of regional food, learning from qualified instructors. I had the opportunity to work with a lot of people who are excellent cooks in large Thai restaurants and overseas.
I, myself, love eating and cooking especially Thai food. We have a great technique to help you to learn local Thai food. We hope that you will enjoy learning and ensure you will not be disappointed after finishing the course. Thank you very much for joining us!

 Why Us
 Why Food Lovers Choose Tom Yum Thai 
 01 Individual Cooking Station
 Everyone has their own dedicated gas burner, wok, and prep station. You cook every dish yourself from start to finish.
 02 Pound Curry Paste from Scratch
 Learn the ancient art of grinding fresh Thai herbs, lemongrass, and galangal using heavy granite mortars and pestles.
 03 Local Morning Market Tour
 Walk through the bustling Chiang Mai morning market. Taste, smell, and identify fresh ingredients before cooking.
 04 Basic Vegetable Carving
 Discover traditional Thai fruit and vegetable carving techniques to garnish and present your dishes like a Thai chef.
 05 Small Intimate Classes (2-10 Pax)
 Never feel crowded. Small groups ensure personalized guidance, friendly banter, and hands-on assistance throughout.
 06 Free Round-Trip Hotel Pickup
 Hassle-free transportation to and from your hotel located within 3 km of the Chiang Mai Old City moat.
 Bring Thai culinary mastery to your home, villa, or hotel
 Private Catering & Hotel Cooking Lessons
 We offer a full range of services to teach how to cook Thai food at home and hotel! Thai dishes will be taught and served directly to your table. Please contact us if you are interested in a private bespoke session.
