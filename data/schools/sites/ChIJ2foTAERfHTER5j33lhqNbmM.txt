 FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)
 What academic programs does the institute offer? 
 Our institute offers a wide range of programs designed to support each child&#8217;s growth and development at every stage of learning, including:
 📚 English
Develops listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills while building confidence in using English through engaging, age-appropriate activities.
 🔤 Phonics
Teaches the relationship between letters and sounds, enabling children to pronounce words accurately, develop spelling skills, and build a strong foundation for reading English with confidence.
 ➕ Mathematics
Enhances analytical thinking, numeracy, and problem-solving skills through interactive, age-appropriate learning activities tailored to each child&#8217;s developmental level.
 🎨 Art
Encourages creativity, fine motor skill development, concentration, imagination, and self-expression through a variety of hands-on artistic activities.
 Could you please provide the tuition fees for your programs? 
 Average tuition fees start from approximately THB 2,800.
 What age groups are eligible to enroll? 
 English Corner welcomes learners from 2.5 years of age to adults.
We offer a comprehensive range of age-appropriate programs tailored to each learner&#8217;s developmental stage and English proficiency. 
Our curriculum is designed to provide an enjoyable, engaging, and progressive learning journey that builds confidence and fosters long-term language success.
 What is the class size? 
 Each small-group class is limited to 4–7 students and is led by one dedicated teacher, 
ensuring individualized support and an engaging learning experience.
 Where are your teachers from? 
 Our teaching team consists of both Thai and international teachers. 
Each teacher is carefully selected and trained to provide high-quality instruction in a supportive and engaging learning environment.
 How many branches do you have? 
 English Corner currently has multiple branches to serve students across various locations. 
Please contact us or visit our Branches page to find the location most convenient for you.
 Do you offer programs for working professionals? 
 Yes. We offer programs designed for working professionals and adult learners. 
Our flexible schedules and practical curriculum help learners improve their English skills with confidence while balancing work and daily life.
 How long does it take to complete the program? 
 The duration of the program varies depending on each student&#8217;s age, learning goals, and English proficiency. 
Our teachers assess each student and recommend the most suitable learning pathway to ensure steady and effective progress.
 How often are classes held, and how long is each class? 
 Classes are held once a week, and each class lasts 2 hours.
 Do you offer one-on-one classes? 
 Yes. We offer both one-on-one and small-group classes. Our team will recommend the most suitable learning format based on each student&#8217;s age, 
learning goals, and English proficiency.
