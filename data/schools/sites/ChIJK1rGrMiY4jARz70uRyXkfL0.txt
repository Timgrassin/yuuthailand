 About
 NNP Group provides consulting, legal, tax, accounting & audit services for foreigners doing business in Thailand.
 NNP Group was founded in the year 2006 by Sur Novel, Pattarin Amorntham and Pattaree Amorntham. 
 NNP Group's partners are former employees of "Big 4" consulting firms including Deloitte, Pricewaterhousecoopers and E&Y.
