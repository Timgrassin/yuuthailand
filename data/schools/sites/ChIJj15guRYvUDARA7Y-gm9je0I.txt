 ﻿Intern﻿ational School of Phuket (ISP) 
 ﻿Educating for a sustainable future 
 One of the few accredited Cambridge International Schools in Phuket and the only one in Rawai, one of the island’s most family-friendly areas. Our school is proud to deliver the academic structure that the Cambridge International accreditation is acclaimed for, providing quality education at an affordable price for children aged 6 to 14 years.

 Balanced Education 
 12+ subjects, including the core subjects English, mathematics, science and PE. There are also plenty of opportunities to develop creativity, expression and personal well-being through subjects like art, music and PSHE. At our school, we believe that a balanced education is key to helping students develop into well-rounded individuals who are prepared to succeed in all aspects of life. 
 Trusted Assessments 
 Throughout the school year, teachers monitor a child's development with progress checks and assessments that were built into the Cambridge programme, which helps teachers understand the child's strengths and areas where they need more help. The school shares information about each child's progress with parents by providing a detailed report at the end of each term.

 What If a child's English level is insufficient for Cambridge class? 
 For learners whose English level is insufficient for Cambridge classes, we designed The ELL (English Language Learner) Program. The primary goal is to integrate children into our English-speaking Cambridge classrooms. The programme develops a solid foundation for further study in English, covering knowledge, skills and understanding in reading, writing, listening and speaking. 
 The ELL program consists of 70% English study in separate classes with students whose level of English is the same and 30% of such subjects as Physical Education, Art and Design, and Music.

 ISP Camp 
 During school holidays, we invite you to join our ISP Camp, a fantastic opportunity for children to make new friends and enjoy an active break. The camp welcomes both ISP students and external children, guided by experienced teachers and providing three balanced meals a day. 
 Each programme includes a variety of activities such as sports, swimming classes, field trips, quizzes, games, arts and crafts, and eco-friendly projects, ensuring a fun and enriching experience for all participants.
