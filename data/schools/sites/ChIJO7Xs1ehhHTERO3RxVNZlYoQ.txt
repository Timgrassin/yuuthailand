 THANEE ESTATE & LAW is a distinguished law firm headquartered in the heart of Bangkok , with additional service offices at Somerset in Thonglor (Sukhumvit Soi 55) and in Town in Town, Soi Lat Phrao 94 (Bamboo Whisper Garden). 
 We provide comprehensive legal services across both residential and commercial sectors, serving local clients, international investors, and corporate entities seeking reliable and strategic legal support in Thailand . 
 We are highly experienced in Real Estate Law , with a strong track record in handling complex and high value transactions, including hotels, resorts, and large scale property developments. Our team has successfully advised on numerous major real estate deals, offering practical, results driven legal solutions. 
 In addition, our core practice areas include Real Estate Law , Corporate Law, Commercial Law, and Migration Law. We also act as licensed Notarial Services Attorneys (Notary Public) in Thailand under the Lawyers Council of Thailand, fully authorized to notarize documents for use in Thailand and overseas. 
 With deep industry knowledge and extensive hands on experience, we deliver clear, compliant, and commercially sound legal advice to protect our clients' interests at every stage.
