The Thailand operation has been active since 2018, with the main head office at Phrom Phong, Sukhumvit Soi 39, Bangkok, and a team of 70+ staff across 12 locations in Thailand.
 Is UnionSPACE an AMLO compliant company?
 All transactions are subject to Thailand's Anti-Money Laundering Office (AMLO) standards review and we reserve the right to decline clients who are unable or refuse to comply with AMLO requirements.
