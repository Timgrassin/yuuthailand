 Your coaches
 The camp team includes a Muay Thai world champion.
 Dimitri
 WMO and WPMF Muay Thai world champion
 Muay Thai
 Bio Dimitri Cerberes is a former WMO and WPMF world champion and French FMDA champion who has fought at Thai Fight, MAX, Kunlun and Prison Fight: about 50 amateur and semi-pro fights, and some 70 as a pro. He holds France’s BPJEPS in combat sports and the USMTA instructor certification, and has worked fight camps for Alistair Overeem, Sergei Kharitonov and Mark Godbeer, among other UFC, Bellator, ONE and Karate Combat fighters.

 Victor
 119-79-1 · former GLORY No. 3 in the world
 Bio Victor Pinto fought at Lumpinee Stadium (30+ fights), on Thai Fight (15+), at ONE Championship and at GLORY Kickboxing, where he was ranked No. 3 in the world.  FAQ
 Can’t find your answer? Ask the team on WhatsApp.
 Chat on WhatsApp 
 Which day can I arrive? Any day you like. Your stay starts the day you arrive.
 How do I get from the airport to the camp? A private driver picks you up at Suvarnabhumi Airport in Bangkok and drives you to the camp. The return trip is included too.
 I’m a complete beginner. Is this for me? Yes. Classes are open to every level, from first-timers to experienced fighters.
 Can I fight during my stay? Yes, if you want to. Once you’re ready, your coaches give you the green light and the team books your fight at a local stadium in Hua Hin. It’s included in the price of your stay.
 How many spots are there? The camp has just eight bungalows, so spots are limited. Message us on WhatsApp to see which dates are still free.
 Can I bring a partner or a friend? Message us on WhatsApp: the team will help with stays for couples, friends and under-18s.
 Can I just come to train? Yes, if you already have somewhere to stay. Training only is 9,900 THB before tax for 30 days. See training only 
 How do I book? Tap “Choose your package”, pick a package and how long you’re staying, then book online. For Luxury, message us on WhatsApp.
