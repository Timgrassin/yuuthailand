 Bukit Thai Cooking Class
 1 Person : 1 Cooking Station 
 Every guest gets their own wok, burner, and workspace, so you cook every step yourself. 
 Hands-On & Easy to Learn
 No prior cooking skills needed. Learn essential Thai cooking techniques step-by-step with our friendly guide. 
 Small Group Cooking Class 
 Max 10 guests per session. Personal attention and every dish gets your full hands-on effort. 
 Thai food is more than cultural heritage. It is how we live, and who we are. In every Thai home, cooking is how we look after one another. It is the language we use to say love without saying it.
 At Bukit Thai Cooking Class we do not simply teach recipes. We bring you inside traditions that have been passed down for generations. You will come to know the herbs and spices that shaped the flavours of this country, understand why each one belongs where it does, and make every dish yourself from beginning to end.
 All of it happens in the warmth of a real Thai home, on a hillside with the nature of Phuket in front of you. Not a classroom, but a kitchen where you are part of the family.
 Every dish you make here is a way of understanding Thai life with your own hands.

 FAQS
 Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers! 
 Can I join if I have no prior cooking experience? 
 Absolutely! Our classes are designed for all skill levels, with step-by-step guidance from our guides.
 Do we get to cook the dishes ourselves? 
 Yes, each participant has their own cooking station. However, young children who still need assistance will cook
together with their parents or guardians.
 Can children cook with the adults? 
 Yes, children more than 4 years old are welcome. If they’re too young to cook alone, they can cook together with their parents. If they are old enough, they will have their own cooking station. 
 What if I have a food allergy? 
 Our dishes may include peanuts, shrimp, chicken, and coconut milk. Please inform us of any allergies before the day of
your tour so we can adjust the menu.
 Is vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free food available? 
 Yes! We can prepare vegetarian or vegan dishes without meat. Please inform us in advance.
 Is there Halal food option? 
 Yes, Halal food is available. Please let us know in advance.
 Can we change the protein in the dishes? 
 Yes, seafood can be replaced with chicken or skipped entirely for vegetarian/vegan options.
 Can I adjust the spice level of my dishes? 
 Yes, you can tell the guide your preferred spice level or adjust it yourself while cooking.
 Is hotel pick-up and drop-off included? 
 Yes, round-trip transportation is included from most hotels in Phuket.
