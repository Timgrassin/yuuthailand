 What Is the “ฯ” Symbol in Thai and How to Use It?
 กรุงเทพฯ อุบลฯ นายกฯ You’ll see the symbol “ฯ” very often in written Thai but many learners don’t actually know what it means or how to use it. What Is the "ฯ" Symbol in Thai? The marker ฯ is called ไปยาลน้อย (bpai yaan nói). It’s a punctuation mark in Thai used to shorten words, especially proper names or familiar expressions. How does "ฯ" work?  What courses do you offer?
 We offer Speaking Thai, Writing Thai , Specialized lessons for intermediate/advanced learners, Kids & Teens programs, 1-on-1 private sessions, and short workshops. 
 I’m a complete beginner. Which course should I start with?
 If you have 0 background , we recommend: 
 Speaking Thai Level 1 (Live session) — best for structure and teacher support

 All classes follow Bangkok time (GMT+7) . We teach students worldwide and have flexible options for different time zones.
