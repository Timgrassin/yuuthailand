 Common questions
 Straight Answers
 Message WhatsApp or use the contact page with your dates and experience if you need more.
 Can beginners train at Cobra? Yes. Plenty of people start with no Muay Thai experience. Book a group class, tell the coach your level, and the session is scaled to you. Sparring is not forced.
 Is there a separate fitness class? No. Fitness and strength are built into every Muay Thai class. Sessions suit people training for fitness as well as people improving their Muay Thai.
 What should I bring? Light training clothes, water, and a towel. Ask on WhatsApp before you arrive if you need gloves, wraps, or rental details.
 Can I book online? Online booking and card payment are being wired up. For now WhatsApp or the contact form is the fastest way to lock in a class, pass, private session, or stay-and-train plan. Booking notices go to the Cobra team email.
 Do beginners have to spar? No. Tell the coach if you are new. First sessions are stance, movement, basic strikes, pads, bags, and habits. Sparring is optional and coach-led.
 What is a training camp versus a holiday fighting program? Training camp is planned before you arrive. We ask about goals, experience, and the training you want so a plan is ready when you get here. A holiday fighting program is different. It is set up after you start training, once coaches have assessed your skill, attitude, and training ethic.
 How does stay and train work? The gym sits inside Chalong Chalet Resort, so rooms and the pool sit next to training. Send your dates. We help check rooms and a training plan. Confirm rates before you book flights.
 Where is Cobra Muay Thai? Near Kata Beach, Phuket, in Chalong, inside Chalong Chalet Resort and Longstay. Phone +66 82 367 6424. Use the Google Maps link for the entrance and parking.
