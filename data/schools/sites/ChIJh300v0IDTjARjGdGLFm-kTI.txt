 Lanta International Language School has a holistic and unique way of teaching Thai and provide top quality Thai language courses. Before opening our courses to the public, we spent thousands of hours planning, testing, analysing, and improving teaching methods and curriculums so that we were sure that our students would get the best experience possible. 
 We are located on the beautiful island of Koh Lanta in Krabi Province. You can learn the Thai language while enjoying beach life, diving and other awesome activities in a relaxed atmosphere. 
 ​We have three types of courses for you to choose from, Intensive Course, Standard Course and Family Course. All our courses are taught in smaller groups so we can give all student the help they need. We do not offer private classes as our teaching style works best in groups.
