 Sirada the Language and Computer School 
We are located in the heart of Hua Hin, less than 5 minutes&#8217; walk from Hua Hin Municipality.
Courses at Sirada 
 Thai language :::
 Our Thai language curriculum has been approved by the Thailand Ministry of Education.
 All four language skills are taught: speaking, listening, reading and writing. Three levels are offered: Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced.
 Our Thai language teachers are graduates of well-known Thai universities, the class language is Thai and English.
 * We are offering group classes, however private classes are available at times agreeable between you and the school
 English language   :::
 We will introduce students to the English conversation.
Our teachers are Native speaker and also Thai teacher.
We have designed our lessons to be interactive, and stimulating to help all students ever the very beginners.
 We aim to introduce students to the basics of
 The Fundamentals of Computers and
 Microsoft Word
 Microsoft Excel
