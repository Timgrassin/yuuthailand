 About J&A International Law 
 A regional bilingual law firm for business, investment, and disputes in Thailand.
 J&A International Law is an independent regional law firm with its office in Bangkok, advising foreign investors, international companies, Thai businesses, and private clients on matters that require legal analysis in both Thai and English. The firm combines regulatory, corporate, real-estate, private-client, and dispute-resolution work under one accountable professional structure.
