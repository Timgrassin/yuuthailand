 Join Us at TTC Spa School: Learn Thai Massage in the Heart of Bangkok
 Welcome to TTC Spa School! We’re grateful that you’re considering us for your education in Thai Traditional Massage. Our school is recognized by the Thai Government and international professional bodies, which reflects our commitment to providing quality training. Our experienced instructors are here to guide you through a blend of traditional techniques and modern practices in a supportive environment. We aim to equip you with valuable skills and a deeper understanding of Thai healing traditions, helping you on your journey toward a fulfilling career in the wellness industry. We look forward to welcoming you!
 School Location
 TTC Spa School, located within TTC Massage in Bangkok, offers students a dedicated learning space on the third floor, separate from the massage shop below. Students often enjoy taking advantage of the opportunity to enjoy a massage after class, providing a chance to unwind in a professional environment
