 About 
 Welcome to 
LookNongSaeng Muaythai 
 Looknongsaeng Muaythai was established in 2003 in Kalasin, a northeastern province of Thailand. In 2021, the gym began a partnership with Muaythai Numponthep to continue the legacy of both gyms in Samut Prakarn, at the southern edge of Bangkok. 
​
 We offer authentic Muay Thai training for all levels and abilities.
