 EXPAT CARE﻿
COVERAGE FOR PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS
 The Expat Care  Plan from Pacific Cross is a health insurance policy tailored specifically for expatriates living in Thailand. This plan offers comprehensive medical coverage, including inpatient and outpatient services, emergency medical evacuation, and  coverage for pre-existing conditions.  There are different levels of coverage, allowing policyholders to choose the plan that best suits their needs. The plan also includes options for worldwide coverage, making it ideal for those who travel frequently. Coverage is available up to 99 years of age, ensuring long-term security. 
 LIFE CARE 360﻿
﻿INTERNATIONAL PLAN
 The LIFE CARE 360  plan from Pacific Cross International is a comprehensive health insurance solution tailored for individuals living in Asia. It offers extensive coverage, including inpatient and outpatient care, emergency services, and additional benefits such as maternity, dental, and vision coverage. 
 A significant advantage of the LIFE CARE 360 plan is its  eligibility for individuals up to 90 years of age , ensuring long-term health coverage for older adults. This makes it a robust option for those seeking comprehensive health protection as they age.
