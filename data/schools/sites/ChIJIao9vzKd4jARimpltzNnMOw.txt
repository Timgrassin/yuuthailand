 Our Story of Culinary Heritage
 Food has always been at the heart of our family. My love for Thai cooking began in my grandmother’s kitchen, where I learned that every dish is more than a recipe — it’s a way to bring people together.
 I later studied Thai desserts at Dusit Thani College, and with Chef Ann by my side, we created Homies Thai Cooking School: a cozy space where flavors, culture, and friendship come alive.
 "Every dish tells a story. Every ingredient has a purpose. When you cook with love and tradition, you&#x27;re not just making food – you&#x27;re creating memories that nourish the soul." — Koch, Homies Thai Cooking Class Founder / Traditional Cook. At Homies, your journey goes beyond cooking. From choosing fresh herbs at the local market to sharing a home-style meal around the table, every step is about connection, discovery, and joy.
 Culinary Heritage
 Generations of authentic Thai flavors carried forward with care, tradition, and respect for every ingredient.
 Organic and Market Fresh
 Each class begins with a trip to the local market, where you’ll handpick the freshest herbs, spices, and vegetables that bring Thai recipes to life.
 Cultural Immersion
 From tuk-tuk rides to hands-on cooking, you’ll discover Thai culture through flavors, stories, and the joy of sharing a meal together.
 Our Cooking Classes
 Choose from our carefully crafted cooking experiences, each designed to immerse you in the rich traditions of Thai cuisine while creating unforgettable memories.
