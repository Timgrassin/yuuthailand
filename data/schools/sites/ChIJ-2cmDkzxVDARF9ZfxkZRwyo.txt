 Frequently asked questions
 Quick answers about visas on Koh Samui.
 Where can I do my visa in Koh Samui? Official visa procedures (extensions, 90-day reports, re-entry permits) are processed at the Samui Immigration Office in Maenam, which serves Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao. For preparing your file and avoiding a second trip, Visa Koh Samui (visakohsamui.com) is a private trilingual assistance service based on the island — answers the same day on WhatsApp +66 82 819 4733.
 Do you display your prices? No. Each file is assessed individually, then a tailored proposal is sent privately on WhatsApp. Only official government fees (like the 1,900 THB extension fee) are mentioned on this site, because they are public and paid at Immigration.
 Which islands do you cover? Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao. All three depend on the Samui Immigration Office in Maenam, and we prepare files remotely on WhatsApp before your trip.
 Which visa categories can you help with? Tourist visa extensions, the DTV (Destination Thailand Visa), retirement visas (Non-O, O-A, O-X), marriage and family visas, visa runs, 90-day reports and re-entry permits.
 Can I stay in Thailand without a visa? Since 15 September 2026, citizens of 60 countries receive a 30-day visa-exempt entry (reduced from the previous 60 days), extendable once by 30 days at Immigration — 60 days maximum in total. Land-border entries are capped at 2 per year for most nationalities. We confirm what applies to your nationality before you commit to anything.
