 Committed to Excellence.
 Singha Legal Law Firm thrives to provide a comprehensive service to both local and international clients in Thailand. We have branches located in Bangkok and Phuket for your convenience. Singha Legal Law Firm was founded by lawyer LK, a highly respected attorney with a degree in International Business Management and a proven track record of success for over 18 years. We are experts in Thai law and international law.
Singha Legal Law firm is in an exclusive joint venture agreement with KSCGP JURIS LLP , a leading Singapore law firm with over 30 years of experience in international arbitration, cross-border legal services, and common law expertise. We bring together the strenghts of both firms to deliver the best legal solutions for our clients.
As forward-thinking lawyers, we are strategic partners committed to our client’s success, combining legal expertise with business acumen to deliver optimal solutions.
Where legal prowess meets business wisdom, we are your partner in growth.
