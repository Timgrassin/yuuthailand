 My Gym Patong isn’t just a gym — it’s a full-scale fight and fitness facility for those who take training seriously. With over 1,000 square meters of open space in the heart of Patong, we offer elite coaching in Muay Thai, MMA, Boxing, BJJ, Strength & Conditioning, and recovery — all under one roof.
 Whether you&#8217;re new to training or preparing for your next fight, you&#8217;ll find world-class instruction and a supportive international community.
 With daily group classes, 1-on-1 sessions, and a serious training culture, we’re not just building bodies — we’re building warriors.

 Unlimited 
 Classes 
 At My Gym Patong, we offer specialized programs to meet every level and goal. Our Kids’ Muay Thai classes focus on building discipline, focus, and confidence in a fun, safe, and structured environment — perfect for children aged 6 and up. For women, our Muay Thai program provides a supportive space to develop real skills, whether you&#8217;re training for fitness, self-defense, or competition.
 If you want focused results, our personal training sessions offer one-on-one coaching in Muay Thai, strength, or conditioning — fully tailored to your goals. And for those preparing for the next level, our Fight Camp Prep delivers serious athlete conditioning, technical refinement, and hard sparring, designed to get you ready for the ring.
