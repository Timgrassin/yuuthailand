 WHITE SHARK Chalamkhow   
 MUAY THAI GYM & TRAINING CAMP 
 Klong Muang, Krabi 
 No booking required for Group Training Classes show up and join in! 
 Session times: 8:00 to 10:00 am & 5:00 to 7:00 pm MON to SAT 
 400 THB per session (2 hours) 
 ( Shuttle service available ask for details) 
 Chalamkhow Muay Thai is a pr ofessional Muay Thai gym and training camp where you can learn traditional Thai boxing. The gym is owned by the South Thailand Champion, Dam Chalamkhow, aka White Shark . 
 The gym is located in Klong Muang in Krabi, only 12’ away from the beach, what makes it  the ideal place to enjoy both your training and holidays at the same time.  
 Whether you are a beginner, an intermediate or professional fighter, our trainers will customise each training to fit your skills and get the most out of every session.
