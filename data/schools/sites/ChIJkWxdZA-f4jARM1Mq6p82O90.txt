 GOOD TO KNOW
 A few things before
we get cooking.
 Which cooking class should I choose? Choose Express for three dishes in two hours, or Full for five dishes in three hours, including your choice of Tom Yum Goong or Tom Kha Gai and fire-wok morning glory with turmeric rice. Both include a market visit, a coconut demonstration with optional hands-on participation, and one dessert per guest chosen on the day: Banana Roti or Mango Sticky Rice.
 Can I visit the market in the afternoon? Yes. Full afternoon sessions include an extended guided market tour. Full morning and noon sessions also include the tour; Express morning sessions include a brief visit. The local market is a 2-minute walk from Mango, opposite Big C On Nut. Check the operating days for your selected class.
 Where is Mango Cooking School? At 235/12 On Nut 3 Alley in Bangkok, opposite Big C On Nut and around a 10-minute walk from BTS On Nut. Our location page includes the Google map, walking directions and a photo of the entrance.
 Which class includes Banana Roti? Both the 2-Hour Express and 3-Hour Full classes offer Banana Roti or Mango Sticky Rice. Each guest chooses one dessert on the day. Guests choosing Banana Roti make it hands-on.
 Can I join as a vegetarian? Yes. Advance notice is recommended, but you can also ask on arrival and we will switch to vegetarian ingredients. Always reconfirm with your chef before the class starts. Vegan and allergy needs require a separate suitability check.
 Can children join a cooking class? Children aged five and above can join with a booked, paying adult guardian. Under-fives cannot attend, and unbooked companions cannot observe or wait inside. Adults and children pay the same current per-person price.
 Where can I see prices and offers? The 2-hour Express is THB 750 per person and the 3-hour Full is THB 900 per person. Adults and children pay the same price. These prices include a 25% discount from the regular prices of THB 1,000 and THB 1,200 respectively. SNS codes are not yet active. Online booking and payment are being prepared.
 What are the cancellation terms? For cooking-class bookings made directly on this website. Cancellation deadlines are measured from your scheduled class start time, in Bangkok time (UTC+7). 48 hours or more before the class starts: 100% refund. 24 hours or more, but less than 48 hours before the class starts: 50% refund. Less than 24 hours before the class starts: no refund. Cancelling the day before does not qualify for a refund if fewer than 24 hours remain before your class starts. For bookings made through Klook, GetYourGuide or another platform, the cancellation terms of that booking apply.
 Can I book a private class? Yes. Please email mangocookingschool@gmail.com in advance with your group size and preferred date. We will provide a quote based on the number of participants.
 Do I need cooking experience? The class is guided. Choose Express for three dishes in two hours, or Full for five dishes, a choice of soup, fire-wok morning glory with turmeric rice and more market time. Both offer Banana Roti or Mango Sticky Rice. Ask about any support you need before booking.
