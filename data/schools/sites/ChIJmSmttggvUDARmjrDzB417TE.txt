 About Us
 We have a lot of experience in teaching for over 30 years.
 EDUCATION VISA Thailand | ED visa | Student visa
 ED Visa is available for students if you would like to enroll in our program and obtain a Non-Immigrant ED visa. The process is very easy. If you choose the Thai course, we have experienced teachers who teach the Thai Language to foreigners for over 30 years. Our Thai language system is an interactive system which students will be able to communicate with Thai people in a short time. By enrolling in Chalong Language School, you will study from speaking to reading & writing up to advanced courses as well. You would enjoy your stay in Thailand much more.
