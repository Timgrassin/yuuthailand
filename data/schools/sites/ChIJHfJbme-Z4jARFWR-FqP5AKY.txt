 Top 5 Tom Yum Goong Tips
 Level up your Tom Yum Goong with these top 5 cooking tips from our local Thai expert chef 🌟
1. Smash lemongrass before cooking to release oils 
2. The smaller your chili is cut, the spicier it gets 🔥
3. Shallots! Not onions 🚫🧅
4. Tear, not cut the kaffir lime leaves / bergamot leaves
5. Evaporated milk & lime goes last
