 HALIO - Hua Hin Alternative Learning International Organization 
 ศูนย์การเรียนหัวหิน เพื่อพัฒนาศักยภาพมนุษย์ ภาษา และทักษะสากล 
 ​Halio is a small and nurturing , community-based school 
 where children develop self-awareness, self-discipline, 
 and a sense of ownership over their learning. 
 Our progressive Kindergarten and Primary School programs 
 offer personalized learning pathways for children between the ages of 3 and 12. 
 Our mission is to give children the space and freedom in a stimulating 
 and safe environment to develop their skills and talents and 
 become leaders in their own education . 
 Becoming socially aware, self-directed, and personally responsible, 
 they train for the evolving demands of a rapidly changing future.
