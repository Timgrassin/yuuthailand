 Welcome to Everyday
Thai Language School 
 Everyday
Thai language school ( โรงเรียนสอนภาษาไทยสุภาสอน )
located in Bangkok offer Thai language
lessons for group as well as private Thai language lessons. 
 At Everyday Thai language
school, the curriculum has been designed to meet the
everyday needs of foreigners living in Bangkok with an emphasis on
conversation. 
 Our Thai language courses
are taught by
experienced Thai teachers. Our group classes are relatively small
(3-10 students) compared to other Thai language schools in Bangkok.
This
choice has
been made in order to ensure that all students develop effective
communication skills. 
 Our tuition
fees are
affordable compared to other Thai language schools and are also
inclusive of learning materials. 
 Everyday Thai language
school is conveniently located in the heart of Bangkok near BTS Chong
Nonsi. 
 Learn Thai now!
 Please make an appointment
before visiting the school. No
walk in, appointment only . 
 Please note that the
school does
not offer any ED visa program at the moment. (We will
remove
this notice once Thai language courses with ED visa become available
once again.)
