 Mount to Mount 
 Homestead and Garden 
 "Aonang" / Krabi  
 Welcome   to a peaceful mountain retreat, where every glance reveals the beauty of nature’s design. Our gardens, carefully tended and organic, are a living canvas of vegetables and edible flowers that grow in harmony with the land. Imagine wandering through rows of fresh, vibrant greens and splashes of colorful blooms, all waiting to delight your senses. Here, nature invites you to relax, breathe deeply, and savor the simple joys of sustainable living. We invite you to experience a place where tranquility and nature blend seamlessly into an unforgettable escape. 
 "Travel better—leave a positive impact through sustainability, local support, and nature-friendly practices." 
 Our Inspiration   comes from the belief that travel should enrich both the traveler and the destination. That’s why we’re committed to promoting eco-friendly practices and sustainable living at every step of the journey. From reducing waste through 'Reduce, Reuse, Recycle' and composting food scraps, to choosing biodegradable products, upcycling, and supporting local farmers markets—we aim to minimize environmental impact and preserve the natural beauty of the places we explore. Whether it's planting a tree, growing a garden, or planning zero-waste meals, every mindful action matters—and together, we can leave the world better than we found it.
