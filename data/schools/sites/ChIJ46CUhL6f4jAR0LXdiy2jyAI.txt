 FAQ
 Before you contact us
 Is the firm licensed to practise in Thailand? Yes. Our Thai lawyers hold the Thai law licence (Lawyers Council of Thailand) and represent our clients before the Thai courts. They work alongside several international foreign legal consultants, under the direction of the firm's three founding partners.
 I am moving to Thailand: where will I pay tax? It depends on your tax residency (in Thailand, notably linked to spending 180 days or more in the country in a year), the nature of your income and any applicable double tax treaty. Since 2024, foreign-sourced income remitted to Thailand is also taxable. Our tax department reviews your situation with you during a free first consultation.
 Do you work in English? Yes. You deal with the firm in English, with an English-speaking contact. Your documents are drafted in English or translated into English, and contracts and official documents are prepared in bilingual Thai–English versions. French is also available.
 How are your fees set? We only work under a written fee agreement : you know all the prices before we start.
 Fixed fees , no hourly rates
 No surprises from uncontrolled costs
 Staged payments , as your matter progresses
 Free first consultation in Thailand
 In France, the first consultation with our partner firm is free of charge 
 Do you have partners outside Thailand? Yes. We work with a partner firm in France (Paris and Nice) and with partner firms in Hong Kong and Singapore for corporate services. Depending on our clients' needs, we also work periodically with partner firms in Italy (Rome and Milan), Germany, the United States, Cambodia, Vietnam and the Philippines.
 Do you handle matters outside Bangkok? Yes. The firm has three offices in Bangkok and branches in Phuket, Pattaya, Koh Samui and Koh Phangan. We work throughout Thailand: Chiang Mai, Hua Hin, Krabi, Khon Kaen, Udon Thani, Hat Yai and beyond.
 Can you help me from abroad? Yes. Everything, including signatures, can be handled remotely through secure platforms. The first consultation takes place by video call or phone.
