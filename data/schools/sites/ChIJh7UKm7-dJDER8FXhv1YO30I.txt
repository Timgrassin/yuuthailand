 Volunteer in Thailand with Volunt2Thai – Experience Real Thai Village Life 
 Volunt2Thai is a non-profit community project in rural Northeast Thailand that has been supporting local villages, schools, and disadvantaged children for more than 10 years. 
In many rural areas of Northeast Thailand, families face serious economic challenges. Parents often need to leave their villages for long periods to work in larger Thai cities or abroad, while children stay behind with grandparents or relatives. Many children grow up with limited educational opportunities and little emotional support while also helping care for younger siblings and elderly family members. 
Local schools in the region often face a shortage of teaching materials, educational resources, and trained English teachers. Because there is very little tourism, few international businesses, and almost no contact with foreigners in our area, students have very limited opportunities to practice English with native or international speakers — except through interaction with our project participants. 
Volunt2Thai works closely with local schools and communities to support education, cultural exchange, and sustainable village development. Project participants help teach English at the V2T Campus and nearby village schools, creating valuable learning experiences and helping children build confidence for their future. 
 More Than Volunteering - a Real Cultural Experience 
 Even though our campus is a modern and comfortable place that offers many amenities, Volunt2Thai is not entertainment tourism or a commercial volunteer business. Life in rural Thailand can be challenging, and many local families face real social and economic problems every day. 
The project encourages participants to open their eyes, learn from a completely different way of life, and better understand the realities of rural communities in Thailand. Before trying to change the world, participants are invited to first listen, observe, learn, and build genuine human connections. 
At Volunt2Thai, cultural exchange and mutual learning are at the heart of the experience. Even small contributions can make a meaningful difference — whether through teaching English, sharing skills, supporting activities, or simply spending time with children and local families. 
 Meaningful Opportunities in Rural Thailand 
 At Volunt2Thai, project participants are encouraged to bring in their own skills, ideas, talents, and life experiences. The project management supports participants in developing meaningful activities based on what they can genuinely contribute to the local community. 
 Rather than following a fixed volunteer program, Volunt2Thai grows through the creativity and individual strengths of its participants. Real life experience, cultural exchange, and mutual learning are at the center of the project. Whether you are experienced in education, sports, music, gardening, wellness, cooking, or simply enjoy working with children and local families, your contribution can become part of the community experience. 
 Volunt2Thai has the facilities and offers a wide range of opportunities and activities based on the interests, skills, and experience that participants bring with them. These may include: 
 English and language teaching at local schools (with Interpreter) 
 Teaching practice for TEFL, TESOL, and CELTA participants 
 Sports and outdoor activities 
 Music and creative learning 
 Yoga, wellness, and fitness activities 
 Support for elderly people and physiotherapy 
 Organic gardening and food forest projects 
 Language and cultural exchange, including Thai language learning 
 Baking bread and preparing meals with homegrown vegetables 
 Community development projects in rural villages 
 Whether you are a student, graduate, teacher, retiree, family, veteran, gap year traveler, or a first-time participant, the project offers a unique opportunity to experience authentic Thai village life while contributing in a meaningful and ethical way.

 Introduction to V2T Campus 
 In the serene landscapes of North-East Thailand, the V2T Campus emerges as a hub of learning, cultural exchange, and community support. The campus is equipped with a variety of outdoor amenities that provide an enriching experience for volunteers. 
It includes comfortable bungalows, a family room with veranda upstairs, seminar corners, and a modern communal outdoor kitchen. A lush food forest with an herb garden and a gym for physical well-being enhances the overall well-being of volunteers. This unique facility, integrated into a traditional Thai farm, is surrounded by lush rice fields, stables, and fish ponds, nestled amidst little farms and small villages.

 The Campus, the Clubhouse - Language Café and More 
 The Volunt2Thai Clubhouse – Language Café is part of the Volunt2Thai Volunteer Campus – a place where volunteers, children, and villagers come together to learn, create, and share experiences. Comfortable sofas invite you to read or relax. Laptops and a large smart TV offer inspiring opportunities in the multimedia room – perfect for language lessons, computer training, presentations, or cozy movie nights. 
 The space was also designed as a demo kitchen, where we enjoy creating and baking a variety of homemade breads together.
Upstairs, you’ll find the Tree House Terrace – an open-air area surrounded by nature. Whether you’re working on your laptop, practicing yoga, or taking part in small community events on the stage, this space encourages creativity, exchange, and connection. More than just a place for baking – this is where cultures meet, friendships grow, and meaningful community projects come to life. 
 Diverse Facilities for Various Needs 
 Volunteers can also make use of diverse facilities to cater to their specific needs. The Family Room with a Veranda provides a comfortable and private space for families, while Seminar Corners offer dedicated areas for focused learning or discussions. Our Equipment Room is stocked with tools for various activities. In addition, a Thai-style but modern and clean sanitary area ensures that the well-being of the volunteers has top priority. 
 There are also sports facilities on campus 
 Types of Volunteers Welcomed, Volunt2Thai embraces diversity, welcoming a broad range of volunteers, including students, professionals, retirees, veterans, and families with teenagers and children. The program is designed to accommodate different life stages and experiences.
 Benefits of Volunteering 
 Volunteering at V2T Campus is an enriching experience, offering personal and professional growth. It's an ideal opportunity for recent graduates, TEFL, TESOL, or CELTA certificate holders, aspiring teachers, retirees, veterans, and families with teenagers and children. Participants gain firsthand experience of village life, immerse themselves in Thai culture, and learn about Buddhism. This unparalleled experience allows volunteers to meaningfully contribute to the community by teaching English and sharing their talents with children and adults alike. 
 Volunt2Thai Program Overview 
 As a volunteer, you become an integral part of the village's daily life. Your involvement can range from teaching in local schools to building infrastructure and supporting social projects. The program offers a comprehensive cultural exchange, allowing volunteers to work closely with villagers, participate in Buddhist ceremonies, and learn more about Thai traditions. Managed by V2T in collaboration with local authorities, Volunt2Thai is a testament to the power of community-centric projects.
 In addition to the wide range of activities available for volunteers, the most important task of Volunt2Thai is to provide underprivileged children with an opportunity for education. We achieve this by sending volunteers to the schools we partner with. Volunt2Thai, celebrating a decade of dedication to children's education and village development, offers a unique opportunity for volunteers of all backgrounds and life stages to learn, contribute, and grow. It’s more than just a program; it’s a journey into the heart of Thai culture, an avenue for impactful community service, and a platform for creating beautiful memories. Volunteers leave with not only skills and experiences but also the satisfaction of having made a significant difference in the lives of many.

 Working the whole week with the children is a very fulfilling task but also a challenge it can be tough and you need a break. As the working week runs from Monday to Friday, Saturday and Sunday is considered the weekend for volunteers. During the weekends, volunteers have spare time and usually just relax in the village or take the opportunity to explore other parts of northeast of Thailand.
Nevertheless, there are a lot of things to do in Udon Thani and some appealing tourist attractions to view, volunteers can book weekend trips for themselves. Besides this being that you are in a rural village, which offers many special events and activities you never know what will pop-up. There is no set schedule here and the villagers do not have the same concept of a weekend here as in the West. There may be nothing to do Saturday morning, but by the afternoon your day may fill up.  The Organizers of V2T
 The project and organization were founded in 2013 by Raimund Wagner and his family. Since then, many kind-hearted people have joined in and helped shape it into the special initiative it is today. Together, we are a cheerful and dedicated community where everyone can help make the world a little better – each in their own unique way. And the best part: helping others can be a lot of fun! 
 Raimund Wagner
 President, Initiator & Founder
 Raimund is the founder and project manager of Volunt2Thai. He learned project management from scratch and has decades of experience in Asia and other parts of the world. 
 He speaks Thai and other foreign languages, he takes care of communication and administration. 
 He is a person who brings about events and results, he loves new projects especially when project participants bring special skills and talents which are then turned into projects. 
 Linly Wagner
 Coordinator, Organizier
 Linly is a passionate mother, she herself comes from a big Thai family. Professionally, she is a trained tourism specialist, she takes care of the volunteers on campus that they have everything they need. 
 In parallel and seasonally she opens the small store and coffee shop in front of the campus. If there is time on weekends, she also organizes excursions for the volunteers.

Volunt2Thai vrijwilligerswerk buitenland Das Volunt2Thai-Freiwilligenprojekt ist ein Dorfentwicklungsprojekt, das sich auf benachteiligte Kinder konzentriert. Denn in Thailand, insbesondere im Nordosten Thailands, sind die Menschen sehr arm. Einzelne Familienmitglieder sind oft gezwungen, ihre Kinder und älteren Menschen für längere Zeit zurückzulassen, um in städtische Zentren oder ins Ausland zu reisen und dort zu arbeiten. Die Situation ist sehr schlecht für die Entwicklung der Kinder, die ohne ihre Eltern zurückgelassen werden. Die Kinder müssen sich um ihre Geschwister und auch um die alten Menschen kümmern. Das Schulsystem ist kaum entwickelt, den Schulen fehlt es an Material und Lehrern. Deshalb ist es die wichtigste Aufgabe von Volunt2Thai, diesen unterprivilegierten Kindern eine Chance auf Bildung zu geben. Wir tun dies über Freiwillige, die wir in die Schulen schicken, mit denen wir zusammenarbeiten. Im V2T Campus & Cultural Centre in Thailand heißen wir Freiwillige oder Praktikanten/Projektteilnehmer aus der ganzen Welt und aus allen Lebensbereichen willkommen. Unabhängig von Ihrem Alter oder Ihrer Erfahrung werden Sie als freiwilliger Projektteilnehmer unabhängig in einer Kommune auf dem Campus leben. Der Campus ist umgeben von Natur, Feldern und thailändischen Dörfern, mit denen wir zusammenarbeiten. Die Freiwilligen übernehmen die Verantwortung für ihr eigenes tägliches Leben auf dem Campus. Ihre Hauptaufgabe als V2T-Freiwilliger wird es sein, mit den Einheimischen zu interagieren und in den Schulen Unterstützung zu leisten, indem Sie durch Spiele und Klassenarbeiten grundlegende Englischkenntnisse vermitteln. Zusätzlich können die Freiwilligen je nach Datum eine kostenlose Lehrerausbildung erhalten.
