 Welcome To
Fairtex Training Center Pattaya
 Opened in 1971 Fairtex has produced champions for over forty years. The Fairtex Training Center in Pattaya is the heart of the Muay Thai, and Jiu Jitsu program for Fairtex worldwide. It has produced renown fighters such as Yodsanklai Fairtex, Naruepol Fairtex, Neungsiam Fairtex, Jongsanan Fairtex, Bunkerd Fairtex and many, many more. We welcome beginners to advanced fighters and students.
