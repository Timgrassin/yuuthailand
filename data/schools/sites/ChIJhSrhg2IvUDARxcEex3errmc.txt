 FREQUENTLY ASKED 
 Questions, answered.
 How much does Muay Thai training cost in Phuket? 
 At Lions on Soi Ta-iad: 450 THB per drop-in class , 3,000 THB for a week of one discipline, or 9,000 THB per month . The All-Inclusive pass — 13,000 THB/month — covers every class on the timetable: Muay Thai, BJJ, MMA and boxing. Loaner gloves and shin guards are free for beginners, so your first session costs 450 THB total.
 Do I need to be fit before I start training? 
 No — you get fit by training. Classes scale to your level: coaches put first-timers on pads at their own pace, and nobody spars until they&apos;re ready. Most people find their rhythm within the first week. Come as you are; conditioning is part of the program, not a prerequisite.
 I&apos;ve never trained before. Can I really show up? 
 Absolutely. Most of our members started as complete beginners. All group classes are beginner-friendly — the one exception is MMA — Pro , which is for experienced fighters, not first-timers. Try a 450 THB drop-in first (see the hero banner), or go straight to the All-Inclusive monthly — 13,000 THB for unlimited classes. We loan gloves and shin guards to newcomers.
 Can I pay and book through the website? 
 Yes. View the timetable and prices on Schedule & Pricing , pay for your pass with the purple Pay buttons (secure Stripe checkout on this site), then use Book a Class to confirm your training time on WhatsApp. Drop-ins and day passes can still be paid at the front desk.
 How do I start training? 
 On the website: open Schedule & Pricing , pay online (best value: All-Inclusive monthly 13,000 THB), then book your first class via Book a Class . Walk-in? drop-in 450 THB — pay at the gym and arrive 10 minutes before class.
 Where exactly are you? 
 45/28 Soi Ta-iad, Chalong, Mueang Phuket District 83000 — the legendary Soi Ta-iad fight street in Phuket. Full location guide → · Map & directions → 
 What should I bring? 
 Workout clothes, a water bottle, mouthguard if you have one. For BJJ Gi classes, bring your own gi or buy one locally — we do not lend gis. We loan gloves and shin guards to newcomers. Shower amenities provided.
 Do you have accommodation? 
 We do not provide accommodation. Most visitors book their own place nearby — Soi Ta-iad has plenty of short-stay options. Message us on WhatsApp for tips on areas and what to look for; we&apos;re happy to point you in the right direction.
 Message on WhatsApp → 
 Can my kids train here? 
 Yes — Kids BJJ group classes and private sessions are for ages 4–12 at Lions. Class times and pricing on our Kids BJJ programme page. For schedules, competition wins, and parent-focused info, visit Lions Kids BJJ — our dedicated kids website.

 Do I need a visa to train in Phuket? 
 Most nationalities get a 30-day visa-exempt stay on arrival — perfect for a holiday camp. For longer stays, our Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) package includes 9 months of training plus a 5-year visa, all-in from 74,000 THB (embassies now require 9+ months). We handle the paperwork.
