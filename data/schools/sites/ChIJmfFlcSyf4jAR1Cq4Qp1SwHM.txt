 INTERNATIONAL HOUSE
 Committed to better education
 We are part of a global network of over 140 affiliated private language schools in 45 countries, spanning every continent. We are committed to learning experiences that will help you feel confident, empowered and a step closer to achieving your goals.
 Quality
standards
 As part of the International House World organisation, we are regularly assessed to ensure the highest standards in education.
 Expert
teachers
 All our teachers are CELTA or DELTA qualified and are dedicated to tailoring lessons to match your needs, wants and preferences.
 Effective
instruction
 We believe that the best teachers never stop learning. We continuously innovate to maximise the impact of our teaching on your learning.
 International
environment
 When you take a course at International House, you join a truly global community of students and teachers from all over the world.
 Ideal
locations
 Our schools offer convenient and modern facilities, each set within ideal locations to experience the best of Thai life and culture.
 Personal
support
 We understand that every individual needs something different and we aim to provide personalised support, in and out of the classroom.
