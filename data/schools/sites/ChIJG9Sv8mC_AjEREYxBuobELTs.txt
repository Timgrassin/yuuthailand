      Official Welcome Message – Grand Opening of Happy Company 
 Welcome to Happy Company. 
 We are proud to announce the official opening of our flagship office in Pattaya — a destination designed to support visionaries, entrepreneurs, investors, and global citizens who seek seamless business and lifestyle solutions in Thailand. 
 At Happy Company, we specialize in delivering premium services including: 
 – Corporate Formation & Legal Advisory 
 – Executive Visa Solutions & Immigration Support 
 – Luxury Real Estate Investment Services 
 – Virtual Office & Business Concierge 
 As we begin this exciting new chapter, we wholeheartedly welcome collaboration with strategic partners, service providers, and professionals who share our commitment to excellence, transparency, and client-centered care. 
 Whether you are building a new venture, relocating your life, or simply seeking trusted local guidance — we are here to serve you with integrity and discretion. 
 We look forward to growing together, and to making Thailand a happier place to live and work. 
 — The Happy Company Team 
 Please Notice Us Before The Day 11/08 For Join 
 What: 
 Happy company opening Party 
 Where Will Do: 
 At Happy Company  
 When: 
 13.08 2025 17:00PM 
 Party Night, Ready To Party? 
 We are truly delighted to invite you to the grand opening celebration of our company. 
 Join us for an unforgettable evening at our new headquarters located in Naklua, Pattaya, as we mark this special beginning. 
 The night will unfold with a warm and festive atmosphere, accompanied by light refreshments and meaningful connections. 
 Following dinner, the celebration will continue with an exclusive DJ set by PPEDDLE PIE , creating a vibrant ambiance until the very end of the event. 
 We look forward to sharing this milestone moment with you. 
 Dress Code 
 Smart casual or semi-formal attire is kindly requested. 
 Please note that sleeveless tops and slippers are not permitted.   
 Guest Arrival: 5:00 PM – 5:30 PM 
 Upon your arrival, we kindly invite you to settle in at your leisure. 
 Please refer to the seating chart to find your designated place and enjoy a welcome beverage as we begin the evening.  
 Appetizer Service: 5:30 PM – 6:30 PM 
 During this time, our distinguished MC will present key announcements along with a few unexpected highlights to enhance your evening.  
 Dinner Service: 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM 
 Indulge in a curated array of 20 gourmet dishes, thoughtfully crafted by our guest chefs exclusively for this celebration. 
 A premium BBQ selection will accompany the meal.  
 Lucky Draw & Closing Ceremony: 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM 
 A variety of exclusive prizes will be awarded through a lucky draw using 
  The ticket numbers distributed at check-in. 
 We warmly welcome you to join us for the prize announcements and to receive a curated gift set prepared in appreciation of your presence.  
 Closing: 9:00 PM onward 
 We sincerely thank you for joining us in commemorating this special milestone. 
 After the official festivities conclude, we invite those who wish to continue the celebration to enjoy an informal after-party in the company of friends and fellow guests.  
 Venue Information & Arrival Instructions 
 The party will be held at the headquarters of Happy Company, located in the Naklua area of Pattaya. 
 The grand opening ceremony will commence promptly at 5:00 PM, so we kindly ask that you arrive on time to ensure a smooth start. 
 Upon arrival, please check in with our staff by providing your name and stating that you are attending the grand opening celebration. 
 Our team will then escort you to your designated seat. 
 If you plan to arrive by car, we kindly request that you notify us in advance. 
 Approximately 30 parking spaces will be available in the vicinity of the venue for your convenience.
