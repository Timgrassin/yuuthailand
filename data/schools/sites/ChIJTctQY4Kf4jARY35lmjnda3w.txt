 About Studio
 GIGIPAYNE Studio is led by Gigi Payne, a textile designer and educator in Bangkok. She trained in fashion design at CIDI Chanapatana International Design Institute, where she now teaches textile design and craft techniques to fashion students.
 ​ 
 Since 2019, the studio has brought that same teaching to anyone who wants to learn a craft properly: traditional techniques learned from working craftspeople, taught in small groups and at your own pace. You walk out with a finished piece and the know-how to make the next one yourself.

 Frequently asked questions
 Where is the studio?
 GIGIPAYNE Studio is at 1034/8-9 Rama IV Road, Sathorn, Bangkok 10120 — next to One Bangkok and near MRT Lumpini (Exit 1A, opposite One Bangkok).   Open in Google Maps 
 What workshops can I book?
 22 hands-on workshops across textile and visual crafts. Dye with natural indigo in shibori and katazome, draw with hot wax in batik, press real leaves into cotton in eco print. Stitch in sashiko, embroidery, sewing and crochet, or paint in watercolor and mandala dots. Every session ends with a finished piece you take home. 
 Do I need any experience?
 No. Every workshop starts from the first step. Gigi or a studio artist shows you each stage and works at your pace. 
 Is there a schedule? How do I book?
 There&#x27;s no fixed schedule — you pick the workshop and the time. Sessions run daily, 9 am–12 pm or 1–4 pm. Message us on WhatsApp  or LINE  with the workshop, date and number of people. We usually reply within an hour. 
 How much does a workshop cost?
 Prices depend on the workshop and group size. Send us a message and we&#x27;ll share current rates. 
 How do I pay? Is there a booking fee?
 Every booking is confirmed with a booking fee, which is deducted from the final price. We accept Thai QR PromptPay, Visa, Mastercard and Apple Pay via Stripe, and cash. 
 Can I join today, or walk in?
 Same-day sessions are possible for some workshops — message us first and we&#x27;ll tell you what&#x27;s available. Some crafts need materials prepared a day ahead, so walk-ins without a message usually can&#x27;t be served. 
 Can I book a private session?
 Yes. Every session is by appointment — solo, as a couple, or with family and friends. 
 Can children join a workshop with their parents?
 Yes, children from age 6 can join most workshops with a parent. Tell us your kids&#x27; ages when you message, and we&#x27;ll suggest a craft that suits them. 
 Do you host kids&#x27; birthday parties?
 Yes. We run 2-hour workshops for 5–30 kids aged 5–15, with at least one accompanying adult. We provide the studio and run the workshop; cake, balloons, decorations and food are yours to bring. Kids Workshops 
 Do you run team-building and corporate workshops?
 Yes. Host your team event at our Sathorn studio for up to 60 people, or we come to your venue — up to 200 people, in Bangkok and across Thailand — with all materials and equipment. We run the workshop; participant transport and catering are arranged by you. Past clients include Levi&#x27;s, Agoda and Corona. Team Workshops 
 Can I learn online?
 Yes. The Ecoprint Essential Online Course is a set of self-paced videos by Gigi Payne with lifetime access. Online Courses
