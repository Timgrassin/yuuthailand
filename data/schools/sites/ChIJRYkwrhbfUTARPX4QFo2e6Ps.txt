 Frequently Asked Questions
 1. Do I need prior experience to join your Muay Thai gym in Krabi? 
 No experience is needed! Our Muay Thai gym in Krabi is completely beginner-friendly. Our trainers will adjust the intensity and techniques based on your current fitness and skill level. 
 2. Where is SONGTHAI MUAYTHAI located?
 We are proudly the original Muay Thai school in Koh Phi Phi , located in the Krabi province, Thailand. (Please check our contact section for the exact map and directions). 
 3. Do you provide boxing gear for the classes?
 Yes, we provide essential training gear such as boxing gloves and pads. However, you are welcome to bring your own hand wraps and Thai boxing shorts, or purchase them at our gym. 
 4. Can I book a private Thai boxing class in Phi Phi? 
 Absolutely! We offer 1:1 Private Training sessions for those who want personalized coaching to improve their Muay Thai skills rapidly. 
 5. How far in advance should I book my Muay Thai class? 
 While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend booking your Muay Thai session at least 1-2 days in advance to secure your spot, especially during the high tourist season in Koh Phi Phi .
