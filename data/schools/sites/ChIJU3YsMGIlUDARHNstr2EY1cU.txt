 THAI COOKING CLASS
 Experience Thai culture through cooking! 
 Immerse yourself in a 3-course culinary experience, guided by our homey chef. Enjoy your delicious creations with unlimited soft drinks. Bring home photos, a recipe booklet and new skills! 
 Contact Us 
 Programme
 • Meet at Sawasdee Thai Cuisine, visit the local market*. 
 • Cook under guidance of Sawasdee Thai Cuisine chef.
 • Once done, enjoy your creations with unlimited soft drinks. 
 • Morning class: 11:15 a.m. / Afternoon class: 03:00 p.m.
 • Dishes can be adjusted for a vegetarian diet, please let us know in advance.
 • Programme is approximately 2 hours.
