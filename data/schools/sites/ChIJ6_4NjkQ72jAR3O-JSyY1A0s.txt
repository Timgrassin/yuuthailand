 Frequently Asked Questions
 Common questions about visas, company registration, and working in Thailand.
 What is a DTV visa and who qualifies? The DTV (Destination Thailand Visa) is a 5-year multiple-entry visa for digital nomads, remote workers, freelancers, and people participating in qualifying activities like Muay Thai or Thai cooking courses. You need to show 500,000 THB in financial resources and proof of your qualifying activity.
 How much does a visa consultation cost? MJ & Co offers an initial consultation where we assess your situation and recommend the best visa pathway. Contact us via WhatsApp or chat for current consultation pricing. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden fees.
 How long does company registration take in Thailand? Standard company registration takes 2-4 weeks. Name reservation takes 1-3 business days, and the registration itself takes about 1 week after all documents are prepared. MJ & Co handles the entire process from start to finish.
 Do I need a work permit to freelance in Thailand? If you freelance for Thai clients, yes: you need a work permit. If you work remotely for foreign clients on a DTV visa, you don&#x27;t need a Thai work permit. MJ & Co can advise on which visa and permit combination suits your situation.
 Can MJ & Co help with visa extensions and renewals? Yes. We handle annual visa extensions, 90-day reporting, TM30 address registration, re-entry permits, and all renewal paperwork at Chiang Mai Immigration. Whether it&#x27;s a retirement visa extension, work permit renewal, or marriage visa extension, we&#x27;ve got you covered.
 What documents do I need for a retirement visa? You need a valid passport, proof of age (50+), financial evidence (800,000 THB in a Thai bank or 65,000 THB monthly income), health insurance meeting Thai requirements, and passport-size photos. MJ & Co prepares and reviews all documents for you.
