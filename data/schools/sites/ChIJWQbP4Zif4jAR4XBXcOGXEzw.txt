 Compare seat classes at Rajadamnern Stadium
 All four public seat classes watch the same fights. What changes is your distance from the ring, viewing height and the crowd around you.
 Ringside
 Closest to the ring
 Assigned seating beside the action for guests who want proximity above everything else.
 See Ringside seating 
 Club Class
 Elevated, assigned seating
 A clear view across the ring with assigned seats, making Club Class an easy choice for many first-time visitors and groups.
 See Club Class seating 
 LEO Section
 Where the local atmosphere is strongest
 Unassigned seating at the same general level as Club Class, with a strong local following and a more involved stadium atmosphere.
 See the LEO Section 
 Third Class
 The lowest-priced public seat class
 An elevated full-ring perspective from the outer tier when Third Class is available for the selected event.

 Muay Thai ticket prices and booking questions
 How much are Muay Thai Bangkok tickets?
 Muay Thai tickets at Rajadamnern Stadium are priced by seat class. As a reference, LEO Section is 1,500 THB, Club Class is 1,800 THB and Ringside is 2,500 THB. Third Class is 1,000 THB when it is open for sale. Select your date in the live availability to see the exact price in your currency.
 Are cheap Muay Thai tickets Bangkok available?
 The lowest-priced option varies by event and availability. Third Class is normally the lowest-priced public seating category when it is open for sale.
 Do I need to print my ticket?
 No. Official QR tickets can be shown on your phone at the stadium entrance.
 Can I arrive after the first fight?
 Event gates normally remain open during the programme, so guests can arrive after the first bout has started.
 How early should I arrive?
 The exact door and fight times are shown on each dated event page. Arriving before the first bout gives you time to enter and find your section without missing the opening action.
 How long does a Muay Thai event last?
 Running times vary by event. Check the dated event page for the most accurate schedule rather than assuming every Rajadamnern night finishes at the same time.
 Rajadamnern Stadium, Bangkok
 Rajadamnern Stadium opened in 1945 and remains one of Bangkok’s historic homes of professional Muay Thai.
 The stadium is on Ratchadamnoen Nok Road in central Bangkok and hosts Muay Thai fights in Bangkok throughout the week, with RWS Rajadamnern World Series on Saturdays.
