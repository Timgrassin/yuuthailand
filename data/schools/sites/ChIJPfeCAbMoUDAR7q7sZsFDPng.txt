 Phuket Cleanse at a Glance
 Phuket Cleanse is a premium all-inclusive wellness, fitness and detox retreat in Rawai, Phuket, Thailand. Founded in December 2011, the retreat has welcomed more than 20,000 guests. Owner-led by Melanie and Stanton Procter, it offers 25 rooms across five properties and a vast and ever-changing weekly schedule of classes, seven days a week, spanning fitness, Muay Thai, yoga, breathwork, longevity science, consciousness work, and excursions. Rated 5.0 on TripAdvisor with 687 reviews. Ideal stay 10–14 days. No minimum stay. Around 80% of guests travel solo. Guests are typically aged 35–75. Around 60% of guests return.
