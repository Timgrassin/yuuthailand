 CONVENIENCE - AUTHENTICITY - FUN
 Join our Thai cooking courses online and learn to cook Thai dishes like a Thai. Chef Dao in affiliation and consultation with Thai Chef School and Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy , all traditional and vegan dishes presented here are based on 15 years of tried and proven recipes and finely-tuned teaching techniques.  We have taught thousands of students over the years in Bangkok.  For those wishing to take in person professional culinary training in Bangkok, our sister school offers Worldchefs Approved Courses . 
 All dishes have been prepared at the School with assistance from our instructors. Now you can have fun learning how to prepare authentic Thai dishes while at home, work, or on a yacht. Learn anytime of the day online from the convenience of your computer or telephone. 
 All videos presented are in full HD and about 20 minutes in length. 
 No need to come to Bangkok to learn Thai cooking.  Learn with us online at your own pace.  We’ve taught thousands of students over the years in Bangkok; now we are bringing our extensive experience to you.
