 Trusted Thailand & American Lawyers Since 2001
 Chaninat and Leeds law firm, established in Bangkok, Thailand in 2001, is comprised of dedicated Thai and Western attorneys working in several areas of law. Our senior attorneys have been assisting and defending clients for several decades. In addition to our success in and out of the courtroom, our lawyers assist with other types of legal work, including trademarks, patents, legal advising, and US Immigration law. Our firm's partners have written numerous legal texts used by practicing attorneys, law schools, and the general public.
 We focus on cultivating long-term relationships with our clients by providing exemplary services. Our work philosophy focuses on results-oriented, direct action to solve our clients’ problems, and assist them in achieving their objectives. Since we have been assisting clients for several decades, we have extensive experience, and we have developed multiple, reliable professional contacts throughout Thailand and abroad.
