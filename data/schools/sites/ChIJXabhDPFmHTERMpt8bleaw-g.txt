 OUR ONE-STOP-SERVICE 
 This is Sanet 
 Your One-Stop Service for Investors & International Businesses in Thailand 
 Since 2003, the Sanet Group has been a trusted partner for investment in Thailand and for globally oriented companies seeking success in Asia. With deep market expertise, we guide European and international clients through every stage of market entry in Asia , with a strong focus on Thailand and the wider APAC region.

 From strategy consulting , project management , and the search for and evaluation of industrial sites through to construction tendering , Sanet took on joint responsibility and rose to the challenge together with its customers.
 OUR EXPERTISE 
 ➤ Market Entry & Investment Advisory (since 2003) 
 We provide hands-on support for investment in Thailand from strategic consulting and project management to industrial site evaluation and tendering. 
 ➤ Sales Structure & Import Solutions (since 2011) 
 We launched our service for setting up sales offices in Thailand with independent Business Units for each of our customers. Today, we also operate as an importer for these customers with an affiliated warehouse.
 ➤ Operational Management & Legal Services (since 2017) 
 Sanet has been expanding its services for the operational business of internationally oriented companies in Thailand and APAC. With our Thai-German law firm , we provide comprehensive legal advice and trustworthy external accounting services for our clients. Our licensed recruitment agency in Thailand is ranked among the best in the market.
 ➤ Digital Marketing & Web Strategy (since 2025) 
 Since 2025, Sanet has been supporting its customers in the second generation with a young team of experts in traditional and digital marketing . With our vision of the future and our commitment, we ensure long-term loyalty and create sustainable success for our customers .
