 Thailand 5 Year Visa (DTV) 
 Dowden Muay Thai is a Thai boxing gym in Koh Samui, Thailand . Our facility is complete with a Muay Thai training area and equipment, a professionally sized Thai boxing ring, and an area for free weight training. Come train with us and exercise the Thai way! 
 ​ 
 Dowden Muay Thai is now a government-approved Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) full service provider! Click the blue button above to learn more. 
 Our Guarantee 
 At Dowden Muay Thai, we guarantee results. Our experienced trainers will work with you to help you achieve your fitness goals and become a better fighter. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced fighter, we have a program that's right for you.

 Our Methods 
 Muay Thai Training
 Group Classes 
 Our Muay Thai training program is designed for all skill levels, from beginners to advanced fighters. Our experienced trainers will work with you to improve your technique, endurance, and overall fitness in a group setting. 
 Private Classes 
 In addition to group classes, we offer private classes for those looking to customize and accelerate their skills. Our trainers will teach you proper technique and help you develop your speed and power. 
 Daily, Weekly and Monthly Memberships 
 Train how and when you want with our one-time training and our flexible membership plans per week or per month. 
 Training Gear Shop 
 Come visit our shop if you'd like to buy and use your own gear. We have shirts, shorts, gloves, hand wraps and more!
