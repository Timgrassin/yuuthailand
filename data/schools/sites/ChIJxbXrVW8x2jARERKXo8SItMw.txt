 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
 Can I Train At The Gym Without A Membership?
 100% you can! We accept daily training, but you will get a lot more training for less with our training packages! Group classes start @400THB, Private @700THB. 
 How Do I Apply For A DTV Visa?
 To get started, there&#x27;s 3 easy steps: 
 1. Message us, or leave us your email, and we&#x27;ll send an information + application pack for you to fill out to return to us. Check our Visa page for more details. 
 2. Pay for your training, and we will send you an invitation letter and all required certificates for your application at your local embassy. 
 3. Once approved, enter Thailand and visit us to begin training when you are ready! Our training is flexible, so you can visit anytime without loss of your sessions. 
 What&#x27;s the fastest way to chat with you?
 We&#x27;re happy to connect any way you like - We reply quickly through Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM, or Whatsapp message. The links on our site connect directly to chat with us on your prefered app!
