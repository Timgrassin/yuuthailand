 What is Muay Thai Island?
 Muay Thai Island is a project focused on creating training, lifestyle, and fight opportunities for visiting fighters and students in Koh Phangan. By partnering closely with the island’s gyms, stadiums, trainers, and legends of the sport, we bring the community together, connecting you to the heart of authentic Muay Thai under one platform and helping shape Koh Phangan as a true Muay Thai island.
