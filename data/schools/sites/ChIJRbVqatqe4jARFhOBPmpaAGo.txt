 ABOUT US
 ORBIS is an international business firm taking its roots in Asia since 2006, grouping different offices
with a dedicated focus in emerging economic markets.
 Our teams include a combination of multicultural consultants and local partners working in synergy upon their respective expertise.
 Orbis is also a premium partner for entrepreneurs and investors offering multi-jurisdictional personalized services and customized expertise.
We provide the same in view of building lasting relationships with our clients while their business evolves.
