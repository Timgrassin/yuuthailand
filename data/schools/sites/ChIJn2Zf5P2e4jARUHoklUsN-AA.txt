 PROGRAMMES Thai Language Courses
 Two distinct tracks, each with structured levels from beginner to advanced.
 Speaking Track A1–A2 Beginner
 การพูดขั้นพื้นฐาน Tones, script, and basic conversation. No prior knowledge needed. You'll start speaking from your first lesson.

 B1–B2 Intermediate
 การพูดระดับกลาง Build fluency, expand vocabulary, and handle everyday situations with confidence.
 Learn more &rarr; 
 C1–C2 Advanced
 การพูดระดับสูง Master complex discussions, formal register, and polished self-expression.
 Learn more &rarr; 
 Reading & Writing Track R&W I Beginner
 การอ่านและการเขียนขั้นพื้นฐาน Master the Thai writing system from scratch — consonants, vowels, tone rules, and syllable structures. 50 hours.
