 About Our Courses 
 We offer 3 hours of joining class everyday with group size of 8-14 students. Each day will have different dishes. Equip yourself at your dedicated station, guided by our chefs, to create a delightful dish that suits your dietary and palate perfectly. Each student will be introduced to Thai herbs and ingredients and will be given a recipe book after the class.
