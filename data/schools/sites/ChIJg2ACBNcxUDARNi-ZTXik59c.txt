 FAQs
 What makes Belingo World Academy different from other learning centres in Phuket?
 Belingo World is a global learning academy and a leading education hub! 
 Our approach combines international school quality teaching, small-group classes, and global academic standards to help students build strong skills and confidence for success in school and beyond.
 We offer academic enrichment — including Cambridge English, Creative Writing, Maths, Mandarin, IGCSE subjects, IB subjects and Exam Preparation, alongside language courses.
 What programmes do you offer?
 For Young Learners 
 English – Cambridge English, Fun WRITER, WRITER studio.
 Math - Math Foundation Course (Singapore Math). Math Master (Kangaroo Math) 
 Mandarin – Belingo originally developed programmes, Reading & Writing, Communication, Pinyin and Reading Club, Mandarin 1st Language course.
 IGCSE - Maths, Science, English Literature, Mandarin, Computer Science, and Exam Prep
 IB - Maths, Science, English Literature, Business, Economics, Psychology, Mandarin, Computer Science, and Exam Prep
 Holiday Camp - Seasonal, project-based learning camp.
 Summer School - Full-day school during summer break.
 For Adults 
 English & Mandarin – All levels.
 How are students placed in the right level?
 Each student completes a placement assessment and consultation to identify their level, learning goals, and the most suitable level and programme, whether for academic support, IGCSE preparation, or language development.
 Who are your teachers?
 Our teachers are qualified international educators with experience in international schools, British, and IB systems, dedicated to inspiring academic growth and real-world confidence.
 What language is used in class?
 Most of our lessons for young learners are conducted fully in English to encourage natural communication and language immersion. 
 For Mandarin lessons and Adult beginner levels, teachers provide bilingual support (English–Mandarin or English–Thai) when needed to ensure full understanding and comfort.
 Do you offer trial lessons before enrolment?
 Yes . New students are welcome to book a trial lesson before enrolling. 
 The trial lesson is priced the same as a regular class to ensure learners approach it with seriousness and full commitment while experiencing our teaching approach.
 What are the class sizes and schedules like?
 Classes are kept intentionally small, usually 6–8 students, to ensure personalised learning focus, individual attention and active participation.
 We offer flexible weekday and weekend timetables to suit school and family schedules. You may refer to our details Centre Calendar and Class Schedule.
 Do you offer any additional courses related to English or Mandarin?
 Both English and Mandarin have WRITER studio courses that build students’ literacy, comprehension, and written expression through a structured, skill-based approach.
 Lessons cover vocabulary development, reading strategies, grammar in context, and both creative and academic writing. Helping students strengthen both fluency and critical thinking.
 Can lessons be rescheduled or make-up?
 Yes . With advance notice, students may reschedule or take a make-up class, subject to availability. 
 Please refer to the Course Policy for details specific to each programme.
 Do you offer holiday camps or short-term learning programmes?
 Yes . Our Holiday Camp provide fun, hands-on learning through physical activities, art and craft, storytelling, science, and cultural projects during school breaks. 
 Our Summer School provides a continuous learning experience and intensive learning progress during summer breaks.
 All designed to build global curiosity and confidence. Please follow our Instagram and Facebook to stay updated.
 Do students receive certificates or progress reports?
 Yes. Every course includes a Progress Report and Certificate of Achievement from Belingo World to recognise students’ progress and milestones.
 What payment methods do you accept?
 We accept international payments via Visa, MasterCard, UnionPay, Alipay, WeChat Pay, and Thai QR Code, as well as domestic Thai cards for local transactions.
