 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
 Planning Your First Class?
 Can beginners train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu at Pure Grappling? Yes. We offer an Intro to Jiu-Jitsu class for first-time students and Fundamentals classes for developing core skills. The first Intro class is free with advance booking.
 Can visitors drop in for BJJ training in Chiang Mai? Yes. Visitors are welcome in suitable Gi and No-Gi classes. An adult drop-in class costs ฿400 and includes Open Mat. Contact us before visiting to confirm the best session.
 How much does Open Mat cost for non-members? Open Mat-only drop-in costs ฿200 for non-members. A regular adult class drop-in costs ฿400 and includes Open Mat.
 Do I need a Gi for my first class? Gi classes require a suitable uniform; No-Gi classes use appropriate grappling sportswear. Gi rental is available for ฿200 per class.
 Who teaches Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu at Pure Grappling? Pure Grappling is led by Ryan Cheng, a black belt in both Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Judo. Bobby Hall regularly teaches Tuesday morning, with supporting instructors covering selected classes.
 How do I book an introductory class? Contact us in advance to arrange your first visit. WhatsApp +66 64 609 5863 , email info@puregrappling.org , or Instagram @puregrap .
