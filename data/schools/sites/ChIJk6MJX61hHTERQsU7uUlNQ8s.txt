 MINDER PATHANA SUKSA
 Minder Pathana Suksa School was founded with love and provides a nurturing and safe environment for young students aged 2 to 12 years. We are committed to setting the highest standards for our children, whether it be in our pristine and modern facilities and bright, open spaces, or in our competent academic staff and advanced teaching tools. We are confident that our students will flourish here, both in and out of the classroom. We have high hopes that students who complete their education with us will grow to be individuals with strong morals, ethics, and kindness, leading happy lives in all aspects, including family, career and social. We would love to have the opportunity to show you why we are so proud of our school.
