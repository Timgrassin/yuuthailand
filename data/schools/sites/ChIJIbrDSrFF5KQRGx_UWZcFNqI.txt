 ABOUT 
 Welcome to the Zouk Island experience! More than a zouk event, it's a full Zouk journey in paradise. 
 Join us for a month of Zouk on the tropical island of Koh Phangan, Thailand featuring workshops, socials, beach dances, and everything in between. 
 In our love for Zouk, we've created a space where connection comes first. Where movement is a language of the soul. And where every moment is infused with music, nature, and community. 
 WELCOME TO THE ROOTS, BUT IN KOH PHANGAN 
 Whether you come for a week or stay the whole month, you'll meet dancers from all over the world who are here to grow, connect, and celebrate life through Zouk. Koh Phangan has become more than a location for us, it's become Zouk Island. 
 ​ 
 This 2027 Zouk Month will look different, since we're bringing a full Zouk Month built around a marathon, intensives, and more . Think all-day dancing, deep-dive training, and a hike to the other side of the island for those who want to explore beyond the dance floor. 
 ​ 
 Marathon tickets and combo tickets are now available. Grab yours early to secure your spot for the all-day dancing experience. Exact location, and further ticket info coming soon, sign up below to be the first to know. 
 ​ 
 Follow us on social media for the latest updates, you won't want to miss what's coming. This is not just an event. It's a journey. A community. A home. We can't wait to welcome you.
