 MORE THAN TRAINING
 Rooted in centuries of tradition, Muay Thai reflects a unique fusion of physical strength and inner discipline. Historically, temples played an important role as centers of knowledge, where certain monks contributed to teaching self defense alongside meditation and moral guidance. This harmony between movement and mindfulness continues to define Muay Thai as both a martial art and a cultural experience.
 Home of “OUD”
 Sea Star Muay Thai was founded with a deep respect for traditional Muay Thai and a passion for sharing the art in an authentic, approachable way. Our gym is a place where discipline, experience, and steady progress come together for students of all levels.
 "OUD" 
Anothai Kaeowichian
 With nearly 400 professional fights across Thailand’s most respected stadiums and international arenas, OUD carries more than 34 years of true Muay Thai battle tested experience into every training session. Forged through real fights, real pressure, and real victories, his knowledge goes far beyond technique. it reflects the heart, discipline, and spirit of authentic Muay Thai.
 Whether you are stepping into the gym for the first time or preparing for competition at Phetch Buncha Stadium, Samui, OUD’s training is intense, precise, and deeply rooted in tradition. Every session is designed to sharpen your skills, build unbreakable confidence, and instill respect for the art of Muay Thai. the way it has been passed down through generations. Under OUD’s guidance, you don’t just train Muay Thai. you live it.
