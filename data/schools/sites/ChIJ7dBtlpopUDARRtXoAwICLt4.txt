 The Best Kindergarten 
 Welcome to Bambini
Nursery & Pre-School 
 Where excellence in early education meets a beautifully designed learning environment .
At Bambini , we provide an exceptional start in life for children from 12 months to 6 years old . Our internationally licensed early learning center in Rawai, Phuket combines the British EYFS framework with Montessori philosophy and STEAM-based education to create an inspiring, holistic experience .
With our world-class educators , carefully curated spaces , and a safe , nurturing atmosphere , Bambini is more than just a nursery and pre-school — it’s a place where curiosity thrives , creativity blossoms , and confidence grows.
At Bambini, we believe that every child is unique. Our mission is to inspire confidence , foster independence , and nurture a lifelong love of learning within a safe , caring , and beautifully designed setting .
 ABOUT BAMBINI 
 A World of Inspired Early Learning 
 At Bambini Nursery & Pre-School, we blend modern teaching methods, a personalized approach, and a warm, nurturing atmosphere to create an exceptional foundation for your child’s growth.
 International community for families
 Bambini is not just a kindergarten, but a club space for families from Europe, Asia and the CIS.
We work on the principle of closed membership: only after the interview and test of the child.
Our mission is the harmonious development of personality, confidence and preparation for the future.
 A Sanctuary of Safety & Care
 At Bambini, your child’s well-being and security are our highest priority. Our campus offers a fully enclosed private territory, 24/7 video surveillance and a team of highly trained staff dedicated to creating a protected, caring environment where children can learn, play, and grow with confidence.
 World-Class Education Framework
 We follow the British EYFS (Early Years Foundation Stage) curriculum, thoughtfully enhanced with Montessori principles and STEAM-based learning. This unique blend nurtures creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills, fostering a lifelong love of learning. Our multilingual environment (English, Thai, Russian) ensures that children develop strong communication and social skills from the earliest years.
 Gourmet Organic Nutrition
 At Bambini, we believe that healthy bodies fuel bright minds. Our in-house chef prepares fresh, organic, and nutritionally balanced meals daily, using only natural, preservative-free ingredients. Each menu is carefully designed to support your child’s growth, boost energy, and promote a lifelong love of healthy eating.
 Sustainable & Eco-Luxury Learning
 Bambini is committed to sustainability and eco-conscious education. Our beautifully designed learning spaces are crafted from natural, sustainable materials, creating an environment that feels calm, safe, and inspiring. Through gardening projects, recycling activities, and eco-friendly practices, children learn to love and protect the world around them.
 Nature-Inspired Outdoor Experiences
 At Bambini, children enjoy daily outdoor adventures and water activities designed to nurture both body and mind. From swimming and movement-based play to nature exploration in our safe, beautifully designed spaces, little ones develop physical strength, coordination, and a deep connection with the natural world.

 Our Advantages 
 Thoughtfully Curated Learning Spaces
 At Bambini, we provide a handpicked collection of safe, modern, and educational materials designed to inspire creativity and ignite imagination. Every toy, game and learning tool is carefully selected to support cognitive development, problem-solving skills and independent discovery, while encouraging teamwork and collaborative play.
Because we believe that at Bambini, learning should always feel like joy.
 An Inspiring World of Knowledge
 Our exclusive educational programs transform learning into an extraordinary journey of discovery. Through interactive lessons, hands-on experiences and creative projects, we nurture:
• Critical thinking
• Curiosity about the world
• A lifelong love for learning
At Bambini, education goes beyond the classroom — it’s an immersive experience designed to unlock each child’s full potential.
 Gourmet Organic Cuisine
 At Bambini, we believe that healthy bodies create brighter minds. Our in-house chef prepares gourmet, nutritionally balanced meals daily using only the freshest organic ingredients. Each menu is thoughtfully crafted to:
• Support physical growth and energy
• Promote healthy eating habits
• Encourage a love for wholesome, natural food
Every meal at Bambini is an opportunity to nourish, energize and inspire.
 Nature-Inspired Outdoor Adventures
 At Bambini, we believe that true learning begins outdoors. Children enjoy daily outdoor explorations, water play and movement-based activities within our safe, private campus, designed to cultivate:
• Physical strength & coordination
• Confidence through active discovery
• A deep connection with the natural world
From our eco-gardens to water activities and nature-based projects, Bambini encourages children to explore, engage and grow in harmony with their surroundings.
 A Private Members’ Learning Club
 Bambini is more than a nursery — it’s an exclusive early learning community. Admission is selective and offered only after a personal interview and child readiness assessment to ensure that every family shares our values of excellence and care.
Our secure campus features:
• Private enclosed territory
• 24/7 video surveillance
• Electronic access control
• Highly trained professional educators
With limited enrollment, Bambini provides an intimate, inspiring environment where every child is known and valued.
 Our Philosophy
 At Bambini Nursery & Pre-School, we see childhood as a journey of discovery.
Our philosophy combines the British EYFS framework, Montessori principles and STEAM innovation to create a world-class learning experience.
Here, education meets elegance:
• Every child’s individuality is celebrated
• Curiosity is nurtured through hands-on exploration
• Confidence and independence are built naturally
With limited enrollment and a personalized approach, Bambini is more than a nursery — it’s an exclusive learning club shaping curious minds and compassionate future leaders.
