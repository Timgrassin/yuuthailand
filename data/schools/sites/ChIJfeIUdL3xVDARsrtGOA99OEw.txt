 Welcome to all
 From beginners to pro fighters
 At Superpro Samui, we extend a warm invitation to all — from seasoned professionals gearing up for a Muay Thai bout to beginners eager to master new techniques. Our well-travelled coaches understand the unique fitness levels and training aspirations each new client brings to our sessions. Our facility boasts a fully-equipped weights gym, four Muay Thai boxing rings, an MMA cage, a huge BJJ/MMA/Judo dojo, a large swimming pool, an ice bath, and much more.

 FAQ
 Frequently asked questions
 Can I train if I've never done martial arts before?
 100%. We welcome complete beginners and make sure you feel comfortable and confident.
 Is Superpro Samui good for professional fighters?
 Yes. We've trained top-level athletes and provide advanced coaching, sparring, and fight prep.
 Do I need to book my training package in advance?
 We recommend booking ahead to secure your preferred training dates, especially during busy periods. Walk-ins are also welcome, subject to availability.
 If I stay with you, can I really train for free?
 Yes — if you stay in one of our rooms, you automatically get a free Platinum membership, giving you access to our classes.
