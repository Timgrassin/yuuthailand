 DID YOU KNOW?
 23 INCREDIBLE YEARS: We’re the original cooking school on Koh Lanta, teaching over 30,000 students. 
 COOK FOR A CAUSE: We don’t just teach the best Thai Cooking Classes on Koh Lanta; we make a difference too! Since day 1 all profits from Time for Lime directly support Lanta Animal Welfare , a charity that provides life-saving help to abused and injured stray animals, and conducts sterilization/rabies vaccination programs on the island and surrounding areas.
This has made Koh Lanta a safe place not just for helpless stray animals but for YOU too!

 TIME FOR LIME&#8217;S ROOTS
 Junie Kovacs  the owner of Time For Lime has a multi-cultural background. She is a true foodie & animal lover. 
 Junie started Time For Lime out of the love of food. 
Junie founded LANTA ANIMAL WELFARE out of the love for animals. There were just too many abused, injured & starving homeless animals and she could no longer watch them suffer.
 By joining Time For Lime, YOU help to support animals in great need.
