 ABOUT US 
 " A Language Lover's Translation Centre Part., Ltd. was established in 1995 by Mr. Rangsi SAPPHAYATOSOK, who graduated from Chulalongkorn University (Thailand) with a Bachelor's Degree of Arts and Sorbornne Nouvelle-Paris 3 University (FRANCE) with a Bachelor's Degree and Master Degree of Linguistics, and his spouse, Mrs. Wilawal SAPPHAYATOSOK, who currently manages and supervises its business operation, in collaboration with a great team of translators and staff members , we have all together welcomed our clients with sympathy and cheerfulness and performed our duties professionally with commitment, honesty and sincerity since the Company foundation in 1995 until now. Notice that A Language Lover's Translation Centre provides services not only in the area of translation firstly in Thai, English and French then in many other languages, namely German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and all AEC languages, but also in the area of comprehensive visa consultation and application both for foreigners intending to live in Thailand and for Thai citizens who wish to live permanently, further their education and travel abroad. 
With regard to our performance, we have always succeeded in providing quality services to the satisfaction of our customers at reasonable price and on time. Besides, we are acknowledged by foreign embassies, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and district offices in Thailand. Finally, we do hereby appreciate the support of all former customers and deeply hope to have an opportunity to provide all those who kindly visit our website and may recommend us to your close family members and friends."
