 Visa Extensions, Border Bounces & Visa Runs in Krabi Province
 Keeping your visa up to date in Thailand is much easier with the right team by your side. SiamConsult supports you every step of the way—from regular extensions to border bounces and embassy visa runs. Explore all your options on our Visa Services in Krabi page.
 Border Bounce: Simple Re-Entry
 A border bounce means crossing a nearby border—typically to Malaysia, Laos, Cambodia, or Vietnam—and returning to Thailand for a new entry stamp (often a 60-day exemption, which you can extend at immigration). SiamConsult handles transport, timing, and all paperwork to make this process easy. Learn more about requirements at the Thai Immigration Bureau website.
 Visa Run: New Visa from a Thai Embassy
 A visa run involves leaving Thailand to visit a Thai embassy or consulate in another country and applying for a new visa, such as a Tourist, Non-B, or Non-O visa. We help prepare your documents and handle consulate bookings for you. For embassy locations and details, see the Thai Embassy official website .

 1. What areas in Southern Thailand does SiamConsult cover? SiamConsult provides accounting, legal, and visa services across Krabi , Ao Nang , Phuket , Koh Lanta , Phi Phi Islands , and Phang Nga .

 5. What is included in your property due diligence service? We check land titles (Chanote), construction permits, and corporate debt before you buy or lease. Details are on our Property Due Diligence page.
