 Welcome to The Old Medicine Thai Massage School Shivagakomarapaj, a sanctuary of ancient healing located in the heart of Chiang Mai, Thailand. As the pioneering school that introduced the Northern Style of Traditional Thai Massage in 1962 by our father, Ajahan Sintorn Chaichakan. We take great pride in preserving the authenticity and wisdom of this centuries-old art. Today The Old Medicine carries on the Shivagakomarpaj lineage, Ajahn Wasan Chaichakan, the second-generation master who remains dedicated to passing down the history of Thai healing arts, health, and well-being. 
 The Northern Style of traditional Thai massage, also known as Nuad Phaen Thai, had its origins here at The Old Medicine Thai Massage School Shivagakomarapaj. 
 For more than 60 years, we've been honored to train thousands of students worldwide, who have since practiced and shared the Northern Style across different parts of the world.
 Today, Old Medicine’s Northern Style is the most widely recognized, followed, and practiced style of traditional Thai massage in the international community, and is the recognized, classic standard / foundation of “Northern Style Traditional Thai Massage” throughout Thailand, and around the world. 
 We are delighted to announce the partnership of the Old Medicine Hospital Thai Massage School Shivagakomarpaj with Bodhi Panya Institute in San Francisco, California - the only Thai massage school approved by California Massage Therapy Council (CAMTC) and approved to operate by the Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education (BPPE) . 
 Together, we look forward to furthering our commitment to providing quality education, resources, and services that our students and practitioners need in order to fully realize their potential. By expanding our reach we will provide our students with even more opportunities to learn and grow in their massage practice. 
 Bodhi Panya Institute is NCBTMB Approved Provider for Continuing Education (CE), we are recognized by the National Certification Board for Therapeutic Massage & Bodywork. This ensures that our courses meet the highest standards and provide valuable continuing education for massage therapists.
