 About Us
 Founded to make Muay Thai and fitness accessible to all, Koh Fight has grown into a leading camp on Koh Samui. We offer state-of-the-art gym facilities, HYROX and strength and conditioning classes along with spa sessions to aid in your recovery. 
Train with experienced instructors in a supportive, inclusive environment. At Koh Fight, you'll find the perfect place to improve fitness, build confidence, and connect with like-minded people.
