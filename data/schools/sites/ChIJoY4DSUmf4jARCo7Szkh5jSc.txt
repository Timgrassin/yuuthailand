 Who we are 
 JT Legal Consult is a well-established legal law firm in Thailand, with our offices in Chang Mai and Bangkok, in which both are strategically situated in the heart of the business centers.
 Our dedicated team of experts endeavor to understand our clients’ needs to provide them with unparalleled legal services, that not only aim to match but even surpasses beyond our clients’ expectations.
 At JT Legal Consult we fully understand the stressful time our clients are facing, relating to whatever legal issues they need to settle therefore, we do not want to add another burden of charging (high fees) to the pre-existing predicament. Our law firm offers fixed and capped fee packages with legal cost that are highly competitive and fair, although we offer a very  reasonable capped legal fee, we do not compromise the international standard of professional service that our clientrs deserve to get.
