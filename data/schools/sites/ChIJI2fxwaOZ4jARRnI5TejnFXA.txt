 I walked in without an appointment and had no trouble getting in. Located on the 5th floor. I chose the 90-minute neck and shoulder massage for 1,700 THB. It’s on the pricier side compared to other massage shops, but it was absolutely worth it.
I specifically wanted a massage that focused almost entirely on my neck and shoulders, and that’s exactly what I got. The therapist really worked through my knots and tension. I walked out feeling noticeably lighter, and my neck and shoulders felt so much better afterward.
The facility was spotless, the atmosphere was peaceful, and the calming music made it incredibly relaxing.
I would definitely come back. If you’re looking for a true neck and shoulder massage rather than a full-body massage with a little attention to those areas, I highly recommend this place.
