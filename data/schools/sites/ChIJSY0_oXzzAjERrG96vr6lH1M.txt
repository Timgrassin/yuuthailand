 Requirements
 Non-O Retirement Visa Extension 
 A completed TM7 form .
 Passport plus photocopies of all relevant pages (main page, plus visa and continuing pages)
 Copy TM30 confirmation
 Copy 90 Day report confirmation
 2 recent 4x6cm full-face photographs.
 A copy of the bank book 1 year back
 A letter from your Thai bank confirming the status of your account.
 The year transaction statement detail (preorder at the bank) and have to be stamped by the bank
 Condo sale or rent contract
 Hand drawn map showing the location of your home
 To be eligible for a Non-O Retirement Visa Extension , applicants must meet certain criteria. They must be at least 50 years old, have a passport valid for at least one year, and show proof of financial stability. The financial requirement is a bank account balance of at least 800,000 Thai Baht or a monthly income of at least 65,000 Thai Baht. 
 Check the official Website: Criteria for Consideration of Granting an Alien's Extension of Stay in the Kingdom for Retirement

 Good to know...
 Bank Documents for Non-O Retirement Visa 
 The year transaction statement detail &lt;- the one you order in advance you can get early.
 The bank LETTER &lt;- that 5-line one that states your name, account, and balance on the day they write the letter can only be no older than 7 days.
 You update your bank book on the date you apply for the extension so it shows the balance on that date and make copies 1 year back.
