 Thai Cooking Class FAQ: Koh Samui 
 Everything you need to know about our authentic Thai cooking classes in Koh Samui
 What makes Jungle Kitchen the best Thai cooking class in Koh Samui?
 Jungle Kitchen offers an authentic Thai cooking experience in a peaceful jungle setting near Fisherman&#x27;s Village. We use fresh ingredients from our organic garden, teach traditional techniques, and limit classes to just 8 students for personalized attention. Our evening classes (5pm to about 9pm) provide a magical atmosphere with jungle sounds and beautiful sunset views.
 How much does the Thai cooking class cost?
 The evening cooking class is 3,000 THB per person plus 7% VAT. Non-cooking companions are 1,500 THB per person, paid in cash on arrival. Private classes start from 24,000 THB plus 7% VAT for up to 12 guests, and Open Kitchen Banquets and custom group events are priced on request.
 How long is the Thai cooking class and what&#x27;s included?
 The class starts at 5pm and runs for a minimum of 4 hours, finishing at about 9pm. It includes a tour of our organic garden, learning to prepare 3 authentic Thai dishes tailored to your group, dinner where you eat your own creations, the recipes to take home, and a free transfer to and from Fisherman&#x27;s Village. We also provide aprons and all cooking equipment.
 Is there a transfer from Fisherman&#x27;s Village?
 Yes. Fisherman&#x27;s Village in Bophut is a 5-minute drive away, and the transfer there and back is free with the evening cooking class. For private classes we can arrange a transfer on request; it isn&#x27;t included in the price by default.
 What is your cancellation policy?
 For the evening class you get a full refund, for any reason, if you cancel up to 2 days before your class (until midnight two days before). The policy is also in your booking confirmation email.
 Do you cater to vegetarian and vegan dietary requirements?
 Absolutely! Before each class, we contact you to ask about dietary restrictions and what you cannot eat. Everyone cooks the same three dishes, but we make ingredient substitutions where necessary to accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and other dietary needs using authentic Thai ingredients and techniques. Our organic garden provides fresh vegetables and herbs perfect for all adaptations.
 How far is Jungle Kitchen from popular areas in Koh Samui?
 We&#x27;re a 5-minute drive from Fisherman&#x27;s Village in Bo Put, just 10 minutes from Koh Samui Airport and 15 minutes from Chaweng Beach. Our jungle location offers peace and tranquility while remaining easily accessible from all major areas on the island. We can arrange transportation if needed.
 What Thai dishes will I learn to cook?
 The menu is set for each class. Before your class we ask every guest two questions: "Do you or does anyone in your group have any food allergies or diet restrictions? (Anything you cannot or will not eat?)" and "Are there any favourite dishes you&#x27;d like to cook?" From the answers we plan 3 authentic Thai dishes, normally a soup or Thai salad, a Thai curry, and a noodle or stir-fry dish. Everyone cooks the same three dishes with ingredient substitutions where necessary. All recipes use traditional techniques and fresh ingredients from our organic garden.
 Should I book my Thai cooking class in advance?
 Yes, we highly recommend booking in advance as we limit each class to just 8 students for the best experience. This ensures personalized attention and a more intimate learning environment. After booking, we&#x27;ll contact you before the class to ask about dietary restrictions and your favorite Thai dishes, so we can create the perfect menu for your group. You can book via WhatsApp (+66 62 341 8004) or email us at JungleKitchenSamui@gmail.com.
 Can I join the cooking class on my own?
 Yes, solo travellers are very welcome. The evening class is a small group of up to 8 guests, so you can book a single place and cook alongside other guests.
 What happens if it rains?
 You stay dry: the kitchen and the dinner area are both covered, so the class and dinner go ahead under a roof.
 What&#x27;s the best time to take a cooking class in Koh Samui?
 Our evening classes (5pm to about 9pm) are perfect as you&#x27;ll cook during the cooler hours and enjoy dinner as the sun sets over the jungle. This timing works year-round in Koh Samui&#x27;s tropical climate. The magical atmosphere with jungle sounds and sunset views makes for an unforgettable experience.
 Is the cooking class suitable for beginners?
 Absolutely! Our Thai cooking class is designed for all skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced home cooks. Our expert instructors guide you through each step, teaching traditional techniques and sharing cultural insights. No previous cooking experience is required - just enthusiasm to learn!
 Can I bring children to the Thai cooking class?
 Yes, we welcome families! Children aged 12 and above can fully participate in the cooking class. Younger children are welcome to observe and enjoy the meal. We provide child-friendly cooking tasks and can adjust spice levels. Please mention children when booking so we can prepare accordingly.
 What should I wear to the cooking class?
 We recommend comfortable, casual clothing that you don&#x27;t mind getting a little messy. Closed-toe shoes are preferred for safety in the kitchen. We provide aprons, but bringing a hair tie if you have long hair is helpful. Most importantly, come hungry and ready to have fun!
