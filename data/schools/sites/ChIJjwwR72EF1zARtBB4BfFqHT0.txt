   Chiang Rai Christian Academy or CRCA stands for:  
 C -&gt; Christ-Centered : Keeping Jesus as the foundation of life's. 
 R -&gt; Ready : Preparing students academically, spiritually, emotionally, socially, and life skills. 
 C -&gt; Confident : Equipping them to stand firm in their faith. 
 A -&gt; Anchored : Grounding them in faith, hope, and love. 
 Vision 
 "Our vision is to share the gospel with every child, nurturing them to stand firm in faith. We aim to build the next generation on a foundation of faith, hope, and love, with Jesus Christ as their cornerstone."  
 Mission 
 "Our mission is to provide a Christ-centered education that equips children with strong faith, academic excellence, and life skills. Through nurturing environments and personalized guidance, we aim to inspire students to grow in faith, hope, and love, empowering them to impact their communities and the world for Christ."  
 P hilosophy  
 "Chiang Rai Christian Academy, we believe God uniquely creates every child with purpose and potential. Our philosophy is rooted in the principles of faith, hope, and love, emphasizing holistic development—spiritual, intellectual, emotional, and social. Through Christ-centered education and a nurturing community, we strive to cultivate individuals who honor God, serve others, and live with integrity."  
 Welcome to Chiang Rai Christian Academy 
 Dear Students, Parents, and Esteemed Guests, 
 On behalf of the Licensee and myself as the Director, it is my great pleasure to welcome you to Chiang Rai Christian Academy (CRCA). Our school is more than just an institution of learning; it is a nurturing community grounded in faith, hope, and love. 
 At CRCA, we are committed to equipping students not only with academic excellence but also with the values and skills necessary to grow into confident, compassionate, and Christ-centered individuals. Our mission is to reach every child with the gospel of Jesus Christ, helping them stand firm in faith and build their future on the unshakable foundation of His truth. 
 We are blessed to partner with parents and guardians in shaping the next generation, providing an environment where children can thrive spiritually, intellectually, and socially. Together, we aim to cultivate a culture of kindness, integrity, and perseverance, empowering our students to shine brightly as they fulfill God’s purpose for their lives. 
 Thank you for choosing CRCA as part of your journey. We look forward to seeing how God will work through each child and family in our community. May His grace and blessings guide us all in the exciting year ahead. 
 With warm regards and prayers, 
 Dr.Yutthasart Laecherkuh (Paul) 
 Licensee and Director of Chiang Rai Christian Academy
