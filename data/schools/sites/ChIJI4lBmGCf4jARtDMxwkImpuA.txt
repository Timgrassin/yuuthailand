 Frequently Asked Questions
 Can I join to get more active or support weight management? + Splash Box offers music-led cardio boxing. Choose a class you can follow at your own pace to work on fitness and build an exercise routine. Personal training is available if you want to add strength work. Weight changes depend on diet and individual factors.
 Which class suits beginners? + No boxing experience is needed. Follow the performer and work at your own pace. For help choosing your first class, ask the staff on LINE.
 What should I bring? + Sportswear, gloves and towels are available to rent so you can pack light.
 What are the trial terms? + 14 days of unlimited classes — 990 THB.  What is Dark Beat Boxercise? + Lights down, EDM on. Follow your performer through punches and footwork on an aqua bag. This is a group workout with no sparring, and no boxing experience is needed.
 How long is a class? + For your first visit, aim to arrive 10–15 minutes before class. Check the start and end times of your chosen class in the current schedule.
 Where is it and how do I get there? + About 650 m from BTS Thong Lo. At Marché Thonglor, use the lift at the very back of TOPS FOOD HALL: only this lift reaches Building A, floor 5M. Find SPLASH BOX in Room 5M01. Free parking for up to 4 hours at Marche Thonglor.
