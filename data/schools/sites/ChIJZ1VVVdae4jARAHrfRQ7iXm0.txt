 Industries 
 Our Industries
 Food & Beverage 
 The Food & Beverage industry in Thailand spans food production, processing, distribution, and foodservice, supported by strong domestic consumption and a vibrant tourism sector. We advise a range of Thailand-based and regional F&B clients, including premium restaurants, quick-service chains, and food and beverage producers operating across Bangkok and key tourist destinations. 
 Technology & Software 
 Thailand’s Technology & Software sector is driven by digital transformation, platform adoption, and emerging technologies across consumer and enterprise markets. We support international and regional technology companies with operations in Thailand, including businesses in blockchain solutions, B2C digital platforms, and PropertyTech serving the local real estate ecosystem. 
 Professional Services 
 The Professional Services industry in Thailand provides essential advisory, technical, and compliance support to both local and international businesses. We work with clients across management consulting, IT and digital services, legal advisory, engineering, and HR/recruitment, many of whom serve multinational clients or operate within regulated Thai markets. 
 Creative Agencies 
 Thailand’s Creative Industries benefit from a strong design culture and growing demand for high-quality architectural and creative services. We service international architecture, design, and creative firms operating in Thailand, supporting their local operations and regional project activities.
 Investment Holdings (SPVs) for Private Equity and Venture Capital 
 We support international and regional Private Equity and Venture Capital firms investing in Thailand, advising on deal structuring and the establishment of Thailand-based and offshore SPVs. Our services include SPV incorporation and administration, regulatory and tax compliance, and ongoing portfolio and investor reporting for Thai operating companies.
 Dormant & Holding Companies 
 We provide ongoing administration and compliance services for Thailand-registered dormant and holding companies across multiple sectors. Our team ensures full compliance with Thai accounting rules, tax filings, statutory reporting, and corporate secretarial requirements, allowing clients to maintain entities efficiently and in good standing.
