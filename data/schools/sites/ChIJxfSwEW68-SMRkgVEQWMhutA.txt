 FAQ Common questions
 Can a foreigner own a company in Thailand? Yes. Depending on your business activity you can operate through a Thai Limited Company, qualify for 100% foreign ownership under a BOI promotion, or use other approved structures. During your free consultation we recommend the most suitable and compliant structure for your goals.
 Do I need a visa before a work permit? Generally yes — a Non-Immigrant B (or Non-IB for BOI) visa is required before a work permit can be issued. We handle both together as a package so the visa, extension and work permit align correctly.
 How long does company registration take? Once documents are signed, incorporation is typically completed quickly. The full timeline depends on name reservation, capital, and any licences required. We give you a realistic schedule at the start.
 Are your prices fixed, or will there be hidden costs? Our professional fees are fixed and quoted upfront. Government fees and official charges (which vary by service) are separate and charged at actual cost — we always make these clear before you proceed.
 Do you provide ongoing accounting after setup? Yes. We offer monthly accounting packages covering bookkeeping, tax filings, payroll, social security and year-end audit coordination — so your company stays compliant all year round.
