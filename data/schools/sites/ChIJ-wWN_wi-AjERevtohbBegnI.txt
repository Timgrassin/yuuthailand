 Discover the Easy ABC Language School in Pattaya!
 Do you dream of mastering a new language while experiencing unforgettable adventures? The Easy ABC Language School in Pattaya is the perfect place to take your language skills to a new level while enjoying the paradise-like ambiance of the stunning city of Pattaya.
Why Easy ABC? Because we believe learning should be fun! Our highly qualified teachers are not only language experts, but also motivated educators who share their enthusiasm for teaching. With interactive teaching methods, lively discussions, and exciting activities, learning at Easy ABC becomes an enjoyable experience you will never forget.

 Border/Visa Run 
 If you require to further extend your stay in Thailand by making use of visa exemption, then a border run (border bounce) is what you need. A border run is a round trip to the border of one of Thailand’s neighbouring countries that allows you to leave Thailand and re-enter the country with a fresh entry stamp (visa exemption).
 Make use of multiple border or visa runs to further extend your stay.

 Why Easy ABC Language School?
 Years of experience 
 On March 1st, 2008, the Easy ABC Language School was founded in Pattaya under German management and has been training individuals, groups and company employees in languages and computers ever since.
We currently teach the following languages: German, English, Thai and Russian. 
 Government-certified school and ASEAN AWARD 2016 winner 
 In 2009, Easy ABC was audited by the Thai Ministry of Education and officially certified as a language school. The owner Wolfgang Payer was awarded the ASEAN AWARD 2016 by the Thai government for his outstanding achievements. 
 We guarantee success on the Start Deutsch 1 exam (Goethe Certificate A1) 
 Any of our group class students who complete the A1 course and despite regular participation in the class, fails the A1 exam – can continue studying with us for FREE in another group course until you pass the exam. WE GUARANTEE IT. 
 Professional and engaging teachers 
 The teachers at Easy ABC are carefully selected according to their qualifications, experience and their ability to effectively interact with a broad range of students.
