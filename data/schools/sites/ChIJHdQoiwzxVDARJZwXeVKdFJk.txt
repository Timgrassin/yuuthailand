 About Us 
 Welcome to Kiddie Garden Nursery and Preschool! 
 We're here to provide a nurturing and enriching experience for children aged 12 months to 4 years. Our commitment extends beyond mere daycare, as we cultivate a holistic learning experience that encompasses a variety of developmental activities, pre-school education, and sensory enrichment. 
 At Kiddie Garden, every moment is an opportunity for growth, discovery, and creativity. Nourishing both body and mind, we offer a menu of wholesome, healthy foods to fuel their budding energy. 
 Join us in creating a vibrant journey of exploration and development for your child at Kiddie Garden!
