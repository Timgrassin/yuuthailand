 Welcome to rittisak muay thai
 Where we will make you fall in love with the beautiful art of Muay Thai (Thai boxing) 
 ***Warning*** Muay Thai is seriously addictive.
 Your Muay Thai oasis on Koh Phangan Island, Thailand!
 Do you dream of training Muay Thai on a tropical island? Are you wanting to take your Muay Thai skills to the next level? Would you like to meet likeminded people from all over the world? And most importantly, are you ready to change your life? Well, you are in the right place.  
 Leave with a smile!
   Here at Rittisak Muaythai we believe in making hard work fun and guarantee that you will leave with a smile.  We welcome everyone from all walks of life, no matter your age or level of experience.
 Home of Rajadamnern Stadium Champion
 Owner and Head Trainer, Kru Chok Rittisak, has had an extensive career fighting at an elite level during the golden era of Muay Thai.  He graced the stage at both Rajadamnern and Lumpini Stadiums in Bangkok, Thailand.  
 However, it was Rajadamnern Stadium where he reached his height of fame, going on to win the coveted and elusive Golden Jacket (seua samart) for 10 straight wins.  This was no easy feat in the golden era of Muay Thai where competition was fierce.  Read more about Kru Chok&#8217;s career here .  
 Why Choose Us?
   We are more than just a team, we are a  family.  Our mid size gym with 13 full time professional Thai trainers,  means that you will never get lost in the crowd and you will receive the attention you deserve.  
 Whether you are a complete beginner or a professional fighter, we will take care of you.  What are you waiting for? Come see for yourself the magic that is Rittisak Muaythai!
 Meet our elite team of trainers!
 Our Trainers are highly experienced former Thailand Muay Thai champions, who want to bring out the best in you. They also love to have fun, and will ensure that you have a good time while working up a sweat. We pride ourselves on being a heavily technique focused gym, with no egos and a Muay Femur flair.  location
 Our Muay Thai gym is located in a peaceful and quiet Thai fishing village called Chaloklum on Koh Phangan Island. 
 We are surrounded by cascading mountains, palm tree lined streets, pristine white sandy beaches, and clear turquoise water.  Could you picture yourself running here before training? It sure makes for breathtaking views that&#8217;s for sure.  
 We are located 700 metres from Chaloklum Village where you can find a variety of restaurants (Western and Thai), cafes, 7/11, Tesco Supermarket and amenities.  The village is located right on the stunning Chaloklum Beach.  
 Close to the gym there are mountain trails for you to hike, waterfalls and clear natural mountain swimming pools (they&#8217;re actually a secret but we can show you).
