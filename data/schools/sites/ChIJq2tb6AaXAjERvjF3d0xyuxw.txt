 Nah Pah
 Thai Cooking Class in Pattaya by Award-Winning Chefs
 For those who are seeking a Thai cooking class in Pattaya, look no further than Nah Pah Thai Cooking School at Royal Cliff Grand Hotel . Teaching the art of Thai cuisine since 2000, culinary traditions meet modern instruction in the state-of-the-art facility at our Pattaya hotel. Our fun and immersive Thai cooking class takes place over the course of 3 hours and transforms passionate food lovers into skilled practitioners of authentic Thai cooking.
 Led by award-winning chefs, each session provides hands-on instruction in a professional-grade workspace equipped with all necessary tools, plus a complimentary apron and chef&#8217;s hat.  Our intimate class sizes ensure personal attention from our expert instructors, allowing for detailed guidance throughout each recipe. What sets our Thai cooking school in Pattaya apart is our commitment to authentic ingredients. Students can hand-select fresh herbs from our on-site nursery garden, learning about the essential components that make Thai cuisine undeniably flavorful.
 Our Thai cooking class in Pattaya includes:
 Welcome drink upon arrival
 Class including alcohol drinks options
 Step-by-step guidance from expert Thai chefs
 Complete recipe collection to take home
 Lunch featuring your own creations
 Professional-grade kitchen facilities
 Fresh ingredients from our herb garden
 Our Thai cooking class in Pattaya takes place in a spacious open-concept kitchen at our luxury Pattaya hotel. It serves an ideal learning environment for our budding chefs. There is also a nursery herb garden close by where our learners will select the freshest seasonal ingredients to use in their Thai cooking class. At the end of the session, students can enjoy their tasty creations in our traditional Thai dining room, enjoying the authenticity and immersion of the experience. 
 Our Thai cooking school in Pattaya offers specialized programs catering to different skill levels and interests, including dedicated children&#8217;s classes that introduce young chefs to the joys of Thai cooking. Each class is thoughtfully structured to ensure our young participants learn essential techniques in a safe environment from a well-experienced instructor.
 Contact our team by sending an email to relax@royalcliff.com  to book your culinary journey into authentic Thai cuisine ahead of time. Group bookings and private classes are available upon request.
