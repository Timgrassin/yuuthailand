 Where is WKK Gym 
 located in Bangkok ? 
 We are located in the Huai Khwang District at 153/2 Lat Phrao 34, Lat Phrao Road, Samsen Nok Subdistrict, Bangkok, Thailand
 Is WKK Gym 
 suitable for beginners? 
 Yes. We offer a "Muay Thai for Beginners" guide and specific Group Classes designed to help you take your first steps in the sport
 Who are the 
 trainers at WKK Gym? 
 Our "Authentic Thai Coaching" team includes Kru Udd Noi (Senior Coach with 330 Pro Fights), Narak WKK (Ranked RWS Fighter, 9-0), Kru Boonrod, and Kru Mach
 How much does Muay Thai training cost at WKK Gym? 
 Our prices start at 700 THB for a single Fighter Training session (Day Pass). We also offer a 10-session Group Class package (valid for 30 days) for 5,300 THB/month
 Do you offer drop-in 
 day passes? 
 Yes, we offer a "Muay Thai Fighter Training - Day Pass" for 700 THB per session. This session lasts 2 hours and 30 minutes and includes strength and conditioning
 What is included in the 
 WKK Elite Camp? 
 The WKK Elite Camp is an "All Inclusive" 6-week program costing 40,000 THB. It features an assigned personal coach, hypoxic (elite cardio) training, and a full media package
 When are the Muay Thai 
 classes scheduled? 
 Train in authentic Muay Thai sessions twice daily at 8:00-9:30 AM and 4:00-5:30 PM.
 Can I buy Muay Thai gear 
 at the gym? 
 Train in authentic Yes, we sell "Authentic Muay Thai Gear" including WKK Grenade Muay Thai Gloves (Black Edition), Premium Shin Guards, and T-shirts
 Do you offer 
 private Muay Thai training? 
 Yes, in addition to group classes and fighter training, we offer "Private Coaching" options found under our "Discover Our Classes" section
 Is the training at 
 WKK Gym authentic? 
 Yes, we pride ourselves on bringing "the golden era of Muay Thai for a new generation." Our programs focus on authentic Thai coaching and a full Muay Thai curriculum.
