 Property Due Diligence
 Any time you acquire property in Thailand, you face certain risks. It doesn’t matter how you acquire the property, unforeseen problems can appear. The most common problems the experts at Professional Corporate Services have seen by property buyers or transferees include eviction issues, owner issues (the seller is not really the owner), restrictions on land use, or on building construction.
 The importance of legal due diligence in any merger or acquisition transaction should never be underestimated. It is critical that underlying ownership, any third-party rights, and any terms and conditions relating to relevant assets and liabilities are checked, known, and confirmed legally – not least for reassurance that what you think you are buying or selling is actually what you are buying or selling and any legal risk is identified.
 Investing in real estate certainly involves a huge amount of money. We cannot deny that there are some fraudulent dealers and developers taking advantage of naive investors. Therefore, investors must take extra precautions before finalizing any transactions. Due Diligence is a useful tool for estimating the status of a prospective project and the developer.
