 Frequently Asked Questions About MAVINN Muay Thai Boxing Camp in Thailand
 How much do MAVINN's Muay Thai Stay & Train packages cost?
 MAVINN Muay Thai gym in Pattaya, Thailand, offers flexible packages for every type of visitor. A single session pass at 700 THB per day, and a daily all-access pass at 1,000 THB per day. Longer Stay & Train packages are: 1 Week Pass (6 training days) at 4,500 THB, 1 Month Pass (24 days) at 16,000 THB, and 3 Month Pass (72 days) at 42,000 THB. Every package includes full access to training and facilities with no hidden fees.
 Is the MAVINN Muay Thai training camp in Thailand suitable for beginners?
 Yes. MAVINN Muay Thai Pattaya camp welcomes complete beginners. You don't need any previous Muay Thai experience or a particular fitness level to start. Trainers guide every session at your own pace, so beginners feel comfortable from the first class. The camp is designed for all levels, so as your technique and conditioning develop, the training develops with you. 
 How long can I stay and train at MAVINN Muay Thai boxing camp in Thailand?
 You can stay and train at MAVINN Muay Thai gym in Pattaya for as long as you like, depending on your goals and schedule. Packages run from a single day up to three months. Many visitors stay for one week to experience authentic Thai training, while others choose extended stays of a month or longer for full immersion in training, recovery, and the Muay Thai lifestyle in Thailand. The flexible package structure suits both casual practitioners and serious athletes.
 Where is the MAVINN Muay Thai gym in Pattaya located?
 The MAVINN Muay Thai training camp in Pattaya is located along Thepprasit Road, Bang Lamung District, Chon Buri. For directions on how to get there, contact the MAVINN team when you enquire or book; they'll inform you of the easiest way to reach the camp.
 What facilities are included at the MAVINN Pattaya Muay Thai camp?
 Every MAVINN package includes full access to the camp's facilities: Muay Thai classes, boxing, MMA, strength & conditioning, yoga, HYROX training, and open gym access, plus a swimming pool, sauna, ice bath, dedicated recovery zone, and high-speed WiFi. Training and recovery sit together under one roof, so hard sessions are matched by proper recovery.
 What gear do I need to train at MAVINN?
 You can bring your own Muay Thai kit, or gear up before your trip using the MAVINN collections. A typical training kit includes boxing gloves, hand wraps, Muay Thai shorts, and shin guards for sparring. MAVINN's gear collection covers everything you'll need: gloves, shorts, protective gear, and apparel, built by the same brand you'll train with, so you can arrive ready to go.
 How do I book a Stay & Train package at MAVINN?
 To book, contact MAVINN through the website with your preferred package, rough dates, and training goals. The team will confirm availability, package details, and the easiest way to plan your stay in Pattaya.
