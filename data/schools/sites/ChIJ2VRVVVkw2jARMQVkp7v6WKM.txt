 Frequently Asked Questions
 How do I find a Notary in Chiang Mai? 
 Our lawyer Apichart Mattayanuwat is a licensed lawyer practicing in Thailand and also a licensed notarial services attorney by the Lawyers Council of Thailand. He could notarize a document or signature of the applicant and provide other legal services.
 How to get married in Chiang Mai for US citizens? 
 US citizens can get married in Chiang Mai, firstly, by obtaining the marriage affirmation and a certified copy of passport at the US Consulate in Chiang Mai. These documents will then be translated into Thai by our lawyer and submitted to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs - Chiang Mai branch for legalization. The legalized documents will be submitted to the district office. At the district office, the couple will be accompanied by our staff as their interpreter and two other witnesses.
 Can I apply for a US visa in Chiang Mai? 
 Yes, you are able to apply for a US Visa at the US Consulate in Chiang Mai. Siam Legal Chiang Mai provides you with US Visa services, which may include but not limited to: full review of your personal, financial, and employment history, an extensive checklist of documents and professional and accurate preparation of your US visa application. For the K1 Fiancée Visa and US Spouse Visa, the visa interview will be conducted at the US Embassy in Bangkok.
 I want to retire in Chiang Mai, how do I go about it? 
 The process to retire in Chiang Mai involves applying for an initial non-immigrant O visa from the Thai embassy in your home country. You will use this visa to enter Thailand and open the bank account for the transfer of funds. If you are unable to obtain this non-immigrant O visa beforehand, you may enter on a tourist visa and then change the visa into a non-immigrant O visa in the Chiang Mai immigration office. After the funds have seasoned for two months the non-immigrant O visa will be converted into a one-year retirement visa.
 Can I apply for UK Visa in Chiang Mai? 
 Yes, Thai nationals and foreigners living in Chiang Mai may apply for a UK Visa through VFS Global office in Chiang Mai. You should tender your UK visa application at the VFS office which in turn forward your application to the UK Embassy Bangkok governed by the UK Border Agency. Before doing anything, you may get a consultation with our UK Visa and Immigration Specialist to make sure that you are qualified for the visa you are applying for and you will have a better chance of obtaining the UK Visa.
 What is the most popular and easiest business structure to set up in Chiang Mai? 
 The most popular Thai legal entity would be the Thai Limited Company, or a Private Thai Limited Company, to be exact. Thailand has a lot of foreign investment restrictions. The Private Thai Limited Company is flexible because it allows for 51% Thai ownership, while still allowing foreign investors to manage and control the company. According to Section 1096 of the Thai Civil and Commercial Code, a limited company requires a minimum of 3 shareholders (promoters) and shares are privately owned. The personal liability of the shareholders is limited to the amount unpaid on their shares. As mentioned, the Thai limited company also allows separation between the owners and management.
