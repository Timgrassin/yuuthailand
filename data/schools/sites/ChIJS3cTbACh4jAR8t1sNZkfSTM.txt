 Need to Know
 Questions clients usually ask first
 Common questions we answer before any consultation.
 Can I change from tourist status to a long-stay visa? + 
 Sometimes, but eligibility, timing, and local immigration practice matter. We check the route before relying on an in-country conversion.
 Which visa is best for a remote worker? + 
 DTV may be relevant for eligible remote workers or freelancers, while LTR can suit higher-income work-from-Thailand professionals. The right choice depends on your income, nationality, and intended stay.
 Do retirement applicants need health insurance? + 
 Insurance requirements depend on route and filing context. O-A cases commonly require insurance review, while other routes should still be checked before filing.
