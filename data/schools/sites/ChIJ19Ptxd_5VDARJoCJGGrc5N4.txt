 ABOUT LIONHEART SAMUI: 
Your Premier Muay Thai 
Training Destination
 We are a Muay-Thai camp located in the tropical paradise of Koh Samui. Our facility is nestled away in a quiet and peaceful part of the island surrounded by lush tropical gardens. A short 200 m walk away from the gym is the beautiful Ban Tai beach which is widely considered Samui's no.1 beach and best kept secret by residents of the island.
We accept all levels at our camp, from fitness enthusiasts and beginner trainees to seasoned professionals and upcoming champions looking for a place to get new experience.

 F.A.Q. 
 How do I pay for my booking? 
 You can pay online using PayPal or by card. Prepaid bookings are charged 50% at the time of booking confirmation.
 Can i book for 3 weeks or over 4 weeks? I only see options for 1 week, 2 weeks and 4 weeks 
 You can book your package for as long as you like . Just send us an email for custom quote.
 What level do i need to be to attend the packages? Are you beginner-friendly? Do you do fight preps? 
 Our camp and training programs suits trainees of all levels. Beginner or professional we have got something for everyone
 How is personal training structured? 
 The personal training sessions are all booked in on Monday and you will be given a schedule to when they are due. In case you are not doing a Premium package we just need a 1 day notice to book in your session. The sessions all consists of warm-up, stretching, shadow-boxing skill work, pad work and technical sparring with the trainer. If you wish you can show up for the PT earlier and do your own warm up to get more time out of the session
 Can i use the facility after class or before? 
 If you are paying for the classes or any of the packages you are free to use the facility for your own training. The gym is open between 8am and 8pm. 
 Are taxes and fees included? 
 Prices are subject to 7% government tax and 3% transaction fee.
 Are airport transfers provided? 
 Airport transfer is not included in the price of the room and should be arranged prior to arrival. A flight number is required for a pick-up to be arranged. Please send full guest name, arrival date, time, and flight number at least 48 hours prior to arrival. Transfers are provided only to and from Koh Samui airport.
 Do I need to get a visa to come to Thailand? 
 Standard tourist visa upon arrival is normally valid for 30 days upon arrival to Thailand without prior application for the most of passport holders. Please consult a Thai embassy representative in your home country just to double-check. To extend your visa duration you have several options.
 Travel Insurance 
 Although our staff will try to provide supervision, instruction and assistance during training sessions, physical exercise involves the risk of injury. As the company is not responsible for self-inflicted accident or injury caused throughout your stay (within reasonable grounds), we advise all guests to equip themselves with full comprehensive travel & health insurance prior to any travels!
