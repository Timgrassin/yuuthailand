 Prepare your holiday, business trip or your path to a new life with our expert guidance and support. Trust our experienced team to help you navigate through every step of the application process. 
 TRAVEL MEDICAL INSURANCE 
 Enjoy peace of mind and ensure your safety during your next adventure by securing the right travel medical insurance  that offers comprehensive coverage for unexpected health needs. Don’t let any surprises derail your journey.  
 For Schengen visa applications, travel insurance is mandatory and must comply with EU requirements. Travel medical insurance is available through Insurte, a third-party insurance broker. TLScontact acts solely as an introducer, and you remain free to choose any provider.

 ADDITIONAL SERVICES 
 Throughout the application journey, we offer a range of additional services to meet different customer needs for convenience, comfort, or additional reassurance. Discover some of our best-selling services below. Please note that the availability of these services depends on the location where you submit your application and the country that you are applying to.

 You’ll enjoy privacy and a selection of complimentary refreshments while receiving one-to-one assistance and support from one of our experienced agents.

 Express Courier Return 
 Get your passport returned directly to you.
 Avoid unnecessary travel, save time, and avoid the crowds by having your passport returned to you by courier. As soon as your passport can be returned to you, we’ll send it directly to the address of your choice* using a trusted and secure courier service, so you don’t need to travel to collect it.
 *In certain locations this is limited to within the country in which you applied.

 If you are organising travel for yourself, a group, a company, as a family, or if you simply live a long way from our visa application centre, we can simplify the visa application process by bringing it straight to your door.
 Our specialist agents can travel to the location of your choice and assist you from document submission right through to biometric enrolment.
