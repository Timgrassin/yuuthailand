 Suwan Films is an international production company specialising in action design for feature films, TV series, TV commercials, game animation and 3D motion capture based in Bangkok, Thailand. 
 ​ 
 We provide professional stunt performers, wire work & rigging team(s), guns & special effects, safety equipment (e.g. crash pads) and supply industry -leading equipment, facilities, and training, with 20 years of experience in Thai and International productions. 
 ​ 
 Our mission is to produce/create content that is innovative, creative, can meet and adapt to unexpected challenges and bring our diverse knowledge and expertise to life. This along with our aspiration approach ensures we deliver the highest standards of action design.
