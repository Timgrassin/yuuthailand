 Founded in 2000, Thai Cottage Home Cookery School is a family-run cooking school in Chiang Mai offering an authentic home-style approach to learning Thai cuisine. Set in a tranquil, garden-like setting, the school welcomes small groups for a warm and personalized experience.
Guests begin their culinary journey with a guided visit to a local market and the school’s organic herb garden, learning about traditional ingredients and their cultural significance.
In the open-air kitchen, friendly instructors guide participants step by step in preparing classic Thai dishes — often including curry paste made from scratch, stir-fries, soups, and desserts like mango sticky rice.
With its emphasis on traditional flavors, hands-on learning, and heartfelt hospitality, Thai Cottage has earned a reputation as one of Chiang Mai’s top cooking schools. The experience is not just about cooking — it’s about sharing the warmth, stories, and flavors of a Thai family kitchen.

 Frequently Asked Questions
 Everything you need to know about our Thai cooking classes
 How much does a Thai cooking class cost? Our cooking classes start from ฿800-1,200 THB per person depending on the season. This includes a market tour, all ingredients, recipe booklet, and free hotel pickup/drop-off service.
 How long is the cooking class? Our cooking classes are approximately 3-4 hours long, including a guided visit to a local market and our organic herb garden, followed by hands-on cooking and tasting your creations.
 Do you provide hotel pickup and drop-off? Yes! We provide free hotel pickup and drop-off service within Chiang Mai city for all our cooking class participants.
 Can you accommodate dietary restrictions? Absolutely! We are flexible with dietary needs including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and food allergies. Please let us know when booking and our chefs will accommodate your requirements.
 What is the maximum class size? We keep our class sizes small (maximum 30 people) to ensure personalized attention and a hands-on experience. Every participant gets their own wok and cooking station.
 Do I need cooking experience to join? No prior cooking experience is needed! Our classes are designed for beginners to advanced cooks. Our fluent English-speaking instructors guide you step by step through each recipe.
 What dishes will I learn to cook? You&#x27;ll learn to cook a variety of classic Thai dishes which may include Pad Thai, Green Curry, Tom Yum Soup, Mango Sticky Rice, Som Tum (Papaya Salad), Khao Soi, and more. You can choose from our menu of available dishes.
