 About Us 
 House of Taste provides the best hands-on Thai cooking classes in Bangkok and Phuket. Our school is located in Bangkok (Sukhumvit & Sathon, nearby BTS skytrain and MRT subway) and Phuket (Kata Karon). We are a group of people with keen interests in Thai food and culinary arts. We are eager to share our expertise and knowledge to our guests.

 Cooking Classes 
 Our classes are suitable for everyone, from beginners to experienced chefs. 
 All of our recipes are MSG-free. Vegetarian, Halal, Kosher, and allergy-friendly options are available upon request. 
 Cooking Classes 
 Our classes are suitable for beginners through to experienced chefs. All of our recipes are MSG free. Options for veg­e­tar­ian, Halal, Kosher, and aller­gic peo­ple are available upon request. 
 GROUP CLASS 
 This 3.5-hour class offers you a chance to meet new friends from around the globe. You will explore a local fresh market in a small-group setting. The class is conducted in English. There are four fixed menus available for each class.
 Book now 
 PRIVATE CLASS 
 This 3.5-hour class offers you private time with your friends, family, and instructor. Explore a local fresh market with a class size of 1–15 people. The class can be conducted in English or Thai upon request. Select your favorite menu.
 Email us 
 TEAM BUILDING CLASS 
 Strengthen your team’s connection with our Team Building Cooking Class. Enjoy a fun mini cooking competition where Thai cuisine is the heart of collaboration. Our venue can accommodate more than 60 participants, and you can customize the menu to your preference.
