 Who we are
 Forvis Mazars Group SC is an independent member of Forvis Mazars Global, a leading professional services network. The partnership draws on the expertise and cultural understanding of over 40,000 professionals across the globe to assist clients of all sizes at every stage in their development. Forvis Mazars in Thailand is part of the Forvis Mazars Group SC. Our clientele benefits from the combined expertise of 400+ Thailand-based professionals and our international team.
