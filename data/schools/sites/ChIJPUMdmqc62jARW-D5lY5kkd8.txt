 The Little Things That Make a Big Difference 
 Why we are diferent? 
Students who enroll at We Learn Thai Chiang Mai are enrolled for the same purpose as you, which is to Learn Thai. We have maximum group sizes which are followed to ensure the success of our students. Our Classrooms are large, air conditioned and comfortable.
 The smiles tell everythings 
 At WeLearn Thai, every progress, every word, and every conversation brings a smile — and that smile tells everything.
 Our qualified teachers 
 Good energetic, outgoing, Fun, English speaking teachers are hard to find. Even though almost all of our teachers hold degrees in education from reputable universities, we don’t overlook opportunities to hire from other fields of study.

 Speak Thai in 15 Days Why are we different? 
 Speak Thai in 15 Days will get you speaking quickly. It meets everyday for three weeks. Our method focuses on learning easy patterns of Thai language structure and reinforcing it through intensive Thai conversation practice followed by e-learning that Includes audio samples so you can tune your ears, practice pronunciation and improve your ability to differentiate tones. You will be practicing conversation with classmates from the 1st day and by the following week talking about your plans and what you did the night before.
