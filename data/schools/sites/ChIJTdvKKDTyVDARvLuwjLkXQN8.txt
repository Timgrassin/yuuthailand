 Authentic training in Thailand, built by fighters for every level. 
 Your Muay Thai camp in Thailand, professional and personal.
 Punch it Gym Koh Samui is an authentic Muay Thai camp in Thailand for beginners, active travelers and professional fighters. Located in Lamai on one of the most beautiful islands in South Thailand, we combine original Muay Thai training with experienced Thai top trainers, modern training areas, on-site accommodation, a Healthy Cafe and personal support from your first request until departure. Whether you want your first Muay Thai experience, improve your technique, enjoy a training holiday in Thailand or prepare for real fights, every level is welcome. Everything in one place, in the Muay Thai paradise of Koh Samui, with real passion for the sport.

All prices include free access to our fitness centre.
