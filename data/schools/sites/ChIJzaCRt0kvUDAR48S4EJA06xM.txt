 ACTION POINT
 Fitness, Weight Loss & Yoga Transformational Retreats
 Action Point Fitness and Wellbeing Retreat was created with one goal in mind. To develop the most vibrant, authentic health and fitness retreat experience in Phuket, Thailand. Action Point launched in September 2012 and has grown to become the leading retreat for successful Weightloss , Fitness and Wellbeing transformations in Thailand. 
 At Action Point you will be personally guided towards the most effective and sustainable fitness and nutrition program to suit your goals and needs. The Action Point program is designed to create optimal results in regards to your mind, body and overall wellbeing .

 Our Benefits
 At Action Point, it&#8217;s all about your experience and your results! Here is a quick list of the benefits resulting from your weight-loss, fitness or yoga retreat with us.
 Your retreat begins with a personal evaluation with our certified Health Coach and your Personal Trainer , who all specialize in total body transformation. You will receive a comprehensive snapshot of your current status and the data will be used to develop a program to fit your goals.
 Throughout your program, you will receive continuous support from our coaches and support staff . We will track your progress, sharpen your focus and make any adjustments as needed. The entire retreat experience is optimized to help you achieve your fitness , weight-loss and wellbeing goals.

 FAQs
 What fitness, wellness, and training programs are offered at Phuket Action Point?
 We offer science-backed, results driven programs that focus on:
 Weight loss &#8211; sustainable fat loss and muscle gain through nutrition, movement and coaching to establish of fundamental habits.
 Fitness &#8211; focused on driving strength and conditioning through building functional capacity and knowledge in a fun, supportive and outcome-driven manner
 Yoga Reset and Rebalance &#8211; A program developed to focus more on Yoga capabilities, helping guests to reconnect and recharge through private and group yoga classes.
 All packages at Action Point are totally all-inclusive &#8211; meaning no missing elements or surprise extra costs.
 Where is Phuket Action Point located in Phuket and what facilities are available?
 We are located in the south of Phuket in a suburb called Rawai. Known for Niahan beach, Rawai is a beautiful part of the island with great mix of Thai and Western amenities. Action Point is the leading Wellbeing Retreat in Thailand and offering facilities that include a seaview yoga studio, crossfit gym, weights and cable gym, cardio area, infinity pool and rooftop cafe along with beautiful, modern accommodation.
 Can beginners join fitness retreats, gym sessions, or Muay Thai training in Phuket?
 Absolutely. Action Point is designed to be a retreat that caters to all levels and ages. We are not a bootcamp. We are a professional, science-driven retreat that focuses on helping guests find the best weightloss and fitness plan that works for them 
 How long do fitness and weight loss retreats in Phuket usually last?
 Guests come to stay anywhere from 1 week to 2 months. The recommended length of time to see results and feel a big improvement in your wellbeing is 3 weeks. 
 What is included in a typical day at a fitness retreat in Phuket?
 Action Point is the most inclusive retreat in Thailand. All meals and shakes are included, 3-4 fitness and yoga classes each day, private PT and Muay Thai sessions are included, sauna and ice bath, massage, workshops, walks, mobility classes &#8211; each day is packed with included options and each day is able to be tailored to your needs.
