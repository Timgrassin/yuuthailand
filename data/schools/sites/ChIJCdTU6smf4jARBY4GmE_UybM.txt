 About us
 Law and More . . . 
 We at A&T Law have been serving clients from all over the world with legal and consulting services for more than twenty years. Our team includes six qualified lawyers and an experienced management consultant so that we are able to offer you a package of coordinated services to meet your personal needs whether it be a simple visa application or the establishment of a company with BoI approval and staff training or coaching.
 Situated in the central business area of Bangkok we are easy to reach and can converse with you in Thai, English or German. Our aim is not only to provide you with professional services but to ensure that you feel at ease dealing with our team of experts.

 Management Training Services
 Our Management Training Programs include: 
 Effective Communication in the modern workplace
 Cross-Cultural Understanding, for both Thai and Western associates
 Team Skills Development
 Leadership in the Thai Environment
 Our training programs help to improve behavior thus increasing capabilities. The emphasis is always on ‘practicality and reality’ rather than on theory and traditional style presentational techniques. The group captures the emotional, legal and cultural realities of a range of workplace issues.
 If you wish we can work with your HR and management specialists tailoring our programs so that they are pertinent to current issues in your workplace. These may relate to customer service, cross cultural differences, communication deficits, office relationships, performance management and management issues.
