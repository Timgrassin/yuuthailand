 Introduction
 Welcome to Oak Meadow International School Phuket, a American-curriculum international school where we believe true education sparks enduring inspiration. Our philosophy centers on an individualized approach to learning, drawing from globally recognized frameworks such as Teaching Strategies, McGraw Hill, and StudySync. We go beyond academics to nurture each student's unique potential, fostering critical thinking, creative problem-solving, and collaborative social skills through dynamic, inquiry-based programs in literacy, social studies, science, and mathematics.
At Oak Meadow, we bridge educational standards with the rich cultural values of our community. This fusion ensures that learning is not only academically rigorous but also deeply meaningful and relevant to our students' lives. We are more than a school; we are a vibrant learning community dedicated to empowering compassionate, curious, and capable global citizens.
 ACADEMICS PROGRAMS
 Kindergarten Program
 Our Kindergarten Program combines two internationally recognized approaches to early childhood education.
 From Nursery to Kindergarten 2, we follow The Creative Curriculum, which emphasizes play, exploration, and hands-on learning to nurture creativity, curiosity, and social-emotional growth.
 In Kindergarten 3, we implement McGraw-Hill Education resources, providing structured preparation for primary school and aligning with the Common Core State Standards (CCSS).
 Together, these curricula support holistic child development—balancing creativity, exploration, and academic readiness to prepare children for future success.
 View Details 
 Primary School
 Our primary school program, based on the renowned CCSS American curriculum, ensuresa strong academic foundation across all core disciplines. Students are encouragedto think critically and collaborate effectively.
 View Details 
 Middle & High School Program
 The Middle School Program, developed with the CCSS American curriculum, offers students a deeper academic challenge and fosters independence, critical thinking, and leadership skills. The program prepares students for advanced studies in high school while nurturing personal growth and confidence.
 The High School Program, built on the Edmentum American curriculum, provides academic flexibility and prepares students for international exams and university admission. It emphasizes independent learning, analytical thinking, and practical applications.

 Facilities
 Oak Meadow International School features a sprawling 29-hectare lakeside campus in Phuket. Its modern facilities include digitally-equipped classrooms, advanced science labs, and dedicated arts studios.
