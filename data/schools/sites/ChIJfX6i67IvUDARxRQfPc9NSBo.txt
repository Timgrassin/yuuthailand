 Frequently asked questions
 FAQ: Frequently asked questions 
 What visa do I need for long-term training?
 That depends on your passport. For most nationalities, you can stay for 60 days and extend it for another 30 days — so up to three months in total. If you plan to stay longer, it’s better to get the right visa, as visa runs are not really encouraged anymore. 
 A good option is the DTV (Destination Thailand Visa) . With the DTV visa, you can stay in Thailand for up to 5 years , and we&#x27;ll help you get started with a 6-month-training package at Eagle Muay Thai. Train hard and let us handle the boring stuff. We&#x27;ll check if you qualify and take care of everything from start to finish. ✔️ Contact us now for more details. 
 Muay Thai camp: What should I bring with me?
 For training, you’ll need gloves, shin guards, hand wraps, and a mouthguard. If you’re planning to prepare for a fight, daily runs are part of the program — so don’t forget your running shoes. You’ll also need towels and, of course, your training clothes. Don’t worry if you’re missing some gear — there are plenty of fight shops nearby, and many of our guests enjoy going on a little shopping tour when they arrive. 
 Can beginners train at Eagle Muay Thai?
 Yes, totally! All levels are welcome. 
 Whether it’s your first time ever wearing gloves or you’re already an experienced fighter, we’re here to support your journey. If you feel more comfortable starting with a private session, that’s totally fine — but you’re also welcome to jump straight into our group classes. And if you’re not ready for sparring or clinching yet, no worries at all! You can just watch, take a break, and join in when you feel ready. Our trainers will guide you step by step and make sure you feel confident from day one. 
 What’s the best time of year to train in Phuket?
 Training is great all year round in Phuket. The dry season (from November to April ) is sunny, hot, and the island is full of energy — perfect if you like busy gyms and lots of people around. The rainy season (from May to October ) is cooler, quieter, and has a more relaxed vibe. Training still runs as usual; some people even prefer it because the temperature is easier to handle. So really, the best time depends on your personal preference — you can train any month of the year . 
 How long should I stay to see real progress?
 It really depends on your goals and how your body adapts, but most people start seeing real progress after about 2 to 4 weeks. Keep in mind that the first few days are all about settling in — getting over jet lag, adjusting to the heat, and finding your rhythm with the training schedule. Once your body gets used to the climate and intensity, things start to click fast. If you can stay a bit longer, you’ll notice major improvements in technique, conditioning, and overall fitness. 
 Can I fight while I’m here?
 Yes, absolutely — no problem at all! If you already have some experience and you’re ready to train hard, we’re happy to help you prepare for a fight. We love supporting our fighters and being part of that journey into the ring. Just keep in mind that a fight camp isn’t a vacation — it means daily training, running, and full focus . But stepping into the ring in Thailand is a truly unique experience — intense, fun, and something you’ll never forget. 
 How do I get to Eagle Muay Thai from Phuket Airport?
 Taxi is the easiest option. You’ll find taxi stands right outside the terminal. The ride to Eagle Muay Thai costs around 800 THB and takes about one hour, depending on traffic. 
 A cheaper option is to use ride-hailing apps like Bolt, Grab, or inDrive. They usually can’t pick you up inside the airport grounds, so you’ll need to leave the terminal area and meet your driver at the designated pick-up point or on the main road just outside. 
 What’s the accommodation like?
 We offer two types of rooms: 
 Our standard rooms are located right inside the camp, close to the training area. You’ll be in the heart of the action and get the full experience ;) It can get a bit noisy during group sessions, but hopefully you’ll be part of those sessions — so that shouldn’t be a problem! 
 Our premium rooms are just around the corner. They offer a bit more privacy and include a small outdoor area to relax. 
 All rooms come with a king-size bed, private bathroom, fridge, air conditioning, and Wi-Fi. 
 Is food provided?
 No, our room and training package doesn’t include food. But don’t worry — there are plenty of options nearby. Just around the corner, you’ll find a small supermarket (Tops Daily) and a few local Thai restaurants. 
 About one kilometer from the camp is the famous fitness street Soi Ta-iad , where you’ll find all kinds of restaurants — from local spots to clean-eating places with macro-friendly meals. And of course, many of our guests like to order food through apps like Grab , which makes it super easy.
