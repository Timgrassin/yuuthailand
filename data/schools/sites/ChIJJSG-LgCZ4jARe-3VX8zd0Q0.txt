 MEET YOUR INSTRUCTOR 
 Cook with Joyce 
 Learn Thai cooking with Joyce, your friendly local instructor and the owner of Mahanakorn Thai Cooking Class. 
 Joyce loves sharing Thai cooking with guests from around the world, with a warm and friendly approach that makes every class easy to follow. 
 In each small-group class, she guides you step by step as you prepare, cook and season every dish yourself &mdash; from pounding curry paste to squeezing fresh coconut milk. 
 More than following recipes, you'll learn about Thai ingredients, flavors and everyday cooking techniques that you can take home with you.
