 “No Frills. No Excuses. Old School Gym.”
 Welcome to Battle Conquer! The gym for people who train to win, not to pose (ok you can pose a little bit). We’re an old school, family-run gym in Pattaya, built on sweat, grit, and tradition. Forget the shiny fitness chains and Instagram workouts this is the real deal.
 Here you’ll find:
 Muay Thai that&#8217;s raw – authentic, hardcore training with seasoned fighters who know what it takes to step into the ring.
 Weights that bite back – a fully equipped iron paradise for bodybuilders, lifters, and athletes who respect the grind.
 Recovery for warriors – sauna, cold plunge, and the space to reset before you go again.
 Whether you’re chasing titles, building muscle, or just pushing yourself past your limits, this is the place. We train fighters. We build strength. We create results.
 This isn’t a spa. It’s not a social club. It’s a battleground. And if that excites you, then welcome to the family.
