 Frequently Asked Questions 
 Phuket Visa FAQs 
 What visa do I need to live in Phuket? 
 It depends on your purpose. Retirees (50+) use the Thailand Retirement Visa; remote workers use the DTV; people starting a business need a Non-B visa and work permit; and those wanting a long, flexible stay often choose the LTR or Thailand Privilege visa. NuoBello matches you to the right one.
 How much money do I need for a Thailand Retirement Visa? 
 You need 800,000 THB deposited in a Thai bank (held for at least two months) or a monthly income of 65,000 THB &mdash; or a combination reaching 800,000 THB per year. You must be at least 50 years old.
 What is the DTV visa and who is it for? 
 The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) is a 5-year, multiple-entry visa for remote workers, freelancers and people joining long soft-power activities (like Muay Thai or Thai cooking courses). It allows up to 180 days per entry, extendable once, and generally requires around 500,000 THB in available funds.
 What is the difference between the LTR and Thailand Privilege (Elite) visa? 
 The LTR is a 10-year visa granted by category &mdash; wealthy global citizens, pensioners, work-from-Thailand professionals and highly-skilled professionals &mdash; and comes with tax and convenience benefits. The Thailand Privilege visa is a paid membership (from roughly 650,000 THB) that grants long-stay rights and concierge perks without needing to meet an income category. We will advise which suits you.
 Can NuoBello handle my visa renewals and 90-day reporting? 
 Yes. We manage the full lifecycle &mdash; extensions, re-entry permits and 90-day reporting &mdash; so you never miss a deadline or a trip to immigration.
 Do you help with more than visas? 
 Absolutely. Once your visa is sorted, we support property, banking, business setup, tax, healthcare and everything involved in settling into life in Phuket.
