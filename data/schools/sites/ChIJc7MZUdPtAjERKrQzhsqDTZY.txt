 Welcome to St. Andrews International School, Green Valley
 At Green Valley, learning isn’t just about books and classrooms — it’s about exploring, discovering, and growing in a beautiful, green environment where every child is valued and supported.
 “Our students don’t just excel academically—they develop a love for learning, confidence to take on challenges, and friendships that last a lifetime.” 
 We offer an outstanding IGCSE and IB programme. In 2025, our IB graduates achieved a 95% pass rate, with a top score of 42 and an average of 34—well above the global average .
Green Valley is a place where students thrive, dream big, and build a future they’re excited about through personalised learning, small class sizes, and a strong sense of community.

 Discover A School Like No Other
 We believe that education should empower every child to thrive — academically, emotionally, and socially. Set in a unique natural green campus our school blends 3 core strength and values that set us apart:
 Success Beyond the Grades
 At Green Valley, academic excellence is just the beginning. With personalised pathways, small classes, and inspiring teachers, our students achieve outstanding IGCSE and IB results. 95% of our graduates attend their best-fit universities — but more importantly, they leave with the skills, confidence, and values to thrive in life, not just in exams.
 Learning That Sparks Joy
 We inspire curiosity and a lifelong love for discovery through hands-on learning and over 80 opportunities in sports, arts, leadership, and sustainability. From Forest School to student-led projects, our students explore passions, uncover new talents, and grow as creative, critical thinkers.
 Strong Community, Lasting Connections
 Our inclusive community is built on respect, trust, and care—for each other and the planet. Students, parents, and teachers work together to create a nurturing environment where every child feels known, valued, and inspired. These close connections help students thrive and be part of the wider community.
 The Heart of Green Valley Education 
 Be Adventurous . Be Brilliant . Be Happy . This is more than a motto! It’s the heart of our school culture. Every lesson, experience, and challenge helps students live these values, growing into confident, well-rounded individuals ready to make their mark.
 Be Adventurous – We encourage students to step beyond their comfort zones, explore new passions, and embrace challenges. From outdoor expeditions to creative projects and leadership roles, they learn to take bold steps toward the future.
 Be Brilliant – Excellence means more than strong academics. Through a personalised approach, we nurture curiosity, determination, and a love of learning, helping students shine in the classroom, on stage, in sports, and beyond.
 Be Happy – Success begins with happiness and belonging. At Green Valley, students feel seen, supported, and celebrated. Our students form lasting friendships and thrive emotionally, socially, and academically.

 Work For Us 
 St Andrews International School, Green Valley is an outstanding school situated on the Eastern Seaboard conveniently located between Pattaya and Rayong. With around 450 students, from over 30 nationalities, we have a strong community family feel, while still being able to offer the opportunities and diversity of a larger school.
We provide a stimulating, child-centred learning environment for our students, offering the best of international learning by integrating the British curriculum with the inquiry based International Baccalaureate Programme (IB PYP and IB Diploma). Green Valley is the first and only Gold level (outstanding in all areas) on the Eastern Seaboard, awarded by independent inspectors.
