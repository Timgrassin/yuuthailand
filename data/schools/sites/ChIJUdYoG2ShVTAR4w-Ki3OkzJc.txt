 Private and Group Thai Cooking Classes on Koh Tao 
 Learn Authentic Thai Cooking You Can Recreate at Home
 Small-group and private Thai cooking classes on Koh Tao with local instructor Parawan &mdash; learn real Thai flavours, authentic techniques, and how to recreate dishes confidently back home.
 Learn how to cook amazing Thai food in a real Thai cooking class in Koh Tao &mdash; with a friendly local teacher, at my rooftop home cooking school in Sairee Village. I'm Parawan, and I teach small-group and private lessons where you choose 3 dishes from the class menu including curries, stir-fries, noodle dishes, soups, and fresh curry pastes.
 Classes are hands-on, beginner-friendly, and designed to help you understand how Thai food actually works &mdash; not just how to follow recipes step-by-step.
 Many visitors on Koh Tao want to learn something meaningful during their holiday, and a Thai cooking class is one of the most popular things to do on Koh Tao besides diving. During the class you'll learn how to balance sweet, sour, salty, and spicy flavours while cooking authentic Thai dishes you can recreate at home.
 Pad Kra Pao with fried egg &mdash; one of the most popular dishes guests learn during class.
 My Thai cooking school is located on the rooftop of my house in Sairee Village, Koh Tao, with fresh ingredients, your own cooking station, and a relaxed island atmosphere. Group classes (1&ndash;7 people), private classes (1&ndash;6 people), and online Thai cooking lessons are available every day.
 If you are searching for the best Thai cooking class on Koh Tao &mdash; this is a friendly, personal experience where you learn real Thai family recipes, understand substitutions, taste fresh herbs from southern Thailand, and cook with confidence.
 About me: I am Parawan &mdash; a friendly, fun teacher who loves Thai food culture and keeps learning new cooking techniques. I teach group classes and private classes at my home in Koh Tao and also offer one-to-one online lessons.
 I'm originally from Kalasin in northeast Thailand, later studied and worked in Bangkok, and moved to Koh Tao about 15 years ago with my family.
 Over the years I've adapted my teaching style based on feedback from students visiting from around the world. I discovered that many guests wanted to recreate authentic Thai dishes once they returned home &mdash; even if they couldn't easily find every Thai ingredient where they live.
 That's why I teach not only traditional Thai cooking techniques, but also which ingredients are essential, which can be substituted, and how to adapt recipes while still keeping authentic Thai flavours.
 So I decided to open my own Thai cooking school and do something I truly love &mdash; now it doesn't even feel like work anymore!
 Designed For Small Hands-On Thai Cooking Classes
 I converted the roof space of my house into a purpose-built area for my Thai cooking classes on Koh Tao. I can comfortably teach up to seven people in a class, with enough space for everyone to prepare ingredients safely and cook comfortably.
 Each person has their own prep area and cooking station with cutting boards, knives, cooking equipment, and access to the stove. The open layout allows me to demonstrate each step clearly before students repeat it at their own stations.
 Popular dishes taught in class include:
 Pad Thai
 Thai Spring Rolls
 Tom Yum Soup 
 Thai Green Curry
 Massaman Curry
 Panang Curry 
 Thai Red Curry
 Homemade Thai curry paste from scratch
 How Group Class Booking Works
 Group Classes are shared cooking classes where individual travellers, couples, or small parties join together in the same class.
 Private Classes are booked exclusively for you, your family, or your own group.
 The first person to book a Group Class chooses the 3 dishes for everyone in that class to cook together. For Private Classes and Online Classes, you always choose your own dishes.

 Thai Cooking Class FAQ
 How long is the cooking class?
 Most classes take around 2.5 to 3.5 hours, depending on the dishes selected and the pace of the group.
 What time do group classes start?
 Group classes usually start at 9:30am or 2:00pm. Some private classes can also start at 6:30pm.
 How does the menu work?
 The first person to book a group class chooses the three dishes for everyone to cook. Once a class is booked, the selected menu is shown on my public calendar above.
 Where is the cooking class in Koh Tao?
 The class is at my home in Sairee Village, Koh Tao. After you book, you'll get clear directions and the exact location details.
 Is it suitable for beginners?
 Yes &mdash; it's beginner-friendly. I'll guide you step-by-step, and you'll learn techniques you can use again at home.
 Can you cater for vegetarian or vegan diets?
 Yes. I can adapt many dishes for vegetarian, vegan, and pescatarian preferences &mdash; just let me know when you book.
 Do you offer private classes?
 Yes. Private classes are available for couples, families, and groups. You can choose your preferred start time (subject to availability), with extra availability for evening private classes.
 Do you have online Thai cooking classes?
 Yes &mdash; online classes are available worldwide. Timing is flexible and can work across different time zones (subject to availability).
 How do I book?
 Book on the website using the booking form below to see live availability and class options. If you're unsure what to pick, message me and I'll be happy help.
