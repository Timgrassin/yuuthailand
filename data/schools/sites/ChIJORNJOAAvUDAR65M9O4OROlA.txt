 About Kru Marn 
 A champion's experience, passed on one student at a time
 Kru Marn (ครูหม่าน) is a former Southern Thailand Champion with a professional record of 180 fights and 150 victories. After more than 15 years of teaching, he is one of the most respected private Muay Thai trainers in Phuket.
 He trains his students at Dragon Muay Thai on Phuket's famous Fitness Street (Soi Ta-iad), teaching authentic Muay Thai the traditional way: powerful strikes, knees, elbows and clinch work. Fighters, fitness enthusiasts and complete beginners from all over the world come to him for genuine Thai boxing skills.
 His approach combines everything he learned in the ring with modern training methods. Every session is private and one-on-one, adapted to your level and your goals, whether you're preparing for a fight or throwing your first kick.

 Frequently Asked Questions 
 Everything you need to know before your first session
 Do I need experience to train with Kru Marn?
 No. All training is private 1-on-1 and all levels are welcome: complete beginners, fitness enthusiasts and competitive fighters. Every session is adapted to your level and goals.
 How much does private Muay Thai training cost in Phuket?
 A single session costs ฿700. A 20-session package costs ฿14,000, which is ฿700 per session. The padholder course costs ฿12,000 for 10 one-hour sessions and includes an official certificate.
 Where does the training take place?
 At Dragon Muay Thai on Fitness Street (Soi Ta-iad) in Phuket, Thailand, the island's best-known training street. Training equipment is included.
 How do I book a session with Kru Marn?
 Message Kru Marn on WhatsApp at +66 86 268 8174 . That is the best way to reach him. He can work around your schedule.
