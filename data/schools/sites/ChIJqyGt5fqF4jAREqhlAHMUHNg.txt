 BUILT BY FAMILY. 
SHAPED BY TWO TRADITIONS. 
 Camp Holland was built on a simple belief: strong people are not built alone. Founded by Pahtee Maurice Awuy&mdash;a former soldier, fighter, coach, and father&mdash;the camp is grounded in discipline, responsibility, and respect.
 We combine the technical precision of Dutch kickboxing with the heart and fighting culture of Muay Thai in Thailand. Serious enough for fighters, welcoming enough for complete beginners, children, and families.
 Whether your goal is competition, fitness, confidence, or simply becoming more capable, start where you are. Train with purpose. Grow together.
