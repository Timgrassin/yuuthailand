 Charldon (From South Africa)
 Native English speaker 
 Loves helping others learn languages 
 Has been teaching for over 8 years 
 Charldon holds a degree in teaching English and specializes in helping students master everyday English, including grammar, conversation, and business English. He creates a comfortable and fun classroom environment, encouraging students to speak freely. Charldon enjoys meeting students from different countries and watching them improve their English skills.
 Setthawut (From Thailand)
 Native Thai speaker 
 Has been teaching for over 11 years 
 Makes learning fun and easy 
 Setthawut excels in teaching Thai reading and writing. His classes are active and engaging, with plenty of practice opportunities. He uses humor and hand movements to make lessons memorable. He is passionate about sharing his love of the Thai language with his students. 
 Chlida (From Thailand)
 Native Thai speaker 
 Has been teaching for over 17 years 
 Holds a master’s degree in language studies 
 Chlida is an expert in Thai pronunciation and the Thai alphabet. She simplifies complex topics into easy-to-follow steps. Although she may seem strict at first, her friendly personality and sense of humor quickly put students at ease. She loves seeing her students progress in their Thai language skills. 
 Kee (From Canada)
 Native English speaker 
 Teaches students of all ages 
 Has been teaching for 8 years 
 Kee studied English in college and teaches both online and in person. He tailors lessons to suit each student’s unique learning style. Outside of teaching, Kee enjoys music, food, and sports. He values learning about different cultures while helping students improve their English. 
 Dale (From England)
 Native English speaker 
 Engages all students in class 
 Has been teaching for over 10 years 
 Dale taught in England for two years before moving to Thailand in 2009. Dale’s friendly teaching style helps students feel comfortable, ensuring everyone participates. Outside of teaching, Dale enjoys football, swimming, and concerts, and spending time with family and friends. 
 John (From Australia)
 Native English speaker 
 Specializes in the direct method of teaching 
 Has been teaching for over 20 years 
 John has over 20 years of experience teaching English and enjoys helping students develop their own ideas and think critically. He’s an expert in the direct method, which focuses on creating a lively, interactive classroom where students actively build their language skills. 
 Tony (From England)
 Native English speaker 
 Teaches children and adults 
 Has been teaching for over 9 years 
 Tony has extensive experience teaching students of all ages both online and in person. He emphasizes building strong connections with students to enhance learning. Outside of teaching, Tony enjoys traveling, exploring new cultures, and shopping for stylish clothing. 
 Ong (From Thailand)
 Native Thai speaker 
 Fluent in Thai, English, and Japanese 
 Has been teaching for over 20 years 
 Ong has over 20 years of experience teaching Thai to diverse learners, including business professionals and public officials. She excels at preparing students for Thai language tests and tailoring her teaching to meet various learning needs. 
 Midori Tsubata (From Japan)
 Native Japanese speaker 
 Over 19 years of teaching experience 
 Extensive international background 
 Midori has been teaching Japanese for nearly two decades across Thailand, Malaysia, and the Philippines. She holds a degree in International Cultural Studies from Kyushu Sangyo University and completed the 420-hour Japanese Language Teacher Training Course at Washington Foreign Language Institute.
 Her experience includes teaching at Maejo University, Imperial Malaysia Japanese Language School, JFE Techno Manila, and King Mongkut’s Institute of Technology, Ladkrabang. A former JICA volunteer, Midori is passionate about cultural exchange and education. Her lessons combine professionalism, warmth, and global perspective — helping students not only learn Japanese but also appreciate its culture.
 Brendon (From England)
 Native English speaker 
 Creates personalized lessons 
 Has been teaching for over 10 years 
 Yuki has been teaching Japanese since 2017 in Japan and Taiwan. He holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering from Meiji University and passed the Japanese Language Teaching Competency Test in 2016.
 His experience includes teaching at language schools in Tokyo, working as a full-time instructor in Tainan, Taiwan, and providing online lessons to international students. He has taught learners from beginner to advanced level, both in small group classes and one-to-one settings.
 Yuki’s teaching motto is “fun and usable Japanese.” His lessons focus on conversation and real-life application, helping students actively use what they learn. He designs his own visual materials, incorporates role play and practical speaking tasks, and creates a relaxed atmosphere where students feel comfortable speaking.
 In addition to textbook content, Yuki introduces natural expressions, cultural topics, and everyday communication styles that students are genuinely curious about. With experience teaching in Japanese, English, and Chinese, he brings flexibility, cultural awareness, and a global perspective to every class.
 Chris
 Native English speaker 
 IELTS & Business English specialist 
 Over 15 years of teaching experience 
 Chris has over 15 years of international teaching experience across Asia, working with students, professionals, and corporate clients. He specializes in General English, IELTS preparation, and Business English, and has extensive experience creating lessons tailored to each learner’s goals. He also holds CELTA, TESOL, and TEFL qualifications and has trained professionals from major international companies.
 Why Choose GEOS?
 GEOS Thailand is among the leading language schools in the country. 
 1.
 Fantastic location in Bangkok&#8217;s Central Business District and only a short walk from Asoke BTS and Sukhumvit MTRT. 
 GEOS Thailand is located on the 22nd floor of the prestigious Jasmine Building in the centre of Bangkok. Our location is extremely convenient with nearby stations for both the BTS and MRT.
 2.
 Qualified and passionate native teachers.
 We work hard to find the best native-speaking teachers for our school. Each and every GEOS teacher is carefully selected for their skills, knowledge, and passion. 
 3.
 Flexible learning schedules. 
 We know that time is of the essence and offer our students the option to choose flexible learning schedules at times that are convenient to them. We accept lesson requests in as little as 24 hours in advance. 
 4.
 Guaranteed results (and fun!).
 All of our courses are carefully designed and planned to assist you with achieving your language goals. With a combination of world-class learning materials, fun games, and attentive teachers, we guarantee you will vastly improve your language skills with GEOS.
