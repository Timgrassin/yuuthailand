 What is included in a Thai cooking class in Phuket? 
 A  Phuket Thai cooking class at Phuket Thai Cooking Academy is a complete, hands-on experience designed to teach you real Thai cooking from start to finish.
 Here’s what is included:
 Hands-on cooking class Learn step-by-step with your own workstation. This is not a demonstration. You cook every dish yourself.
 4 authentic Thai dishes Choose from a variety of classic Thai recipes and prepare a full meal from scratch.
 Market tour Visit a local fresh market to learn about Thai ingredients, herbs, and how locals shop.
 All ingredients and equipment Everything is prepared for you, including fresh ingredients, cooking tools, and recipes.
 Professional Thai chefs Learn from experienced instructors who guide you through each step clearly and efficiently.
 Eat what you cook Enjoy your dishes at the end of the class in a relaxed setting.
 Hotel transportation Free pickup and drop-off from most areas in Phuket for a smooth, stress-free experience.
 Recipes Take home access recipes so you can recreate authentic Thai cooking anywhere.
 This Thai cooking class Phuket experience is designed for beginners, couples, families, and solo travelers looking for a fun and authentic activity in Phuket.

 Do I get to eat the food I cook? 
 Yes. In our Thai cooking class , you will enjoy all the dishes you prepare.
 After cooking, you sit down and eat your full meal in a relaxed setting. Since everything is hands-on , you cook each dish yourself, so you get to experience the results of your own work.
 If you prefer, you can also take your food home. This is part of the full Phuket cooking class experience at Phuket Thai Cooking Academy.
 How long does a Thai cooking class in Phuket last? 
 A Thai cooking class  at Phuket Thai Cooking Academy typically lasts about 3.5 to 4 hours , depending on the session you choose.
 This includes preparation, hands-on cooking of 4 dishes, and time to sit down and enjoy your meal.
 The schedule is designed to give you a complete Thai cooking class experience without feeling rushed, while still fitting easily into your day in Phuket.
 Do you offer hotel pickup for Thai cooking classes in Phuket? 
 Yes, Free transportation is provided for Patong, Phuket Town, and Kathu.  Free pick up and drop off provided for 3 or more guests from anywhere on Phuket Island.  For 1-2 students outside of our complimentary service area, an additional fee of 400 Baht/pax will apply.
