 WHo we are 
 BX12, Bangkoks premium western boxing gym was launched in 2021. A gym born from passion, BX12 welcomes individuals from all levels from beginners to pros. A place where championship mindsets are made through hard work and dedication. A community where anyone can train and forge bonds and better themselves in a positive environment. We believe through boxing we can inspire individuals to be the best version of themselves, making them champions in all aspects of life. "If we can do it, you can too".
