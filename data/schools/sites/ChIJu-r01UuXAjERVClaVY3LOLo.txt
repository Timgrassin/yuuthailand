 Welcome 
 Offshore Migration Agents Specialise in ICT, Science and Technology Skilled migration from Russia, Ukraine, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Romania, Georgia and CIS counties.
Should you have any immediate queries in relation to migration to Australia please contact us using the contact form provided.
You can also find us on vkontakte and facebook

 Your pathway to successful Migration to Australia 
 We aim to help steer you through the mind field of paperwork , documentation and legal requirements to ensure that both the employer and the employee achieve their preferred outcomes and pathway to success 
 Tourist Visa 
 Subclass 600 Business, Tourism, Conference or family reunion visas
Our goal is to guide you though the application process to maximise your success and remove the uncertainly. 
 Partner, Family Visas. 
 Prospective spouse visas. Separation can be the hardest time. Our staff have the experience in putting your application together to be successful the first time and to avoid having to lodge appeals for review 
 Skilled Visa Support 
 Employer sponsored ENS, RMS, 482, 186, 187,SkillSelect 189, 190, Independent or State sponsored Visas. If your migrating to Australia or you want to employ migrant workers we can assist you in understanding the government&#039;s procedures and requirements that best meet your situation. 
 We offer a knowledge base solution to offshore immigrants seeking to travel or migrate to Australia
