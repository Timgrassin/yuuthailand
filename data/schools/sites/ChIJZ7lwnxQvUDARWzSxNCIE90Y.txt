 WHY TRAIN WITH US?
 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
 IS YOUR GYM BEGINNER FRIENDLY?
 Yes, absolutely! We cater to all levels. If you're a complete beginner, our trainers will work with you on the basics first (you won't be thrown straight into sparring - don't worry!)
 If you want to get some experience before joining our group classes then you can consider doing a private session with any of our trainers.
 DO YOU PROVIDE TRAINING GEAR?
 We have hand wraps, gloves and shin pads that you can borrow from the gym, however if you plan to train with us often then we'd highly recommend purchasing your own gear. You can check out our shop at the gym.
 WILL YOU BE ABLE TO GET ME A FIGHT?
 It's very easy to get a fight here in Thailand, whether it's at the local stadiums or part of an amateur event. But first, our gym owner Jack will assess your skill & fitness level before letting you jump in the ring. 
 We want to make sure you're ready and we get you a fair match up! 
 If you're already an experienced fighter, you'll likely be invited to our fighters class where you can train to either fight at a local stadium, or one of the bigger promotions.
