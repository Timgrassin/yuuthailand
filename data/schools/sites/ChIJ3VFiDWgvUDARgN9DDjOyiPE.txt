 Choose Your Training 
 CHOOSE YOUR TRAINING 
 Classes 
 At Uppercuts, you are not just another person in a large class. Our trainers take the time to understand your experience level, goals and individual training needs, providing guidance and personal attention throughout every session. 
 Our classes teach the traditional fundamentals of Muay Thai, including stance and footwork, punches, elbows, kicks, defense and the technical intricacies of the Muay Thai clinch. 
 Each session is designed to improve your fitness, strength, coordination, confidence and overall technique. 
 Training includes warm-ups, shadowboxing, technical drills, pad work, bag work, conditioning and stretching. 
 Complete beginners are guided step by step, while regular students and experienced fighters are challenged to refine their technique, timing, speed and power.

 Private - 1 hour 
 Book a private session tailored to your experience, fitness level and individual goals. Whether you are stepping into a Muay Thai gym for the first time, looking to improve your fitness or preparing for a fight, your trainer will customize the session around what you want to achieve. 
 Focus on pad work while refining your technique, timing, speed, accuracy and power, while getting an effective high-intensity workout at the same time. Sessions can cover any or all of the traditional Muay Thai fundamentals, including punches, elbows, kicks, knees, defense, footwork and clinching. 
 Private training gives you more time for individual feedback, focused repetition and detailed technical corrections. For fighters, sessions can be tailored toward specific areas of fight preparation.

 Fight preparation 
 Whether this is your first fight or your 30th fight, we will help you enter the ring ready. 
 Be prepared to train hard and then train even harder. Your training will focus on developing the conditioning, stamina, technique, confidence and competitive edge needed for fight night. Sessions may include intensive pad work, sparring, clinching, defensive drills, fight specific combinations and physical conditioning. 
 We will tailor your preparation to your experience, goals and areas that need improvement, helping you build the physical and mental readiness required to perform under pressure. 
 As part of your preparation, you can learn the traditional Wai Kru, performed before each fight to show respect to your trainers, family, gym and the traditions of Muay Thai. 
 When fight night arrives, we will be in your corner to support you. We can also help coordinate suitable fight opportunities based on your experience, preparation and readiness. We will give you the best possible fight experience. 
 Train with purpose. 
 Respect the tradition. 
 Fight with confidence. 
 We are in your corner.

 Meet our Kru 
 MEET OUR KRU 
 Meet our experienced Kru, who bring authentic Muay Thai knowledge, skill and passion to Uppercuts. Our trainers combine strong, supportive coaching with plenty of energy and personality. Training is focused and challenging. 
 Expect encouragement, loud motivation, lots of smiles and Kru who will keep pushing you to give your best. 
 Kru K 
 Kru K is Uppercuts' lead trainer, bringing 35 years of Muay Thai experience and over 150 fights throughout his career. 
 His decades in Muay Thai have shaped a highly technical coaching style built on fundamentals and fighter development. Kru K has a sharp eye for detail and a demanding approach that keeps every student focused on improving. 
 Come ready to work hard because Kru K will know if you are trying to sneak in an easy round! 
 Kru Mang 
 Kru Mang has over 22 years of experience in Muay Thai and brings a wealth of experience from more than 250 fights. His extensive experience gives him a deep understanding of technique, timing, strategy and ring awareness. 
 Known for his upbeat personality and high-energy coaching style, Mang will keep you moving, keep your technique sharp and push you to give your best. You may not literally crawl home, but you will certainly feel like you earned your session! 
 Kru Nes 
 Kru Nes brings over 24 years of Muay Thai experience and more than 300 fights throughout his career. His impressive fight history includes championship success, competing in the prestigious 16-man tournament at Lumpinee Boxing Stadium, and fighting for ONE Championship. 
 Kru Nes brings a hardworking, focused approach to every session and expects the same commitment from his students. His coaching is energetic, highly technical and full of personality, with plenty of attention to timing, strategy and the finer details that take your training to the next level. 
 He will push you to work hard, sharpen your technique and make sure there are no lazy rounds on his watch. You will work hard, you will be exhausted, and somehow you will still leave smiling! 
 ​ 
 Kru Beer 
 Kru Beer brings over 20 years of Muay Thai experience and more than 200 fights in his career. His extensive time in the sport has given him a strong foundation in traditional Muay Thai technique, timing and conditioning. 
 Beer brings a positive and supportive energy to every session, helping students build confidence, sharpen their technique and keep progressing while still having fun!

 Our Uppercuts Story 
 More Than A Gym 
 Uppercuts Muay Thai was built with more than training in mind. From the beginning , we wanted to create a gym that could support our local Thai community, celebrate Thai culture and help grow the next generation of Muay Thai. 
 ​ 
 Some of our Kru began fighting as young as 8 years old. Along the way, they were guided, encouraged and mentored by positive coaches who helped shape their skills, discipline, character and love for the sport. We are grateful for those who invested their time and knowledge in our trainers, and today they are proud to pass that same support, knowledge and respect for Muay Thai traditions on to the next generation. 
 ​ 
 Our community spirit goes beyond the ring. Kru K, our lead trainer, discovered his passion for cutting hair more than 10 years ago. That passion became Uppercuts Barber, located right here at the gym. It is another part of what makes Uppercuts feel like home, a place where people can train, connect, share stories and be a part of the local community. 
 We are proud to help young athletes learn Muay Thai, build confidence and discipline, and develop their skills both inside and outside the ring. For us, this is more than training. It is about giving back to the sport, the culture and the community that helped shape who we are. Together, we are the Uppercuts Muay Thai family.
