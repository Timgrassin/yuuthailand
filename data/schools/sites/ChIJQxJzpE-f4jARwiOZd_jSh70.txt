 Our School
 We are delighted to introduce you to the Dadi Trilingual Kindergarten; the first truly trilingual English-Chinese-Thai preschool in Thailand.
 Dadi Trilingual Kindergarten is a joint venture between the China based Dadi International Preschool Group and a Thai-British educational initiative that aims to bring the best of educational practices to Thailand.
 The Dadi International Preschool Group was founded in Taiwan in 1985 and is currently operating over 670 preschools in 45 major cities across mainland China. The Dadi group currently caters to the educational needs of more than 400,000 children and is still expanding.
 During your child&#8217;s critical early years, they are forming the vital foundation for future learning. In our changing modern world, where an increased global perspective and language fluency is the key to success, we believe it is important for your child to immerse themselves in exploration that focuses on this need.
 At Dadi Trilingual Kindergarten, we believe that in order for children to develop their full potential, they should enjoy and be willing to open themselves up to the learning experience. By providing a friendly and welcoming environment, set aside from the hectic pace of urban Bangkok, we have provided the ideal environment for your child to grow and flourish. All of our classes are taught by highly experienced and qualified native-language speakers, and we aim to give an equal balance between classes taught in English and Chinese in order that your child will develop proficiency in both languages.
 The fun and varied activities we provide stimulate interest and curiosity in children and foster in them a life-long love of learning. We keep all of our classes small to allow us to fully cater to your child&#8217;s individual needs and enable us to provide the personal care and attention that is crucial to development.

 These three pillars articulate the core benefits and distinguishing features of Dadi Trilingual Kindergarten, reflecting our unique Chinese-Thai-British collaboration and trilingual educational model:
 Trilingual Education:
 Fostering fluency in English, Chinese and Thai from an early age, equipping children with global communication skills.
 Whole-child Development:
 Our curriculum extends beyond academics, nurturing emotional, social, physical, and cognitive growth.
 Global Mindset:
 Preparing children to thrive in an interconnected world, encouraging curiosity, adaptability, and cross-cultural understanding.
 Our curriculum itself reflects the cooperative nature of the school. The English language curriculum is based on the British Early Years Foundation Stage with a key focus on child-centered learning through the exploration of distinct themes and activities. Our Chinese program is the result of the culmination of research by the Dadi International Preschool Group and incorporates an equally child-centered approach to learning through structured language activities, Abacus Maths, MIT and Brain Training techniques to fully develop your child&#8217;s potential.
