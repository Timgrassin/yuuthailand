Kindergarten 
 Welcome to Storytime Preschool Kindergarten Bangkok. Storytime Preschool Kindergarten has been established in Bangkok area for over 25 years and is committed to bringing the highest standards of education in a positive, stimulating and fun environment. Preschool is an important time for a child's development and at Storytime Preschool Kindergarten, we have carefully structured our approach to encourage curiosity and promote a harmonious learning experience. 
 Accreditation 
 Licensed by the Ministry of Child and Social Welfare in 2003, our name is synonymous with quality child care. Our founders have over 30 years of experience & continue to bring the highest quality of education, safety & fun to our students. 
 Qualification 
 We're proud to share that our dedicated staff and teachers are experts in early childhood education, with some of them bringing up to 25 years of experience to Storytime Preschool. To ensure we maintain an international standard of excellence, we provide regular workshop training for our team. 
 Communication 
 At Storytime Preschool, we are committed to open communication with parents. We provide daily updates through our "My Day Book," and we also keep you informed through Parent-Teacher Meetings, Reports, Menus, and Newsletters. 
 Values 
 We support students to become the best versions of themselves and we believe play is extremely important in young child development.
