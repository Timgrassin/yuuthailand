 Questions people ask before they start
 What is DISC?
 DISC is a well-known model of behavioural style with four dimensions: Dominance, Influence, Steadiness and Conscientiousness. It describes how you tend to act and communicate at work, not how smart or capable you are. There are no good or bad profiles.
 How long does it take?
 About 10 minutes for 32 short questions. Your progress is saved on your device, so you can stop and continue later.
 What do I get?
 Your profile (one of 17), your scores on the four styles, your strengths, watch-outs, communication style, development tips and roles that suit you. You see it straight away, it is emailed to you, and you can save it as a PDF.
 Can I take it in Thai?
 Yes. The questions and the report are available in English and Thai; switch language at the top of the test.
 What happens with my data?
 We follow the Thai PDPA. Your result is kept for 13 weeks so you can open your report again, and every report email has a link to delete your data at any time.
 Can I get a refund?
 Yes, within 14 days if you have not started the test, and always if something went wrong on our side. See our refund policy or ask support .
