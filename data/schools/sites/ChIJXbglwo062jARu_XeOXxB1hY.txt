 Sports medicine-based massage training 
 RSM is a Sports Medicine Massage School
 Train to Be a Top Therapist. At RSM, this means connecting functional anatomy, clinical palpation and movement assessment with precise manual skills, and explaining why you choose each technique.
 Build practical skills in movement assessment, clinical palpation and targeted massage to support pain relief and posture correction through hands-on English-language training in Chiang Mai. Classes of up to 14 are led by Hironori Ikeda, MSc Sports Medicine, with over 30 years of clinical and teaching experience.
 RSM’s core courses follow a required progression. Trigger Point Therapy (TPT) comes first, establishing clinical palpation and muscle-function assessment. Deep Tissue Massage (DTM) requires prior TPT; Dynamic Myofascial Release (DMR) follows completion of both TPT and DTM. Each core course runs for five weekdays (20 hours) and costs THB 12,000. This sequence develops technique selection from assessed findings rather than offering three interchangeable options. Check the course pages and current schedule for dates and full entry requirements before applying.

 Trigger Point Therapy Course for Chronic Pain Relief 
 Trigger Point Therapy Course Chiang Mai, Thailand
 Trigger Point Therapy (TPT) is the starting point for RSM’s core manual-therapy pathway. At RSM International Academy in Chiang Mai, Thailand, students connect functional anatomy, clinical palpation and muscle-function assessment with a reasoned technique choice.
 Practical work compares the person’s history, a relevant movement task, range of motion, muscle function and palpation findings. Students identify anatomical landmarks, choose contact and pressure direction, and repeat the same task after a focused intervention. A tender point alone is not treated as a diagnosis.
 Hironori Ikeda, MSc Sports Medicine, gives direct feedback on palpation and technique. The five-day course is 20 hours at THB 12,000. It provides the required foundation for DTM and, later, DMR. Check current dates and confirm entry requirements before booking.
 Deep Tissue Massage Course for Pain Relief and Postural Correction 
 Deep Tissue Massage Course Chiang Mai, Thailand
 Deep Tissue Massage (DTM) develops deep-tissue technique selection and pressure control for qualified practitioners who have completed TPT. Students compare posture and movement, range of motion, muscle function and palpation findings before selecting a focused approach.
 Supervised practice develops control of contact, direction, depth, speed and therapist weight transfer. Students consider pelvic, hip, thoracic and shoulder movement, then repeat the same task to judge whether the next step should continue or change. Posture alone does not establish the cause of pain or nerve symptoms.
 Hironori Ikeda provides direct practical corrections. DTM is five days, 20 hours, at THB 12,000, and includes the integrated Deep Shiatsu material. The optional Remedial Massage afternoon component adds five hours at THB 6,000. View dates and confirm prerequisites and places .
 Dynamic Myofascial Release: Mobility, Movement and Reassessment
 Dynamic Myofascial Release Course Chiang Mai, Thailand
 Dynamic Myofascial Release (DMR) combines movement, stretching and soft-tissue techniques after completion of both TPT and DTM. The five-day, 20-hour course costs THB 12,000. Read the course page for the full experience requirements.
 Students use clinical palpation, range of motion, muscle function and a defined movement task to select contact, direction, speed and load. They coordinate movement with soft-tissue work, monitor the response and repeat the same task. Joint mobilization is selected within training and scope, with non-thrust practical techniques.
 Hironori Ikeda gives direct feedback on therapist positioning and technique selection. The student benefit is a repeatable process for explaining and adjusting practical work from assessed findings. Sports Medicine Massage provides a related afternoon application option. Check current dates before applying .
 Dynamic Posture Assessment & Kinetic Chain Training Course 
 Posture Correction Studio Chiang Mai, Thailand
 Dynamic Posture Assessment & Kinetic Chain Training develops observation and comparison of movement, loading strategies and task-specific responses. It is relevant to massage practitioners, sports coaches, personal trainers and yoga or Pilates instructors who work with movement.
 Students record and compare their own movement through practical and video analysis. Breathing, trunk control, weight transfer and balance tasks provide ways to examine how a movement changes under different conditions. Static alignment alone is not used to establish the cause of pain.
 Hironori Ikeda connects functional anatomy with exercise selection and practical feedback. The two-day course is eight hours at THB 7,000, including gym admission for both days, round-trip taxi transport to and from the gym, and movement video recording and analysis. View current dates and confirm suitability and available places .

 Sports Massage Course for Injury Prevention and Performance Enhancement 
 Sports Massage Course Chiang Mai, Thailand
      Sports Massage develops the selection and timing of manual techniques for an athlete’s training and competition needs. It follows the Trigger Point Therapy course during the same teaching week.
 Students compare the reported problem, training load, range of motion, muscle function and a sporting task. They choose contact, pressure, direction and duration for a defined purpose, then repeat the relevant task. Case discussions can include calf, knee, hip, shoulder and neck presentations without assuming that one technique fits every athlete.
 Hironori Ikeda supervises practice and gives direct corrections. Afternoon sessions are Monday–Friday, 15:10–16:10, five hours at THB 6,000. TPT plus Sports Massage totals 25 teaching hours at THB 18,000. Check dates and entry requirements .
 Remedial Massage Course: Personalized Approaches for Long-Term Pain Relief and Postural Correction 
 Remedial Massage Course Chiang Mai, Thailand
      Remedial Massage develops assessment-led treatment planning after Deep Tissue Massage. Students compare the reported symptoms, movement, range of motion, muscle function and palpation findings to form a practical hypothesis.
 Case discussions explore upper- and lower-limb, hip, back and neck presentations. Students distinguish local muscle or joint findings from signs that may require further medical assessment, rather than diagnosing nerve entrapment from posture or tenderness. They adjust contact, direction, pressure and load, then repeat the same task.
 Hironori Ikeda gives direct feedback during supervised practice. Sessions run Monday–Friday, 15:10–16:10. DTM plus Remedial Massage totals 25 teaching hours at THB 18,000; prior TPT completion remains required. View dates and confirm eligibility .
 Sports Medicine Massage Course: Biomechanics and Kinetic Chain Assessment for Enhanced Client Outcomes 
 Sports Medicine Massage Course Chiang Mai, Thailand
 Sports Medicine Massage integrates assessment and manual skills for practitioners with relevant sporting or clinical experience. It extends the DMR week through a focused afternoon application course.
 Students compare the person’s history, training and competition load, range of motion, muscle function, palpation and a sporting task. They choose an appropriate soft-tissue or non-thrust manual technique, adjust contact, pressure and movement, and reassess the same task. Hironori Ikeda provides direct correction and asks students to explain the reasoning behind their choices.
 The afternoon component runs Monday–Friday, 15:10–16:10, five hours at THB 6,000. DMR plus Sports Medicine Massage totals 25 teaching hours at THB 18,000. Prior TPT and DTM study and the required DMR progression apply. Check dates and confirm eligibility and places .

 Deep Shiatsu Massage Course  
 Deep Shiatsu Massage Course Chiang Mai, Thailand
 Deep Shiatsu material has been integrated into Deep Tissue Massage (DTM) . It is no longer offered as a separate weekend course. The Deep Shiatsu guide explains the approach and its place in the current curriculum.
 Students develop controlled contact, therapist posture and weight transfer, comparing traditional Shiatsu methods with functional anatomy and tissue response. They adjust direction, depth and load rather than treating stronger pressure as the goal. Hironori Ikeda gives direct feedback during supervised practice.

 Balance Ball YOGA 
 Balance Ball YOGA posture training
 Balance Ball Yoga combines breathing and movement tasks to explore trunk control, weight transfer, balance responses and spinal mobility. Hironori Ikeda uses functional anatomy and practical demonstrations to connect the exercise with the task being assessed.
 Students compare movement under different support and loading conditions, then adjust the task to the person’s ability and response. Breathing, range of motion and reported symptoms inform the next step. Static alignment or a single movement pattern is not used to establish a diagnosis.
 The learning benefit is a practical way to observe, explain and adapt an exercise. Read the programme page for its current format and contact RSM about suitability and availability .
 What makes RSM International Academy in Chiang Mai different from other massage schools?
 RSM International Academy in Chiang Mai, Thailand, focuses on connecting functional anatomy, clinical palpation, movement assessment and manual techniques. Hironori Ikeda, MSc Sports Medicine, teaches the practical work in regular groups of up to 14 students.
 Students learn a connected process: define a relevant task, compare history and assessment findings, choose an intervention, adjust contact and load, and reassess the same task. Direct correction helps students recognise and explain practical errors rather than simply memorise a sequence.
 The core route begins with TPT , followed by DTM and DMR when their prerequisites are met. The instructor profile and student reviews provide further information about the teaching.
