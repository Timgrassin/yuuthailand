 What is Muay Thai? + 
 Muay Thai, also known as Thai Boxing, is a traditional martial art from Thailand using stand-up striking and clinching techniques. Known worldwide for discipline and effectiveness.
 Do I need previous experience? + 
 None required. Our classes are structured for all skill levels — complete beginners to advanced fighters. Show up and we'll handle the rest.
 Are there age restrictions? + 
 We offer kids classes for ages 6–11 and adult programs for 12 and above. Families are very welcome at our gym.
 What should I bring to my first class? + 
 Sportswear, a towel, and a water bottle. Initial equipment is provided. Just show up ready to train.
 How often to train for results? + 
 2–3 sessions per week is ideal for beginners. More frequent training accelerates progress significantly.
 Can Muay Thai help with weight loss? + 
 Absolutely. Muay Thai is a full-body workout that burns serious calories while building strength, agility, and cardiovascular fitness.
