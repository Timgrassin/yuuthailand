 Why choose UDIS?
 We are a full IB world school with the PYP, MYP and DP programmes fully accredited by CIS
 We develop a love of learning and help learners thrive, think for themselves and ask questions
 We support and challenge all students with small classes, learning support, counselling and careers programmes
 We focus on the development of multilingual learners with strong English skills
 We are an inclusive school who value all of our school cultures and perspectives
 We are friendly and responsive to our community
 Our location
 We are located 10 minutes north of Udon Thani on Mittrapab Road.
 Our story
 UDIS was founded in 2013 and offers creative and inquiry based teaching mixed with the rigour of the National Curriculum of England. We have grown year by year and are now a three programme IB World School authorised to offer the Primary Years Programme (PYP), Middle Years Programme (MYP) and the Diploma Programme (DP). We are fully accredited with CIS and are the first and only CIS accredited in the Northeast. We have been successful in offering a student centered, creative programme where the cultural identify and diversity of our learners is valued.
