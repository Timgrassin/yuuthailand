 About
 Who We Are
 We are a 100% Muay Thai-dedicated gym run by experienced Thai and international coaches. We focus on helping people grow through disciplined training, authentic technique, and a supportive gym culture.
 Our Philosophy
 Muay Thai is more than just a sport – it’s a path to self-mastery. We believe in quality over quantity, in doing the basics right, and in guiding every student toward their full potential, whether they aim to get fit, get sharp, or get in the ring.
 Training
 Beginners
 The perfect starting point for anyone new to Muay Thai. Learn the fundamentals in a safe, structured environment. No prior experience needed.
 What You’ll Learn
 - Stance, guard & basic footwork
- Straight punches, kicks, knees, elbows
- Basic shadow boxing and heavybag training
- Padwork & basic partner drills
- Basic clinch concepts (optional)
- Intro to Thai culture & gym etiquette
 Format
 - Warm-up (light cardio, stretching)
- Technique of the day
- Padwork with coach
- Partner drills
- Conditioning / Cooldown
 Benefits
 - Improved coordination & fitness
- Confidence & mental strength
- Solid Muay Thai foundation

 Advanced
 Take your skills to the next level. Perfect combinations, sharpen timing, and begin controlled sparring. Ideal for consistent hobbyists or returning fighters.
 Focus Areas
 - Advanced striking combinations
- Movement & defense
- Counter-attacks
- Technical sparring & clinch work
- Fight simulation drills
 Format
 - Technical drills
- Partner work
- Light/controlled sparring
- Situational clinching
- Conditioning
 Benefits
 - Tactical understanding
- Enhanced reflexes
- Ring awareness
 Book now 
 Fighters
 A full-scale fight camp experience. Designed for amateur & professional fighters preparing for competition. Includes roadwork, padwork, sparring, clinch, and strength training.
 What’s Included
 - Morning & evening training
- Hard padwork sessions with coach
- Daily sparring & clinch
- Strength & conditioning
- Fight prep support (weight cut, strategy, rituals)
 Add-ons
 - Fight booking (local promotions)
- Coach cornering & wrap
- Wai Kru / Mongkhon ceremony training
- Nutrition & recovery advice
 Requirements
 - Prior experience & fitness level
- Must be approved by coaches to join sparring sessions
