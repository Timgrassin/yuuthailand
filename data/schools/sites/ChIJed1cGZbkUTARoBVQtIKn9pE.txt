 Welcome to RIM TANG COOKING SCHOOL !
 Are you interested in learning how to cook traditional Thai food? We welcome you to RIM TANG COOKING SCHOOL in Koh Jum (also known as Koh Pu) island, Thailand!
 Our chef and the owner of the place, Mrs Pavinee, has over 25 years experience of running a Thai restaurant. She would be very pleased to teach you how to cook traditional Thai food from our well-rounded menu – and she will do it in ENGLISH language 
 Khau-niow-ma-muang, sticky rice with sweet mango and coconut sauce.

 a private lesson to learn to cook Thai dishes from scratch. You may choose the dishes you would like to learn from our wide-ranged menu .
