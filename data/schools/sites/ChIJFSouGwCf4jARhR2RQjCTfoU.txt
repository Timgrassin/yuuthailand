 Business in Thailand 
 Starting a business in Thailand: what foreign founders should consider before registering a company 
 Registering a Thai company is usually the easy part. What needs thought first is how the business will actually operate: what it will do, who will own it, and whether that combination triggers foreign business restrictions, a licence or another route such as BOI promotion. Tax, accounting and your own work authorisation should be planned at the same time. Settle the structure first, then treat registration as carrying out that decision. 
 Visa & Work Permit 
 Changing jobs in Thailand: what happens to your visa and work permit when you resign? 
 If your permission to stay in Thailand was extended because of your job, it ends when that job ends, not on the date stamped in your passport. The Immigration Bureau treats that permission as ending immediately when you stop working for the employer, and expects you to deal with the cancellation on your last working day. Your work permit is a separate matter with its own steps, and you need the right authorisation in place before you start with the new employer. Plan all of these dates together, before you resign.
