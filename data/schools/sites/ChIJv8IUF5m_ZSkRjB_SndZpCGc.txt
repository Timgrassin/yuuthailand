 OUR FOUNDER & HEAD COACH, MARCELO SILVA DO NASCIMENTO

 MARCELO SILVA DO NASCIMENTO
 My name is Marcelo Silva, and I am originally from Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. I began my Jiu-Jitsu journey in 2000 as a young practitioner, and by 2013, I transitioned into teaching. Since then, I have dedicated myself to training athletes who have gone on to become champions at the state, national, and world levels in both Jiu-Jitsu and no-gi wrestling.
 As a certified referee, I have completed courses with multiple federations and remain actively involved in the refereeing community. I was honored to receive the Best Referee of the Year award from the Brazilian Confederation of No-Gi Luta Livre  in 2018, recognizing my commitment and expertise in the field. 
 Marcelo, our Black Belt practitioner, is committed to ensuring that our Academy offers the best group programs and personalized coaching. His expertise will help create an exceptional experience for all our members. With Marcelo&#8217;s guidance, you can look forward to a supportive environment that fosters growth and skill development. Join us and discover the difference! 
 &#8220;While my expertise lies in BJJ, my vision has always been to create a complete martial arts academy. By incorporating Muay Thai, we now offer our students a comprehensive curriculum covering both the best of grappling and striking. I am proud to lead this new chapter of our academy.&#8221;
 Head Muay Thai & MMA Striking Coach, Kru Viroj Wanram

 Kru Viroj Wanram
 Looking for top-tier Muay Thai training ? Meet Kru Viroj Wanram , the Head Muay Thai Instructor at Marcelo Silva Martial Arts . Kru Viroj offers a rare and powerful training experience by fusing razor-sharp Traditional Muay Thai striking with an accomplished background in Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) and grappling.
 Experience & Achievements Kru Viroj is a complete martial artist who has mastered both striking and ground fighting. His competitive accolades prove his versatility:
 Lam Sing MMA Champion (2014): Demonstrated the effective application of Muay Thai power within the MMA cage.
 PMD Champion (2015): Highlighted his consistent success in competitive combat sports.
 Jiu-Jitsu National Selection (2013): Proved high-level grappling skills and ground control-essential for modern self-defense.
 Volunteer Trainer: A dedicated teacher committed to community service and martial arts development.
 Qualifications & Philosophy Train with confidence under a fully certified professional. Kru Viroj holds an official Muay Thai Trainer Certificate and is certified in CPR , ensuring a safe training environment.
 What to Expect in Class: 
 Solid Foundations: Focus on correct form, explosive power, and fight strategy.
 Striking for MMA: Learn to manage distance and seamlessly transition between striking and takedowns—perfect for self-defense or aspiring fighters.
 Expert Instruction: Whether your goal is professional competition or simply the best striking workout of your life, Kru Viroj delivers results.
