 We provide effective and cost-efficient services on company registrations, amendments of corporate structures, such as, change of share structure, directors, objectives, addresses, among others. We likewise provide drafting and review of commercial agreements, i.e., Shareholders’ Agreements, Share Purchase Agreements, Service Agreements and the like.
 In addition, we assist clients in opening corporate bank accounts as well as VAT and SSO
registrations.
 We aim to provide our clients with the most convenient and practical services related to estate management and the sale and purchase of properties. From the initial negotiations and offers, drafting of relevant contracts, due diligence of subject properties up to the registration of ownership at the Land Office, we have our clients covered.
 Our coordination with respected agents, developers and property owners has a proven record
of hassle-free registration of property purchases, transfers and leases.
 Relative to our Property Law and Family Law practices, we assist our clients in drafting their will under Thai Law. The will shall govern the distribution of his assets in Thailand in the event of his death. These assets may include real or personal properties such as condominium units, motor vehicles, personal effects, jewelries, bank accounts and others.
 In the event of probate of the will, our litigation lawyers are also handling probate cases.
 Our team of family lawyers and their expertise will facilitate our clients in all aspects of matrimonial and family law. We can assist our clients in the drafting of prenuptial agreements and registration of marriage with the District Office.
 In collaboration with our litigation team, in any case that the marriage ends up in divorce, we likewise represent our clients in both non-contested or contested divorce, including support and child custody cases.
 The hiring and maintenance of the workforce is vital for any business. If you are an employer in Thailand, it is very important to know the labor laws and regulations set forth by the government to ensure that you are providing the labor standards to your employees. By following these labor and employment regulations, an employer may reduce the risk of legal disputes and maintain a good reputation in the business community.
 We can assist you with your labor and employment-related matters in your business to ensure that your business is in compliance with Thai labor laws. We also provide drafting of employment contracts, employer and employee registrations with the Social Security Office and registration of tax ID for the employees.
 Litigation is the process of taking a case to a court of law for settling controversies. Some lawsuits may have quick resolutions while other cases may take several years before judgment can be rendered. The litigation process depends on the complexity of issues in each case.
 Before filing a lawsuit, our team of litigation attorneys will initially gather all the relevant facts and necessary evidence to support your case. Our lawyers will evaluate your case, develop strategies and plan the entire litigation aspects to help our clients resolve their legal issues efficiently and effectively.
 Any foreign national who wishes to stay in Thailand either in short-term or long-terms stays need an appropriate visa in order to avoid being apprehended by authorities for violation of the immigration rules.
 At SCJ, our immigration lawyers and consultants help foreigners in keeping their stay in Thailand worry-free. From the simple tourist visa applications to a more complex non-immigrant O or B visas, we got you covered. We compile and review the required documentation relevant to your visa application, assist at the Immigration Office and until your passports are validly stamped with the right visas.
 SCJ also offers a wide range of foreign visa application services. We assist locals and foreigners who wish to obtain different types of foreign visas, i.e., tourist, business, spouse among others for the US and Canada, the UK and Schengen countries.
