 Thai Cooking Course Hua Hin
 We are very passionate about Thai food and love to teach others how to cook and prepare it.  Our Thai cooking classes have been running since 1999 and we teach classical Thai cookery in a professional but relaxed atmosphere.  Our workshops are very interactive and hands-on.  Attendees explore the local market, study the concepts behind Thai cooking and learn by doing as they create their own delicious dishes.  Read more..

 Your introduction to the Thai Kitchen
 Our one day course starts at 9am with a shopping trip to Hua Hin’s 100 year old morning market. Here we will buy all the ingredients that we will need for the day together.
 From the market we will continue to our traditional Thai teak home, where we will take a short coffee break and start making preparations for the course.
 cont&#8230; &#8220;Learn by doing&#8221; 
 Cook delicious Thai food
 During our most popular course we will teach you how to make curry-paste the traditional Thai way, followed by the first dish: Keang Keaw Wan Gai (green curry with chicken).
 First we will prepare the ingredients together. Then after discussing the nuances of the dish, it will be your turn to make your own. Our English speaking instructors will guide you as needed.
 cont.. &#8220;Authentic recipes&#8221; 
 Dishes worth learning about
 Other dishes on this course are Pad Thai (famous Thai style noodles), Tom Yum Goong (4 flavour soup w/ shrimp) & Khanom Lot Chong (jackfruit dessert).
 We only use the freshest ingredients & authentic, original recipes.  But we will give you lots of ideas for adapting to a western kitchen.  We often hear from our attendees about the food they cook: &#8220;this is the tastiest Thai food I have had on my entire trip&#8221;.  cont.. &#8220;Great Value&#8221; 
 A day of learning & fun
 The classes start at 9am and finish by around 1pm.  During busy times of the year we also offer afternoon/evening workshops.
 The fee for the class is 1,800 baht & 1,000 baht for kids 11 and under .  Included in the course are transportation from and back to your hotel within Hua Hin town, bottled drinking water, a Certificate, our own recipe booklet and 4 full size delicious Thai dishes.  We recommend a light breakfast;)  Reserve your place now 
1 Day Course - Yum Woon Sen
 Here is a video of our Chief Instructor, Beau, preparing and cooking Yum Woon Sen (Spicy Glass Noodle Salad). This is a really tasty and healthy dish that you can maybe have a go at. You will also see Hua Hin's main fresh market, "Talad Chat Chai". We visit and shop here each morning as part of every cooking class.
