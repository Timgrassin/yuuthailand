 Before you train
 What people ask us.
 Still unsure about something? Send us a message and we will help you plan your visit.
 Do I need my own gloves and gear? 
 No. Gloves, hand wraps and shin guards are free to borrow at the gym, in adult and kids' sizes. There is no deposit and no ID to leave. A small shop at the gym sells gear and E.N. merch if you would rather have your own.
 Are complete beginners welcome? 
 Yes. Group classes welcome first-timers. Tell the coach it is your first session so the pace and corrections can be adjusted.
 Do I need to book a group class? 
 No. Group classes are drop-in. Check the current schedule, arrive for the morning or afternoon session, and pay at the gym.
 What should I wear and bring? 
 Wear comfortable sports clothing and bring a water bottle. There is a free refill station at the gym, and training gear is provided.
 Can I train only once while visiting Krabi? 
 Yes. A single group-class drop-in is 400 THB. Longer passes are available for visitors training over several days or weeks.
 How do I request private training? 
 Choose Request a private class on the Train with us page, add your date and experience, and WhatsApp opens with the message already written.
 Can two people train privately together? 
 Yes. A one-hour private session for two students with one coach is listed at 1,200 THB, subject to coach availability.
