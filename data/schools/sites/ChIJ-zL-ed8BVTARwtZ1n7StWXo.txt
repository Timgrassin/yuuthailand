 Welcome to &#8220;C&M&#8221; 
 Koh Phangan Vocational School 
 Explore the richness of Thai culture on Koh Phangan 
 At &#8220;C&M&#8221; Koh Phangan Vocational School, we are more than just a learning center.
 Established in November 2011 by two passionate Thai sisters, our Culture Center is driven by pure love and a deep desire to share the beauty of Thai culture through engaging activities and creative approaches.
 Inspired by the meaningful concept of &#8220;ENJOY LEARNING NEW THINGS&#8221; , our commitment is to provide a welcoming space where each day brings new opportunities for visitors to uncover the profound richness behind Thai traditions.
 Join us in this journey of cultural exploration, where every lesson is infused with the joy of learning and the power of sharing .
