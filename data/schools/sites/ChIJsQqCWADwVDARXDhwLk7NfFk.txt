 Welcome To Samui Good Herb, Thai Cooking Class

 Samui Good Herb , Thai Cooking Class Samui is operate by local people that want the tourist who like Thai food able to cook at home by concept “ Thai food is not difficult “
 We look forward to welcoming you to our class, introduce ingredients, herb and health benefit and you can pick up some herbs in the herb garden. You will learn how to prepare and cook many favorite Thai dishes from our recipes. You can eat dish by dish, a delicious three-dishes meal prepared by you or eat at the end of the class, and can take away too.
 Motto is: 
 You will learn more than cooking
 Keep it simple, easy, use natural ingredients
 We hope our class to be the sauce of learning and can do self- sufficient economic, low carbon footprint as much as we can and serve for Samui Going Green with Sustainable Tourism
 I hope Samui Good herb will be one of the memorable highlights of your holiday at Samui.
 Enjoy the food you have created, just the perfect thing, when you want a break from the beach and shopping !
