 Rose Marie Academy: 
An American Curriculum Private School 
in Greater Bangkok
 Rose Marie Academy is an internationally-oriented inclusive private school situated in Pakkret, Nonthaburi, Thailand. It is run under the guidance and supervision of Rose Marie Wanchupela, a professional American teacher with over forty years experience teaching in Thai and international schools.
 Rose Marie Academy provides academic options for students and their parents which are not readily available in the Thai educational system nor in international schools
 About Rose Marie Academy 
 Programs
 Rose Marie Academy offers accredited programs that focus on the holistic development of all students. Our programs are registered with the Thai Ministry of Education and is accredited by Cognia
