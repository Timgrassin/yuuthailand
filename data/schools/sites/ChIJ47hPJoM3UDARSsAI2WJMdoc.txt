 An IB World School 
 HEI Schools Phuket is proud to be an IB World School , offering the International Baccalaureate (IB) Primary Years Programme (PYP) as part of our commitment to providing a high-quality, internationally recognised education.
 Our IB PYP is designed to nurture curious, knowledgeable and caring young learners through a student-centred, inquiry-based approach . Rather than simply memorising information, students are encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas, make connections and apply their learning to meaningful, real-world situations. This approach supports children in developing not only strong academic foundations, but also essential skills such as critical thinking, communication, collaboration, creativity and problem-solving .
 The IB framework also places strong emphasis on developing the whole child . Students are encouraged to become internationally minded, respectful and responsible members of their communities, while developing the confidence and independence to take ownership of their learning.
 Growing with Our Learners 
 As HEI Schools Phuket continues to grow, so does our educational pathway. In 2027 , we plan to extend our IB education to students aged 11 and above , beginning with Grade 6 through the IB Middle Years Programme (MYP) .
 The MYP builds naturally upon the foundations of the PYP, providing students with a challenging and engaging programme that connects academic learning with the world around them. Through interdisciplinary learning, inquiry and real-world applications, students are supported in becoming independent thinkers, effective communicators and confident lifelong learners .
 Our vision is to create a continuous educational journey in which children can grow from curious young learners into capable, reflective and internationally minded young people—equipped with the knowledge, skills and mindset to thrive in an ever-changing world.
