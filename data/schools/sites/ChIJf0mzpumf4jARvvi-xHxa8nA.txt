 About Bubble Language School
 A friendly Thai & English language school near BTS On Nut
 Bubble Language School is a Thai and English language school in Bangkok, a short walk from BTS On Nut on Sukhumvit 81 (Watthana). We offer Thai group classes , English group classes , private Thai and English lessons , and IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC and SAT preparation — onsite, online or hybrid.
 Our small classes, flexible schedules and friendly native-speaker teachers make learning practical, personal and fun, whether you are new to Thailand, improving your English for work, or preparing for university.

 Common questions
 Where is Bubble Language School? 
 We are at 161 Soi Sukhumvit 81, Watthana, Bangkok — a short walk from BTS On Nut. You can also study with us online.
 What courses do you offer? 
 Thai group classes, English group classes, private Thai and English lessons, and IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC and SAT preparation for teens and adults.
 Is the consultation really free? 
 Yes. Visit us, call or chat on LINE — we'll help you find the right class at no cost.
 I'm a complete beginner. Can I join? 
 Of course. All classes welcome beginners, and we place you at the right level.
 When can I start a group class? 
 Group classes run every week and you can join any time.
 Can I study online? 
 Yes — onsite, online or a mix of both.
