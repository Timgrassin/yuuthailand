 Application Process 
 How to apply for the
Thailand Privilege Visa. 
 A clear, government-compliant process. Background check is around 4–6 weeks; the whole application process typically takes 6–8 weeks. Start your Thailand Privilege Visa application when you're ready.
 01 
 Apply
 Submit your passport bio page, photo, signed application form, and PDPA consent. The application requires no income proof, bank statements, or medical exam. (Proof of funds may be requested by Immigration when your visa is affixed on arrival.)
 02 
 Background Check
 Clearance by five Thai government agencies. Around 4–6 weeks. We handle every submission and communication on your behalf.
 03 
 Approval & Payment
 Once approved, you receive an official approval letter. Only then do you pay the one-time membership fee — never before.
 04 
 Visa Issued
 Your Privilege Entry Visa sticker is affixed in your passport — at Chaengwattana Immigration in Bangkok, or on arrival at Suvarnabhumi or Phuket airport.

 Frequently Asked 
 Common questions about the
Thailand Privilege Visa. 
 Quick answers to the questions most applicants ask before starting. For the full list, see our complete Thailand Privilege Visa FAQ .
 What is the Thailand Privilege Visa? 
 + 
 The Thailand Privilege Visa (formerly Thailand Elite Visa) is an official long-term residency program operated by Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd., a state enterprise under the Tourism Authority of Thailand. It offers visas ranging from 5 to 20 years with VIP benefits, launched in 2003 and rebranded to 'Thailand Privilege' in late 2023.
 What is the difference between Thailand Privilege and Thailand Elite? 
 + 
 They are the same program. 'Thailand Elite' was the original brand from 2003-2023. It was rebranded to 'Thailand Privilege' in October 2023, and a new tier structure (Bronze, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Reserve) replaced the legacy Elite Easy Access, Elite Plus, and Elite Superiority products.
 How much does the Thailand Privilege Visa cost? 
 + 
 The Thailand Privilege Visa costs ฿650,000 (Bronze, 5 years) to ฿5,000,000 (Reserve, 20 years) as a one-time fee covering the entire membership duration. Payment is only collected after your application is approved by Thai authorities — rejected applicants pay nothing.
 How do I apply for the Thailand Privilege Visa? 
 + 
 Submit your passport bio page, a digital photograph, and a signed application form. The background check by five Thai government agencies takes around 4 to 6 weeks; the whole application process typically takes 6 to 8 weeks, with visa affixation booked after that. Payment is only collected after approval.
 Is Thailand Privilege a government program? 
 + 
 Yes. Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd. is a state enterprise fully owned by the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT). The visa is officially issued by the Thai government and recognized at all Thai ports of entry.
 Do I need to prove income or show bank statements? 
 + 
 No income proof, Thai bank deposit, or financial disclosure is required to apply — a genuine advantage over the retirement and LTR visas. Note that Thai Immigration may still ask to see proof of funds when your visa is affixed on arrival at the airport, and may ask about the source of the funds used to pay the fee; this varies by officer, and your Elite Personal Liaison will brief you on what to have ready.
