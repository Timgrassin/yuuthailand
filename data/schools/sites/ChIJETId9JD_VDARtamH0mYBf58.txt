 Before you apply
 What else you need for the DTV
 We provide the proof of your activity. These are the documents you prepare yourself.
 New since 31 August 2026: you must apply from your country of citizenship or permanent residence, and a police clearance certificate is mandatory.
 Passport Valid for at least 6 months 
 Proof of funds At least THB 500,000 (about €13,000), held for 3 months 
 Police clearance Issued within the last 6 months 
 Proof of residence In the country where you apply 
 Accommodation in Thailand Hotel booking or your host's details

 FAQ
 Questions we get every day
 Can I apply for the DTV from inside Thailand? No. Since 31 August 2026 you have to apply at the Thai embassy or e-Visa office responsible for your country of citizenship or permanent residence. Border runs to neighbouring countries no longer work for most applicants.
 Do I really have to attend the classes? Yes. The DTV is granted for a real activity. You join one 3-hour class per week on a day you choose, and you receive a certificate of completion at the end.
 How long does it take to get my documents? We prepare your enrollment letter, curriculum and receipt as soon as your payment is confirmed. Message us with your planned arrival date and we'll tell you exactly when your documents will be ready.
 What happens if my visa is refused? We refund your course fee minus a THB 5,000 processing fee, as long as you have not attended a class yet. Just send us the refusal notice. The embassy's visa fee is not refundable.
 Can my partner and children come too? Yes. Your spouse and children can apply as dependants of your DTV. They do not need to book their own course.
 How long can I stay? The DTV is valid for 5 years with multiple entries. Each entry gives you up to 180 days, and you can extend once per entry for another 180 days at immigration.
 How do I pay? By bank transfer or online payment. Message us on WhatsApp or email visa@proudhomecooking.com and we'll send you the details.
