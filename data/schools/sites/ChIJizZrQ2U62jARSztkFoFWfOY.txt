 Study on the picturesque campus of Chiang Mai University
 Learn Thai CMU at Chiang Mai University Language Institute offers a wide variety of courses and programs to suit your needs. All courses are delivered by professional and experienced instructors from Chiang Mai University. Learners will have the chance to experience what its like to study at a university in Thailand. We are confident that by studying our courses, you will be communicating more fluently and accurately in Thai. For those who wish to broaden their knowledge of Thailand and Southeast Asia, our Lecture Series brings academics from across the breadth of Chiang Mai University to deliver content designed to enhance your understanding on a variety of different issues. All courses offered through Learn Thai CMU will be accompanied with a Non-Immigrant Education visa supported by Chiang Mai University for up to 1-year.
