 WHY CHOOSE JENTANA & ASSOCIATES?
 For every J&A student our primary aim is to make learning Thai a fun experience in every possible way. During each lesson a J&A student will enjoy learning Thai language needed for daily life in Thailand in a relaxed, friendly and natural learning environment.
 J&A offers to its students a complete syllabus for Speaking, Reading & Writing Thai from Basic level to Advanced with lesson programmes tailored to suit both individual and business needs.
 Experienced J&A teachers guide each student through a question and answer approach to learning Thai that prepare most students to start speaking conversational Thai almost from the first lesson.
 The J&A teaching methods and study books have been refined over the past 25 years to ensure that every J&A student can maximise their learning experience to “Speak Thai with Confidence”
 Contact J&A now and speak personally to Jentana for an obligation free discussion on learning Thai.
 A TIME AND PLACE FOR EVERYTHING
 Learning Thai has never been easier with a variety of learning environments available to suit all needs. Students are welcome to study in one of the private study rooms at the J&A School located in Sukhumvit Soi 31 road or alternatively from the comfort of home or office. If meeting a teacher in person is not possible then Online Lessons via SKYPE can be easily arranged.
 J&A offers to its students both Private & Group lessons at a time and location that is convenient to the demands of a busy life in Bangkok.
 Students can enjoy 90 minutes lessons that start between the hours of 7.00 a.m. to 7.30 p.m. from Monday to Saturday with the office being closed every Sunday.
