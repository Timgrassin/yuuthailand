# Visa rules reviewer shortlist

Compiled 6 Nov 2026 for one fixed-fee review of about 12 visa tracks and the `rules` table, plus an optional review of the PDPA consent text. Nothing has been sent.

Every fact below comes from the linked page as it read on 6 Nov 2026. "Not verified" means the page we read did not show it. Before paying anyone, check their name on the Lawyers Council of Thailand register (lawyerscouncil.or.th) and ask for their licence number in writing.
