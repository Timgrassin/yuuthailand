1. Who this route fits

You want to train seriously for months. You can pay the full course up front. You can show 500,000 THB in savings.2

2. What counts as a real enrollment

The gym must hold a valid license. Sports Authority of Thailand accreditation is the common proof.3

Embassies look for a long course, often 6 months or more. Some applicants were asked for 9.4 Drop-in classes do not count.

3. Documents you need

  • Enrollment letter on the gym's letterhead, signed
  • Receipt for the full course, not a deposit
  • Training schedule with start and end dates
  • Copy of the gym's business license
  • Bank statements in your name2

4. Where embassies differ

London added two documents on 31 Aug 2026.5 Berlin applicants report a 3-month statement window.6 Check your embassy page before you pay.

5. Why applications fail

ReportedFrom applicants, not official

  • Receipt shows a deposit, not the full fee
  • Statements in a partner's name
